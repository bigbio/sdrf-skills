## Study
Hutti CR, Welle KA, Hryhorenko JR, Ghaemmaghami S. "Global analysis of protein degradation in prion infected cells." *Sci Rep* 2020;10:10800, doi:10.1038/s41598-020-67505-5, PMID 32612191, PMC7329860 (CC BY). Full text and Supplementary Tables S1–S6 came from the PMC OA S3 bucket. Not in sdrf-annotated-datasets (`sdrf-tools curated` returned none); not in DUPLICATES.md or BLOCKED.md before this run.

The study is **dynamic SILAC (dSILAC)**. N2a-Cl3 mouse neuroblastoma cells were:
- **−QA**: infected with the RML prion strain;
- **+QA**: the same cells cleared of infection with quinacrine, then grown for one week without quinacrine.

Cells were switched to medium with 13C6-Arg and 13C6-Lys and harvested after 1, 2, 3 and 5 days. Light = the pre-existing proteome; heavy = protein made during the label period.

SILAC check (rule 14):
- the PK and NaBu `summary.txt` have Multiplicity 2, Labels1 Arg6;Lys6;
- the whole-proteome `msms.txt` carries Labeling State 0/1 in every run.

## Why 10 whole-proteome runs are blocked
Heavy share of identified MS/MS per run (msms.txt). Within the R arm, these runs contradict their day:

| run | value | rest of the group | fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1-7 | 0.815 | R1 0.53–0.55 | day 2 |
| R2-7 | 0.599 | R2 0.77–0.85 | day 1 |
| R3-5..8 | 0.97 | R3-1..4 0.87–0.88 | day 5 |
| R5-5..8 | 0.91–0.93 | R5-1..4 0.95–0.96 | day 3 |

The Q arm is monotone. The medians agree: MaxQuant "RML day 3" has median H/L 7.41, higher than "RML day 5" at 7.28. This looks like two file or tube swaps, but no source says so. Rule 6 and the PXD023790 precedent apply: the runs are left out, not re-labelled. The published RML kinetics include them as named.

Because those fractions were removed, `build` would renumber R1-8 and R2-8 as fraction 7. The scripted post-build step `fix_fractions.py` restores fraction 8 from the file name and asserts it equals msmsScans `Fraction`. It changed 4 cells and is justified in validation.txt (rule 17).
