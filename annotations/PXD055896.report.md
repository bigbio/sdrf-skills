SU-DHL-10 DLBCL cells grown 7 d in light SILAC RPMI 1640, treated 24 h with 12.5 nM zotatifin or DMSO (3 independent cultures each), pulsed with heavy Lys8/Arg10 for the last 8 h, digested, TMT-labelled, pooled, high-pH RP fractionated into 11 fractions, SPS-MS3 on an Orbitrap Fusion Lumos.
