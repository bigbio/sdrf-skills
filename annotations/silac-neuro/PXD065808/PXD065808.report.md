# PXD065808 - screening report (SILAC x neurological campaign)

**Label: exclude** | failed criterion: **2** (neurological disease) | STATUS: EXCLUDED, no SDRF written.

Publication: Heo S ... Huganir RL. *Sex- and experience-dependent regulation of synaptic protein
turnover.* PNAS 2026 123(28):e2602111123 (PMC13367811, PMID 42412931). Full text read via Europe PMC.

## Criteria
1. SILAC-type labelling - **pass.** SILAM pulse-chase: dams and pups on Lys-13C6 "heavy diet" until
   10 wk, then chase on Lys-12C6 (Methods, manuscript line 55); RIA-based half-life fitting.
2. Neurological disease - **fail.** Every animal is wild-type C57Bl/6J (Jackson), Methods line 53/55/60.
   Groups are sex (male vs female) and experience (contextual fear conditioning vs control) over
   chase timepoints (0 wk, 1 wk, 1/3/6 mo). No disease model, no mutant/knockout line (Shank3 KO,
   Cdkl5, Taok1 models are cited only from other studies in the Discussion), no patient material.
   ASD enters only post hoc: turnover hits were cross-referenced to the human SFARI ASD risk-gene list
   (Methods line 75) and discussed as possible relevance to sex differences in psychiatric risk.
   Shank3 is a protein whose turnover was measured in wild-type hippocampal PSD, not studied in a
   disease context. This is disease-as-interpretation/background, which rule 2 excludes.
3. No existing SDRF - not assessed further (moot).
4. Vendor raw deposited - pass (40 Thermo .raw, plus MaxQuant txt.zip and FASTA).
5. Mapping - likely recoverable from file names (PSD_<timepoint><C|FC><rep>, PSD_Female_...),
   not assessed further (moot).
