# PXD012462 — screening report (SILAC x neurodegenerative disease campaign)

> **Criteria (added after review).** Screened under `silac_neuro.md`, the campaign's first criteria file, before the scope was widened to neurological diseases (`silac_neurological.md`). Under the widened rule 2 the decision stands: the three post-subarachnoid-hemorrhage patients are a source of ventricular CSF for a protein-turnover method study; the hemorrhage is not studied and Alzheimer's disease appears only as motivation, so the disease is not the study's subject under either version of rule 2.

**Label: exclude** | failed criterion: **rule 2** | STATUS: EXCLUDED (no SDRF written)

## Evidence
- Publication: Lehmann S ... Colinge J. *In Vivo Large-Scale Mapping of Protein Turnover in Human
  Cerebrospinal Fluid.* Anal Chem 2019, PMID 31730336, doi:10.1021/acs.analchem.9b03328 (closed access;
  abstract only in Europe PMC). Full text read from the bioRxiv preprint of the same work,
  doi:10.1101/710418 (JATS XML, saved as preprint.xml), plus the PRIDE record (evidence/pride.json).

## Rule 1 — SILAC-type metabolic labelling used for quantification: PASS
Deposit protocol and preprint Online Methods: in vivo 13C6-leucine infusion (SILK; 10 min bolus 2 mg/kg
then 8h50 at 2 mg/kg/h), ventricular CSF sampled at 10 time points over 24 h; Skyline quantifies
light vs 13C6-Leu-shifted (+6 Da/Leu) peak areas, ratio A'1/(A1+A'1). PRIDE's "MS1 label-free" quant
field is wrong for this study: the quantification is light/heavy isotope ratios.

## Rule 2 — neurodegenerative disease context: FAIL
Preprint Results: "Ventricular CSF and blood were collected from three **post subarachnoid hemorrhage
patients** ... patient 1, who was chosen for HRMS profiling" (the deposited Impact II runs). Online
Methods: "Patients hospitalized in neurosurgery unit having in place a temporary ventricular
derivation of the CSF were enrolled." No participant has a neurodegenerative disease; no disease
protein is studied in a neurodegeneration context. Alzheimer's disease / ALS appear only as
motivation (project description), as the name of the umbrella clinical protocol ("In Vivo Alzheimer
Proteomics (PROMARA)", NCT02263235) and as funding ("Plan Alzheimer"). Per the criteria, disease named
only as background motivation does not count. (A follow-up 2024 paper, PMC11232576, from the same
protocol likewise describes a subarachnoid-hemorrhage patient, P017.)
