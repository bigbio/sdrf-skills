## Study
- PRIDE PXD004561, "Proteome-wide changes in proteostasis in C. elegans models of longevity and age-related disease". PRIDE links no publication.
- Publication found through Europe PMC by author/title: Visscher M et al., *Cell Rep* 2016;16:3041-3051, doi:10.1016/j.celrep.2016.08.025, PMID 27626671. The paper is not in PMC, and the cell.com PDF returned 403. The full text, including the Supplemental Experimental Procedures, came from the Ghent University repository copy (biblio.ugent.be/publication/8121767). The paper lists "PXD004561" under Accession Numbers.
- Design: pulsed SILAC. Worms were switched to HT115Δlys *E. coli* grown on 15N2-13C6-L-Lys and harvested at several pulse times. MaxQuant SILAC H/L ratios give the "time until half of the protein is labeled heavy" (THPLH).

## Screen (campaign SILAC x neurodegeneration): **include**
1. SILAC: yes. The pulsed Lys8 label is present in both deposited MaxQuant searches (multiplicity 2, labelMods Lys8).
2. Neurodegenerative disease: yes. Strain OW40 (zgIs15[P(unc-54)::α-synuclein::YFP]) is the paper's Parkinson's model, compared with the YFP-only control OW450. **Deposited runs include it**: `20160205_F1_RM_SH_OW40_{6,24,48}h_{AF,WF}.raw` (day-5 adults, Fig. 5). The developing OW40 experiment (Fig. 3) cannot be identified among the deposited runs (see below).
3. No SDRF in sdrf-annotated-datasets (given by the caller). PRIDE has no submitter SDRF.
4. 82 Thermo .raw files are deposited.
5. The map can be recovered for 68 of the 82 runs. Run names match the MaxQuant experiment and fraction design. Pulsed SILAC needs no cross-sample channel map: light and heavy come from the same worms.

The two sub-experiments are split because their deposited searches differ. Day-5 (`oldworms.7z/mqpar.xml`) uses Acetyl (Protein N-term) and Oxidation (M) as variable modifications. Developing (`20160223_..._Bio21.7z/mqpar.xml`) also has Deamidation (NQ) as a variable modification. Both have Carbamidomethyl C as fixed and Lys8 as the label, and both use MaxQuant 1.5.2.8.

- Templates: ms-proteomics v1.1.0 and invertebrates v1.1.0 (C. elegans). Both are pinned with `-t name@1.1.0`, the released versions in spec templates.yaml. `comment[sdrf version]` = v1.1.0. invertebrates licenses `strain or breed` and `genotype`.
- Factor values: genotype and sampling time (hours on heavy Lys). The day-5 file also has disease (OW40 = Parkinson disease, MONDO:0005180; all others = normal).
- Runs: 68 annotated out of 82 deposited.
- **Excluded: 14 runs**, `20141107_F1_HRV_SM_B1-B7` and `C1-C7`. Their search (`20141222_sasha.7z`, experimentalDesignTemplate.txt) labels the experiments only as letters A-L by digits 1-7 (84 runs, only B and C deposited). Two 7-time-point experiments exist: developing OW40/OW450 and the daf-2 vs daf-2;daf-16 duplicate (Fig. S4). Nothing public says which strain B and C are, or which hour each digit is. Assigning them would be guessing.

## Key values and evidence
- **Instrument:** Orbitrap Fusion (MS:1002416), from the raw header method summary and the paper ("Orbitrap Fusion Tribrid"). PRIDE says Orbitrap Fusion ETD, but neither the raw file nor the paper supports that.
- **MS method:** DDA, HCD at 30 NCE, MS2 in the ion trap, MS1 scan range 400-1500 m/z (raw method).
- **Tolerances:** 4.5 ppm precursor, 0.5 Da fragment (MaxQuant ITMS), from mqpar.xml.
- **Reagents:** DTT and IAA (paper and PRIDE protocol).
- **Enzymes:** Trypsin (MS:1001251) and Lys-C (MS:1001309), as two repeated `comment[cleavage agent details]` columns. The source is "100 ng trypsin + Lys-C (Promega)": both were added together, with no sequential step.
- **Modifications:** from the deposited mqpar.xml, read with `sdrf-tools search-params`. UNIMOD accessions 1, 4, 7, 35 and 259 were all checked in OLS.
- **Labels:** SILAC light / SILAC heavy. Source names carry `_Lys0` / `_Lys8` so that the light and heavy pools of the same population get unique row coordinates.
- **Sample fields:**
  - developmental stage: `adult` (EFO:0001272) for day-5 worms. `not available` for developing worms, because a 4-24 h pulse from L4 runs from L4 into young adulthood.
  - organism part: whole body (BTO, as in PRIDE).
  - material type: whole organism.
  - biological replicate: 1.

## Limitations / open items (also in the manifests)
- **AF/WF meaning is unknown.** The suffixes are annotated only as MaxQuant fractions 1 and 2. No fractionation method is claimed.
- **14 runs (B/C) are unannotatable**, as described above.
- **Search settings differ from the paper text.** Paper and PRIDE say MaxQuant 1.5.1.0 with deamidation + oxidation. The deposited searches are 1.5.2.8, add N-term acetyl, and the day-5 search has no deamidation. The deposited search was followed.
- **Lys8 is written as `MT=Variable`**, which is what search-params emits.
- **"Bio21" naming** suggests biological replicate 2 / technical replicate 1 of a series. Biological replicate is set to 1 within this file.
- **Developmental stage for developing worms is `not available`.**
- **Points for the reviewer to check hardest:**
  - disease = Parkinson disease for the α-syn worm model
  - the `_Lys0` / `_Lys8` split of source names
  - the generic Fusion instrument term versus Fusion ETD
  - leaving out the B/C runs
