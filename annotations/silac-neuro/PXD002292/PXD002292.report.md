## Key decisions and evidence
- Cell line: hCMEC/D3, CVCL_U985. Cellosaurus cross-references PXD002292 and PMID 26718731. Sex female, age group adult,
  origin temporal lobe (UBERON:0001871) blood microvessel, endothelial cell (CL:0000115).
- Disease: `normal`. Weksler 2005 (PMID 16141364) derives the line from "normal human brain endothelial cells".
  Ischemia is applied in vitro (the OGD treatment). It is not a donor property, so it is not written as a disease.
- Instrument: maXis impact (MS:1003462). Methods say "Maxis Impact Q-TOF" and both BAF headers contain "maXis impact".
  PRIDE's structured field gives the generic `maXis` (MS:1001541). Raw file and Methods win.
- Modifications come from the PRIDE-converted Mascot results (mzTab/PRIDE XML) and Methods:
  - Carbamidomethyl C, Fixed
  - Oxidation M, Variable
  - Label:13C(6) K (UNIMOD:188), Variable
  - Label:13C(6)15N(4) R (UNIMOD:267), Variable

  Heavy Lys is +6 Da (13C6). That matches Methods and the PRIDE PTM MOD:00544 "6x(13)C labeled residue", and it is
  not 13C6 15N2. The PRIDE mzTab header lists every modification as variable ("No fixed modifications searched").
  That is a converter artefact: 100% of PSM cysteines carry carbamidomethyl, and Methods say fixed.
- Tolerances 10 ppm / 0.05 Da come from Methods and the PRIDE XML search parameters. DDA top-20, trypsin.
- Each experiment's mixture was injected once, unfractionated, so fraction = 1 and technical replicate = 1.
