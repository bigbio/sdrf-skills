Study: Sampedro-Castañeda et al., *Nat Commun* 2023, "Epilepsy-linked kinase CDKL5 phosphorylates Cav2.3…" (PMC10713615, doi:10.1038/s41467-023-43475-w). Mouse primary cortical neurons (E16.5 male embryos, DIV12) from Cdkl5 KO (-/Y) and WT (+/Y) littermates. Each embryo gave two cultures, one in K4R6 and one in K8R10. ## Key decisions (attack these first)
1. **The design sheet is wrong in two places, and I overrode it.**
   - (a) MS method: raw headers say RW8 = Top10CID, RW9 = Top10MSA, RW10 = Top10HCD. The sheet says RW10 = CID, RW8 = MSA, RW9 = HCD.
   - (b) Incorporation runs: the sheet lists A1–A8 as K8R10 and A9–A16 as K4R6. The deposited incorporation search (summary.txt) labels A1–A8 Arg6;Lys4 and A9–A16 Arg10;Lys8. Its evidence.txt maps A1–A8 to "medium 2.1…3.5" (the calc sheet's name for K4R6). Median log2(H/L) for those labels is 3.7–5.9, which shows real incorporation.
   - Identities per run (A1/A9 = Control1, A3/A11 = KO1, …) are still taken from the sheet. The A1↔A9 … A8↔A16 pairing matches the calc-sheet culture IDs.
2. **Genotype per channel was checked independently.** In Phospho (STY)Sites.txt, normalized H/L for EB2 pS222 is 9.0, 5.4 and 5.8 in E1–E3 and 0.13, 0.23 and 0.24 in E4–E6. CDKL5 pS407 and Cav2.3 pS15 go the same way, and C1–C4 are about 1. Which KO/WT embryo sits in which mixture rests on the sheet alone.
3. **K4R6 is written as `SILAC light` and K8R10 as `SILAC heavy`.** This follows the authors and MaxQuant (there is no K0R0 arm). The label mods are listed as Variable: Label:2H(4) K, Label:13C(6) R, Label:13C(6)15N(2) K, Label:13C(6)15N(4) R.
4. **Controls 1–4 are same-sample in both channels, but their biology is unstated.** Only organism (Mus musculus, inferred) and biological replicate are filled. Everything else is `not available`.
5. **Technical replicate = injection index within a source.**
   - Phospho: CID = 1, MSA = 2, HCD = 3.
   - QC: incorporation = 1, mix check 1 = 2, mix check 2 = 3. Control 3/4 have no incorporation run, so their mix checks are 1 and 2.
6. **MSA runs are written as collision-induced dissociation at 35 NCE.** There is no PSI-MS term for multistage activation.

## Open items / limitations
- Cleavage agent is written as two columns.
  - Phospho SDRF and the QC RW5 runs: Trypsin (MS:1001251) then Lys-C (MS:1001309). The digest was trypsin overnight, then Lys-C 2 h plus trypsin overnight. The search enzyme was Trypsin/P.
  - QC RW3 runs (36 rows: incorporation and mix check 1): Trypsin and `not applicable`. Per Methods, these checks were taken after the first digest, which used trypsin only. Their raw headers read "Digest of …", where RW5 reads "Digest of Digest of …". This was reviewer finding qc-1. Reconcile is clean.
- Label convention: K4R6 is `SILAC light` in both files. The calculation sheet and its QC search experiments call it "medium". I kept "light" in QC so that the same culture dishes carry the same label term as in the approved phospho file, and to follow the authors and the main search. Note that there is no unlabelled arm.
- QC SDRF: precursor tolerance is not in the deposited QC search output (no mqpar.xml for those searches, and none in parameters.txt or summary.txt). I omitted it rather than assume a MaxQuant default, so convert-openms assumes 10 ppm.
- Trypsin on the A1–A16 incorporation lysate digests is inferred. The Methods digest text describes the mixed samples; the QC summary.txt protease is Trypsin/P.
- Control 1–4 organism part, cell type and material type are kept `not available`. They are probably from the same culture series, but nothing states it.
- The Arg→Pro conversion re-search (Pro5/Pro6) of A1–A16 is not represented.
- A13RW3 is called "…E2-blank" in the incorporation search. Its header says "Digest of LB-13" and it gives normal IDs and incorporation, so I treated it as the WT2 K4R6 sample.
- Reduction/alkylation reagents (DTT/IAA) are stated in Methods but not annotated.
- Developmental stage is "mouse embryo stage" (EFO:0005857), because EFO has no E16.5 term.
- Strain is written "C57BL/6" (backcrossed). The validator gives a warning only: it is free text in the template.
- mqpar.xml names some runs with a timestamp suffix. The SDRFs use the deposited file names.
