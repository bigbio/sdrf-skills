## Study
- PRIDE PXD031830, "Proteome-wide pulse labeling reveals amyloid-impaired protein degradation in Alzheimer's disease" (St Jude, Peng lab). It is linked to Yarbro et al., *Human and mouse proteomics reveals the shared pathways in Alzheimer's disease and delayed protein turnover in the amyloidome*, Nat Commun 2025 16:1533 (PMID 39934151, PMC11814087, doi:10.1038/s41467-025-56853-3).
- The deposit holds 400 Thermo .raw files, 400 JUMP pepXML files and one `PRIDE_sample_information.xlsx`. files/all lists 801 entries, and every entry's projectAccessions = PXD031830.
- It contains two sub-experiments, and both are annotated:
  1. **Protein turnover** (`mtob1_f001-f160`, `mtob2_f001-f160`; 320 runs): pulsed SILAC (Mouse Express L-Lysine-13C6 diet) combined with TMTpro-16. Samples are male WT and 5xFAD mice (~9 mo) at 0/4/8/16/32 days of labelling, 3 replicates each, in 2 batches. Each batch also carries one fully labelled SILAM reference (female WT, 8 mo) in TMT126. Tissue is cortex.
  2. **Plaque proteome** (`plaque_f001-f080`; 80 runs): LCM amyloid plaques and non-plaque areas from 5xFAD mice (3/8/12 mo), plus WT brain (8/12 mo), measured as one TMTpro-16 batch. There is **no SILAC** in these runs.

## Sample design and factor values
**Turnover**
- 32 source rows: 30 mice plus the SILAM reference, written as two sources (SILAM_B1, SILAM_B2) because the sample sheet does not say whether both batches used one reference lysate:
  - WT1-15 and FAD1-15, giving 3 replicates x 5 time points per genotype.
  - SILAM_B1 and SILAM_B2.
- `characteristics[age]` = 9M for the 30 mice and 8M for SILAM. The sheet's 'Age (mo)' column gives these values, and Supplementary Data 2 says '~9'.
- Biological replicate is (n-1) mod 3 + 1. This follows the sheet: WT1/2/3 are the three 0-day mice, WT4/5/6 are the 4-day mice, and so on.
- Factor values:
  - `factor value[genotype]` = 5xFAD / wild type
  - `factor value[exposure duration]` = 0/4/8/16/32 day. The SILAM rows have `not applicable`.

**Plaque**
- 16 sources:
  - 6 plaque and 6 non-plaque samples (5xFAD, 3/8/12 mo, 2 each)
  - 4 WT samples
- Factor values:
  - `factor value[genotype]`
  - `factor value[organism part]` = amyloid plaque (BTO:0002774) / brain (UBERON:0000955)
  - `factor value[age]` = 3M / 8M / 12M. This was added after review 1. WT controls exist only at 8 and 12 months, so genotype is confounded with age at 3 months.

**Both sub-experiments**
- Disease: Alzheimer disease (MONDO:0004975) on 5xFAD rows and normal (PATO:0000461) on WT rows. The disease is not broadcast onto controls.

**Reduction and alkylation**
- Plaque: DTT and IAA, from Methods; consistent with the static Carbamidomethyl in the search.
- Turnover: `not available`. The general Methods say DTT/IAA, but the turnover search has no Carbamidomethyl, and Methods apply it only "if the residue was alkylated".

## Values left as reserved words, and why
- `comment[precursor/fragment mass tolerance]` = not available in both SDRFs. The sources conflict: the paper says 10 ppm for both, while the PRIDE data-processing text says 6 ppm precursor and 15 ppm fragment.
- `characteristics[strain or breed]`:
  - 5xFAD rows = `B6.Cg-Tg(APPSwFlLon,PSEN1*M146L*L286V)6799Vas/Mmjax`. The Methods cite stock #034848, and the MMRRC catalog record for 034848 gives this name.
  - WT rows = not available, because no source names the WT background.
- `characteristics[sex]` = not available in the plaque SDRF. The plaque sheet has no sex column, and Supplementary Data 2 omits these mice.
- Turnover reduction and alkylation reagents: not available, as above.
- `characteristics[exposure duration]` = not applicable for SILAM, which was labelled for life over two generations (sheet value "inf").

## Inferred rather than stated
- `developmental stage` = adult, inferred from ages of 3-12 months.
- WT plaque-experiment rows: organism part = brain. The sheet's region for them is "N/A".
- SILAM batch assignment: the xlsx row that opens the batch-2 block is typed `Batch=1`. It is placed in batch 2 (mtob2, TMT126) by its position, because batch 2 would otherwise have no TMT126 sample.
- The two SILAM sources: they share one Sample ID. They are written as SILAM_B1 and SILAM_B2, one per batch, because `sdrf-tools structure` rejects mixed technical replicates within one run. The SDRF therefore does not assert that they are the same lysate.

## Conflicts and reconcile findings
- **reconcile BLOCKER on the plaque SDRF** (`run_names_name_another_organ`: "plaque" taken as carotid artery) is a keyword false positive. These are LCM amyloid plaques from brain sections (Methods "Collection of amyloid plaques by LCM"), and half of the rows say brain.
- **reconcile MINOR**: Trypsin is not named in the PRIDE record. It is supported by Methods and the pepXML.
- Supplementary Data 21 reports only 4 plaque vs 4 non-plaque samples (8-12 mo). The deposited batch also contains the 3-mo and WT channels, which are annotated from the PRIDE sheet.

## Limitations, which are open items
1. `characteristics[age]` is not in the contract of the pinned template set; it is a human-template column. It was added at the coordinator's and reviewer's request, and parse_sdrf accepts it.
2. Turnover reduction and alkylation stay `not available` after review-1 minor finding 2. The general Methods state DTT/IAA, but the deposited turnover search has no Cys carbamidomethylation. Its database is `..._c0`, while the plaque search uses `_c57`. Methods also add Carbamidomethyl only "if the residue was alkylated with iodoacetamide". So the Methods do not state DTT/IAA for this experiment specifically, and the search argues against IAA.
3. Whether plaque and non-plaque samples with the same index come from one mouse is not stated. Biological replicate numbers are per group only.
4. The mass tolerances, strain, plaque sex, turnover reduction/alkylation and SILAM batch issues described above.
