# PXD034016 - screening report (SILAC x neurodegenerative disease campaign)

**Label: exclude** | failed_criterion: 1 | STATUS: EXCLUDED (no SDRF written)

## Decision
The deposited runs are a label-free (spectral-counting) G4C2 RNA-oligo pulldown, not SILAC.
PRIDE's `quantificationMethods = SILAC (PRIDE:0000315)` is a deposit-metadata error; the
manuscript and the deposit's own protocol text both say label-free. Rule 1 fails.

## Evidence (manuscript > deposit metadata)
- Publication: Luteijn et al., Nucleic Acids Res 2025 53(7):gkaf253, PMID 40207633, PMC11983130
  (origin paper; it lists PXD034016 as its own deposit).
- Data availability: "PRIDE submission #586786: Content: G4C2 pulldown (30/32 files); Project
  Accession PXD034016" (the 2 missing files are PXD034097). By contrast the SILAC experiments are
  explicitly labelled "SF3B1 SILAC pulldown" (PXD034093) and "SRSF1 SILAC pulldowns" (PXD034092).
- Methods, RNA oligo IP: biotinylated (G4C2)4 RNA oligos on streptavidin beads, nuclear lysate of
  HeLa cells pre-treated with PlaB or DMSO (Results / Fig. 7D) - no isotope labelling described.
- Methods, Mass spectrometry: 16 gel slices per lane, Trypsin/Lys-C, Q Exactive, Mascot 2.4,
  Scaffold 4.6.4, "Spectral count was used as a proxy for protein abundance."
- Methods, SILAC: heavy/light HeLa (13C/15N Arg+Lys), MaxQuant 1.5.8.3 - applies ONLY to the
  SRSF1 and SF3B1 protein IPs, not to the G4C2 RNA pulldown.
- PRIDE record: sampleProcessingProtocol "Pulldown with G4C2 combined label-free analysis of
  purified proteins"; dataProcessingProtocol "Mascot search and Scaffold analysis of label-free
  data".
- File names: 30 x `PXP0486_G4C_{1,3}_{1..16}_BQE1_2018_*.raw` = 2 lanes x 16 gel slices
  (G4C_1_* complete; G4C_3_7 and G4C_3_13 missing, i.e. in PXD034097), acquired 2018, with no
  SILAC token - consistent with label-free GeLC-MS. The SILAC siblings are
  `PXP0486_ML_SILAC_*_2019_*`.
- No search output deposited (only `id_placeholder.xlsx`), so no SILAC search parameters exist.
