# SILAC neurodegenerative-disease campaign — inclusion criteria

1. SILAC-type metabolic labelling (SILAC, SILAM/SILAC mice or worms, pulsed/dynamic SILAC) is used
   in the deposited runs and is part of their quantification.
2. The study addresses a neurodegenerative disease (e.g. Parkinson's, Alzheimer's, Huntington's,
   ALS/FTD, spinocerebellar ataxia, prion disease): a disease model, patient material, or a
   disease protein studied explicitly in that disease context. Disease named only as background
   motivation does not count.
3. No SDRF for the accession exists in bigbio/sdrf-annotated-datasets (datasets/ or sandbox/),
   and PRIDE lists no submitter SDRF.
4. Vendor raw files are deposited and listed in the repository.
5. The run -> sample and SILAC channel -> sample mapping is recoverable from public evidence
   (file names, protocol text, publication or supplementary tables) without guessing.
