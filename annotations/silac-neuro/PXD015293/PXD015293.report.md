## Key decisions (attack these hardest)

1. **Two SDRFs, split by digest.** The A53T samples were digested in sequence. The LysC flow-through gave the `_LysC_` series, then trypsin on the retained material gave the `_Tryp_` series, and each series was fractionated into 24.
   - In one file, the LysC and Tryp series of the same mouse and injection would collide on (source, bio rep, tech rep, fraction).
   - Recoding the digests as technical replicates would be false.
   - The deposited search combined both series per mouse and replicate in one mzid. Anyone reprocessing A53T needs both SDRFs.
2. **Fraction identifier is the nominal fraction from the file name (revision 2).** Re-acquired fractions are separated by technical replicate (rep + 2n), as described above.
3. **Cleavage agent:**
   - The LysC files are Lys-C (MS:1001309).
   - The Tryp files declare Lys-C then Trypsin (MS:1001309, MS:1001251) since revision 3: trypsin was added to the retained material after the overnight Lys-C step, and the deposited mzid searched them with LysC specificity. `reconcile` is clean on the trypsin file.
   - *Superseded (revisions 1-2): the Tryp files declared Trypsin only, with an expected `cleavage_agent_incomplete` finding.*
4. **Modifications** come from the deposited mzid Peptide elements, which agree with the Methods:
   - Carbamidomethyl C, Fixed (UNIMOD:4)
   - Oxidation M, Variable (UNIMOD:35)
   - **Label:13C(6) K, Variable (UNIMOD:188, +6.020129)**: the SILAM heavy label. Lys-only labelling; no Arg label.
   - **Acetyl, Protein N-term, Variable (UNIMOD:1)**: in the mzid only, not in the prose. All 766 acetylated peptides in the PBS_rep2 mzid start with M and have pre='-'.
5. **Disease of a model mouse = the modelled disease** (MONDO:0005180), and controls are `normal`. The AAV1-GFP TetP-hA53T mouse carries the transgene, but the transgene is not induced, so it is `normal`.
6. **Organism part = midbrain** (UBERON:0001891). UBERON has no "ventral midbrain" term. The BTO "tegmentum" synonym was rejected because its bare label is ambiguous.
7. **Sex = not available on all rows.** Male and female mice were used and the per-mouse sex is unreported. The sex of the SILAM F3 pups is not stated; only the F2 dams are said to be female.
