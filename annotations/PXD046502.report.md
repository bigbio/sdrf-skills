- Conditions exist only as file-name abbreviations: `BSA`/`LINO` (HF190619), `TM_B`/`TM_P` (HF200217), `spleen`, `PDAC`, `CD8` (QEP runs).
- `Exp_des.txt` (the only design file) pairs spleen_6/8/9/10 with PDAC_1/3/4/5 and CD8_2…13 with QEP240701_RN_TM_PDAC_CD8_*, **none of which is deposited as raw**. The deposited PDAC_6/8/9/10 are not listed. `early`/`late` is undefined. Whether samples are sorted CD8+ T cells or whole tissue is not stated.
- The 8 deposited QEP240804_RN_CD8_* runs appear in no deposited EpiProfile output (`Manzo.zip` holds 28 non-deposited QEP240701 CD4/CD8 runs instead).
- Paper full text unreachable: Sci Immunol closed (Unpaywall), bioRxiv HTTP 429 (not bypassed), Europe PMC PPR965614 fullTextXML HTTP 500. The OA commentary PMC13390978 has no MS design.

Per campaign rules 6–8, conditions were not inferred from abbreviations (e.g. B/P = BSA/palmitate, LINO = linoleate). Rule 7 forbids keeping rows with `not available` conditions.
