# PXD014256 — annotation report

## Study

Single-cell proteomics of the embryonic day 15 (E15) chicken utricle, comparing presumptive hair
cells against progenitor (supporting) cells. Cells were FACS-sorted by FM1-43 uptake into nanoPOTS
nanowells. The headline result is that thymosin β4 (TMSB4X) drops ~10-fold as hair cells
differentiate.

### The screen row's "30 single cells" is wrong by one — and it is the paper that is right

The Methods state 5+5+10+10 = **30** single cells, and Figure 4 says "Analysis used 30 single cells".
Only **29** single-cell runs are deposited. This is not a parsing artifact: the deposited MaxQuant
`summary.txt` (`SEARCH/Experiment 1 + 2/txt 2019-05-09a/`) lists **62** raw files, and exactly one is
absent from PRIDE:

```
701376_Single_Hair_Cell_OHSU_1cell_Low_090618_4_30um_031218_300bar   (MaxQuant experiment 1L1d)
```

So the 30th single cell (an FM1-43-low cell from Experiment 1) was searched and analysed but never
uploaded. The SDRF describes the deposition, so it has 29 single-cell rows. The other 61 files match
the summary one-for-one, with zero orphans in either direction.

### The 20-cell counts: Methods vs figure legend

Methods claim Experiment 2 had "four samples with 20 cells" per FM level. The Figure 1 legend says
`20 cells, N = 3`, the deposition has 3, and the MaxQuant summary has 3 (`2H20a/b/c`, `2L20a/b/c`).
Three independent sources beat one sentence: **3**.

## Why the blanks are annotated rather than dropped

The brief permits excluding sampleless runs only when a required column forbids both reserved words.
No required column here does: `organism`, `organism part`, `disease`, `developmental stage`, and
`single cell isolation protocol` all set `allow_not_applicable: true`. So the blanks stay, marked
`not applicable` throughout, with `sample type` = `empty` (`PRIDE:0000903`, *"an intentionally empty
channel or well used for signal-to-noise assessment"* — a precise fit for a run named `NoiseControl`)
and `cell identifier` = `empty`, which the single-cell template lists as an explicit `special_value`.

**`cells per well` = 0 is evidence, not assumption.** The paper never mentions noise controls at all.
But the MaxQuant experiment codes follow a strict `{exp}{H|L}{n_cells}{letter}` grammar — `1H20a`,
`2L1j`, `1H5c` — and the three blanks are coded **`2N0a`, `2N0b`, `2N0c`**: Experiment 2, Noise,
**0 cells**. The submitter's own naming states the cell count.

## Key decisions and evidence

**Instrument — PRIDE's structured field is wrong, and PRIDE's own prose proves it.** The
`instruments` field says `LTQ Orbitrap Elite`. Both the paper's Methods *and* PRIDE's own
`sample_processing_protocol` say "An **Orbitrap Fusion Lumos Tribrid** mass spectrometer (Thermo
Fisher) was used for data collection". Two independent statements against one submitter form field
that its own submission text contradicts. Annotated `MS:1002732`.

I could not use raw headers to arbitrate, per the brief's preferred method: the deposited `.raw`
files are **not Thermo-native**. All 61 begin with the identical 16 bytes
`1e9a0b68d52238745a2a6d0ac837f9f6` (a Thermo raw starts `01 a1` + "Finnigan" in UTF-16), and a
mid-file 64 KB block measures 7.997 bits/byte of entropy — an opaque, compressed-or-encrypted
container. Identical via HTTPS and FTP, so it is not a transfer artifact. The evidence above is
sufficient without them; this is recorded as an observation about the deposition, not a defect in
the annotation.

**FACS instrument and nozzle — PRIDE and the paper conflict; the paper is internally consistent.**
PRIDE's protocol says "sorted with a FACSARIA II instrument … 100 µm nozzle". The paper's proteomics
section says "a **BD Influx** Instrument … equipped with a **85 µm** nozzle". The paper mentions a
BD FACSAria Fusion only in its *single-cell RNA-seq* section — a different experiment in the same
paper. PRIDE's text conflated the two. Annotated `85 um`; the sorter model itself has no SDRF column.

**Cell type — the obvious ontology hit is the wrong one.** The utricle is a *vestibular* organ. OLS
offers `macular hair cell` (CL:0005008), which looks ideal for a utricular *macula* — but its
definition reads *"An **auditory** hair cell located in the macula that is sensitive to **auditory**
stimuli"*, which is false for the utricle. Rejected. Used `vestibular hair cell` **CL:0000609**,
defined as *"a mechanoreceptor located in the acoustic maculae … mediates the sense of balance"* —
exactly the utricular macula.

For the FM1-43-low population, `labyrinth supporting cell` (CL:0000631) is likewise a trap: it is
defined as supporting *the organ of Corti* (cochlear). `auditory epithelial supporting cell`
(CL:0005014) is auditory too. **No vestibular supporting-cell term exists in CL**, so the generic
`supporting cell` **CL:0000630** is used — which is also the paper's own word ("progenitor
(supporting) cells").

**Organism part = the macula, not the whole utricle.** Methods: utricles were "treated with
thermolysin to remove the sensory epithelium; cells in the epithelium were dissociated". The
utricular sensory epithelium *is* the macula, so `macula of utricle of membranous labyrinth`
(UBERON:0002214) is directly supported rather than an over-reach.

**Isolation protocol = `FACS`, not `nanoPOTS`.** Both terms are in the fixed enum and both are true
of this workflow, but the column means "method used to *isolate* single cells" — that is the FACS
sort. nanoPOTS is the downstream prep vessel and has its own column
(`comment[nanopots chip version]` = `5x13 nanowell chip`, from the paper's "5 × 13 hydrophilic
nanowells").

**Search settings come from the deposited `mqpar` equivalent, not the prose.** Pulled
`parameters.txt` and `summary.txt` out of the 1 GB `SEARCH.zip` by HTTP-range-reading the zip central
directory (no full download): fixed `Carbamidomethyl (C)`; variable `Oxidation (M)` and
`Acetyl (Protein N-term)`; enzyme `Trypsin/P`, specific, 2 missed cleavages; `MS/MS tol. (FTMS) =
20 ppm`. `Acetyl` is `UNIMOD:1` and sits in `PP=Protein N-term` — **not** `TA=`, and not the
`UNIMOD:21` phospho swap.

PRIDE's `modifications` field lists only "iodoacetamide derivatized residue", omitting both variable
modifications. The deposited search parameters win.

**`cell viability` = `live` is evidence-backed:** SYTOX Red Dead Cell Stain was used to gate, and
only "SYTOX Red-negative" cells were deposited into wells.

## Values deliberately left `not available` / `not applicable`

| column | value | why |
|---|---|---|
| `comment[precursor mass tolerance]` | `not available` | Genuinely unrecoverable. `parameters.txt` lists only fragment tolerances (`MS/MS tol. (FTMS) 20 ppm`); no `mqpar.xml` exists anywhere in `SEARCH.zip` (176 entries checked) and neither the paper nor PRIDE states it. MaxQuant's 4.5 ppm default was **not** written in — that would be a guess. |
| `characteristics[sex]` | `not available` | Never determined. Utricles were pooled from multiple E15 embryos; chick embryos are not visually sexed and the paper is silent. `pooled` was rejected — it would assert a mixed-sex composition nobody measured. |
| `characteristics[strain or breed]` | `not available` | Eggs came from "Texas A&M University Poultry Science Department"; no breed is named anywhere (White Leghorn is the likely answer and precisely the kind of guess to avoid). |
| `characteristics[cell identifier]` (pools) | `not applicable` | The column means "unique identifier for each **single cell**". A 20-cell pool has no single-cell identity. The template's `special_values` (`carrier`, `reference`, `empty`) cover other non-single-cell roles but not pools, so `not applicable` is the sanctioned escape. Well identity is carried by `source name`. |

## Known limitations

**`characteristics[developmental stage]` = `embryo stage` (EFO:0007725) loses "E15".** TERMS.tsv
restricts this column to EFO. EFO carries a mouse and a *C. elegans* embryo-stage series but **no
chicken series** (E15 ≈ Hamburger-Hamilton stage 41, which EFO does not model). `embryo stage` is
the most specific true EFO term available. The precise age is unrecoverable from the SDRF.

**`comment[cleavage agent details]` = `Trypsin/P` under-reports the digestion.** The wet-lab protocol
adds **Lys-C then trypsin** sequentially. The column resolves to cardinality `single` in this
template, and the deposited MaxQuant search used `Trypsin/P` alone — so the search-side value was
chosen, since that is what a reprocessing pipeline needs. Lys-C is recorded here rather than silently
dropped.

**Duplicate MaxQuant experiment names in the deposited summary (submitter bug, not propagated).**
`2H1d` is assigned to *both* `..._030819_R4_...` and `..._030819_R5_...`, and `2L1d` likewise —
the letter series runs `a,b,c,d,d,f,g,h,i,j`, skipping `e`. These are 10 distinct wells/cells per the
paper, so the collision is a typo in the submitter's design template. Rather than "fix" it to `2H1e`
(fabricating an ID absent from the deposited tables) or copy the collision (breaking cell-identifier
uniqueness), MaxQuant codes were **not** used as `cell identifier`; identifiers are derived from the
unambiguous raw-file identity instead.

## Design notes (properties of the study, recorded faithfully)

- **`comment[sample preparation batch]`** carries `Experiment 1` / `Experiment 2`. This is a real and
  consequential covariate — the paper explicitly removes a batch effect with `sva` before trajectory
  inference. The two batches also differ in LC pressure (300 vs 350 bar per the filenames) and in
  design: only Experiment 1 has the 3- and 5-cell pool titration.
- **Cells-per-well is confounded with batch**: 3- and 5-cell pools exist only in Experiment 1.
- **The factor is `factor value[cell type]`** (hair cell vs supporting cell), mirroring
  `characteristics[cell type]`. The cells-per-well ladder is a sensitivity titration, not a biological
  contrast; it lives in `characteristics[cells per well]`.
- **`biological replicate`** numbers sequentially within each (FM level × cells per well) group across
  the study; the paper confirms "in all cases, samples were biological replicates — none of the
  biological samples were split to be run separately as multiple technical replicates", so
  `technical replicate` is 1 everywhere.
