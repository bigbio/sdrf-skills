   - **Rules broken:** campaign rule 9 and sdrf-annotate cellline.md §4.8.
   - **Fix:**
     - Set every row to e.g. `lysine- and arginine-free DMEM/Ham's F12 (1:1) with 10% dialyzed FBS (SILAC)` in the build tables. The wt:wt cells are SILAC-labelled too.
     - Add a manifest claim citing dib §2.3 and the PRIDE protocol.
     - Remove the "no NCIT term" limitation from the manifest.
2. minor – **Record two things in the manifest limitations:**
   - **The source conflict on the wt:wt cell state.** DiB says "wt versus wt cells". The paper's Supp Fig legend says "control cells versus control cells". `wild type` stays, backed by DiB and by the fact that the wt:wt runs predate the transduced-cell runs.
   - **That WTWT1/WTWT2 being separate mixtures is an inference.** It rests on acquisition dates and the MaxQuant Ele/Lele experiments; Table 1 does not state it. The paper speaks of a single "triplicate SILAC proteome of WT:WT cells".
3. minor – **The `comment[fraction identifier]` numbering differs between mixtures.** In A, C and D the pooled-well files (well17_18, well19_20) make fraction 19 = well 21, while in the 24-file sets fraction 19 = well 19. Using OFFGEL well numbers would make fractions line up across mixtures. This is optional; the current choice is documented.
