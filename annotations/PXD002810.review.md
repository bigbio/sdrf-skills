## Key finding: the authors' Data in Brief descriptor

The producer missed one source: **Mihailovich et al. 2016, *Data in Brief*, "MS-analysis of SILAC-labeled MYC-driven B lymphoma cells overexpressing miR-17-19b"** (PMID 26977435, PMC4781929, doi:10.1016/j.dib.2016.02.031). It describes PXD002810 file by file. It:

- **settles the Rev orientation explicitly** (§2.3): "for the Dir experiments miRNA cells were labeled with heavy medium, and control cells with light medium, while for the Rev experiment, we grew control cells in heavy, and miRNA cells in light medium." The SDRF channel map is correct (rule 6 is now met by an explicit statement as well as by the data).
- **contradicts the WT:WT sample prep**. Per Table 1 rows 9–11 and §2.5, the wt:wt runs were in-gel digested from 15 SDS-PAGE slices with trypsin only. The SDRF gives OFFGEL IEF + Lys-C for them.
- **documents the 45 blocked "Lele" runs** (Table 1 rows 12–13) as wt:wt, in-gel, 15 slices. Footnote: "yeast" only means the sample was run after a yeast QC sample, and no yeast extract is present. `f080917TB_2567` = "15 raw files in duplicate".
- **gives the clone structure**: Dir1, Dir1bis, Rev1, Rev1bis, Dir2 and Dir2bis are clone #1; Dir2_Sort3 and Rev1_Sort3 are clone #2. These are two independent GFP sorts, i.e. the paper's #2567-1 and #2567-2.
