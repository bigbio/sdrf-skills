### Key checks

**The two injection series (internal names S22043 and S22041).** The 11 `S22041-fraction-N.raw` files carry the internal name `S22043-fraction-N` (D:\2022-Jul-25/26, sequence rows 105–115). The 11 `S22041-2ndrun-N.raw` files carry `S22041-2ndrun-N` (D:\2022-Sep-29). Four things point to one TMT mix injected twice:
  - The provider's PEAKS project `S22041 1+2batches ... 1st+2nd light and heavy`, created 2022-10-03, already listed the first series as `S22041-fraction-N.mgf`. So the files were renamed by the provider, not at deposit.
  - In the mzid, the identified peptide sequences of fraction-N and 2ndrun-N overlap with Jaccard 0.55–0.58. The neighbouring fractions overlap at 0.12–0.18 (this includes the 1↔11 concatenation wrap), and all others at ≤0.04.
  - Heavy-labelled PSMs are 18.5–20.7% of PSMs in the first series and 17.1–19.4% in the second.
  - The paper describes only one 6-sample TMT-pSILAC experiment.

  Comparing reporter ions (MS3) between the series was not possible: there is no Thermo raw reader here, and the GitHub release download returned 403. Encoding the series as technical replicates 1/2 with the same 6 source names is supported.

**TMT channel map.** Fig. 1E, re-read: `Intensity TMT6-126/127/128` = DMSO and `TMT6-129/130/131` = zotatifin. The SDRF matches this: 126–128 `untreated`, 129–131 `Zotatifin`.

**Raw method (fraction-1).**
  - MS1: 400–1700, Orbitrap 120K.
  - MS2: CID 35%, ion trap.
  - MS3: HCD 65%, Orbitrap 50K, 100–300, SPS.
  - The method name is "TMTpro SPS MS3", but the search used TMT 229.163, so TMT10/6 reagents.

**mzid search protocol.** TMT N-term fixed and K variable; Carbamidomethyl fixed; Oxidation, Deamidated, Lys8 (UNIMOD:259) and Arg10 (UNIMOD:267) variable; Trypsin semi-specific; 15 ppm / 0.6 Da. All match the SDRF.

### Findings (all minor)
1. **Series identity.** It rests on inference, and the manifest leaves out the strongest data evidence. Add the peptide-overlap numbers above to claim[0] and keep the limitation.
2. **Acquisition date in claim[0].** It says D:\2022-Jul-26 for the whole first series, but `S22041-fraction-6.raw` is in D:\2022-Jul-25. Change it to "Jul 25–26 2022".
3. **TMT labels.** The SDRF uses TMT6 names although the TMT10plex kit was used. The TMT6 masses equal 126/127N/128C/129N/130C/131 of the 10plex, so the labels reproduce the PEAKS quantification. This is already disclosed.
4. **Biological replicate numbers.** They follow channel order; no source says which channel holds which replicate. The numbers are still unique per culture within each group (rule 3 holds), and this is disclosed.
5. **Dissociation method.** Only the MS2 CID scan used for identification is encoded; the SPS-MS3 HCD 65% reporter scan is in the manifest only. Acceptable, since the column is single-valued.
