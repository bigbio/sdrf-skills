## Open items / limitations
- **Paper not retrieved.** The Blood 2022 full text (PMC8938936) is a PDF-only record, and the publisher and Europe PMC returned 403/500. The design rests on the deposit files, the PRIDE protocol, the MaxQuant outputs and the raw headers.
- **pYome rep1/rep2 are encoded as technical replicates of one mix.** Whether they are repeated IPs or re-injections is undocumented.
- **Undeposited runs in the pYome search.** The pYome MaxQuant summary also lists runs pYome_R1/R2_277/280, which are not in PXD018961. They are not annotated.
- **MaxQuant version conflict.** The protocol says 1.6.0.1, but the gProteome parameters.txt says 1.5.2.8. The precursor tolerance of 6 ppm comes from the protocol text for both searches.
- **`not available` values:**
  - gProteome reduction and alkylation reagents.
  - Passage number.
  - Sample storage temperature.
- **Gel fractionation term.** The gel type is not stated, so the parent term "Gel electrophoresis" is used.
- **`sdrf-tools cellline lookup BL60` returns the wrong line.** It matched NCI-BL1339; Cellosaurus CVCL_7034 is used instead.
