## Study
"Discovery of TBC1D7 as a potential driver for melanoma cell invasion" — Qi TF, Guo L, Huang M, Li L, Miao W, Wang Y. *Proteomics* 2020;20(14):e1900347, doi:10.1002/pmic.201900347, PMID 32510182, PMC9362880 (NIHMS author manuscript). The PRIDE record was published 2022-08. The task called it "Proteomics 2019/2022": the DOI is 2019-numbered, the paper is the 2020 issue, and the PRIDE release is from 2022. SILAC (Arg6/Lys8) comparison of the paired melanoma lines WM-115 (paper: primary) and WM-266-4 (paper: metastatic) from one patient. Off-line SCX gave 21 fractions per mixture, run on an LTQ Orbitrap Velos and searched with MaxQuant 1.3.0.5 against IPI human 3.68.

## Key decisions and evidence

### Channel map (rule 6)
- **Explicit source.** The PRIDE sample protocol says: forward = light WM-266-4 + heavy WM-115; reverse = heavy WM-266-4 + light WM-115. The file prefixes F1/F2/R1 identify the forward and reverse mixtures.
- **Check against the deposited ratios.** The paper reports TBC1D4/14/16/7/10A up and TBC1D24 down in WM-266-4. Deposited log2 H/L (normalized):

  | protein | F1 | F2 | R1 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | TBC1D7 | -1.43 | -1.16 | single ratio (NaN) |
  | TBC1D4 | -2.17 | -2.36 | +1.77 |
  | TBC1D16 | -1.55 | -1.83 | +1.19 |
  | TBC1D24 | +0.79 | +0.86 | -0.84 |

- **Global check.** Pearson correlation of log2 H/L across about 5,000 proteins: F1 vs F2 +0.92; F vs R -0.86 / -0.88.
- **Conclusion.** The map is confirmed. No conflict between sources.

### Modifications and search settings
Taken from the deposited MaxQuant `parameters.txt` and `summary.txt`, identical in all three searches:
- fixed: Carbamidomethyl (C);
- variable: Oxidation (M);
- SILAC labels: Arg6 = UNIMOD:188 and Lys8 = UNIMOD:259, `MT=Variable` (rule 11). PRIDE protocol: heavy Lys [13C6,15N2], heavy Arg [13C6].

Acetyl (Protein N-term) appears only as an unused first-search option, so it is not listed. PRIDE's "No PTMs" field is ignored.

### Instrument and acquisition (from range-read raw headers of one file per mixture)
- Instrument: "LTQ Orbitrap Velos MS", which agrees with PRIDE.
- Acquisition: top-20 DDA. Full MS is FTMS at 60k resolution, m/z 350-1500; MS/MS is ITMS CID at NCE 35.
- So the MS2 analyzer is a linear ion trap, and the fragment tolerance is `0.5 Da` (MaxQuant "MS/MS tol. (ITMS)").

### Other technical values
- **Cleavage agent**: Trypsin (PRIDE: "digested with trypsin", FASP). MaxQuant's Trypsin/P rule is the search setting (rule 2).
- **Fractionation**: SCX, PRIDE:0000561.

## Stated vs inferred
- **Stated**:
  - channel map, digestion, SCX and medium (PRIDE protocol);
  - modifications and tolerance (MaxQuant files);
  - instrument, DDA, CID and NCE (raw headers);
  - donor attributes (Cellosaurus);
  - same patient (paper).
- **Inferred**:
  - F1/F2/R1 = forward rep 1 / forward rep 2 / reverse. The F/R prefix plus the ratio orientation are strong evidence for this.
  - Biological replicate numbering 1/2/3. The paper says "replicates of SILAC-labeling experiments" but never says the cultures were independent.

## Conflicts
- **WM-115 primary vs metastatic.** The paper and PRIDE call WM-115 the *primary* line. Cellosaurus records its derivation site as "Metastatic; Right anterior leg, skin". No primary/metastatic column is asserted, and the comparison is encoded by cell-line identity.
- **"WM-115 and WM-266-4 cells were cultured in the heavy DMEM"** (PRIDE). Read literally, this says both lines were heavy. It is contradicted by the explicit forward and reverse mixing sentences and by the ratios, so it was read as describing the labelling procedure in general.
