## Scope under review

The SDRF covers the long-term proteome: 24 RAW `20190618_CF_COLLAB_Dan_A375_SILAC_0_72_PRO_0[1-3]_0[1-8].raw` × 2 SILAC channels = 48 rows. It leaves out the 6 triple-SILAC phospho RAW `*_0_30_120_STY_0[1-3]_0[1-2].raw`.

**Partial scope is stated and justified.** It is stated the same way in three places: the manifest `limitations`, the report's "Partial scope" section, and `BLOCKED.md` § PXD017487. I reproduced each stated reason:

1. **The medium channel is undocumented.** `summary_ST.txt` (authors' GitHub, 1f9fafa) gives Multiplicity 3, Labels1 Arg6;Lys4 and Labels2 Arg10;Lys8 for STY_01..03. The preprint Fig. 3A (p.49) shows only Light = DMSO / Heavy = A922500, 4h. No text or figure mentions a medium channel.
2. **The time points conflict.** The file names read `0_30_120`. Preprint line 231 and Fig. 3A say "4h", and the authors' code names the ratios `STY_30m` (M/L) and `STY_4h` (H/L).
3. **The data check fails.** The deposited `PhosphoSTYSites.txt` gives RPS6 normalized ratios:
   - S235 H/L 1.42 / 1.55 / 1.49
   - S236 H/L 2.17 / 2.29 / 2.02
   - M/L 0.88–1.18

   Preprint lines 235–238 and Fig. 3B report a loss of pS6 after A922500.

Reason 1 alone prevents a documented channel→condition map. The `0_30_120_PRO` runs are absent from PRIDE (files/all: 30 RAW), as stated.
