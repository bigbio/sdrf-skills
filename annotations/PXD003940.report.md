## Design
- **Samples:** conditioned medium (secretome), collected for 24 h in serum-free medium from MDA-MB-231 (CVCL_0062) cells.
- **Pairing:** each run holds the control (heavy) and X2-shRNA1 (light) conditioned media from one biological replicate, mixed 1:1 by protein amount.
- **Runs:** 3 biological replicates (S1–S3 = BR1–3) × 10 SDS-PAGE gel fractions (_01–_10) × 2 injections (_A = TR1, _B = TR2) = 60 raw files. All 60 appear in the MaxQuant summary.txt, with experiments BR<n>_TR<m>.
- **SILAC:** MaxQuant multiplicity 2; Labels0 is empty (light) and Labels1 = Arg6;Lys8. This confirms the dataset is SILAC.
- **Channel map**, from the PRIDE sampleProcessingProtocol:
  - heavy = "control stable cell line (no POPX2 shRNA; control construct not stated in deposit)"
  - light = "shRNA targeting PPM1F (POPX2; X2-shRNA1)"
- **Biological replicates:** numbered 1–3 within each genetic-modification group. There are 6 sources, one per (cell population, BR).
- **Factor:** factor value[genetic modification].

## Technical values
These come from the MaxQuant summary.txt and parameters.txt, the PRIDE text and the raw file header.
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive, confirmed by the raw header.
- **Acquisition and fragmentation:** DDA top-10, HCD at 28 NCE, MS2 in the orbitrap, MS1 range 350–1600 m/z.
- **Sample preparation:** trypsin digestion (the search rule was Trypsin/P), DTT reduction, iodoacetamide alkylation, SDS-PAGE fractionation.
- **Tolerances:** precursor 4.5 ppm (main search), fragment 20 ppm.
- **Fixed modification:** Carbamidomethyl (C).
- **Variable modifications:** Acetyl (Protein N-term), Deamidated (N,Q), Oxidation (M), Arg6 (UNIMOD:188, R) and Lys8 (UNIMOD:259, K).
  - Acetyl (Protein N-term) is in summary.txt but missing from the PRIDE text.
  - This MaxQuant version's summary.txt has no 'Multi modifications' column.
