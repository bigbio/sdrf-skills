### Findings
1. **IMPORTANT (design / source evidence / omission) — heavy reference rows (even rows 2-32).**
   - **What the SDRF does:** heavy sampling time and serum stimulation are `not available`. Heavy replicates are numbered by RB only, within one factor group (`not available|untreated`).
   - **What the sources say:**
     - Supplementary Methods: "After 10min and 24h of FBS stimulation, heavy cells and light cells were ... lysed".
     - Fig. 1C (HAL p.31): the Heavy/vehicle dish has "+ FBS 2%: −" and sits inside both the 10min and 24h frames.
     - Data processing: heavy medians are normalised "between each biological replicate at the two time of treatment".
     - PRIDE: heavy = "starved untreated cells".
   - **What that means:** there are 8 heavy cultures (RB1-4 × 10 min/24 h), and they are unstimulated. In the SDRF, the 10 min and 24 h heavy cultures of one RB share a replicate number in the same group (batch rule 3). The manifest's statement that the sources say "only stimulated or not" omits this evidence.
   - **Fix (scripted in make_tables.py, then rebuild):**
     - heavy sampling time (characteristics and factor value) = the run's time point;
     - heavy serum stimulation = an explicit "no stimulation" value;
     - heavy source names include RB and time.
   - **Follow-on:** heavy rows (untreated, unstimulated) and the light FBS controls (untreated, +FBS) would then share treatment and time. Keep them distinct, e.g. with `factor value[serum stimulation]`, and re-check the convert-openms conditions. Update the manifest claims and limitations to match.
2. **minor — orientation data check.** The producer's correlation is weak (r ≈ 0.13–0.15). My own bait query of 4 msf files (RPS6 RLSSLR, GSK3B S9 peptide) was cut off by FTP disconnects and was not informative. This does not block, because the orientation is stated explicitly in three sources and the "FBS/NT" ratio naming fits it.
3. **minor — enrichment process.** The Supplementary Methods mention "flowthrough peptides" being analysed as well. The main text says the 40 deposited files are TiO2-enriched (5 conditions × 2 times × 4 RB = 40). Cite this in the manifest.
4. **minor — missing column.** `characteristics[individual]`, which the human template recommends, is absent. It could be `Capan-1`.
