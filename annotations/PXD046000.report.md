## Study
Villamar-Cruz O, … Chernoff J, Camacho-Arroyo I, Arias-Romero LE. *A PTP1B-Cdk3 Signaling Axis Promotes Cell Cycle Progression of Human Glioblastoma Cells through an Rb-E2F Dependent Pathway.* Mol Cell Biol 2023;43(12):631-649, doi:10.1080/10985549.2023.2273193, PMID 38014992, PMC10761042.

The paper is about human glioblastoma cells, but **the deposited SILAC experiment uses mouse embryonic fibroblasts.** Methods: "Ptp1b −/− and Ptp1b +/+ MEFs were a gift from Dr Benjamin Neel". Ref. 26 (Haj 2003) describes "fibroblasts from PTP1B(-/-) mice".

The workflow: the cells were EGF-stimulated after 3 h starvation and the lysates mixed 1:1. Phosphoproteins were enriched by anti-pTyr (4G10) IP, digested with Lys-C then trypsin, and the phosphopeptides enriched on TiO2. The runs were acquired by DDA CID with MS2 in the ion trap and searched with MaxQuant 1.2.0.18 against IPI **human** v3.68.

## Channel map (rule 6): annotated, with a recorded conflict
**For +/+ = light, −/− = heavy (explicit author statements):**
1. Fig. 1B (PMC image TMCB_A_2273193_F0001_C.jpg) labels "PTP1B +/+ cells grown in ¹²C₆¹⁴N₄-Arg, ¹²C₆¹⁴N₂-Lys" and "PTP1B −/− cells grown in ¹³C₆¹⁵N₄-Arg, ¹³C₆¹⁵N₂-Lys".
2. The Table 1 column "KO/wt Ratio" reproduces Supplemental Dataset 2's column headed **`Ratio H/L`** exactly: EGFR 1.09023, CTNND1 2.02001, p38 1.6018, EphA 1.518, Cdk3 1.9824. The authors therefore read H/L as KO/WT.
3. Data check: the paper's headline site, CDK Tyr15 (KIGEGTYGVVYK), has a deposited Ratio H/L Normalized of 2.04 in experiment S, and 1.36 in NS in Supplemental Dataset 1. This agrees with Table 1 (1.98).

**Against:**
- The PRIDE protocol lists "Ptp1b−/− and Ptp1b+/+ cells were grown in media containing [light] or [heavy]". It does not say "respectively", but read in order it gives the opposite map.
- In the deposited experiment S, other Table 1 sites have H/L Normalized below 1:
  - EGFR pY1172: 0.089 (count 15)
  - CTNND1 pY904: 0.19
  - p38 pY182: 0.32
  - EphA2 pY772: 0.87

  Table 1 claims KO/wt values above 1 for all four. Haj 2003 reports increased EGFR phosphorylation in these −/− cells.
- Supplemental Dataset 1 shows that the authors' `Ratio H/L` column differs from both its NS and S ratios for these rows (equal to S in only 16 of 48 rows). Table 1 numbers therefore cannot be re-derived from the deposit in either orientation.
- Across all pY sites, the median H/L Normalized is 0.75 (15 sites, 4 above 1).

**Decision.** Rule 6: "explicit label statement + data beat inference". The two explicit statements and the only numerically reproducible check (CDK Tyr15) support +/+ = light. The opposing evidence is word order and biological expectation. A reviewer may reasonably weigh this differently. If the map is rejected, all 16 rows must be blocked, because no orientation-free encoding exists. This is recorded in the manifest limitations and in BLOCKED.md.

## Other key decisions
- **Organism: Mus musculus**, not PRIDE's *Homo sapiens*. The reconcile `organism_contradicted` finding is refuted in validation.txt. **Disease: `normal`**: these are non-disease MEFs, and PRIDE's "glioblastoma" refers to the paper's GB cell work, which is not in this deposit.
- **Instrument: LTQ Orbitrap XL (MS:1000556).** All 4 raw headers read say "LTQ Orbitrap XL" and Eksigent NanoLC. PRIDE and the paper say LTQ Orbitrap Velos with Dionex, which the raw files contradict.
- **SILAC labels: Label:13C(6) (UNIMOD:188) on R and K**, from the deposited search (`Arg6;Lys6`). The text and figure state Arg10/Lys8 (¹³C₆¹⁵N₄ / ¹³C₆¹⁵N₂), but the search's heavy identifications (702; 213 with PEP<0.01, 93 K-only and 90 R-only) sit at a median |mass error| of 0.32 ppm. A +8/+10 Da partner could not match a +6.02 Da search at that accuracy, so the labels actually used were 13C6 Arg and 13C6 Lys. The modification type is `MT=Variable` (batch convention).
- **Search modifications:**
  - Carbamidomethyl C: Fixed.
  - Variable: Oxidation M, Acetyl Protein N-term, Phospho STY (summary.txt; this MaxQuant version has no "Multi modifications" column).
- **Cleavage:** Trypsin, plus a second column for Lys-C (Lys-C 4 h, then trypsin overnight; rule 2). MaxQuant's rule was Trypsin/P.
- **Fragment tolerance:** 0.5 Da (ITMS). **Precursor tolerance:** `not available`, because it is not recorded and no mqpar.xml is deposited.
- **Treatment:** Epidermal Growth Factor (NCIT:C20425), applied to both genotypes, so it is not a factor. The protocol says 10 min; the Fig. 1B legend says 15 min.
- **Enrichment:** enrichment of phosphorylated protein (EFO:0010806).
- **Search database:** MaxQuant searched a human database although the cells are mouse. The SDRF has no column for this; it is a note only.
