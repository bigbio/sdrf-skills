## Multiplexing verified (rule 14)
summary.txt: every deposited file has `Multiplicity 2`, `Labels0` empty, `Labels1 Arg10;Lys8`. Paper: "SILAC was employed ...
two MS1 labels reflecting the SILAC heavy labels (Lysine8 and Arginine10)". Channel map (rule 6): Methods — each clinical
lysate "combined with equal protein amounts of the super-SILAC mix" of heavy-labelled cell lines; analyses use "ratios of the
unlabeled samples towards the same heavy labeled super-SILAC mix" -> light = patient, heavy = reference. A super-SILAC design has
no condition-dependent label swap, so the deposited-ratio check of rule 6 is not applicable; the label assignment is explicit.

## Judgement calls a reviewer should attack hardest
1. **Precursor mass tolerance 20 ppm**: only the first-search tolerance is documented; MaxQuant's main search (default 4.5 ppm) is
   undocumented. `not available` is more honest but makes convert-openms fail.
2. **Disease/subtype encoding**: IHC subtypes put directly into `characteristics[disease]` / `factor value[disease]` with MONDO subtype
   terms (MONDO:0006244 is defined as the HER2-enriched expression subset; MONDO:0005494 as invasive TNBC); the 9 unannotated
   samples get generic breast carcinoma and form their own factor group.
3. **Run layout**: the extra unfractionated run of each SAX-fractionated patient as technical replicate 2 (not a separate source,
   not MaxQuant's Fraction 2), and sex left `not available` for 69 patients although the cohort is almost certainly female.
