### The key question: the channel→cell-line map

The producer's premise was that no written source states the map. That premise is wrong.

**The deposited study states the map.** The total-proteome Proteome Discoverer study, `TotalProteome_24Fractions_Triplicate_IsogenicKRASmutants_SW48-2.msf` (49.7 GB), was not opened by the producer. I read its `AnalysisDefinition` table by HTTP-range SQLite (an apsw VFS, about 33 range requests). It defines two study factors:

- **`Cells`**, a categorical factor with options Parental, G12D and G13D.
- **`Replicates`**, with options Rep1, Rep2 and Rep3.

In all 72 TP files the values are the same:

| Channel | `Cells` |
|---|---|
| `[Light]` | Parental |
| `[Medium]` | G12D |
| `[Heavy]` | G13D |

Each of RP01, RP02 and RP03 has 24 files per channel, and RP0N = RepN. This is an explicit sample sheet written by the authors (`D:\Raiha\…`), so it counts as an explicit source under rule 6. It agrees exactly with the SDRF.

**The deposited data confirm it:**
- **Mutant-KRAS peptides.** My own parse of `tp_kras.xml` found the G12D peptide only with Lys4 (medium), in 4 PSMs. The G13D peptide appears with Lys8 (heavy) in 2 PSMs. There is one light G13D PSM. It is the K0 partner of a heavy G13D PSM in the same fraction (Δ 8.014 Da), which fits incomplete labelling.
- **Reference channel.** The PD studies mark Light as Control, and PRIDE describes the ratios as "G12D/Parental".
- **MPZL1 marker.** The pY peptide is up in medium only (M/L about +1.3, H/L about −0.6).

**The pY runs.** The pY study has no factors. Its map follows from three things:
- the PRIDE protocol says the pY and TP material of each replicate come from the same mixed SILAC digest;
- the TP orientation is the same in all three replicates;
- the MPZL1 check above.

**Rule 6 judgement: the map is acceptable, and the runs should not be blocked.** The SDRF already satisfies rule 6. The precedents that were blocked (PXD002454, PXD035841) had no such factor table. What remains is to record this source in the manifest.
