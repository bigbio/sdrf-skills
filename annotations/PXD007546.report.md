## Study
"LC-MS/MS analysis of Mannose 6-phosphate containing proteins" — the M6P-secretome arm of Di Lorenzo G et al., *Mol Cell Proteomics* 2018;17(8):1612-1626 (PMID 29773673, PMC6072544). The sister deposit PXD007547 holds the lysosomal-proteome arm. Conditioned media of Gnptg-knockout (light) and wild-type (heavy) mouse embryonic fibroblasts were concentrated and mixed. M6P proteins were captured on scFv M6P-1 beads and digested with trypsin on the beads. Each mixture was run once on an LTQ Orbitrap Velos (ion-trap CID, top-10 DDA) and searched with MaxQuant 1.5.2.8.

## Key decisions
- **Lysine label: Lys6 (UNIMOD:188), not Lys8.** The PRIDE prose says "lysine 13C6 15N2", but the deposited MaxQuant search used `Lys6`, and the data agree. `evidence.txt` has 215 K-ending multiplets with ratios. MS/MS-identified heavy K peptides (PEP<0.01, n=8) have a median mass error of −0.02 ppm under the +6.020 Da definition, which a Lys8 peptide could not give. The SDRF follows the search (rule 5); the conflict is recorded.
- Mods: Propionamide C fixed (acrylamide alkylation). Acetyl protein N-term, Oxidation M, Arg10 (UNIMOD:267) and Lys6 (UNIMOD:188) are variable.
- Cleavage agent: Trypsin (digestion). MaxQuant used the Trypsin/P rule.
- Fragment tolerance: 0.5 Da (ITMS). Precursor tolerance is `not available`: no mqpar.xml was deposited and parameters.txt does not list it.
- Measured material: characteristics[cellular component] = extracellular region. The sample is M6P-enriched conditioned medium.
- Disease: KO = GNPTG-mucolipidosis (MONDO:0009652); the paper says the KO mice mimic MLIII. WT = normal.
- Strain: C57BL/6, from the same lab's 2020 paper describing this Gnptg-KO line.
