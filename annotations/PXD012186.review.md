I re-downloaded Labellingscheme.xlsx and experimentalDesignTemplate.txt, re-fetched the PRIDE record, the FTP listing and the paper text, read summary.txt, parameters.txt and proteinGroups.txt from MaxQuantoutput.zip myself by HTTP range, and re-ran every check. ## What holds up
- **The channel map is correct for all 12 mixes.** I parsed Labellingscheme.xlsx myself (sheet 1, "SILAC mixing scheme", G5:L35, with its merged cells). The scheme gives:

  | Mix | Light | Medium | Heavy |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1 and 4 | E6E7 | E6E7 Ctr | pWPI |
  | 2 and 6 | pWPI | E6E7 | E6E7 Ctr |
  | 3 and 5 | E6E7 Ctr | pWPI | E6E7 |
  | 7a and 8a | pWPI | pWPI Ctr | (unused) |
  | 7b and 8b | pWPI Ctr | (unused) | pWPI |
  | 7c and 8c | (unused) | pWPI | pWPI Ctr |

  All 30 sources in the SDRF match this map: label, genetic modification, infection and time point. The left-hand "Labelling scheme" block, which shows the three label permutations, agrees with it.
- **The deposited data confirm the map.** I ran my own check on proteinGroups.txt, using 350 C. trachomatis-only groups, non-reverse and non-contaminant. Their summed intensity peaks in the Ctr channel of every mix, 6–15x above the other channels. For duplex mixes I mapped the MaxQuant L/H channels through each experiment's Labels0/Labels1. CPPED1, which the paper reports as strongly down in E6E7, is highest in the pWPI channel of all 6 triplex mixes.
- **The unused channels are handled correctly.** summary.txt has Multiplicity 2 for the duplex mixes:
  - 7a/8a: Labels0 empty, Labels1 Arg6;Lys4
  - 7b/8b: Labels1 Arg10;Lys8
  - 7c/8c: Labels0 Arg6;Lys4, Labels1 Arg10;Lys8

  The SDRF has no rows for the unused channel, and its physical label names (medium = Arg6/Lys4, heavy = Arg10/Lys8) are right.
- **Files are all mapped.** The FTP listing has 132 .raw files plus 5 other files. All 132 appear in the SDRF, none is missing and none is extra. Every file appears 3x (triplex) or 2x (duplex). Experiment and fraction match both experimentalDesignTemplate.txt and summary.txt. Fraction identifier = MaxQuant fraction − 2 for every file, and fraction 11 is the pooled fr1-2-13.
- **Technical values match the sources.** Q Exactive, DDA, HCD at NCE 27, Orbitrap MS2 and 350–1650 m/z agree with the paper. From the deposited search: Carbamidomethyl (C) is fixed (parameters.txt); Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (Protein N-term) are variable (summary.txt); MS/MS tolerance is 20 ppm; 4.5 ppm is the second-search precursor tolerance (PRIDE/paper); trypsin was the digestion enzyme (paper), with a Trypsin/P search rule. The UNIMOD accessions 188, 481, 267 and 259 are the right ones for Arg6, Lys4, Arg10 and Lys8.
- **Ontology terms are valid.** In OLS4, UBERON:0002424 oral epithelium, CL:0000312 keratinocyte, PATO:0000461 normal, NCBITaxon:813, MS:1001911, MS:1000422, MS:1000484, PRIDE:0000627, 0000564, 0000608 and 0000599, MS:1001251, and the EFO column terms (genetic modification, sampling time, infection) all resolve and none is obsolete.
- **The cell-line naming is justified.** I queried Cellosaurus myself:
  - `id:NOK` returns only the mouse hybridomas NOK-1..5 and the male NOK-SI (CVCL_BW57).
  - "Piboonniyom" returns no hit.

  The female sex is stated in the paper ("Immortalized female human oral keratinocytes"), so it is not NOK-SI. A free-text name that cites the originating paper, with `not available` Cellosaurus fields, is the honest encoding.
- **The infection encoding is sound.** It uses the species only (NCBITaxon:813), because no strain, serovar or MOI is stated anywhere. That matches precedent: PXD001457 has factor value `Chlamydia trachomatis`/`uninfected`, and PXD011848 uses `uninfected`.
- **Factor choices are right.** The three factors are genetic modification, infection and sampling time. That gives 8 conditions, matching the 2x2x2 design in the scheme.
- **Template versions and disease follow the campaign rules.** sdrf version is v1.1.0; ms-proteomics is 1.2.0 (upstream 7da1747 templates.yaml:150), with human 1.1.0 and cell-lines 1.1.0. Disease is `normal`; the PRIDE record's "Cervix carcinoma" is the study motivation, not a property of the cells.

## Important: F1, label modification parameters do not describe the search the duplex files went through (campaign rule 5)
All 330 rows carry all four SILAC label modifications: UNIMOD:188 R, 481 K, 267 R and 259 K. In the deposited MaxQuant search the duplex experiments were separate label parameter groups (summary.txt, every raw file):
- 24h_7a/48h_8a (22 files, 44 rows): only Arg6;Lys4 were searched. Arg10 and Lys8 were not.
- 24h_7b/48h_8b (22 files, 44 rows): only Arg10;Lys8 were searched. Arg6 and Lys4 were not.
- 7c/8c and mixes 1–6: both label sets were searched.

The manifest claim for `comment[modification parameters]` says "Labels Arg6;Lys4 / Arg10;Lys8 for every raw file (one search covering all 132 files)". That is contradicted by the summary.txt it cites. Campaign rule 5 says modification parameters must describe the search each file actually went through, and sub-experiments searched differently must be built separately with `not applicable` where a modification is absent. A reanalysis pipeline that reads this SDRF would add heavy labels to the 7a/8a search and medium labels to 7b/8b.

**Fix:**
1. Rebuild as three sdrf-tools builds and concatenate them. All three must have identical headers, with the same seven `comment[modification parameters]` columns in the same order:
   - (a) 7a/8a rows: the UNIMOD:267 and UNIMOD:259 columns = `not applicable`.
   - (b) 7b/8b rows: the UNIMOD:188 and UNIMOD:481 columns = `not applicable`.
   - (c) mixes 1–6 and 7c/8c: unchanged.
2. Correct the manifest claim so it states the per-experiment Labels0/1/2 from summary.txt.
3. Re-run validate-sdrf, reconcile and convert-openms, then request a new review.

## Minor
1. **Biological replicate numbering (rows of the pWPI uninfected sources from 7a–c and 8a–c, numbered 4–6).** Numbering the part-2 pWPI uninfected cultures 4–6 depends on part 2 using new cultures. PRIDE supports this: "In the second part …" and "Triplicates were prepared in each group". Label reuse does not rule out reused lysates, though: pWPI-light occurs in both mix 2 and 7a. No source states it either way. The choice is the one that is safe under rule 3, and the manifest limitations disclose it. No change is needed. Optionally, quote the PRIDE sentence in the claim.
2. **`characteristics[culture medium]` = `not available` although the medium is documented.** The paper gives SILAC FAD medium: 75% Ham's F-12 + 25% DMEM, plus 5% FBS, insulin, EGF, cholera toxin, adenine and hydrocortisone. I confirmed that NCIT has no FAD, Ham's F-12 or DMEM/F12 term (OLS search). The template examples list "Ham's F-12", but that would misdescribe a 3:1 blend. The installed validator gave an ERROR on free text. `not available` is acceptable given the disclosure in the limitations. No change is needed.
3. **`characteristics[organism part]` = oral epithelium is inferred.** It follows from "normal oral keratinocytes"; ref 29 (PMID 11854066) concerns oral keratinocytes and SCC lines and does not give a finer site. This is disclosed. Acceptable.
4. **Sampling time on uninfected channels.** 24/48 h is applied to the uninfected controls of each mix. The scheme's merged "Time Course" cells cover the uninfected rows too, so this is supported. A short manifest note that the time point means harvest time post-infection of the paired infected culture would help readers.
5. **The validation log has a stale statement.** validation.txt says round-1 culture medium "failed" with ERROR. The upstream cell-lines 1.1.0 YAML sets `error_level: warning` for that validator. State which template copy produced the ERROR (the installed parse_sdrf one), so nobody mistakes it for the upstream rule.
6. **Informational: one 48h_8a fraction (fr1-2-13) was acquired on 06262017; the other 8a fractions were acquired on 06122017.** This is not an error, but a manifest note on the two acquisition dates (8a partly, 8b and 8c) would help with batch interpretation.
