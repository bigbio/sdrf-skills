## Which paper describes the deposited data — a split decision

The two versions are **not interchangeable**, and this dataset sits between them:

| | preprint v1 (2021-04) | published (2023) |
|---|---|---|
| Search engine | **SpectroMine 2.0**, UniProt 2020-10-12 | CHIMERYS / Proteome Discoverer 3.0, UniProt 2021-06-30 |
| Lysis | 40–200 nL, **20 ng/µL** trypsin, 50 °C 30 min + 4 h 37 °C | 40 nL, 10 ng/µL trypsin, 50 °C 2 h |
| TMT | **22 mM** | 10 mM |
| HeLa/HEK runs | **11** (110 cells) | 17 (170 cells) |
| Variable mods | N-term protein acetylation, oxidation (M) | oxidation (M) only |

The deposited result files are **`.psar` (SpectroMine archives) dated 2021-04-14** — the preprint's
posting date — and PRIDE's `data_processing_protocol` reproduces the preprint's Methods verbatim.
The deposit contains 11 HeLa/HEK runs, not 17.

**Therefore:** sample preparation, search settings, and modifications come from the **preprint +
PRIDE** (the deposition-contemporaneous sources). The published 2023 paper's CHIMERYS parameters
describe a *later re-analysis of a superset* and were deliberately **not** used.

**One exception, deliberately made:** the **channel layout** is taken from the published paper. The
preprint states no layout whatsoever. This is justified because the layout it states reproduces the
deposited file counts exactly — see below.

### Risk 1 — mixed labelling: resolved per run, and it splits the dataset

The screen's `labelling="TMT; label free"` is correct: both arms exist and are separable by filename.
The label-free arm (67 runs) is fully annotated. Of the TMT arms, the four **HeLa-only** arms are
annotated and the two **HeLa/HEK** arms are blocked.

### Risk 2 — carrier: resolved, a carrier exists in 21 of 118 annotated runs

`carrier=unclear` is now **determined**. The published Experimental Design states plainly:

> "…one sorted single-HeLa cell per channel except for '20× carrier samples' using one channel
> (126) for 20 sorted HeLa cells, leaving the adjacent 127C TMT-channel empty to minimize isobaric
> interference."

The carrier is a distinct sample and is annotated as one: `characteristics[sample type]` =
`NT=carrier;AC=PRIDE:0000901`, `characteristics[cell identifier]` = `carrier` (a template special
value), `characteristics[cells per well]` = `20`, `comment[carrier channel]` = `TMT126`. The
`20x` token in the filename marks exactly these runs. The carrier cells are HeLa and were sorted in
the cellenONE identically to the single cells ("All carrier channels were sorted within the
cellenONE and prepared identical to the single-cell samples"), so `single cell isolation protocol`
= `cellenONE` is correct for them.

### Why the HeLa-only layout is *stated*, not inferred

The layout is quoted above; the deposited files then confirm it by four independent identities that
had to hold simultaneously and were never used to derive it:

| arm | files × single-cell channels | = cells | paper states |
|---|---|---|---|
| TMT10 no carrier | 25 × 10 | 250 | "250 single HeLa cells … in 25 injections" |
| TMT10 20× carrier | 7 × 8 | 56 | "56 single HeLa cells … in seven analytical runs" |
| TMTpro no carrier | 5 × 16 | 80 | "80 single cells … in five analytical runs" |
| TMTpro 20× carrier | 14 × 14 | 196 | "196 single cells … in 14 injections" |

The two carrier arms only reach 56 and 196 if exactly one channel is carrier **and** one is empty —
which is what makes 8/10 and 14/16 rather than 9/10 and 15/16. The blocked arm closes the same way:
11 × 10 + 6 × 8 = **158**, precisely PRIDE's "158 multiplexed single cells".

## Excluded: 21 empty channels

The 127C channel of every 20× carrier run is deliberately empty. It is **not annotated**.
`characteristics[cell line]` is `required` in `cell-lines/1.1.0` with **both** `allow_not_applicable`
and `allow_not_available` set to `false`, so a sample-less channel has no legal value — the same wall
that excluded the blanks in `PXD052416` and `PXD016921`. This is sharper here: `single-cell/1.0.0`
*explicitly* provides `empty` as a special value of `characteristics[cell identifier]` and
`NT=empty;AC=PRIDE:0000903` as a sample type, so the SCP template wants these rows and the
cell-lines template forbids them. Recorded in `BLOCKED.md`.

## Key decisions and evidence

**HeLa is the parental line.** `EFO:0001185` / `CVCL_0030`. The brief's OLS trap was live: smart-mode
`searchClasses("HeLa", "efo")` is not what was used — `mode="fuzzy"` returned `HeLa` (EFO:0001185)
first, with `HeLa-S3`, `HeLa M`, `T-REx-HeLa` and `HEp-2` behind it. Unlike `PXD052416` there is no
S3 twist: these are cultured HeLa from the authors' own flasks, not a commercial digest.

**Modifications follow the deposited search, and the label-free arm differs.** The `.psar` archives
carry their own modification strings:

- `20210414_153730_TMT10-plex_HeLaHEK_final.psar` → `TMT_Lys;TMT_Nter;Acetyl (Protein N-term);Oxidation (M)`
- `20210414_190457_scLF_final.psar` → **`Acetyl (Protein N-term);Oxidation (M)`** — no TMT

So the two TMT columns are `not applicable` on the 67 label-free rows as a matter of **deposited
evidence**, not assumption. `UNIMOD:1` = Acetyl and `UNIMOD:21` (Phospho) appears nowhere; the
positional value sits in `PP=Protein N-term`, never `TA=`. TMT10 → `UNIMOD:737` (TMT6plex, the
correct accession for the TMT10 reagent set), TMTpro → `UNIMOD:2016`.

PRIDE's `modifications` field — "monohydroxylated residue", "TMT6plex-126 reporter+balance reagent
acylated residue", "acetylated residue" — **agrees** with the paper for once, which is worth
recording given how often it does not.

**Instrument.** PRIDE, preprint and published paper all say Orbitrap Exploris 480 (`MS:1003028`).
No conflict; no raw-header check needed.

**TMT10's tenth channel is `TMT131`, not `TMT131N`.** The deposited SpectroMine schema calls it
`TMT10_131N`, but the official SDRF example `spec/examples/PDC000126` uses `TMT131` for TMT10 and
`PRIDE:0000529` carries the label `TMT131`. TMTpro uses `TMT131N`/`TMT131C` (`PRIDE:0000580/0581`).
All 17 distinct channel accessions were verified individually against OLS.

**`assay name` is shared across the channels of one raw file** (`run 26` covers all 9 rows of a
TMT10 carrier run), following `PDC000126` — the assay is the MS run, not the row.

## Constructed, not asserted

`source name` and `characteristics[cell identifier]` (e.g. `TMT10nc_r01_TMT126`) are **synthetic
labels built from run + channel**. The deposition contains no per-cell well IDs or FACS index data.
These columns ask for a unique within-experiment identifier, so a deterministic label is a faithful
answer — it encodes only the run and channel, both of which are known, and asserts nothing about the
cell beyond what the layout already states. It is *not* the well position the cellenONE recorded.

### The 32 label-free runs whose cell count is not deposited

67 label-free runs split into **35** whose filename carries an explicit count token
(`..._4cell_...`, `..._2cells_...`) and **32** that carry only `scLF`. For the 35, `cells per well`
is that number and `sample type` is `single cell` (n=1) or `pooled` (n=2–6). For the 32, both are
`not available`.

**This is deliberate.** It is tempting to read `scLF` as "single cell, label free" and stamp
`cells per well = 1` on all 32 — the count even lines up with the preprint's Fig. 5b caption
("label-free single cells (n=32)"). Three things stop it:

1. `scLF_0402_2cell_01.raw` exists, so `scLF` demonstrably does **not** imply one cell — the count
   token is what carries that, and these 32 lack it.
2. The preprint contradicts itself: Fig. 5b says **n=32**, the body text says "1,422 protein groups
   across all **30** single cell measurements". A source that disagrees with itself cannot pin 32 rows.
3. The deposited `scLF` psar analysed only **22** of the 67 label-free runs, so it does not close the
   gap either.

`cells per well` is `recommended` and permits `not available`, so the honest value is available and
was used. This is the weakest area of the file and the first thing a reviewer should attack.

## No factor value column

Every annotated row is a HeLa cell: **the annotatable subset has no biological factor.** The study's
real variables here are technical — labelling reagent (TMT10 / TMTpro / label-free), carrier ratio
(20× / none), and vessel (funnel vs PEG-coated vial, the label-free Fig. 5a comparison) — and all
are properties of the analytical run, not of a sample characteristic that `factor value[…]` could
mirror. The dataset's one biological factor, HeLa vs HEK, lives **entirely inside the blocked arm**.
`parse_sdrf` accepts the file without a factor value. The funnel/vial distinction survives only in
`source name`; no licensed column expresses it.

## Notes for tooling

- **PRIDE `publications: []` is not evidence of no paper.** This dataset has both a preprint and a
  peer-reviewed MCP article; the record links neither. `is_open_access` never entered into it —
  there was no identifier to check. Title search plus `get_pdf_by_unpaywall` recovered both.
- **`.psar` (SpectroMine) archives are greppable.** They are not zip or SQLite, but UTF-16LE strings
  extract cleanly and carry the run list, the modification set, and the labelling schema. Here they
  proved the label-free arm had no TMT, and proved the negative that mattered most: the archive
  contains `NewTMTSchema` and `TMT10_126`…`TMT10_131N` but **zero** HeLa/HEK condition strings —
  the SpectroMine equivalent of Proteome Discoverer's `"Sample, n/a"`.
- **Scratchpad collision:** a concurrent session overwrote a generically-named `files_all.json` in
  the shared scratchpad with a *different* project's file list (PXD041879) mid-run. Caught only
  because a filename-prefix assertion failed. Everything derived from the file list was re-fetched
  to a project-scoped path and re-verified. Worth naming scratch files by accession.
- `single-cell/1.0.0` declares `comment[carrier channel]` with `ontology_accession: PRIDE:0000941`,
  but `PRIDE:0000941` resolves to **`environmental sample`** in OLS. The field is documentation-only
  and is not validated, so it has no effect here, but the accession looks wrong.
