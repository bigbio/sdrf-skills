## Study and duplicate

**Explicit sources.**
- The experiment names in `experimentalDesignTemplate.txt` (PXD002270 and `combined.zip`) and in `summary.txt` agree on all 124 runs.
- The run names agree too. `Rho_SI_143B_LSI` means ρ0 heavy ("S") and 143B light ("LS"); in `Rho_LSI_143B_SI` the labels are swapped.
- PRIDE says "biological quadruplicates, including a label-switch".

**Checks against the deposited ratios.**
- **mtDNA-encoded protein MT-CO2:** protein-level log2 H/L is −4.58 and −4.28 in H1/H2, and +4.46 and +6.12 in L1/L2.
- **Other mitochondrial markers:** TFAM is −3.1/−3.0 vs +3.2/+2.8, and NDUFS1 is −2.1/−2.2 vs +2.1/+2.1.
- **Pearson correlations:** H1 vs H2 +0.885, L1 vs L2 +0.873, H vs L −0.53 to −0.59.
- **Per file:** 123 of 124 runs correlate with the ρ0-heavy reference in the sign their experiment predicts, including every TiO2 and every GlyGly run.
- **The one exception** is `150212_Rho0_143B_5_c`, a low-yield fraction: r=+0.30 over 346 pairs, and only 4 strongly regulated peptides, of which 2 agree. That is uninformative, so the explicit assignment is kept.

- **Acquisition:** DDA Top12, HCD at NCE 25, Orbitrap MS2 at 35,000.

- **Enzyme:** Trypsin. MaxQuant used the Trypsin/P rule.
- **Tolerances:** precursor 4.5 ppm and fragment 20 ppm (PRIDE protocol and parameters.txt).
- **Fixed modification:** Carbamidomethyl (C).
- **Variable modifications:** Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term), and the labels Arg10 = UNIMOD:267 and Lys8 = UNIMOD:259, all `MT=Variable`. Phospho (STY) is set on the TiO2 rows only and GG (K, UNIMOD:121) on the GlyGly rows only; it is `not applicable` elsewhere.

## Open items / limitations
1. **Paper full text not retrievable.** PMC4858937 is not in the OA subset, mcponline.org returned 403, and PMC is behind a reCAPTCHA (not bypassed). As a result, the following are undocumented, and the corresponding columns are omitted:
   - fractionation chemistry;
   - reduction and alkylation reagents;
   - the diGly enrichment method.
2. **Nature of the 1502xx non-TiO2 series is unknown.** These runs are fractions searched without Phospho. Whether they are TiO2 flow-through or aliquots taken before enrichment is not documented.
3. **`*_1D` is inferred.** It is treated as an unfractionated injection (technical replicate 2).
4. **Biological replicates follow the MaxQuant experiments.** Nothing documents whether the arms of one replicate (measured Nov 2014 – Feb 2015) come from the same culture.
5. **Weak orientation check for one run.** `150212_Rho0_143B_5_c` gives a weak, uninformative result; details above.
6. **Validator warnings (accepted).** `143B rho-0 EtBr`, `enrichment of ubiquitinated protein` and `13-year-old stage` are not in the ontologies the validator checks. Each is justified in validation.txt.
7. **Values left as `not available`:** cell type and passage number.
8. **Overridden archive fields.** PRIDE's "bone marrow" organism part and "Q Exactive" instrument are superseded by Cellosaurus and the raw headers.
