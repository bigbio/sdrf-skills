## Study
"Time resolved quantitative phospho-tyrosine analysis reveals Bruton's Tyrosine kinase mediated signaling downstream of the mutated granulocyte-colony stimulating factor receptors". Dwivedi P, Muench DE, Wagner M, Azam M, Grimes HL, Greis KD. *Leukemia* 2019;33(1):75-87, doi:10.1038/s41375-018-0188-8, PMID 29977015, PMC6320735 (author manuscript; the Supplementary Methods docx was retrieved from PMC OA).

The model is mouse Ba/F3 pro-B cells, retrovirally transduced to express one of three receptors:
- wild-type G-CSFR;
- the proximal T618I mutant;
- the truncated Q741x mutant.

Each line was SILAC-labelled. Heavy cells were stimulated with G-CSF (40 ng/mL) for 12.5 or 90 min, and light cells were left unstimulated. Each light/heavy pair was mixed 1:1 (10 mg + 10 mg), digested with trypsin and enriched on P-Tyr-1000 beads. Each mixture was run once on a TripleTOF 5600+ and searched with ProteinPilot 5.0. Design: 3 receptors × 2 times × 2 biological replicates = 12 mixtures and 12 runs.

**Not a duplicate.** The companion deposit PXD009311 (already annotated) holds the pS/pT data. Per the Sci Data paper, those samples come from the flow-through of this pY enrichment. That makes them different runs with a different enrichment. This deposit is the pY eluate.

## Key decisions and evidence
- **SILAC label masses: conflict, resolved as Lys8/Arg10.** The Supplementary Methods reagent paragraph says "13C6-Lysine and 13C6-Arginine". Five other sources say +8/+10:
  - the same document's search paragraph: "SILAC (Lys+8, Arg+10)";
  - all 12 `.group` settings;
  - the PRIDE protocol: "C-13, N-15 Lysine and Arginine";
  - the CIL catalogue numbers CNLM-539 and CNLM-291 (13C,15N series);
  - the decoded WT 12.5 min Rep 2 search, where 2,027 of 2,876 confident matches carry K[+08] or R[+10].

  The SDRF uses UNIMOD:259 (K) and UNIMOD:267 (R), `MT=Variable`. This matches the companion PXD009311.
- **Instrument: TripleTOF 5600+ (MS:1002584).** The `.wiff` device record reads "Component ID: TripleTOF 5600+". Two instrument serials appear: one for the 2015 WT runs and another for the 2016 mutant runs. The PRIDE dropdown value TripleTOF 5600 (MS:1000932) is generic.
- **Modifications.** ProteinPilot Paragon is probabilistic rather than fixed/variable. The search used the special factor "Phos-Tyr affinity column", which adds Phospho on S/T and on Y with emphasis (Y occurrence probability 0.49). The SDRF lists:
  - Carbamidomethyl C as Fixed (alkylation with iodoacetamide);
  - Phospho S/T/Y;
  - Oxidation M and Deamidated N/Q, the most frequent biological mods in the confident matches;
  - the two SILAC labels.
- **Tolerances: 0.05 Da (MS) and 0.1 Da (MS/MS).** These come from the ProteinPilot "TripleTOF 5600" instrument preset (INSTRUMENT:60) that the searches selected, with auto-calibration on.
- **Cell line.** BA/F3 (EFO:0022749), CVCL_0161.
  - Cell type: pro-B cell (CL:0000826).
  - Organism part: bone marrow.
  - Strain: C3H. Cellosaurus notes the line was once thought to be BALB/c but is C3H.
  - Disease: `normal`. The line is non-leukaemic; PRIDE's "Acute leukemia" describes the study context.
- **Enrichment.** `enrichment of phosphorylated protein` (EFO:0010806). OLS has no pY-specific child term.
- **Culture medium.** RPMI 1640 (SILAC RPMI, CIL #RPMI-500).
- **Fractions.** None: one injection per mixture, so fraction 1 and technical replicate 1 throughout.
