## Channel → condition map: evidence
1. **Reagents.** The PRIDE protocol names each reagent by catalogue number:
   - Solvent control: CNLM-291-H ([13C6,15N2]Lys = Lys8) and CNLM-539-H ([13C6,15N4]Arg = Arg10), which is **heavy**.
   - TSA: DLM-2640 ([2H4]Lys = Lys4) and CLM-2265-H ([13C6]Arg = Arg6), which is **medium**.
   - The MaxQuant `summary.txt` label definitions agree: Labels1 = Arg6;Lys4 (M) and Labels2 = Arg10;Lys8 (H).
2. **HDAC-inhibitor marker signature** in the authors' `proteinGroups.txt`, log2 normalised H/M (Repl1/Repl2):
   - Higher in medium: CDKN1A/p21, the canonical protein TSA induces (−2.98/−3.18), and H1F0 (−2.41/−2.77).
   - Higher in heavy: the E2F and S-phase targets that HDAC-inhibitor G1 arrest represses, MCM3 (+2.35/+2.24), MCM7 (+2.29/+2.32), RRM2 (+2.61/+2.65), TOP2A (+2.41/+2.90) and CCNB1 (+2.61/+2.48).
   - Lower global new synthesis in medium (median H/L 0.55/0.49 > M/L 0.43/0.39) also fits TSA growth arrest.
3. **No label swap between replicates.** Log2 H/M of rep1 vs rep2 gives r = 0.857 (n = 2744).

**Curator note: the abstract runs the other way.** The abstract says TYMS and DUT are "strongly induced" and SOD2 "strongly inhibited" by reactivation. With medium = TSA, the deposited H/M ratios show the reverse in the TSA arm (TYMS log2 H/M +4.99/+2.96, SOD2 −2.65/−2.76). The abstract gives a biological direction and never says which label each arm carried. Its statements seem to follow the H/M ratio as reported. This is recorded as a limitation, not used as a basis. A human should still check the full text.
