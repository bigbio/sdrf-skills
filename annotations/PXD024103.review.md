## Key verifications

- **Files:** PRIDE lists 48 RAW files. Each appears exactly twice in the SDRF, once as SILAC heavy and once as SILAC light. No file is missing or extra. `assay name` equals the file stem, and the fraction equals the file-name suffix.
- **Label orientation:** Heavy = Nedd4 siRNA, as stated in the paper Methods ("5 mg K8R10 Nedd4 siRNA and 5 mg K0R0 control siRNA") and the Fig. 1a legend. The data agree: in the Total `proteinGroups.txt`, NEDD4 normalized H/L is 0.281 (Rep1) and 0.179 (Rep2), against GAPDH at 0.92/0.94.
- **Search parameters:** `summary.txt` shows Q Exactive, Arg10;Lys8, multiplicity 2, and variable modifications Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (Protein N-term), plus GlyGly (K) in the Ub search only. `parameters.txt` shows fixed Carbamidomethyl (C) and an MS/MS FTMS tolerance of 20 ppm. The paper gives 6 ppm for the main search.
- **Ontologies:** All 22 accessions resolve in OLS with matching labels. No ubiquitin-specific child of EFO:0009090 exists, so "sample enrichment" is justified. Searches of the Cellosaurus API return no NCU10K.
- **Design:** 2 treatments x 3 series x 2 arms x 8 fractions = 96 rows across 12 sources. The series match the MaxQuant design templates: Ub Exp1/2/3 and Total Rep1/2.

## Findings

### M1 (minor, usability): converter fails because of rows 81-96
- **Location:** `comment[precursor mass tolerance]` and `comment[fragment mass tolerance]`, rows 81-96 (PM20131206_Total_1uL1-8).
- **Evidence:** `convert-openms` exits 1 with "Cannot read precursor mass tolerance: not applicable". The value is legal, because the template sets `allow_not_applicable: true`, and these runs are absent from both deposited searches. However, the PRIDE dataProcessingProtocol gives the 6 ppm main-search tolerance study-wide. These runs also used the same LC/MS method as the PM20131210_Ub runs, which were searched at 6/20 ppm.
- **Fix (optional):** Either fill in 6 ppm / 20 ppm and cite the study-wide processing statement, or keep the sentinel and state in the report that the full file cannot be converted as is.

### M2 (minor, design): third biological replicate and PM20131206 pairing are inferred
- **Location:** `characteristics[biological replicate]` = 3 and the `*_rep3_*` sources.
- **Evidence:** The paper text reports two biological replicates. The Ub design template defines three experiments, and the Fig. 2e legend says "Quantitation of the ubiquitin linkages was performed from 3 independent experiments", which supports the annotation but is not cited in the manifest. The PM20131206_Total pairing rests on the raw folder name `OK20131206_PMNedd4Total_1uL`, the date and the identical LC method. The manifest limitations disclose this.
- **Fix:** No value change. Add the Fig. 2e sentence to the biological-replicate claim in the manifest.

### M3 (minor, spec): recommended `characteristics[sample type]` is absent
- **Evidence:** In base 1.1.0 this column is recommended, with default "study sample".
- **Fix:** Add `characteristics[sample type]` = `study sample` on all rows.

### M4 (minor, validation log): `sdrf-tools check` result is not recorded
- **Evidence:** The command exits 1 and flags NCU10K. NCU10K appears verbatim in the paper Methods and the PRIDE protocol and is absent from Cellosaurus, so the flag is a false positive.
- **Fix:** Append the command, its output, its exit code and a one-line justification to `PXD024103.validation.txt`.
