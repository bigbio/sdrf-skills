## Study

First single-cell proteome of a human oocyte. Human oocytes discarded from an IVF program were
analysed by LC-MS/MS after SP3 sample preparation, in pools of 100, 10, 5, or as single cells, plus
oocyte secretome and a medium-only control. The study compares immature (GV) against mature (MII)
oocytes.

| | |
|---|---|
| Publication | PMID:27215607 / PMC4974340 / doi:10.1074/mcp.M115.056887 — Virant-Klun, Leicht, Hughes, Krijgsveld, *Mol Cell Proteomics* 2016 |
| Instrument | **Orbitrap Velos Pro**, DDA, CID in the LTQ (MaxQuant 1.3.0.5) |
| Isolation | manual denudation-pipette transfer of individual oocytes |
| Screen row | `include` — `label free`, `carrier=no`, PMID:27215607 |

## Screen verification (STEP 3) — the screen was right, but only about part of the deposit

The screen labelled this `include` from the abstract, reading "individual GV and MII oocytes
analysed as single cells via SP3". **Verified from Methods: true, but it describes only 7 of the 21
deposited runs.** PRIDE's own description is the tell — *"the amount differed from 1 to 100 oocytes
per experiment"*. This deposit is the whole study, not just the single-cell arm.

**Do individual oocytes map 1:1 to raw files?** Yes, for the single-cell arm, and the mapping is
deposited rather than inferred. `140818SL_GV3/GV4/GV5/MII3/MII4/MII5.raw` are one oocyte each, and
`140611SL_1oocyte.raw` is a seventh. **NOT BLOCKED.**

The remaining 14 runs are pools (100/10/5 cells), secretome, a medium blank, and three
**dimethyl-labelled duplex** runs. The duplex runs were the one real blocking risk — a multiplexed
design needs a channel → sample map or it is not annotatable. **That map exists and is deposited**
(see below), so the whole deposit is annotatable.

## Publication access

PRIDE reports `is_open_access: false`, and per the brief that is unreliable — so it was tested:
Europe PMC full text 404s (not in the OA subset), Europe PMC PDF 404s, the PMC OA service says
"not Open Access", and Unpaywall resolves a cc-by PDF at mcponline.org that returns HTTP 403.
The **PMC article HTML page is public** and was used; full Experimental Procedures and Results were
read. Nothing here rests on the abstract alone.

**One source was left unexhausted, deliberately.** Supplementary Table S10 (the 1/5/10-oocyte
titration results, the only place the titration samples' maturation stage might be recorded) sits
behind an NCBI proof-of-work anti-bot challenge. Solving that challenge would mean circumventing an
access control, so it was not done. The affected fields are marked `not available` rather than
guessed — see below. This is the single lead a future annotator could pull.

## This deposit is one of three siblings — and that is what made it annotatable

PXD003121, PXD003691 and PXD004142 all carry PMID:27215607. **The paper cites only PXD004142.**
Comparing the three file lists (read-only; no sibling annotation touched):

- **PXD004142** — 18 raw files, same runs, *more descriptive names*.
- **PXD003691** — the same data bundled as 4 zip archives, including the MaxQuant `txt/` outputs.
- **PXD003121** (this one) — **21 raw files: a superset.** It uniquely adds
  `140611SL_1oocyte/5oocytes/10oocytes.raw`, the SP3 cell-number titration.

Two facts in this SDRF are recoverable *only* by cross-reading the siblings, and both are load-bearing:

**1. `130403SL_100oocytes_IVF_1/_2.raw` do not say which is mature.** PXD004142 names the identical
runs `130403SL_Klun_sample1_longer_immature` / `sample2_longer_mature`, and PXD003691's MaxQuant
`experimentalDesignTemplate.txt` maps them to experiments `130403_oocytes_immature` /
`130403_oocytes_mature`. So **IVF_1 = immature (GV)**, **IVF_2 = mature (MII)**. Guessing from the
`_1`/`_2` suffix alone had a 50% chance of inverting two rows.

**2. The dimethyl channel → sample map.** PXD003121's own `proteinGroups_10.txt` names its
experiments `immature_light-mature_heavy1/2/3`, and PXD003691's `summary.txt` gives the chemistry per
raw file: `Multiplicity=2`, `Labels0=DimethLys0;DimethNter0`, `Labels1=DimethLys8;DimethNter8`.
**Light = immature (GV), heavy = mature (MII)** — stated by the submitters, not inferred.

This is exactly what `PXD043473` lacks (see `BLOCKED.md`), where every channel reads
`"Sample, n/a"`. Here the map is explicit, so the 6 duplex rows are evidence-backed.

## Key decisions and evidence

**Instrument — the artifact contradicts PRIDE, on triple evidence.** PRIDE's `instruments` field says
`LTQ Orbitrap Velos`. The paper says "coupled to an **Orbitrap Velos Pro** mass spectrometer".
Range-reading the first 256 KB of six deposited raw files across five batches (utf-16-le) returns the
literal string `Orbitrap Velos Pro` in every one. Annotated as **`MS:1003096`** (OLS label:
`Orbitrap Velos Pro` — an exact string match to the raw headers). PRIDE's submitter field is wrong;
this is the same failure mode the brief warns about and that PXD023366 hit.

**Modifications — PRIDE is wrong again.** PRIDE's `modifications` field lists only "iodoacetamide
derivatized residue". The deposited MaxQuant `parameters.txt` and the paper agree: `Carbamidomethyl`
fixed on C (UNIMOD:4), `Oxidation` variable on M (UNIMOD:35). Not the UNIMOD:1/UNIMOD:21 swap class —
neither Acetyl nor Phospho was searched.

**The heavy dimethyl label — a near-miss worth recording.** MaxQuant's `DimethLys8` is **not**
UNIMOD:510. OLS returns `UNIMOD:510` for the plausible-looking query "Dimethyl", but its label is
`Dimethyl:2H(4)13C(2)` (+6 Da). The +8 Da label is **`UNIMOD:330` = `Dimethyl:2H(6)13C(2)`**,
confirmed independently by `sdrf_pipelines/converters/diann/constants.py`, which defines
`DIMETHYL8` with mass delta `8.0444` (= 36.0757 − 28.0313). Light is `UNIMOD:36` (`Dimethyl`).
`comment[label]` uses `DIMETHYL0` (PRIDE:0000848) / `DIMETHYL8` (PRIDE:0000852) — PRIDE's numbering
matches MaxQuant's convention exactly, and both are the channel names `sdrf-pipelines` itself expects.

**Cell type is ontologically exact, not a label copy.** GV oocytes are arrested in prophase I →
`primary oocyte` (CL:0000654, "has not completed meiosis I"). MII → `secondary oocyte` (CL:0000655).
This is also robust to a caveat in the Methods: the authors define immature as "**GV or MI**", and an
MI oocyte is *still* mid-meiosis-I, so CL:0000654 holds either way. The Results independently state
"100 immature (GV stage)" for the pools.

**Search settings** (paper + deposited `parameters.txt`, which agree): precursor **6 ppm** — the
second/main pass, not the 20 ppm first pass; fragment **0.5 Da** — the ITMS tolerance, which is the
applicable one because fragmentation happened in the LTQ; trypsin/P specificity; FDR 0.01.

**Technical replicates, not fractions.** `130724SL_...hangingdrop{mature,immature}1/2.raw` are pairs
sharing one sample. MaxQuant's `experimentalDesignTemplate.txt` encodes them as different *Fraction*
numbers (1/2, 10/11) — but no fractionation exists anywhere in the Methods; that is the standard
trick to make MaxQuant sum repeat injections. The paper calls them what they are: "analyzed in
**technical replicate** LC-MSMS runs". Annotated as `technical replicate` 1/2 with
`fraction identifier` = 1. MaxQuant's fraction integers are arbitrary and are not reproduced.

**The medium blank is included, not dropped.** `130724SL_control-medium.raw` is unconditioned
DMEM/F12, "devoid of albumin or other proteinaceous constituents" — a true negative control, and its
1.87% MS/MS identification rate (vs 10.42%/7.73% for the real secretomes) confirms it is empty. Per
the brief a blank may be excluded only if a required column forbids both reserved words;
`characteristics[organism]` has `allow_not_applicable: true`, so exclusion is not licensed and the
run is annotated with `organism = not applicable`. That is the honest value: no organism was in the
tube.

## Values left `not available` / `not applicable`, and why

The two tokens are **not** interchangeable and were chosen per-column against the resolved template's
`allow_*` flags.

| column | value | why |
|---|---|---|
| `characteristics[age]` | `not available` | Donor ages appear nowhere in the paper or PRIDE. `allow_not_applicable` is **false** for this column, so `not available` is the only legal abstention — even on the medium-blank row, where it is admittedly awkward. |
| `characteristics[disease]` | `not available` | The donors are IVF patients, but no source states a diagnosis. The tempting fabrication is `female infertility`; the same abstention was reviewer-confirmed correct on PXD023366. |
| `characteristics[individual]` | `not available` | Oocytes are never linked to named donors. Donor count is not recoverable — unlike PXD023366, the filenames carry no donor IDs. |
| `characteristics[phenotype]` (3 titration rows) | `not available` | **The one real gap.** The maturation stage of `140611SL_1oocyte/5oocytes/10oocytes.raw` is stated nowhere: not in the Methods, not in the Results, and these three runs have **no deposited MaxQuant result table in any of the three sibling accessions**. Only Table S10 might carry it (see access note above). Not guessed. |
| `characteristics[cell type]` (same 3 rows) | `NT=oocyte;AC=CL:0000023` | Not `not available`: the sample *is* an oocyte — only its stage is unknown. The generic parent term states exactly what is known and no more. |
| control-medium row | `not applicable` ×7 | organism / organism part / cell type / disease / sex / individual / phenotype: cell-free medium has none of these. |
| secretome rows | `not applicable` | `cell type`, `material type`, `cells per well`, `single cell isolation protocol`: the sample is cell-free conditioned medium. `organism = Homo sapiens` and `organism part = ovary` are retained — the proteins are human and oocyte-derived. |

`characteristics[sex] = female` is not an inference — oocyte donors are female by definition.

`characteristics[single cell isolation protocol] = manual picking` applies to all 21 cell-containing
rows and **is** evidenced rather than assumed, unlike in PXD023366: "Each oocyte was washed four
times and denuded by transfer from one droplet of PBS to another by a **denudation pipette** ... This
was continued until the desired number of cells had been collected." That last clause explicitly
covers the pools too. No FACS/microfluidics/nanoPOTS anywhere.

## Known losses and judgement calls (flagged for the reviewer)

- **`comment[cleavage agent details]` = `NT=Trypsin;AC=MS:1001251` under-describes the digestion.**
  The actual digestion used "a mixture of trypsin and **rLysC**". The column's own description is
  "Enzyme or chemical used for protein digestion", but `ms-proteomics` v1.1.0 does **not** declare
  `cardinality: multiple` for it (only `comment[instrument]` and `comment[modification parameters]`
  are multiple), so a second Lys-C column is not licensed. `Trypsin/P` (MS:1001313) was rejected: it
  is the *search specificity*, not a digestion enzyme, so it would contradict the column definition.
  Lys-C cleaves K, a subset of trypsin's K/R, so the value is a narrowing, not an error.
- **The culture system has no SDRF column.** Conventional IVF medium vs hanging-drop DMEM/F12 is a
  real design axis (it is Fig. 1). `characteristics[treatment]` accepts only NCIT/EFO and has no term
  for it; `phenotype` is about the oocyte's state. It is recorded in
  `comment[sample preparation batch]` (`130403_conventional_IVF_culture` vs
  `130724_hanging_drop_culture`), which is free text and genuinely *is* the prep batch. The batch
  tokens derive from the submitters' own date-coded filenames plus the documented culture system —
  but the encoding is a judgement call, not a spec rule.
- **`characteristics[sample type] = pooled` (PRIDE:0000902) is semantically imperfect for the
  label-free pools.** In the PRIDE hierarchy `pooled` is a child of `multiplex sample`
  (PRIDE:0000898, alongside reference/bridge/carrier/empty), which suits the dimethyl rows but is a
  loose fit for a 100-cell label-free pool. It is used anyway because TERMS.tsv explicitly sanctions
  `pooled` as a `sample type` value in the general sense. `characteristics[pooled sample]` carries
  the unambiguous signal. Note TERMS.tsv also lists `study sample`, which **does not exist in the
  PRIDE ontology** (0 hits, exact and fuzzy) — a reminder that its `values` field is prose, not a
  closed grammar.
- **`biological replicate`** numbers distinct biological samples *within* a phenotype × batch group,
  so the value `1` recurs across unrelated groups. Legal, but worth a reviewer's eye.

**`parse_sdrf` exit 0 is worth less here than it looks — measured, not assumed.** Probing the
validator with deliberate corruptions of this file: replacing the label `DIMETHYL0` with
`BOGUSLABEL999` produced **no error**, and replacing the instrument accession `MS:1003096` with
`MS:9999999` produced **no error**. So `comment[label]` terms and `AC=` accessions are effectively
unchecked. The evidence for every accession in this file is therefore the **live OLS lookup**, not
the validator. All 19 distinct accessions were verified live via `searchClasses` in the
label → accession direction: NCBITaxon:9606, UBERON:0000992, CL:0000023/0000654/0000655,
PRIDE:0000848/0000852/0000897/0000902/0000905/0000627, MS:1003096/1002038/1001251/1000133/1000083,

## Incidental discrepancies (recorded, do not affect the artifact)

- **Single-oocyte count: paper says 6, deposit holds 7.** The Results describe one first single
  oocyte (445 proteins, Table S10) plus "data from **five additional** single oocytes" (Table S11).
  But the deposit holds `140611SL_1oocyte.raw` **and six** `140818SL_GV3/4/5,MII3/4/5.raw`, and
  PXD003691's `experimentalDesignTemplate.txt` lists exactly those six as the Table S11 set — so 1+6,
  not 1+5. Either "five additional" is loose, or one run went unreported. The SDRF annotates all
  seven, because an SDRF describes the deposition, not the narrative.
- **Single-oocyte numbering starts at 3.** There is no GV1/GV2/MII1/MII2 in any of the three sibling
  accessions. Consistent with earlier oocytes having been collected but not deposited; not stated
  anywhere.
- **The paper cites PXD004142, not this accession.** PXD003121 is nonetheless the superset. Anyone
  reproducing the study from the citation alone would miss the SP3 titration series.
