## Study
"Proteomic characterization of the interplay of Staphylococcus aureus and human alveolar epithelial cells during infection – host cell proteins".

- **Papers.** Surmann et al., J Proteomics 2015;128:203-217 (PMID 26244908; not open access, not read). The data paper is Surmann et al., Data Brief 2016;7:1031-1037 (PMC5063755; open access, read in full).
- **Infection.** A549 cells were infected with *S. aureus* HG001 pMV158GFP (GFP-expressing) at an MOI of 25 for 1 h. Lysostaphin was then added.
- **Sorting.** At 1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 4.5, 5.5 and 6.5 h post infection, cells were FACS-sorted into two populations:
  - GFP-positive: infected cells;
  - GFP-negative: cells that were exposed to the bacteria but contain none.
- **Control.** Mock-infected A549 cells were sorted with the same settings.
- **SILAC standard.** Every sorted light sample (12C Arg/Lys) was spiked 1:1 with heavy (13C6 Arg/Lys) untreated A549 standard cells.
- **Replicates and runs.** There are 3 independent internalization assays per group. Each sample was measured in one LTQ Orbitrap Velos run.
- **Search.** Rosetta Elucidator with SEQUEST 3.5, against a 2012 human-only SwissProt database.
- **Companion dataset.** The bacterial proteome is deposited separately as PXD002384 (a different dataset, not a duplicate). PRIDE links the two through `otherOmicsLinks`.

## Partial-scope / open items (all also in manifest `limitations`)
1. **Control identity is inferred.** The 18 `120505_OV4_KRIS_HPI_<n>_<t>_BR1..3` runs carry no condition word in their names. They are taken to be the mock-infected controls by elimination:
   - the deposit holds exactly the 3 groups the paper documents;
   - the 36 infected-culture runs are explicitly named gfppos/gfpneg;
   - the remaining runs are exactly 3 replicates × 6 time points and match the `control_sorted_SILAC_BR1-3` tables;
   - the raw headers carry no sample description.
2. **Unsearched control runs.** `120505_OV4_KRIS_HPI_1_1_5h_BR1` and `120505_OV4_KRIS_HPI_8_1_5h_BR2` (control 1.5 h, replicates 1 and 2) are missing from the deposited result tables:
   - the control BR1 and BR2 tables list only 2.5-6.5 h;
   - so these runs are kept, with modification parameters and tolerances set to `not applicable` (rows 105-108; separate build, identical headers, concatenated).

   This pair assumes that the raw BR number equals the table BR number.
3. **Infection batch numbering.** Infection experiments 1-3 are numbered by run date. How they map to the gfp table numbers BR1-3 is not stated.
4. **`6_6h` file names.** Two names say `6_6h_gfpneg` (in experiments 1 and 2). They are annotated as 6.5 hour: the design is hourly, the gfppos partners say 6_5h, experiment 3 says 6_5h, and the tables have no 6.6 h condition.
5. **Re-injections.** Three `_r` names are presumably re-injections. Only one file per sample is deposited, so technical replicate is 1 throughout.
6. **Heavy standard independence.** Whether several heavy aliquots came from one culture or harvest is not documented.
7. **Tolerances and convert-openms.** The precursor and fragment tolerances are `not available`, so `convert-openms` fails.
8. **Unretrieved files.** Two `.xls` proteins tables returned HTTP 403 and were not bypassed; their peptides tables were read instead. The J Proteomics 2015 paper is not open access.
9. **Transient OLS 500s.** The validator's own OLS searches hit transient 500s, but it completed with exit 0. The first `sdrf-tools check` crashed on an OLS 500 and was re-run with `--offline`, which gave CLEAN.
