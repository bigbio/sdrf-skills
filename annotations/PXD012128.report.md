## Study
- *Proteomic Profiling of Mammalian COPII Vesicles* (PRIDE). Paper: Adolf F *et al.*, *Proteomic Profiling of Mammalian COPII and COPI Vesicles*, Cell Rep 2019;26:250-265, doi:10.1016/j.celrep.2018.12.041, PMID 30605680. The companion COPI dataset is PXD012129 (not annotated here).
- Design: COPII vesicles were reconstituted *in vitro* from semi-intact SILAC-labelled HeLa cells (light Lys0/Arg0, heavy Lys8/Arg10). The reconstitutions used recombinant Sar1b, Sec13/31A and an inner-coat Sec23A/Sec24 isoform. Two vesicle preparations were mixed 1:1 and analysed in one run. Every comparison was done as label-switch pairs.
- Full text: there is no PMC copy and Unpaywall/cell.com returned 403. The publisher-hosted "Article plus Supplemental Information" PDF (`ars.els-cdn.com/.../mmc4.pdf`) was read in full, together with Table S1 (`mmc2.xlsx`).
- Step 0.5: PXD012128 is not in `sdrf-annotated-datasets`, and PRIDE has no deposited SDRF.

## Channel → condition map (the crux)
1. **Stated:** each Table S1 column header gives the channel of each condition per Exp, e.g. `Sec24A.(H).vs.Ctrl.(L) - Exp1`.
2. **Exp → raw-file link (reconstructed):** each Table S1 Exp's per-protein *Razor+unique peptide* counts were compared with the deposited `FrankMeta2_proteinGroups4.txt`. All 16 matched exactly one MaxQuant experiment at 100% identity; the next-best match was ≤42% (`evidence/channel_map_razor_match.txt`).
3. **Direction cross-check (independent):** per-channel `Intensity H/L` from the deposited `proteinGroups.txt` was read for known cargo (`evidence/channel_direction_marker_intensities.txt`):
   - In vesicle-vs-mock runs, COPII cargo (SEC22B, LMAN1, SURF4, ERGIC1, TMED10…) is 2–5 log2 units higher in the channel Table S1 calls the vesicle.
   - In Sec24A-vs-Sec24C runs, the Sec24A clients SEC22B/CNIH4 and the Sec24C clients STX5/BET1/ERGIC1 sit in the stated channels.
   - In LIL895AAA runs, STX5/BET1/SCFD1/GOSR2 are depleted in the stated mutant channel, matching the paper's Fig 4A.
   - **0 discrepancies across 16 runs.**
4. **Table S1 typo:** in S2D Exp2 the ratio header reads `(H/L) Sec24C.(H)`, but the peptide headers read `Sec24C.(L)`. The intensities show Sec24C is **light** in 16-186-02, which is what the SDRF says.

## Key technical decisions (deposited search > paper > PRIDE)
- **Modifications** come from the deposited mqpar (`FrankAdolphMeta2_Wieland.xml`), which agrees with `parameters.txt` and `summary.txt`:
  - Fixed: Carbamidomethyl C.
  - Variable: Oxidation M; Acetyl at Protein N-term (`PP=`).
  - SILAC heavy `labelMods` = `Arg10;Lys8`, written as `NT=Label:13C(6)15N(4);AC=UNIMOD:267;TA=R;MT=Variable` and `NT=Label:13C(6)15N(2);AC=UNIMOD:259;TA=K;MT=Variable`. This matches the paper (15N4 13C6-Arg, 15N2 13C6-Lys, Silantes kit) and the community convention for SILAC.
  - **Deamidation (NQ) is omitted.** The paper and PRIDE list it, but the deposited search does not contain it.
- **Labels:** `SILAC light` / `SILAC heavy` (PRIDE:0000326 / PRIDE:0000325), written bare, as the spec examples do.
- **Instruments** come from range-read `.raw` headers:
  - 14 runs are **Q Exactive HF** (MS:1002523). The paper and PRIDE say "Q Exactive"; the raw headers say "Q Exactive HF - Orbitrap MS".
  - 2 runs are **Orbitrap Elite** (MS:1001910).
  - Elite: CID with ion-trap MS2, method `CID_Top20` (the paper says "up to 30"), fragment tolerance 0.5 Da (MaxQuant ITMS).
  - QE HF: HCD with Orbitrap MS2, TopN 15, fragment tolerance 20 ppm (MaxQuant FTMS).
- **Precursor tolerance:** 4.5 ppm (MaxQuant main search).
- **Reduction / alkylation:** DTT / IAA (STAR Methods).
- **Acquisition:** DDA.
- **Cleavage agent:** `Trypsin` (MS:1001251), the digestion enzyme stated in the PRIDE protocol and STAR Methods. The deposited MaxQuant search used the rule Trypsin/P with 2 missed cleavages; this is recorded in the manifest only.
- **Fractions:** 1 per file. The mqpar has no fraction numbers and each file is its own experiment. The PRIDE protocol says "2 gel pieces per sample"; whether those were combined before injection is not stated.
