# PXD004241 — SDRF annotation report

**Status: REVIEW_PENDING_EXTERNAL**. This is a draft. It has not been independently reviewed and no gate approval was given.
**Scope: complete.** All 120 deposited .raw files are annotated. No runs were excluded or blocked.

Dataset: *Quantitative proteomic analysis of HIV-1 Tat-induced dysregulation in SH-SY5Y neuroblastoma cells* (Ganief et al., Proteomics 2017, doi:10.1002/pmic.201600236, closed access).

## Design
- **SILAC duplex**, with MaxQuant 1.3.0.5 Multiplicity 2: light = Arg0/Lys0, heavy = Arg10/Lys8 (summary.txt Labels1 `Arg10;Lys8` for all 120 files).
- **Channel map.**
  - The map comes from the PRIDE sample protocol ("Heavy and light ... treated with 1 ug/mL HIV-1 Tat protein or control, respectively").
  - The first author's PhD thesis (UCT, hdl 11427/22759) confirms it: "K0/R0 ... vehicle, K8/R10 ... HIV-Tat".
  - The deposited ratios agree. ARHGEF17, SHROOM3 and CRMP1 are down-regulated by Tat (abstract), and their Ratio H/L normalized is 0.007–0.69 in every experiment. That confirms heavy = Tat.
  - **light = untreated (vehicle) control, heavy = HIV TAT Protein (NCIT:C17186)**, 1 µg/ml for 24 h.
- **Biological replicates.** There were 3 dish pairs. Each pair was mixed 1:1 and lysed. The lysate then went through a QIAGEN phosphoprotein column, giving two parts:
  - the **PE** eluate (raw prefix P)
  - the **PD** flow-through (raw prefix D)
- **Fractions and injections.** Each part was run on 1D SDS-PAGE and cut into **10 gel slices**, and each slice was **injected twice**.
- **Sources.** 12 sources = {vehicle, Tat} × rep 1–3 × {PE, PD}. Biological replicate is numbered 1–3 within each treatment group, and PE and PD from the same dish share the number.
- **Columns per row.**
  - Fraction identifier = gel slice 1–10.
  - Technical replicate = injection order, taken from the raw-header acquisition time. The bare file name is the first injection except for P1-1, where `P1-1_120512125011.raw` came first.
- **Size.** 120 files × 2 channels = **240 rows × 50 columns**.
- **Rows per treatment.** untreated: 120 rows (6 sources); HIV TAT Protein: 120 rows (6 sources).

## Templates
ms-proteomics v1.2.0, human v1.1.0 and cell-lines v1.1.0 (upstream sdrf-templates 7da1747; the spec submodule copy is older). The sdrf version is v1.1.0.

## Key values
- **Cell line and donor:** SH-SY5Y (CVCL_0019). Organism part is bone marrow (Cellosaurus derived-from site). Disease is neuroblastoma. Donor is 4Y, female, pediatric stage, European ancestry. Medium is DMEM.
- **Instrument:** LTQ Orbitrap Velos, from the raw header, the thesis and the abstract. PRIDE's "LTQ Orbitrap Elite" was overruled.
- **Acquisition:** DDA top-10, HCD at 35 NCE, Orbitrap MS2.
- **Sample preparation:** trypsin, DTT, IAA, SDS-PAGE fractionation.
- **Search:**
  - Fragment tolerance is 20 ppm (FTMS).
  - Modifications: Carbamidomethyl C (fixed); Oxidation M, Acetyl Protein N-term and Deamidated N/Q (variable); Label 13C6 15N4 R and 13C6 15N2 K (variable).
- **Custom and enrichment columns:** the custom `characteristics[protein fraction]` records PE or PD. `characteristics[enrichment process]` is "enrichment of phosphorylated protein" for PE and "not applicable" for PD.
