- **PMID 32161263** (Zhang et al., *Nat Commun* 2020, BRAWNIN) is only a reanalysis of the mitochondrial raw files.

## Design
All samples are HEK293T: CVCL_0063, HEK-293T cell, female, fetal stage, kidney.

### Knockout SDRF
- **Factors:** `genotype` × `cellular component` (mitochondrion / whole cell), 52 conditions in total.
- **Mitochondrial runs** (001–129, single injections):
  - 38 knockout lines: 30 accessory subunits, 7 assembly factors and COA6.
  - 3 SILAC experiments each; NDUFA13 has 6 (batches R and I).
  - T1–T5 are technical replicates (repeat injections).
  - **COA6 exception:** T4/T5 of J-102/103/104 are a separate 8 M urea preparation (Sep 2014, vials RE1–RE3). T1–T3 (Mar 2014, vials RC7/RC8/RD1) are earlier injections. `comment[sample preparation batch]` records the split: "Elite MB307 (2014-03/04), mitochondrial digest, injections T1-T3" and "Elite MB531 (2014-09), separate 8 M urea digest of the same SILAC mixture, injections T4-T5". It is `not available` on every other row. The "same mixture" assumption rests on MaxQuant, which groups T1–T5 into one Experiment.
- **Whole-cell runs:** 11 lines × 3 experiments, each fractionated into 6 SCX fractions.
- **Controls:** each experiment's partner is HEK293T wild type, with these exceptions in the NDUFAB1 experiments O-168..170:
  - The knockout is written "NDUFAB1 knockout; yeast ACP1 expressing".
  - Its partner is "HEK293T expressing yeast ACP1".
  - Evidence: yeast ACP1 (P32463) is requantified in both channels at ratio counts 6–8. Plain-HEK293T partners (O-171, O-173) have none. Supp Table 1 names the knockout clones ACP1-1 and ACP1-3, and Ext Data Fig. 3a/3e describe yACP1Flag complementation.
- **Biological replicates** are numbered per factor group:
  - wild type, mitochondrion: 1–114
  - wild type, whole cell: 1–33
  - knockout lines: 1–3 (NDUFA13: 1–6)
  - control numbers are an upper bound (one per SILAC mixture).

### AP-MS SDRF
- **Factors:** `genotype` × `detergent`, 8 conditions in total.
- **Baits:**
  - DMAC1-FLAG and ATP5SL-FLAG ("expressing"; the background is not stated, detergent not available);
  - NDUFV3-FLAG in the NDUFV3 knockout, with Triton X-100 and digitonin enrichments as separate groups;
  - NDUFAB1-FLAG in the NDUFAB1 knockout, Triton X-100, Doxycycline.
- **Controls:**
  - All experiments: HEK293T wild type.
  - NDUFAB1-FLAG runs 25–27: the control is DOX-treated HEK293T, as the Ext Data Fig. 3d legend says. The Supp S2 header "HEK293TACP1" is contradicted by the data: the yeast ACP1 construct is FLAG-tagged and was searched, yet it is absent from all 15 anti-FLAG eluates. Its controls are numbered 4–6 in the wild type / Triton X-100 group.
- **Biological replicates:** 1–3 per (genotype, detergent) group.

### Channel map and its source
- **Rule:** an HL tag means the knockout or FLAG line is SILAC heavy and the control is light. An LH tag means the reverse.
- **Explicit statement:** the Supp Data column definitions call HL/LH the "label orientation identifier". The AP-MS tables label the experiments "Expt. 1/2 H/L XFLAG/HEK293T" and "Expt. 3 L/H".
- **Data check:** of the 150 knockout experiments, 122 quantify their target (90 mitochondrial, 32 whole-cell). All 122 agree with the tag. All 15 AP-MS bait ratios agree.
- **Tag conflicts:** two acquisition names carry the opposite tag. V-227 (NDUFB9) was acquired as "…FB9-LH" and T-215 (NDUFAF2) as "…NDUFAF2-HL". In both cases the data support the deposited tag, so the deposited tag is used.

## Limitations (also recorded in the manifests)
- The paper Methods were not read. As a result, culture medium, reagents, passage and Lys-C use are not available.
- "Wild type = parental HEK293T" rests on the AP-MS headers, Fig. 2c and the data.
- Control replicate numbers are an upper bound.
- Sample preparation varies across batches: R-004..006 are "InSolMito Stagetip" (2013), and COA6 has two preparations. Only the COA6 split is encoded.
- The yeast ACP1 constructs in the two NDUFAB1-experiment lines are not documented.
- The whole-cell clone tokens are not encoded.
- Six NDUFV1 runs appear in the design template but were neither deposited nor searched.
