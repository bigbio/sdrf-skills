Stroud DA et al., "Accessory subunits are integral for assembly and function of human mitochondrial complex I", *Nature* 2016;538:123-126, doi:10.1038/nature19754. Its data statement reads: "Data are available via ProteomeXchange under accession PXD004666".

## Design
Every sample is a HEK293T line: CVCL_0063, HEK-293T cell, female, fetal stage, kidney. The single factor is `factor value[genotype]`.

### SDRF 1: knockout (`combined.zip`)
- **Mitochondria.** Runs 001-129, single injections. Orbitrap Elite (CID, ion trap MS2) or Q Exactive Plus (HCD). Repeat injections T1-T5 are technical replicates; `035..R-005` has T1 and T3 only, so they are numbered 1 and 2.
- **Whole-cell.** Runs `P1DAS_2016*`, QE Plus, SCX fractions F1-F6.
- **Lines.** 38 knockout lines: 30 accessory subunits, the assembly factors NDUFAF1, NDUFAF2, NDUFAF4, NDUFAF6, TIMMDC1, DMAC1 and ATP5SL, and COA6 (the complex IV control).
- **Replicates.** Each line has 3 SILAC experiments. NDUFA13 has 6 (batches R and I). 11 lines also have 3 whole-cell experiments.
- **Controls.** Every experiment has its own HEK293T wild-type partner: 117 mitochondrial + 33 whole-cell. Each partner gets its own biological replicate number, 1-150.
- `characteristics[cellular component]` is `mitochondrion` for the mitochondrial runs and `whole cell` (free text) for the whole-cell runs.

### Channel map and its source
- **HL in the file name:** the knockout or FLAG line is SILAC heavy and the HEK293T control is light.
- **LH in the file name:** the reverse.

Sources:
- **Explicit statement.** Supp Data "Column definitions" defines HL/LH as the "label orientation identifier". The AP-MS tables label the HL experiments "Expt. 1/2 H/L <bait>FLAG/HEK293T" and the LH experiment "Expt. 3 L/H <bait>FLAG/HEK293T". PRIDE says the raw names carry the "applicable label orientation".
- **Data check: AP-MS.** In proteinGroups.txt, the bait H/L is log2 +3.1 to +6.1 in all 10 HL runs and −3.6 to −5.0 in all 5 LH runs.
- **Data check: knockouts.** In 89 of the 117 annotated mitochondrial experiments, the knocked-out protein is quantified. Its H/L is negative in every HL experiment and positive in every LH experiment: 89 of 89 agree and none contradicts.
- **Whole-cell runs.** They follow the same tags. Supp Table S5 column names match them (e.g. FS6 1/2/3 = W.1/2/3.NDUFS6.HL/HL/LH).

### Technical values
- **Search.** MaxQuant 1.5.4.1, Trypsin/P with 2 missed cleavages. Carbamidomethyl C is fixed. Oxidation M, Acetyl Protein N-term, Arg10 (UNIMOD:267) and Lys8 (UNIMOD:259) are variable.
- **Precursor tolerance: 4.5 ppm.** PRIDE says MaxQuant default parameters were used, and 4.5 ppm is the 1.5.4.1 default main-search tolerance. The AP-MS evidence.txt is consistent: |mass error| is ≤0.22 ppm at the 99.9th percentile.
- **Fragment tolerance.** 20 ppm for FTMS (QE Plus) and 0.5 Da for ITMS (Elite), from parameters.txt.

## Limitations (also in the manifests)
- **Methods not read.** The paper Methods are paywalled, so culture medium, reduction/alkylation reagents and passage are `not available` or left out. PRIDE lists "iodoacetamide derivatized residue" but cites Kulak et al. (chloroacetamide), so no reagent is written. Lys-C use in the whole-cell protocol is unknown; only trypsin is written.
- **Control identity.** "Wild type" means parental HEK293T. This rests on the AP-MS headers, Fig. 2c and the ratio data, not on a Methods sentence.
- **Control cultures.** Each control partner has its own biological replicate number. Whether control cultures were shared between experiments is not documented.
- **NDUFAB1 knockout line.** It expresses yeast ACP1 (P32463 is detected in its channel; Supp Table 1 lists clones ACP1-1/ACP1-3 as "KO (NDUFAB1)"). The genotype is written only as "NDUFAB1 knockout".
- **NDUFAB1 AP-MS control conflict.** The Supp Table S2 header says "HEK293TACP1", while the Ext Data 3d legend says "DOX-treated HEK293T". The legend is followed.
- **DMAC1/ATP5SL FLAG lines.** The genetic background is not stated, so no knockout is asserted.
- **Settings sampling.** Instrument settings were read from one raw file per batch (25 files).
- **Precursor tolerance.** It rests on the "default parameters" statement; no mqpar.xml was deposited.
- **NDUFV1 runs.** `experimentalDesignTemplate.txt` lists 6 NDUFV1 runs (124-126) that were never deposited or searched.
