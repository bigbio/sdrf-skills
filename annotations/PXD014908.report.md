# PXD014908 — SILAC of HepG2 or HepAD38 cell lines with HBV

**Status:** ANNOTATED, REVIEW_PENDING_EXTERNAL (the producer ran no review; an external reviewer handles it).
**Scope:** full. All 72 deposited .raw files are annotated, with none excluded. The two MaxQuant txt files (proteinGroups.txt, peptides.txt) are search outputs, not runs.
**SDRF:** `annotations/PXD014908.sdrf.tsv`, 216 rows x 52 columns (72 raw files x 3 SILAC channels), sha256 `6a7d50f7ff1d23b8e6a53309b0bd968f54b9a6a67eccd7a2663fdddf12cc5abf`.

## Study
- Submitted to PRIDE 2019-08-05 by Menghuan Zhang, PI Ronggui Hu (SIBCB, CAS). MaxQuant search. The PRIDE record cites Yuan S et al., J Hepatol 2021 (PMID 33621634). That paper is not open access and was not read.
- The paper used is Yuan S et al., *Cell Discov* 2021;7:105 (PMC8560872). Its Data Availability section names PXD014908. It describes the SILAC arm as triple SILAC of HepG2, HepAD38 Tet+ (HBV transgene repressed) and HepAD38 Tet− (HBV replicating after tetracycline withdrawal).
- Labels:
  - light: normal medium
  - medium ("middle"): Lys4 (D4) + Arg6 (13C6)
  - heavy: Lys8 (13C6 15N2) + Arg10 (13C6 15N4)
- Mixing: 20 µg of each lysate were mixed.
- Design: three mixtures (rep1–rep3), each in 24 fractions, run on an LTQ Orbitrap Velos.
- It is multiplexed SILAC: proteinGroups has Intensity L/M/H and Ratio M/L, H/L, H/M (multiplicity 3).
- Not a duplicate. The linked PXD022646 is a separate HpZ co-IP spectral-counting dataset.

**Source of the map.** Cell Discov Supplementary Data S10 has two sheets, "HepAD38(Tet-)_HepG2" and "HepAD38(Tet-)_HepAD38(Tet+)". Each has the columns Ratio rep1/rep2/rep3. I compared these numbers with every deposited proteinGroups.txt ratio column, in both directions (`chanmap.py`):
- Each replicate matches exactly one normalized ratio column, for 98.9–99.7% of proteins. For example, rep1 Tet−/HepG2 equals "Ratio H/L normalized 1" for 2619 of 2636 proteins.
- Every other column or direction matched 42 proteins or fewer, which is chance level.
- The result is a rotating label-swap design, consistent in both sheets for all three replicates.

No sentence in the paper or the PRIDE protocol states the label assignment. The map is therefore derived from supplementary data matched against the deposited quantities (rule 6).

**Groups and replicates.**
- Groups are HepG2 (HBV-negative), HepAD38 Tet+ (HBV transgene repressed) and HepAD38 Tet− (HBV replicating). Each group has three biological replicates, numbered 1–3 within the group. The paper says "all three biological replicates".
- Source names look like `HepG2_rep1` and `HepAD38_Tet-_rep2`. Each name is one lysate in one mixture.
- Factor values are `factor value[cell line]` and `factor value[infection status]`.
- HBV status is in custom columns:
  - `characteristics[infection status]`
  - `characteristics[infectious agent]`: Hepatitis B virus, NCBITaxon:10407, for HepAD38. HepAD38 carries an integrated, tetracycline-repressed HBV genome (Cellosaurus CVCL_M177). HepG2 rows get `not applicable`.

  - Carbamidomethyl C, fixed. Every Cys peptide in peptides.txt carries +57.021 Da per Cys.

- **Cleavage agent:** Trypsin, inferred. Peptides are fully tryptic. Neither the paper nor PRIDE names the enzyme.
