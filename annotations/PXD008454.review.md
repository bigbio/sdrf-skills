### Key question: is the channel map explicitly sourced (rule 6)?

**Yes. Do not block.** The AACR Supplementary Materials and Methods are openly available on AACR Figshare: article 22516368, DOI 10.1158/1541-7786.22516368.v1, file https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/39978906. Figshare lists its resource_doi as 10.1158/1541-7786.MCR-18-0212 (identity checked), and it is not behind the aacrjournals.org bot check. Under "MS/MS analysis – Samples" it states:

> "For proteomics analysis SILAC labelled cells were cultured in 10cm dishes. One dish per biological replicate was used. Light labelled cells were treated with DMSO, heavy labelled cells were treated with 10 nM Afatinib for 24 hours, 3 days, 5 days or 7 days. Per condition, 3 dishes were made for 3 biological replicates. Quantification was performed by mixing light labelled proteins from DMSO control with each heavy labelled time point."

The deposited ratios agree with this statement: log2 H/L is down for MKI67, MCM, PCNA and CCNB1 at 3D–7D, and up for MARCKSL1, CNN2 and ATP2C1, with positive correlation across all 12 experiments. The map in the SDRF (heavy = afatinib, light = DMSO/untreated) is therefore correct and meets rule 6. However, the manifest, BLOCKED.md and the report all claim that no explicit statement exists. That provenance has to be corrected.
