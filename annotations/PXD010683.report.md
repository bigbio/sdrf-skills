## Key decisions with evidence
1. **Screen-2 channel map.** The file names and MaxQuant experiment names say "A375SR_ML" and "A375SR_LM". I read the letters positionally: in ML, S = medium and R = light; LM is the swap. The paper says "SILAC labeled A375 S and A375 R cells ('light,' 'medium,' and vice versa)". I checked the map against the deposited ratios. Nestin (P48681) is the paper's headline protein, down in R with median log2 R/S −2.71 in all replicates. Its log2 ratio (Arg6Lys4 over light) is +1.70 in ML_BR1, +1.43 in ML_BR2 and −2.09 in LM_BR3, so nestin is high in the medium channel of the ML files and in the light channel of the LM files. That puts S in medium for ML and in light for LM. A per-raw-file check on 207 consensus marker protein groups agrees: every ML file has a median between +0.79 and +1.18 (89-98% sign agreement), and every LM file a median between −0.56 and −0.88.
2. **`20180707_MaS_A375SR_ML_proteome_2_01`.** MaxQuant's experimentalDesignTemplate assigns this file to `A375SR_LM_BR3_proteome`, which looks like a design-file slip. Its own ratios match the ML orientation (median +0.93, 91% agreement), so I annotated it by its file name as BR2 fraction 1, with S = medium.
3. **Part-2 channel map.** The paper states it explicitly: "SILAC labeled Nes-KO, A375 S and A375 R cells were used ('light,' 'medium,' 'heavy')". The file token `33SR_LMH` has the same order. The data agree: nestin log2 M/L is +4.14 and +2.21 (knockout lacks nestin), and H/M is −1.35 and −1.64 (R below S).
4. **Phospho rounds are fractions, with their own source.** In part 2, `phospho_01-09` and `phospho2_01-09` are sequential TiO2 enrichment rounds of one mixture. They are encoded as fraction identifiers 1-9 of a phospho source, which carries `characteristics[enrichment process]` = enrichment of phosphorylated protein. The proteome run of the same mixture is a separate source with `not applicable` and the same biological replicate number. `comment[fractionation method]` = `not applicable` for all part-2 rows, and the high-pH RP PRIDE term for screen 2.
5. **Biological replicate numbering (rule 3).** Screen 2 and part 2 are independent cultures, measured in 2018 and 2017. Within each factor group, S and R are numbered 1-3 for screen 2 and 4-5 for part 2. Nes-KO is numbered 1-2.
6. **Modifications come from the deposited searches (rule 5).** Both searches use Carbamidomethyl (C) fixed, and Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term) and Phospho (STY) variable; Phospho was searched on the proteome runs too. Screen 2 has Arg6 (UNIMOD:188) and Lys4 (UNIMOD:481), and its Arg10/Lys8 columns are `not applicable`. Part 2 adds Arg10 (UNIMOD:267) and Lys8 (UNIMOD:259). Label mods are MT=Variable (rule 11). The two parts were built separately with identical headers and concatenated.
7. **Cleavage.** Digestion was Lys-C (3 h) followed by trypsin overnight, so there are two `comment[cleavage agent details]` columns: Trypsin and Lys-C. The second column is inserted by a deterministic post-build script, justified in validation.txt. The MaxQuant rule was Trypsin/P, which is recorded in the manifest only.
8. **Technical values.** Raw headers give Q Exactive HF, HCD at NCE 27, and an Orbitrap MS2 analyzer. mqpar.xml gives the 4.5 ppm main-search tolerance and the 20 ppm FTMS MS/MS tolerance. The paper gives DTT and IAA.
9. **Cell line.** A375 is EFO:0002103, Cellosaurus CVCL_0132 / A-375, and was bought from ATCC (PRIDE protocol). The donor values come from Cellosaurus: female, 54Y (so adult), Caucasian mapped to European ancestry, amelanotic melanoma, and skin of leg. The R and Nes-KO lines were made in the authors' lab and have no Cellosaurus entries; I searched by name and by the PMIDs of Sinnberg 2016 and this paper. They carry the parental accession and are told apart by two custom columns, `characteristics[phenotype]` and `characteristics[genotype]`. These are free text and not licensed by the declared templates; the spec's "Extending Templates" section allows them (rule 15).

## Excluded runs (partial scope)
- **Screen 1 (33 runs).** All six MaxQuant experiments are named "ML". The deposited ratios show the orientation flips between experiments:

  | experiment | S channel implied by the ratios |
  |---|---|
  | proteome BR1 | medium |
  | proteome BR2, proteome BR3 | light |
  | phospho BR1 | light |
  | phospho BR2, phospho BR3 | medium |

  This comes from per-file marker medians and nestin, and is consistent across all fractions of each experiment. It contradicts the experiment names, and it contradicts the assumption that the proteome and phospho runs of one nominal replicate come from one mixture. No explicit per-run label statement exists in any source I could reach. Rules 6 and 7 apply: a map derived from the data is not a label statement, so the runs are left out. Evidence: `screen1_orientation.txt` and `perfile_orientation.txt` in scratch.
- **FFPE (2 runs).** The deposited FFPE search is of `20181211_MaS_prepostBRAfi_dimethyl_LH_1ug`, a mixed light/heavy dimethyl run that was not deposited. Nothing documents the label state of the two deposited single-condition runs. The per-patient clinical data are in Supplementary Information S3, which I could not retrieve (rule 18).

## Values left as reserved words
- `not available`: cell type (not stated; rule 9), individual, passage number and sample storage temperature (not stated). Phenotype for Nes-KO is also `not available`: the clone's drug sensitivity is the paper's finding, not a defining property of the line.
- `not applicable`: enrichment process on proteome rows; fractionation method on part-2 rows; the Arg10/Lys8 modification columns on screen-2 rows, because that search had no heavy label.

## Conflicts and how I resolved them
- PRIDE `identifiedPTMStrings` says "No PTMs". The searches include Phospho, Oxidation and Acetyl, and the deposited search was used.
- PRIDE lists only SILAC. The FFPE arm is dimethyl, and it is excluded.
- The paper says 143 runs and 10 phospho rounds per replicate. The deposit has 145 raw files, and part 2 has 9 rounds per replicate. All deposited part-2 runs are annotated.
- The MaxQuant design file mis-assigns ML_proteome_2_01. I followed the file name and the data (decision 2).
