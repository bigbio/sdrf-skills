In all three experiments the bait (eGFP-HttEx1) sits in the heavy channel, at 30–108x the light intensity. PRIDE's dataProcessingProtocol says "The Q18 interactome was analyzed using uninduced (without Q18 expression) cells as the background control". Uninduced cells cannot carry the bait, so heavy = Q18-induced and light = uninduced. There is no label swap. No retrievable text gives the orientation in words, and the limitations say so openly. Rule 6 asks for an explicit source to be "checked against" the data. Here the PRIDE text names the two arms and the bait fixes which label each one carries, so I accept the orientation. Small caveat: the manifest compares raw EGFP ratios with a *normalized* proteome median of 0.70 (finding m2). The conclusion still holds with like-for-like numbers.
