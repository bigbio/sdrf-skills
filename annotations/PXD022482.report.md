## Study
- **Paper:** Ferguson ID et al., "The surfaceome of multiple myeloma cells suggests potential immunotherapeutic strategies and protein markers of drug resistance". *Nat Commun* 2022;13:4121, doi:10.1038/s41467-022-31810-6, PMID 35840578, PMC9287322 (open access).
- **PRIDE title:** "Defining the cell surface proteomic landscape of multiple myeloma…".
- **SILAC arm:** in-vitro-evolved lenalidomide-resistant (LenR) NCI-H929 and OPM-2 cells were compared with their parental (WT) lines.
  - Labels: light vs heavy (Lys8 / Arg10). Each mixture was 1:1 by cell count.
  - Sample preparation: macro cell-surface capture (CSC), i.e. periodate/biocytin-hydrazide labelling, NeutrAvidin capture and on-bead trypsin.
  - Design: duplicate forward plus duplicate reverse mixtures, which the paper calls "four biological replicates".
  - MS: Q Exactive Plus, top-20 DDA, HCD at NCE 27.
  - Search: ProteinProspector 5.13.2 (6 ppm / 0.4 Da). Quantification: Skyline MS1.
- **Related deposits:** PXD022553 and PXD032031 hold the micro-CSC and primary/TMT data. They share no raw files with these 16, so this is not a duplicate.

**Factors.** There are two: `factor value[cell line]` and `factor value[phenotype]` (lenalidomide sensitive / resistant). That gives four groups with 8 rows each.

**Check against the deposited data.** I recomputed log2 LenR/WT from the deposited Skyline light/heavy MS1 areas, using the map above:
- **Marker proteins.** CD33 is up in all 8 H929 files and all 4 OPM-2 PNGase F files. PTPRC is up in all 8 H929 files. The paper reports exactly these two (CD33 and PTPRC/CD45) as the common increases.
- **Supplementary Data 6.** For proteins with |log2| > 1 in SD6, the sign agrees more often than not in every one of the 16 files (12–71 agree vs 4–20 disagree).
- **Conclusion.** The map is confirmed.
- **Caveat.** Across all proteins, forward and reverse files of one experiment are weakly anti-correlated (−0.29 to −0.51). A label-specific bias dominates the many unregulated proteins. This is recorded as a limitation.

**Fractions.** Fraction 1 is the tryptic peptides and fraction 2 is the PNGase F-released peptides. Both raws of a pair carry the same source names because they come from one mixture. Two things support this:
- the file description pairs them;
- each pair shares the same mixing offset (median log2 H/L), e.g. H929 mix1 −0.95 / −1.28 and mix2 −0.02 / −0.18.

## Technical values
- **Modifications.** Taken from the paper and the deposited Skyline modified sequences:
  - fixed: Carbamidomethyl (C);
  - variable: Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term), Lys8 (UNIMOD:259) and Arg10 (UNIMOD:267);
  - Deamidated (N) only on the PNGase F rows. N[+1] occurs about 15–18k times in the PNGase F exports and 0 times in the tryptic ones.
- **How the per-fraction modifications were built (rule 5).** I ran two builds with identical headers and selected rows per fraction with a script. The justification is in validation.txt.
- **Heavy label masses.** The heavy-light mass shifts are 8.01 Da (K) and 10.01 Da (R), which agrees with the paper's L-[13C6,15N2]Lys and L-[13C6,15N4]Arg.
- **Instrument.** Q Exactive Plus, from the raw headers of 4 files. PRIDE gives the generic "Q Exactive".
- **Reagents.** TCEP and iodoacetamide, from the paper's macro-CSC digestion buffer.
- **Cleavage agent.** Trypsin.
