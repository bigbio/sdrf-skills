# PXD008100: SDRF annotation report

**Status: BLOCKED. No SDRF was built.** The dataset is genuine triplex SILAC (MaxQuant 1.2.0.18, Multiplicity 3, Labels1 `Arg6;Lys6`, Labels2 `Arg10;Lys8`). It is a phosphoproteome time course (5, 10, 20, 40, 60 and 120 min) of MCF10A cells that were left untreated, stimulated with serum, or stimulated with EGF. No accessible source says which SILAC channel (light, medium or heavy) holds control, serum or EGF. Campaign rules 6–7 therefore block all 102 searched runs. The details are in `annotations/BLOCKED.md`, section "PXD008100".

## Study
Roth L, Srivastava S, Lindzen M, ... Ziv T, ... Admon A, Caldas C, Yarden Y. "SILAC identifies LAD1 as a filamin-binding regulator of actin dynamics in response to EGF and a marker of aggressive breast tumors." *Sci Signal* 2018;11(515):eaan0949, doi:10.1126/scisignal.aan0949, PMID 29382783. Untransformed MCF10A mammary epithelial cells (abstract) were SILAC-labelled and treated. Control, serum and EGF cells from the same time point were mixed into one sample, digested, SCX-fractionated, TiO2-enriched for phosphopeptides and run by LC-MS/MS (PRIDE sample protocol).

## Evidence examined
- **PRIDE v3 project record** and the paginated file list (`files?pageSize=100&page=0..1`). There are 104 files: 103 `.RAW` and `Yarden_txt.zip` (MaxQuant txt). Every entry's `projectAccessions` contains PXD008100. The sample protocol names the three treatments but not their labels.
- **Paper: closed access.** Europe PMC has no PMCID (`inEPMC=N`). Unpaywall reports `closed`, and so does Semantic Scholar. The science.org article and supplement returned HTTP 403, which I did not bypass. I read only the abstract. Europe PMC searches for a preprint or a dataset-descriptor paper (accession, title keywords, authors) found nothing.
- **`Yarden_txt.zip`.** I read the central directory by HTTP range and inflated single members: `parameters.txt`, `summary.txt`, `evidence.txt` (Raw file → Experiment only, then deleted), `proteinGroups.txt` and `Phospho (STY)Sites.txt`. Their sha256 values are in `scratchpad/b11-PXD008100/mq.sha256`.
  - The search used MaxQuant 1.2.0.18 and the IPI human 3.73 database, with trypsin and at most 2 missed cleavages.
  - Fixed modification: Carbamidomethyl (C), from `parameters.txt`. The summary's fixed column is empty in this MaxQuant version.
  - Variable modifications: Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term) and Phospho (STY). This MaxQuant version has no "Multi modifications" column.
  - MS/MS tolerances: 0.5 Da (ITMS) and 20 ppm (FTMS).
  - Six MaxQuant experiments:

- **Raw headers** of 4 files (first 1.5 MB, read by HTTP range and then deleted). The instrument is an **LTQ Orbitrap XL** (PRIDE says "LTQ Orbitrap"), with an Eksigent nanoLC. The method names are `MSA_LM_243min_10msms...` and `SCX_MSA_LM_123min...`. There are no sample descriptions.
- **Data hints only** (`scratchpad/b11-PXD008100/orientation.txt`). Across the time points, EGF-pathway sites are higher in both M and H than in L, and highest in H: EGFR T693 has log2 H/L +2.5 to +5.8 and M/L +1.1 to +2.0, and SHC1 Y428 has H/L +5.3 to +5.9 and M/L +3.3 to +3.9. This suggests L = control and H = EGF, but it is not decisive. Serum also activates these pathways; MAPK1 Y187 at 5 min is M/L +2.4 and H/L +1.0. Rule 6 forbids a map built from data alone.
- **Duplicate and existing-annotation check.** PXD008100 is not in `annotations/` (including DUPLICATES.md and BLOCKED.md) or in `sdrf-annotated-datasets/datasets/`.

## Further open design point
The 60-min experiment pools two separately prepared mixtures in one MaxQuant experiment: 14 `Seq282xx` runs plus 10 `Seq28635` SCX salt steps. Without the Methods, I cannot tell whether these are biological replicates or a re-fractionation of the same lysate. That also blocks the biological-replicate numbering.

## What would unblock it
- The SILAC label of control, serum and EGF, from the paper's Methods or Fig. 1 or supplement (Science Signaling, subscription), or from the authors.
- The relationship between the two 60-min mixtures.

The map should then be checked against the EGFR/SHC1 ratios above. The planned SDRF would have 306 rows (102 searched runs × 3 channels) and use the human and cell-lines templates:
- cell line MCF 10A, CVCL_0598;
- factor values for treatment (control `untreated`, serum, EGF) and time;
- comment[fraction identifier] for the SCX fraction within each mixture;
- LTQ Orbitrap XL;
- Lys6/Arg6 (UNIMOD:188) and Lys8 (UNIMOD:259)/Arg10 (UNIMOD:267) label modifications;
- Carbamidomethyl fixed; Oxidation, Acetyl (Protein N-term) and Phospho variable;
- fragment tolerance 0.5 Da.

`Seq28635_SCX_load_E2O2.RAW` would be excluded because it was not searched.
