### The producer's judgement calls, tested
1. **Channel map (rule 6).** I re-fetched MMLenalidomideResistantfiledescription.csv from the PRIDE FTP; it is identical to the producer's copy. The sample names in the raw headers of all 16 files (read by HTTP range) agree with it. For example, Q20160815-01 is "H929 Heavy 1 P" and its local name is H929H_PNG1_1, with the resistant line light. I recomputed log2 LenR/WT from the four deposited Skyline exports under this map. CD33 is +1.1 to +4.5 and PTPRC is +0.7 to +3.2 in all 8 H929 files, in **both** label orientations. A wrong map would flip the sign in every file, and a label artefact would flip it between orientations, so the map holds. This also matches the paper's Fig. legend and Supplementary Data 6. The forward/reverse sign flips seen for SDC1 and CD38 are the label bias that the producer recorded.
2. **Tryptic and PNGase F raws as fractions 1 and 2 of one source (rule 16).** This is accepted. The two are sequential releases from the same NeutrAvidin capture of one 1:1 mixture. They are not separate samples compared against each other, and the factor of interest is LenR vs WT. The raw headers pair them directly ("<line> <Heavy|Light> 1 T" with "... 1 P"), and each pair shares its H/L mixing offset. The exception is Q20160815-17, whose header reads "OPM2 Light 2 P" (minor finding 1).
3. **LenR derivatives carry the parental Cellosaurus accession.** This is accepted. Cellosaurus has no entry for these derivatives. The phenotype column keeps them apart from the parental lines, the manifest discloses the choice, and curated precedent does the same (PXD018305, PXD010683, PXD047672).
4. **Constructed characteristics[individual].** This is accepted. The column is recommended for matched designs, its validator takes free identifiers (the template's own example is "donor-A1"), the IDs correctly tie WT and LenR to one donor, and the manifest says they are constructed. It is a minor note only.
5. **Four mixtures (2 experiments × forward/reverse) as four biological replicates.** This is accepted. The forward and reverse mixtures of one experiment necessarily use different cultures, because each was grown for 5 passages in a different SILAC medium. Experiments 1 (Aug 2016) and 3 (Feb 2017) are separate harvests. So each line × phenotype group has 4 distinct labelled populations, numbered 1–4 with no reuse (rule 3), and the paper itself states "n = 4 biological replicates".

7. **The assemble.py post-build step (rule 17).** This is accepted. The step is deterministic, includes assertions and edits no cell, and I reproduced its output exactly. It selects rows rather than concatenating subset builds (minor finding 3).
