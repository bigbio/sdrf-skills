## Focus points

### 1. Arm/day/fraction mapping: confirmed independently
- I located `txt/msmsScans.txt` (offset 253,808,934) and `txt/msms.txt` (offset 485,079,742) inside the truncated `txtfor-QAandQA.zip` by range scan, then inflated them myself.
- msmsScans gives 64 raw files. `Hutti_R<d>-<f>` maps to Experiment "RML day d", Fraction f, and `Hutti_Q<d>-<f>` maps to "RML+QA day d", Fraction f.
- All 108 SDRF rows agree on infection status, sampling time, fraction, assay name and the light/heavy source-name suffix (0 mismatches).
- Supp Table S2 against the MaxQuant proteinGroups I decompressed myself. Exact-ratio matches:

  | Supp S2 column | matches |
  |---|---|
  | −QA day 1 / 3 / 5 | RML day 1 / 3 / 5: 1993 / 2685 / 2596 |
  | +QA day 1 / 3 | RML+QA day 1 / 3: 2068 / 2456 |

  Cross-arm matches are 0–2. +QA day 5 matches *normalized* RML+QA day 5 (median log difference 0.02), which is a quirk of the supplement. R = −QA (infected) and Q = +QA (cleared) are therefore confirmed on several days, not only day 1.

### 2. The 10 blocked RML runs: blocking is the right call
I recomputed the heavy share from a different subsample of msms.txt: the first 125 MB of the compressed member, sequences A…D, 127k PSMs, reverse hits excluded. The result reproduces the producer's table:

| raw | recorded | reviewer heavy share | same-arm/day other fractions | matches |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1-7 | RML d1 | 0.805 | R1 0.525–0.553 | day-2 fraction-7 range (Q2-7 0.815) |
| R2-7 | RML d2 | 0.596 | R2 0.772–0.857 | day 1 |
| R3-5..8 | RML d3 | 0.961–0.971 | R3-1..4 0.874–0.887 | Q5-5..8 0.968–0.973 |
| R5-5..8 | RML d5 | 0.910–0.922 | R5-1..4 0.961–0.967 | Q3-5..8 0.903–0.914 |

The heavy share does rise slightly with fraction in the Q arm (Q2 0.77→0.83). That trend cannot explain R5-5..8 *dropping*, or R3-5..8 jumping to the day-5 level. The fraction-for-fraction agreement with the other day's runs looks very much like an R3↔R5 tube swap for fractions 5–8 plus an R1-7↔R2-7 swap. Consistent with this, the experiment-level median H/L is higher for RML day 3 (7.41) than for RML day 5 (7.28), while +QA rises (7.09 → 7.50).

Both alternatives are worse than blocking:
- **Annotating as named** would put rows into the day-3/day-5 groups that the deposited data contradict.
- **Re-labelling to the inferred day** is unsourced inference (rules 6/7).

Leaving the runs out and listing them in BLOCKED.md, with author confirmation as the unblock condition, is correct. Dropping fractions 5–8 leaves fractions 1–4 for RML d3/d5. That is a valid, honest partial fraction set.

### 3. The 12 blocked sodium-butyrate runs: blocking is right
- I confirmed that Supp S2 "−QA + NaBu + PK digest" Day 1–4 equals MaxQuant experiments 3/6/9/12 (the −S runs) exactly: 1914/1895/1853/1932 matches, ≤5 elsewhere.
- The data argue against the published label. The +P runs have ~650–790 quantified proteins, the same scale as the genuine PK-PTA isolates (572–653). The −S runs have ~1,900.
- The published "PK digest + PTA precipitation" label (the explicit statement) and the deposited data therefore conflict. Nothing defines +P/−P/−S.
- Rule 6 only lets the explicit statement win when the data support it. Here they do not, so blocking all 12 runs is correct.
- BLOCKED.md names the files with their PRIDE spellings (`NB1P`, not MaxQuant's `NB1+P`) and gives a sensible unblock criterion.

### 4. Modifications without the whole-proteome parameters.txt: verified from masses
On 60,001 PSMs from msms.txt, I recomputed each peptide mass from its sequence:
- Every Cys-containing PSM carries exactly +57.021 Da per Cys, so Carbamidomethyl C is a fixed modification.
- Labeling State 1 adds +6.0201 Da per K *and* per R, i.e. 13C6 on both. Arg6/Lys6 = UNIMOD:188 is correct (Arg10/Lys8 are excluded).
- The variable modifications are only Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (Protein N-term).
- Fragmentation is HCD/FTMS throughout.

For the PK search, parameters.txt and summary.txt agree: Carbamidomethyl fixed, Labels1 Arg6;Lys6, Ox/Acetyl variable, MaxQuant 1.6.0.1. The modification columns in both files are therefore correct.

The proteome's 20 ppm fragment tolerance is defensible, because PRIDE says MaxQuant "default parameter settings" and 20 ppm is the FTMS default. However, the manifest's source for it, Table S6, is the 1.6.5.0 export. That is the NaBu search's version, not demonstrably the whole-proteome search (m2).

### 5. Cell line: one important omission (I1)
The Neuro-2a parent attributes are correct for Neuro-2a: CVCL_0470, A/J, male, PNS, neural-crest-derived neuroblast, neuroblastoma. Cellosaurus has no N2a-Cl3 entry; I searched id/sy N2a-Cl3, N2a cl3, ScN2a-cl3 and N2a.cl3, and looked through the 57 children of CVCL_0470. Citing the parent is acceptable.

**But** the paper says: *"we utilized a transgenic clone of N2a neuroblastoma cells that highly overexpress the PrP gene (N2a-Cl3)"* (Results, paper.txt line 67, refs 37/38). The SDRF never says this, and the manifest calls the line only a "Prusiner-lab subclone". In a study whose central analyte is PrP, a PrP-overexpressing transgenic line presented as plain Neuro-2a-derived cells misleads reuse.
