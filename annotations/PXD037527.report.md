### Getting the Methods at all

PRIDE reports `is_open_access: false`, and it is right for once: PMC10529037 is **not** in the OA
subset. `get_full_text_article` → HTTP 404; `get_pdf_by_unpaywall` → Unpaywall reports
`oa_status: green` but returns anti-bot HTML; the EuropePMC `supplementaryFiles` endpoint and the
NCBI OA service both refuse. Two paths worked:

1. **`efetch db=pmc&id=10529037`** returned 81 KB of JATS (Results/Discussion/Conclusion). Verified
   by PMID before use, per the brief's substitution warning.
2. The published paper is Angew Chem style — **Methods live only in the SI PDF**, which is
   unreachable. The **bioRxiv preprint** carries the full Methods inline. Fetched its JATS source
   XML directly (`.source.xml`); title and DOI verified.

**The preprint is not a perfect substitute** and this matters: its author list lacks Andersen,
Tsang and Jayatunge, and its abstract has no ATG9A content. The **ATG9A knockout arm was added at
revision**, so *no accessible source describes how those cells were made* beyond the published
Results sentence: CRISPR/Cas9 knockout of *atg9a* in HeLa, compared to "their isogenic WT parental
line".

### `dia-acquisition` is deliberately NOT declared

The deposition is genuinely mixed: **255 DDA/WWA runs and 70 DIA runs in one file**.
`dia-acquisition/1.1.0` puts a `values` validator with `error_level: error` on
`comment[proteomics data acquisition method]` restricting it to the single literal
`Data-independent acquisition`. That template therefore **cannot express this deposition** — see
issue #35 B2. The options were:

- declare `dia-acquisition` and drop 255 rows → loses the study's primary arm;
- split into two SDRFs → the ATG9A comparison spans both acquisition modes;
- **declare only `ms-proteomics`** (chosen), whose own validator is an *ontology* check against
  PRIDE, so both `PRIDE:0000627` and `PRIDE:0000450` are legal on a per-row basis.

**Cost, stated plainly:** the four DIA-only columns (`comment[dia method]`,
`comment[scan window lower/upper limit]`, `comment[isolation window width]`) are licensed only by
`dia-acquisition` and are therefore **absent**, even though the values are known from the paper
(50 Th fixed windows, m/z 400–800, plus SWATH variants in Table S1). This is a real information
loss forced by the template model, not an oversight. **Primary reviewer target.**

## Key decisions and evidence

### Three cell lines, not two — the standards are not the cells

The preprint Methods are explicit where PRIDE ("Hela and K562 digest standard") is not:

> "**Pierce™ HeLa protein digest standard** and formic acid were purchased from Thermo Fisher
> Scientific… **K562 and yeast digest standards were from Promega**… **HeLa and K562 cells (ATCC**,
> Manassas, VA) were cultured and harvested as described previously [22]."

- **216 standard runs → HeLa S3 (`EFO:0002791` / `CVCL_0058`) and K-562 (`CVCL_0004`).** Thermo's
  page for the Pierce standard states it originates from "a well-established **HeLa S3** reference
  cell line" — a distinct clonal derivative with its own Cellosaurus entry. The parental line is
  the plausible, validator-clean, wrong answer.
- **104 single cells → parental HeLa (`EFO:0001185` / `CVCL_0030`) and K-562 (`CVCL_0004`)**, both
  ATCC.

So `HeLa` and `HeLa S3` coexist in this file and are **not** interchangeable. K562 has no such
split: Promega's product page names only "K562" with no subclone, and ATCC's K-562 is CVCL_0004, so
both K562 groups carry CVCL_0004 — but note the asymmetry is an *absence of evidence* about
Promega's lineage, not evidence of identity.

### Carbamidomethyl — asserted, with an actual alkylation step behind it

The brief warns never to assert Carbamidomethyl without an alkylation step. Here four independent
sources converge, so it is asserted on all 325 rows as `NT=Carbamidomethyl;AC=UNIMOD:4;TA=C;MT=Fixed`:

1. Methods, verbatim: "**Carbamidomethyl (C) was fixed as a static modification** by the CHIMERYS
   software." — this is the deposited search space, which is what the column documents.
2. Promega's protocol for the K562 extract: "reduced with DTT and **alkylated with iodoacetamide**".
3. The nanoPOTS workflow cited for the single cells (ref 10 = Zhu et al. 2018) is the standard
   DDM/DTT → **IAA** → Lys-C → trypsin one-pot protocol.
4. PRIDE `modifications` = `iodoacetamide derivatized residue` — agrees for once.

No other modification is declared: the search specified none, and Oxidation (M) is **not** in the
stated search space, so it is correctly absent.

### Cleavage agents: Trypsin + Lys-C on every row, for three different reasons

The paper's own text says only "Enzyme was set as **Trypsin**" — but that is a *search* setting,
whereas `comment[cleavage agent details]` documents the digestion. The digestions are:

- **HeLa S3 standard**: Thermo states the standard is manufactured by "digestion of the cell lysate
  with **LysC and Trypsin**".
- **K562 standard**: Promega states "digested with **Trypsin/Lys-C Mix**".
- **Single cells**: the cited nanoPOTS workflow uses Lys-C followed by trypsin.

The single-cell case is **inferred through two reference hops** (this paper → Webber 2022 → Zhu
2018) and is the weakest of the three. **Third reviewer target:** trypsin-only is defensible for
those 109 rows. The column's `allow_not_available: false` means "not stated" cannot be expressed,
which is why a choice was forced at all.

### The mixed-species runs

The 2 `HeYe` runs are 8:2 HeLa:yeast digest at 10 ng/µL, used to estimate the false-matching rate.
`characteristics[organism]` is single-cardinality, so the majority component (HeLa S3) carries
organism and cell line, and the yeast is expressed where it belongs — as
`characteristics[spiked compound]` =
`CT=mixture;CN=yeast protein extract digest standard;SP=Saccharomyces cerevisiae;CV=Promega;QY=2 ng`.
The deposited FASTAs corroborate: `UP000005640` (human) **and** `UP000002311` (*S. cerevisiae*).
The raw header confirms the run used the 10 ng method (`…_2215_1x10nguL_DWP_100QC.meth`).

### Replicate structure and source names

- **Standards are grouped by amount only** (200 pg vs 10 ng) — a dilution is a sample property.
  Gradient length, isolation window and MS2 resolution are *acquisition* properties and do **not**
  split the sample: one commercial vial → one `biological replicate = 1`, technical replicates
  numbered within the group. This deliberately corrects the finding raised against PXD052416, where
  QC source names were split by gradient.
  → 5 standard source names; the 200 pg HeLa S3 group carries 172 technical replicates (the
  7 windows × 4 resolution/IT settings × 2 gradients screen).
- **Each single cell is its own source name and its own biological replicate**, numbered within its
  group, `technical replicate = 1`. Well IDs repeat across groups (`r1c1` exists in `Ctr`, `KO` and
  the plain HeLa set), so the group name is part of both `source name` and
  `characteristics[cell identifier]`.
