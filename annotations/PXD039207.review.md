## Independent evidence checks

- **Template set.** I derived it from templates.yaml myself: human cell lines with no patient or clinical data, so ms-proteomics + human + cell-lines. Every required column is present. Only two recommended columns are missing: `characteristics[sample type]` and `characteristics[individual]`.
- **Ontology.** All 19 non-UNIMOD accessions resolve in OLS4 with exact labels and none is obsolete. The UNIMOD labels for 1, 4, 35, 188, 259, 267 and 481 match NT=. The Cellosaurus IDs match the names in the file.
- **SILAC channels.** I viewed Fig. 1A of PMC11534031 myself: HEK-293 Heavy, 786-O Medium, Caki-1 Light, n=3 each, "High-pH fractionation". The Methods agree: only HEK-293 and 786-O were grown in SILAC medium (heavy K8R10, medium K4R6), and light was normal DMEM. In mqpar.xml (fetched from PXD039207 FTP), multiplicity is 3 and labelMods are ['', Arg6;Lys4, Arg10;Lys8]. The label-mod columns match: 188/481 for medium, 267/259 for heavy.
- **Files and design.** PRIDE files/all lists 27 RAW files plus mqpar.xml (projectAccessions = PXD039207). I checked all 81 rows by script. Each RAW appears 3 times, once per label. In every row:
  - the fraction number is the F-index + 1;
  - the biological replicate is the file suffix, which is also the mqpar experiment (1st/2nd/3rd);
  - the source name is `<line>_SILAC_rep<i>`;
  - the factor value is the cell line.

  There are 0 mismatches and no duplicate (source, assay, label) keys.
- **Technical values.** I range-read 1 MiB of the F4-2 RAW myself (the producer read F0 and F4-1). Its header shows Orbitrap Velos, CID, NCE 28.0, FTMS 300.0–2000.0, ITMS MS/MS and top 10. The paper agrees. In mqpar, mainSearchTol is 4.5 ppm, ITMS tolerance is 0.5 Da, Trypsin/P is the enzyme, Carbamidomethyl is fixed, and Oxidation and Acetyl (Protein N-term) are variable.
- **Paper attribution.** PRIDE links PMID 37889066, but that paper is a TMT tissue study. The 27 RAW files are size-identical to those of PXD045898 (27/27 matched), and PXD045898's paper is PMID 39467626 (PMC11534031). The design was taken from that paper, which is correct, and the manifest discloses the substitution.
- **Cellosaurus.** Sex, age, disease, derived-from site and "Population: Caucasian" match the file for each cell line. The file also leaves out unsupported values: no Kac (acetyl-K) modification, because mqpar is a global search; and PRIDE's "malignant neoplasm of ovary" and generic "LTQ Orbitrap" are not used.

## Findings

All three are minor; none blocks approval.

1. **minor: `characteristics[sample type]` is missing.** It is recommended in base 1.1.0, with default `study sample`.
   - Fix: add the column with `study sample` on all 81 rows.
2. **minor: `characteristics[individual]` is missing.** It is recommended in human 1.1.0.
   - Fix (optional): add a donor identifier per cell line, or `not available`.
3. **minor: the manifest misstates the cell-type evidence.** limitations[5] says cell type is "not stated in any source". The PRIDE record does give organismParts / sampleAttributes `CL:0002518 kidney epithelial cell`.
   - The SDRF value `not available` is the safer choice and should stay. HEK293's cell of origin is uncertain, and curated precedents PXD003539, PXD013127 and PXD005942 use `not available` for 786-O and Caki-1.
   - Fix: reword the limitation to mention the PRIDE value and say why it was not used.

Informational, not a finding: PXD039207 and PXD045898 are duplicate deposits of the same runs. The curated `sdrf-annotated-datasets/datasets/PXD045898` SDRF annotates them as label free, which mqpar's multiplicity of 3 contradicts.
