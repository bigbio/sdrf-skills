## Study

platform for label-free single-cell proteomics: single HeLa and Jurkat cells dispensed into 384-well
PCR plates, one-step lysis/digestion, nanoLC–MS on an Orbitrap Exploris 480.

## Key decisions and evidence

**Jurkat is the E6-1 clone, not the parental line — `CVCL_0367`, not `CVCL_0065`.** This is the
inverse of the HeLa-S3 trap. The paper says only "HeLa and Jurkat cells (ATCC, Manassas, VA)". The
naive mapping `Jurkat` → `EFO:0002796` → `CVCL_0065` is validator-clean and wrong for an ATCC
purchase: **`CVCL_0065` carries no ATCC cross-reference at all** (it is the FHCRC/JM parental
isolate), whereas `CVCL_0367` *is* ATCC TIB-152, the only Jurkat ATCC distributes as "Jurkat".
Corroborated end-to-end: Cellosaurus `CVCL_0367` gives `SX Male`, `AG 14Y`, and `DI NCIt C7953
Childhood T acute lymphoblastic leukemia`, and `MONDO:0000871` xrefs `NCIT:C7953` exactly.
`CVCL_0367 HI CVCL_0065` confirms they are distinct entries, parent and clone.
`characteristics[cell line]` keeps the name the paper uses (`NT=Jurkat;AC=EFO:0002796`, the
ontology-resolvable name); `cellosaurus accession`/`cellosaurus name` carry the clone precision.
No ontology has a clean `Jurkat E6.1` label (BTO:0001948 is `JURKAT E-6.1 cell`; CLO:0007044 is
`Jurkat J6 cell`), which is why the name and the accession are carried in separate columns.
**Flagged for review** — see Uncertainty 1.

**HeLa is the parental line — `CVCL_0030` (ATCC CCL-2) — including the QC digest.** The brief's
warning that "any commercial HeLa digest standard is HeLa S3 (`CVCL_0058`)" was considered and
**rejected for lack of evidence that this digest is commercial**:

- The paper never names a vendor, catalogue number, or the string "S3" for the "10 ng of HeLa digest"
  / "50 ng of HeLa digest" used as the MBR library. It says only "HeLa digest".
- This group cites a vendor for *everything* else — Promega VA1061, Sigma DDM, Eppendorf 0030129547,
  Corning 6570, ArcticWhite, Polymicro, Dr. Maisch, Next Advance, MicrOmics, New Objective, CoAnn.
  A purchased standard would almost certainly have been cited the same way.
- The two companion papers from the same lab and year — Truong et al. (WWA, PMC10529037, the source
  of both the method and the 10 ng library) and Johnston et al. (one-step, PMC11017373) — were
  searched for `Pierce`, `88328`, `digest standard`, `commercial`: **no hit**.
- The only HeLa whose provenance the paper does establish is ATCC-cultured parental HeLa.

Unlike PXD052416 — where PRIDE said "Pierce HeLa protein Digest" and Thermo's page for cat. 88328
states the standard originates from HeLa S3 — there is no such anchor here. Annotating S3 would
assert a commercial origin the record does not support. **This is the largest residual uncertainty
in the file** — see Uncertainty 2.

**`characteristics[single cell isolation protocol] = microfluidics` (a `fixed` enum with no member
for this instrument).** The HP D100 is none of the eight allowed values verbatim. `not available` is
**not** a legal escape: in the resolved `single-cell/1.0.0` this column has `allow_not_available:
false` (only `allow_not_applicable: true`) — TERMS.tsv and the resolved template disagree, and the
resolved template governs. Each member was tested against the instrument's actual mechanism:

| member | verdict |
|---|---|
| `acoustic droplet ejection` | **false** — the D100 is *thermal inkjet* (TIJ), not acoustic. Acoustic ejection is nozzle-free (Labcyte Echo); the D100 fires from a TIJ integrated-circuit pump. This is the tempting, validator-clean wrong answer. |
| `FACS` | **false** — no fluorescence, and the paper is explicit: "the current HP D100 system is a single-cell dispenser and **not a sorter**". |
| `cellenONE` | **false** — a different vendor's instrument, explicitly contrasted as the costly alternative. |
| `nanoPOTS` | **false** — explicitly contrasted; 384-well PCR plates were used *instead of* nanowells. |
| `laser capture microdissection`, `manual picking` | **false** |
| `droplet microfluidics` | connotes water-in-oil emulsion encapsulation; not what happens |
| `microfluidics` | **the only member not affirmatively false** — chosen |

Justification for `microfluidics`: the D100 co-locates a TIJ pump with **package-level microfluidics
and gold electrodes for impedance-based single-cell detection** in one planar cassette; cells are
detected in a microfluidic channel and ejected as droplets (Frontiers in Chemistry 2024,
"Thermal inkjet makes label-free single-cell proteomics accessible and easy", PMC11371764 —
a paper about this exact instrument). The enum's `values` validator is warning-level, so the true
free-text string would have been *permitted*; the brief's instruction to "pick the closest defensible
member" was followed instead, at the cost of precision. **Flagged** — see Uncertainty 3.

**QC rows are not single cells and say so.** The 12 `QC_*_WWA_*` runs are bulk HeLa digest aliquots
serving as the match-between-runs library (paper: "10 ng of HeLa digest sample served as the library
for all of the figures except Figure 4, which used 50 ng"). They carry `not applicable` — legal on
all three columns — for `single cell isolation protocol`, `cell identifier`, and `cells per well`.
Confirmed against the raw headers: QC runs come from autosampler vials `BD20/BD21/BA22/BA23` under a
`22_wellplate_WWA_100uL` method, distinct from the cell plates.

**Replicate structure.** Each dispensed cell is a distinct biological sample → `biological replicate`
numbered sequentially within each group, `technical replicate = 1` (one injection per cell). The 12
QC runs are injections of the same digest stock → `biological replicate = 1`, with `technical
replicate` 1/2/3 recovered from the `_first`/`_second`/`_third` file tokens within each well. The
deposit confirms this structure exactly: each of the 4 QC wells has precisely 3 injections.

**Modifications: `Carbamidomethyl` (UNIMOD:4, TA=C, Fixed) — chemically implausible, correctly
recorded.** The one-step reagent is Trypsin/Lys-C + 0.05% DDM: there is **no reduction or alkylation
step anywhere in the protocol**, so no cysteine should be carbamidomethylated. The paper nevertheless
states "Carbamidomethyl (C) was fixed as a static modification **by the CHIMERYS software**" — the
search engine applies it by default. This column documents the search, so it is recorded as searched,
with the discrepancy noted here rather than silently dropped. PRIDE's `modifications` field says "No
PTMs are included in the dataset", contradicting the paper; the peer-reviewed Methods were followed.

**Instrument verified from the data, not the metadata.** PRIDE says `Orbitrap Exploris 480`; the
paper says the same; HTTP-range reads of the first 256 KB of three raw files (utf-16-le decoded)
independently confirm `Orbitrap Exploris 480` in the header. All three agree — recorded because the
brief warns PRIDE's field is unreliable in this corpus.

**Cleavage agents.** Trypsin (MS:1001251) + Lys-C (MS:1001309) on all 143 rows — the Promega
Rapid-Digestion Trypsin/Lys-C kit (VA1061) for the cells, and the same for the QC digest to the
extent the paper describes it. The database search specified "Enzyme was set as Trypsin"; this column
describes the digestion, not the search.

## Conflicts found (PRIDE submission vs published paper)

The PRIDE `sample_processing_protocol` is an **earlier draft** of the paper's Methods and differs in
three places. The peer-reviewed text was followed in all three; all are recorded because two are
invisible in the final SDRF.

| # | PRIDE says | Paper says | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "In the experiments using **FACS presorting**, 200 nL of the reagent mixture…" | "In the experiments for **Trypsin/Lys-C optimization**, 200 nL…" | See below — the most interesting conflict in the dataset |
| 2 | small cells **10–15 µm**, medium 15–17 µm | small cells **12–14 µm**, medium 15–17 µm | `characteristics[cell diameter]` omitted (optional): these are D100 *gate settings*, not per-cell measurements, and the two sources disagree |
| 3 | modifications: "No PTMs are included in the dataset" | Carbamidomethyl (C) fixed via CHIMERYS | Paper followed |

**On conflict 1 — the `Vol_con_FACS` directory.** The PRIDE draft's "FACS presorting" is not an
isolated slip: the acquisition path embedded in the deposited PD table for every Fig-3 run is
`Y:\2023\6\Vol_con_FACS\`. So there is real evidence that the 52 volume-comparison cells were
FACS-**presorted** before D100 dispensing, even though the published sentence dropped the phrase and
the paper's Discussion presents FACS→D100 only as a *prospective* hybrid ("It should be feasible to
use such a FACS system to isolate a specific subpopulation into a vial…"). This does **not** change
the annotation: even if a FACS step enriched the *population* into a vial, the D100 still performed
the single-cell isolation into wells in every experiment, and that is what the column describes.
Flagged for the reviewer as Uncertainty 4 rather than acted on.

→ "Most seems to be fine. There were only warnings."

One warning, reproducible, and **a defect in `sdrf-pipelines` 0.1.5 — not in this file**:

```
WARNING: Term: NT=T cell;AC=CL:0000084 in column 'characteristics[cell type]',
         is not found in the given ontology list cl;bto;clo
```

`CL:0000084` **is** `T cell` — verified live via OLS and via sdrf-pipelines' *own* `OlsClient`. Root
cause, isolated in `sdrf/validators.py`: the ontology validator lowercases the `NT=` value and then
issues an `exact=True` OLS query, which is case-sensitive for this term:

```
OlsClient().search('T cell', ontology='cl', exact=True) → CL:0000084 present: True
OlsClient().search('t cell', ontology='cl', exact=True) → CL:0000084 present: False
```

`epithelial cell` passes only because CL's label happens to be all-lowercase. Any CL term with a
capitalised label is unverifiable through this validator. The correct term was kept and the warning
accepted. Worth filing upstream.

**`parse_sdrf` exiting cleanly proves well-formedness, never truth.** Every judgement call in this
file — the Jurkat clone, the QC digest lineage, `microfluidics`, the omitted precursor tolerance —
passes the validator in both its right and its wrong form.

## Uncertainties for the reviewer (ranked)

1. **Jurkat `CVCL_0367` vs `CVCL_0065`.** I annotate the E6-1 clone from ATCC provenance; the paper
   names no clone. If the reviewer holds that only stated facts may be asserted, this becomes
   `CVCL_0065` + `cellosaurus name = Jurkat` on 30 rows. I judge E6-1 better supported: `CVCL_0065`
   has no ATCC cross-reference, so it is affirmatively wrong for an ATCC purchase.
2. **QC digest lineage (12 rows).** Annotated parental `HeLa`/`CVCL_0030`. If evidence emerges that
   the digest is a commercial standard (e.g. Pierce 88328), these 12 rows must become
   `HeLa S3` / `EFO:0002791` / `CVCL_0058`, and `culture medium`, `age`, `sex`, `ancestry` should
   then be reconsidered for them. Resolvable only by the authors or a vendor mention I could not find.
3. **`microfluidics` for the HP D100.** The enum has no honest member. A reviewer could reasonably
   prefer the warning-level free-text truth (`HP D100 Single Cell Dispenser`) over a controlled term
   that is merely not-false. The enum needs a `thermal inkjet dispensing` member.
4. **`Vol_con_FACS` (52 Fig-3 rows).** Directory name + PRIDE draft say FACS presorting; published
   Methods say otherwise. If FACS presorting did occur *and* the column is read as "how were the
   cells obtained" rather than "how were single cells isolated into wells", those rows could argue
   for `FACS`. I read the column as the latter and annotate the D100 uniformly.
5. **`comment[sample preparation batch]` as a condition label.** Defensible (the conditions *are*
   separate plates/preps) but it overloads "batch" with "condition".
