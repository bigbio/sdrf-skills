## Study
"Identification of a USP9X Substrate NFX1-123 by SILAC-Based Quantitative Proteomics". Chen X, Lu D, Gao J, Zhu H, Zhou Y, Gao D, Zhou H. *J Proteome Res* 2019, doi:10.1021/acs.jproteome.9b00139, PMID 31059266. The paper is closed access, so only the abstract was read. The study compares stable shUSP9X and shGFP HeLa cells by SILAC: FASP digestion, high-pH RP fractions, forward and reverse labelling. A Flag-NFX1-123 IP-MS in HEK293T follows (the blocked part).

## Channel map (rule 6)
- **Explicit source.** The PRIDE sample protocol says: "For the forward assays, the stable shUSP9X cell lines were cultured in light media (R0K0) and the stable shGFP cell lines were cultured in heavy media (R10K8). For the reverse assays, the label strategy was reciprocal."
- **Which experiment is forward.** No text says this, so the knockdown target settles it. Deposited normalized USP9X (Q93008) H/L:

  | | U1 | U2 | U3 | U4 | U5 | U6 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | USP9X H/L | 2.34 | 4.86 | 1.27 | 0.42 | 0.17 | 0.75 |
  | NFX1 H/L | 1.68 | 2.73 | 1.08 | 0.60 | 0.27 | 0.86 |

  NFX1 moves with USP9X, as the abstract reports (knockdown lowers NFX1-123).
- **Global check.** Pearson correlation of log2 H/L over 5,026 proteins:
  - within the forward group (U1/U2/U3): +0.43 to +0.56;
  - within the reverse group (U4/U5/U6): +0.35 to +0.54;
  - between paired experiments: U1–U4 −0.80, U2–U5 −0.89, U3–U6 −0.85.
- **Result:** U1–U3 are forward and U4–U6 are reverse. No source conflicts with this.

## Technical values
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive (MS:1001911), from all 84 raw headers. PRIDE's "LTQ Orbitrap" is wrong.
- **Acquisition and fragmentation:** HCD at 28 NCE (the method file is named NCE30), DDA Top15. MS1 300–1500 m/z; MS2 in the Orbitrap at 17,500 resolution.
- **Search (MaxQuant 1.6.0.1 per `parameters.txt`; PRIDE says 1.6.1.0, which is the IP search's version):**
  - Fixed: Carbamidomethyl (C).
  - Variable: Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term), Arg10 (UNIMOD:267) and Lys8 (UNIMOD:259); the labels are set to MT=Variable (rule 11).
  - Fragment tolerance 20 ppm (FTMS).
  - Precursor tolerance `not available`: it is not recorded, and no mqpar.xml was deposited.
- **Cleavage agent:** Trypsin (MS:1001251), from MaxQuant's specific Trypsin/P search; the protocol says only "FASP".
- **Fractionation:** high-pH RP (PRIDE:0000564).
- **Fraction identifier:** the file-name suffix 1–14. In U1 MaxQuant assigned U1_2/U1_3/U1_1 to fractions 1/2/3. The file names were kept because U1_2 has the fraction-2 MS/MS-count signature that every other experiment shows.

## Validation
- **validate-sdrf:** "Everything seems to be fine" (exit 0).
- **reconcile:** exit 1 with `cleavage_agent_unsupported`. This is a false positive: the record never names an enzyme. The specific tryptic search identified 1.60 M of 4.35 M MS/MS (30.35%) and 121,111 peptides, which only a tryptic digest gives. The refutation is in `validation.txt`.
- **convert-openms:** exit 1 because of the precursor tolerance, as in precedents PXD014368, PXD006676 and PXD024103.
