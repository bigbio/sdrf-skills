## Study
"Chemical Probe To Monitor the Parkinsonism-Associated Protein DJ-1 in Live Cells". Drechsel J, Mandl FA, Sieber SA. *ACS Chem Biol* 2018, doi:10.1021/acschembio.8b00633, PMID 30011180.

The paper is **closed access**: there is no PMC copy, Unpaywall reports it closed, and the ACS SI page returns 403 (not bypassed). The design was therefore taken from:
- the PRIDE protocols;
- the first author's open-access TUM dissertation (2020, mediaTUM 1551821), whose chapter 1 covers these experiments;
- the raw headers;
- the four deposited MaxQuant txt zips.

The probe is FM247, an alkyne amino-epoxycyclohexenone. Each sample is click-labelled with biotin-azide, enriched on avidin, and digested with Lys-C followed by trypsin.

## Design

### SILAC (`-silac`): chemoproteomic target ID, FM247 10 µM vs DMSO, label-swap
Multiplicity is 2 in all 30 runs. The two channels are:
- **SILAC medium**: Lys4 + Arg6;
- **SILAC heavy**: Lys8 + Arg10.

No light channel is used.

| cell line | instrument | runs | rows | sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A549 | LTQ Orbitrap XL (CID, ion-trap MS2) | 18 (6 mixtures × 3 rounds) | 1-36 | 12 |
| HeLa | Orbitrap Fusion (HCD 30%, ion-trap MS2) | 6 | 37-48 | 12 |
| SH-SY5Y | Orbitrap Fusion (HCD 30%, ion-trap MS2) | 6 | 49-60 | 12 |

**Channel map** (rule 6), for every cell line:

| file group | SILAC medium | SILAC heavy |
|---|---|---|
| `*_D4S8_k` | DMSO | FM247 |
| `*_D8S4_k` | FM247 | DMSO |

The map rests on three sources:
1. **Explicit statements.** The PRIDE protocol and the thesis say "medium ≙ Lys-4, Arg-6; heavy ≙ Lys-8, Arg-10", and that compound or DMSO was added "to a pair of heavy and medium cells and vice versa for label-switch".
2. **MaxQuant labels.** summary.txt defines the labels per file: D4S8 uses Labels0 = Arg6;Lys4 and Labels1 = Arg10;Lys8, and D8S4 reverses them. The protocol says this ordering yields "ratios of probe vs. DMSO". The file names read **D**(MSO)/**S**(ample) followed by the Lys label.
3. **Data check.** The normalized log2 H/L for DJ-1/PARK7 is strongly positive in every quantified file of both orientations:

   | cell line | D4S8 | D8S4 |
   |---|---|---|
   | HeLa | 5.0–5.7 | 5.6–6.0 |
   | SH-SY5Y | 4.9–5.3 | 4.2–4.8 |
   | A549 | 2.0–5.4 | 2.8–5.0 |

   The off-targets HMOX2, P4HB and VDAC3 are also positive, and GAPDH is about 0. A wrong orientation would flip the sign in one orientation. The sources do not conflict.

**Biological replicates.** These are numbered 1–6 within each (cell line, compound) group: D4S8_1..3 → 1..3 and D8S4_1..3 → 4..6. Each mixture combines one DMSO dish and one FM247 dish. The factor values are `cell line` and `compound`. FM247 rows have `characteristics[dose]` = 10 uM; DMSO rows have `not applicable`.

### Label-free (`-lfq`): HeLa, H2O2 0 / 1 / 2 mM for 1 h, then FM247 10 µM
- 9 runs on a Q Exactive Plus (HCD NCE 27, orbitrap MS2), 3 per concentration, biological replicates 1–3.
- The factor is `factor value[hydrogen peroxide concentration]`.
- `characteristics[treatment]` is `untreated` for 0 mM and `hydrogen peroxide` otherwise.
- Data check: PARK7 LFQ intensity falls in the order 0 mM > 1 mM > 2 mM. The thesis reports that DJ-1 labelling decreased under oxidative stress.

## Conflicts resolved
- **HeLa SILAC instrument.** The thesis heading says "Q Exactive Plus (HeLa)", but the HeLa SILAC raw files are Orbitrap Fusion. The raw headers were followed; the QE Plus is the instrument of the HeLa LFQ runs.
- **Cell types in thesis Table 4.** The table swaps the HeLa and A549 entries (HeLa "lung adenocarcinoma", A549 "cervix carcinoma"). Cellosaurus was followed.

## Open items / limitations
- **A549 measurement rounds.** The A549 rounds I, II and III are encoded as technical replicates 1–3 of the same mixture, because the authors' replicate index (_1.._3) is shared across rounds. The deposit does not say whether rounds II and III are re-injections or re-preparations:
  - vials: rounds I and II used RA1–6, round III used RB1–6, and two round-III files are suffixed "neu";
  - ratio correlations do not settle it.

  If the rounds are independent cultures, the biological replicate numbers would need to become 1–18 per group.
- **LFQ replicates.** Treating the LFQ replicates as separate dishes is an inference.
- **SH-SY5Y values.** The medium DMEM/F12 is free text, and the developmental stage is `not available`.
- **Publication.** The paper and its SI were not read (closed access).
- **Review.** Review is pending externally.
