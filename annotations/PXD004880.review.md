## Independent verification
- **Run identities:** the run sheet `SampleInformation_201608upload.txt` was re-downloaded. All 36 `SWATH-MS-ProteinTurnover: <twin>_rep<n>_<t>hr` lines were compared by script against individual, biological replicate, sampling time, disease (characteristics and factor), Cellosaurus accession, data file and source name: 0 mismatches across 72 rows.
- **Pulsed-SILAC semantics:** the paper Methods confirm light-grown cells were switched to heavy medium (Lys 13C6 15N2 = UNIMOD:259, Arg 13C6 15N4 = UNIMOD:267) at t0. So light = pre-existing and heavy = newly synthesized protein, both from the same dish.
- **Heavy share by time:** 40 range chunks of 2.5 MB each were read from the 1.7 GB OpenSWATH/TRIC output, at offsets different from the producer's. Paired H/(L+H) per run:

  | Time | H/(L+H) range |
  |---|---|
  | 0 h | 0.098–0.138 |
  | 1 h | 0.135–0.158 |
  | 4 h | 0.205–0.231 |
  | 8 h | 0.280–0.312 |
  | 14 h | 0.377–0.416 |
  | 24 h | 0.495–0.517 |

  The share is monotone in run-sheet time and matches the paper's "about 50% at 24 h". It confirms the time labels and the pulse direction. It cannot separate the twins, so twin identity rests on the explicit run sheet.
- **DIA and instrument:** the full `yanliu_Y151023_037_SW.wiff` shows TripleTOF 5600, method `YL_SWATH64w_2ul_300nlmin_105min.dam` and sample `TM_36`. The paper lists 64 variable windows from 399.5 to 1200.5. Scan window, `isolation window width = not applicable` and `SWATH MS` (PRIDE:0000447) all conform to dia-acquisition 1.1.0.
- **Cellosaurus:** CVCL_B4L1 Twin-DS (SY T1DS) and CVCL_B4L2 Twin-N (SY T2N) were re-fetched. Both are Female, AG 16FW, Population Caucasian, forearm skin, CL_0002620, and both cross-reference PRIDE PXD004880. Downstream values: `16W`, `fetal stage`, `European ancestry`. The check tool's "hallucinated" flag on the cell names is a false positive.
- **Replicate numbering:** biological replicate is 1–3 within each disease × time group, which satisfies rule 3. Each (twin, rep, time) is a separately harvested dish.
- **Exclusions:** PRIDE holds 140 vendor raws. 36 are annotated. The other 104 are 56 label-free SWATH, 34 light-only DDA library and 14 dimethyl Q Exactive runs. All are out of the SILAC campaign's scope, not blocked for a missing channel map, so rule 7 does not strictly apply. They are listed by pattern in the manifest limitations but are absent from BLOCKED.md (minor finding 1).
- **Tolerances:** the runs went through OpenSWATH extraction, so they are not "unsearched". `not available` is therefore correct and `not applicable` would be wrong.
