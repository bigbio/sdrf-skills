## Study
The paper is Braun F et al. "Accumulation of α-synuclein mediates podocyte injury in Fabry nephropathy", *J Clin Invest* 2023;133(11):e157782, doi:10.1172/jci157782, PMID 37014703, PMC10232004.

- **Cells:** conditionally immortalized human podocytes (CIHP-1 / AB8/13, CVCL_W186). Three GLA-knockout clones were made with CRISPR/Cas9 and compared with three wild-type clones.
- **SILAC design:** triple SILAC, MaxQuant Multiplicity 3 (light; Arg6/Lys4; Arg10/Lys8). Each mixture combines one KO culture and one WT culture 1:1, with a label swap between mixtures.
- **Acquisition:** 10 runs per mixture on an LTQ Orbitrap XL. MS1 in the FTMS at 60k; MS2 by CID in the ion trap.
- **Search:** MaxQuant 1.4.1.2.

Multiplexing check (rule 14): `summary.txt` gives Multiplicity 3, Labels1 Arg6;Lys4 and Labels2 Arg10;Lys8 for all 40 searched runs.

### Where the channel map comes from (rule 6)

**Which channels are used.** These come from the summed channel intensities in the deposited `proteinGroups.txt`:
- The #867 mixtures are medium + heavy. Light holds only 3–4% of the intensity, which is residual.
- The #899 mixtures are light + heavy. Medium holds about 4%.

The unused channel produced no rows.

**Which channel is KO.** The paper's Figure 2B is a per-replicate heatmap of log2 GLA-KO/WT for 20 lysosomal proteins, in 6 columns. The deposited ratios reproduce one column per experiment, and only in one orientation each. The table counts matching cells out of 20 (`scripts/fig2b_match.py`):

| experiment | orientation used | best column | matches (used) | matches (flipped) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| #867_sample1 | KO = heavy | 1 | 19/20 | 6/20 |
| #867_sample2 | KO = medium | 2 | 18/20 | 11/20 |
| #899_sample1 | KO = heavy | 3 | 17/20 | 4/20 |
| #899_sample2 | KO = light | 4 | 16/20 | 4/20 |

Examples for #867_sample1:
- DPP7 and GSTM4 are missing from both the data and column 1.
- The data give TTYH3 +2.17, MFGE8 +2.33 and ANPEP −1.42.

**Conflict, recorded.** The Figure 2A schematic draws every mixture as Arg6Lys4 vs Arg10Lys8, with KO-1 medium in mix 1. The deposited data contradict this in two ways:
- the #899 mixtures use light, not medium;
- the experiment that matches column 1 has KO heavy.

The deposited data were followed. Clone identity (KO-1/WT-1 and so on) is not asserted. The CFP numbers suggest that 867 and 899 are separate clone pairs, with a label swap inside each pair, but no per-file statement confirms this.

## Limitations
- Partial scope: the 20 CFP910/CFP911 raws are blocked (see above).
- The channel→genotype map is not based on an explicit per-file label statement. It rests on the deposited data reproducing the Fig. 2B heatmap, and the Fig. 2A schematic conflicts with the data.
- Precursor tolerance and fractionation method are undocumented. convert-openms fails because of the precursor tolerance.
- The paper and Cellosaurus disagree on sex.
- One row of `proteinGroups.txt` contains MaxQuant buffer garbage. It was parsed as latin-1 and the non-numeric values were skipped.
- The review is pending: per the caller, no review-gate approve and no review file.
