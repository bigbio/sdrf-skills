## Study & publication
- **Title (PRIDE):** "Sup:EasySCP Unveils Extensive Liver Zonation at Single-cell Proteomics Resolution".
- **Method:** EasySCP — FACS-based single-cell sorting into 384-well plates, an all-in-one single-step
  in-well digestion, and sensitive LC-MS. The full study spans HEK293, HeLa and murine liver hepatocytes.
- **This deposition (PXD074900)** is the **HeLa** component: FACS-sorted single HeLa cells plus 20-cell
  and blank controls, used to develop/benchmark the EasySCP single-cell workflow across digestion conditions.
- **Publication:** No PMID/PMCID resolvable. PRIDE lists only a **Zenodo** DOI `10.5281/zenodo.18824148`
  (authors Li Baoguo, Hao Bingbing, Wei Jinghui; Zhengzhou University). No preprint found via Europe PMC
  or web search — data are very recent (raw files dated 2025-12-23 → 2026-01-05; deposited 2026/05).
  PRIDE `sample_processing_protocol` and `data_processing_protocol` are detailed and were the primary
  methods source, supplemented by the deposited curator key (see below).

**Blank exclusion (documented trap #35 B1):** the two `*_blank_*` runs are empty FACS wells (no cell
sorted). `characteristics[cell line]` is **required and forbids both reserved words**
(`allow_not_available=false`, `allow_not_applicable=false` in the resolved `cell-lines` template).
A blank has no cell line, and asserting `HeLa` for a no-cell contamination control would be false.
Per the annotator brief, a run may be excluded only when a required column forbids both reserved words —
this is exactly that case. The 2 blanks are therefore excluded rather than fabricated. The 3 twenty-cell
wells **are** genuine HeLa cells, so they are annotated (cell line = HeLa, `cells per well = 20`,
`sample type = pooled`).

## Key decisions & evidence
- **Cell-line identity — parental HeLa CVCL_0030, not HeLa-S3.** These are FACS-sorted single cells from
  an in-house HeLa culture, **not** a commercial Pierce digest standard (which would be HeLa-S3 / CVCL_0058).
  Nothing in PRIDE or the Zenodo record indicates a suspension/S3 subclone. Default parental **HeLa =
  EFO:0001185 / CVCL_0030** (`tools cellline lookup HeLa`, exact match). Cellosaurus-derived fields:
  disease `cervical adenocarcinoma` (MONDO:0005153), origin `uterine cervix` (UBERON:0000002),
  `epithelial cell` (CL:0000066), age `30Y6M`, sex `female`, ancestry `African American` (HANCESTRO:0568).
- **Label-free, 1 file = 1 cell → annotatable.** DIA, no multiplexing → no channel-map risk.
  `comment[label] = label free sample` (MS:1002038); carrier/reference channel = `not applicable`.
- **Acquisition:** Astral DIA, `Data-independent acquisition`. Precursor range 400–800 m/z
  (`scan window lower/upper = 400 / 800`), isolation window 20 m/z (`isolation window width = 20`),
  HCD `beam-type collision-induced dissociation` (MS:1000422) at NCE 25% (`collision energy = 25% NCE`).
  All from the PRIDE sample-processing protocol.
- **Modifications = Oxidation (M, Variable; UNIMOD:35) + Acetyl (Protein N-term, Variable; UNIMOD:1)** only.
  Taken from the data-processing protocol. **No Carbamidomethyl** — the EasySCP single-step protocol
  documents **no reduction/alkylation step**, so the reflex fixed Cys carbamidomethylation was deliberately
  omitted (adding it would be wrong on every row and still pass validation). Phosphorylation is mentioned in
  the protocol only for a separate phospho search arm, not these HeLa proteome runs.
- **Digestion condition preserved:** the primary experimental axis here is the digestion/reaction condition
  (SCP, Mann et al. 2022, Slavov et al. 2018, Olsen et al. 2025, Yun Yang et al. 2025, NH4HCO3 buffer,
  TEAB buffer, Boiling, without Boiling). No standard SDRF `characteristics[...]` column captures a
  sample-prep protocol, so it is recorded in **`comment[sample preparation batch]`** (one label per plate/
  condition) to keep the grouping visible. `biological replicate` = the submitter's per-condition replicate
  index (1–6) from the curator xlsx; each single cell is a distinct biological entity.
- **Factor value:** `factor value[cells per well]` (mirrors `characteristics[cells per well]`; captures the
  1-cell vs 20-cell quantitative comparison, matching the community single-cell SDRF convention).

## Inferred vs stated
- **INFERRED — cleavage agent = Trypsin (MS:1001251).** The enzyme is **not stated** in PRIDE, the Zenodo
  record, or the deposited reports. `comment[cleavage agent details]` cannot be `not available`
  (`allow_not_available=false`) yet a digestion demonstrably occurred, so `not applicable` would be wrong.
  Trypsin is the near-universal bottom-up / Spectronaut directDIA default; annotated as Trypsin and flagged
  here as the single weakest inference. **A reviewer should attack this first** (Trypsin vs Trypsin/P vs
  another protease is unverifiable from available sources).
- **STATED:** organism, HeLa identity keyword, DIA acquisition, instrument (raw + PRIDE), modifications,
  collision energy, scan/isolation windows, per-file sample map, cells-per-well.
- **INFERRED from cell-line reference (Cellosaurus), not the paper:** age, sex, ancestry, disease,
  organism part, cell type — standard for a cell-line dataset.
