## Study
Di Camillo B, Puricelli L, Iori E, Toffolo GM, Tessari P, Arrigoni G. "Modeling SILAC Data to Assess Protein Turnover in a Cellular Model of Diabetic Nephropathy." *Int J Mol Sci* 2023;24(3):2811. doi:10.3390/ijms24032811, PMID 36769128, PMC9917874 (open access; the supplementary zip was retrieved from PMC OA S3).

The paper cites only PXD038541, and no duplicate deposit was found. A PRIDE keyword search for duplicates timed out.

**Samples.** Primary skin fibroblasts from 10 patients with type 1 diabetes mellitus (T1DM): 5 with DN and 5 without.

**Labelling (dynamic SILAC).**
- Cells were grown to confluence in light DMEM, then switched to 13C6-Lys DMEM at T = 0.
- They were harvested at 4, 7.5 and 24 h.

**Sample preparation and MS.**
- Lysates were run on SDS-PAGE, and each lane was cut into 5 bands (A-E).
- Bands were digested in-gel with Lys-C.
- Each band was analysed on an LTQ Orbitrap XL, top-4 CID.
- Each band was injected twice: a first pass, then a re-run with a static exclusion list.

**Search.** Proteome Discoverer 1.2 with Mascot. Each (subject, time point) has one msf.

## Is it multiplexed SILAC? (rule 14)
**Yes.** All 30 deposited `.msf` files have the quantification method "SILAC 2plex (Lys6) (Custom)" (read by HTTP range from the SQLite `ProcessingNodeParameters` table).

This is pulse labelling, not mixing of samples:
- **light** = proteins that existed before the switch;
- **heavy** = proteins synthesised after T = 0.

Each run therefore gives 2 rows: `…_pre-existing-proteins` (SILAC light) and `…_newly-synthesized-proteins` (SILAC heavy). This follows the campaign precedents PXD006015 and PXD063206.

## Design / channel map

**Sources.** One source per (subject, time point, channel), giving 10 × 3 × 2 = 60 sources.

**Channel map** (source: PRIDE protocol and paper §4.3):
- light = pre-existing proteome;
- heavy = proteome newly synthesised during the 13C6-Lys pulse.

**Factor.** `factor value[sampling time]` = 4 hour / 7.5 hour / 24 hour. The file token `07h` is the stated 7.5 h.

**Biological replicate.** Subjects are numbered 1-10 within every sampling-time group, in ascending cell code: cell36 = 1, cell42 = 2, cell80 = 3, cell160 = 4, cell268 = 5, cell304 = 6, cell325 = 7, cell504 = 8, cell511 = 9, cell515 = 10. `characteristics[individual]` = the cell code.

**Fractions.** `comment[fraction identifier]` 1-5 = gel bands A-E.

## Conflicts and notes
- **Missed cleavages.** The paper says "up to one missed cleavage", but every msf has `MissedCleavages = 2`. There is no SDRF column for this.
- **Instrument variant.** The raw header's PC name is "LTQ Orbitrap ETD", so the hardware may be an XL ETD (MS:1000639). The XL (paper and raw instrument name) is kept.
- **MS1 scan range.** The raw header shows m/z 300-1600, while PRIDE and the paper say 300-1700. No scan-range column is written.
- **Quantification method.** PRIDE also lists "MS1 intensity based label-free quantification". This is the paper's secondary abundance estimate (§4.7), made from the same SILAC runs, so these are not separate label-free runs.

## Judgement calls a reviewer should attack
1. Not encoding DN status at all, versus a custom column of `not available` values. Rule 7 forbids fake grouping, and no linkage exists.
2. Technical replicates 3/4 for the repeat acquisitions, and the scripted fraction 5 for the cell42 band-E-only re-runs.
3. Declaring clinical-metadata (rule 15: the samples are patient-derived) even though none of its optional columns is filled.
4. `skin of body` / `fibroblast` as the general terms, because the biopsy site and dermal origin are unstated.
