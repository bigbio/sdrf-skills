## Study and publication

- **Title:** *Proteomic analysis of saliva reveals changes in proteomic profiles during colorectal cancer in an Iranian cohort*.
- **Publication:** Rezasoltani et al., *Frontiers in Oncology* (2026), PMID 42421682, PMCID PMC13341466, DOI 10.3389/fonc.2026.1854834.
- **Dataset:** PXD078610, public PRIDE Archive, CC0.
- **Design:** label-free bottom-up saliva proteomics comparing stage-I colorectal-cancer participants with non-cancer controls.

## Scope and reconciliation

PRIDE `files/all` returned 104 files: 100 `.raw` files and four Proteome Discoverer result files. Every raw file was asserted to belong to PXD078610 and is represented once in the SDRF. The raw filenames provide the group assignment: 51 filenames contain `_CRC_` and 49 contain `_HC_`. PRIDE's project description says 104 saliva samples, so four samples are not represented by deposited raw files and were not invented in the SDRF.

The first 40 raw files are named `220729_SR_FirstBatch_ForIMSC_{n}CRC/HC.raw`; the remaining 60 are named `221005_SR_{CRC/HC}_{n}.raw`. The annotation uses the filename-declared group, not a demographic inference.

## Evidence-backed decisions

- `Homo sapiens` / `NCBITaxon:9606`: PRIDE project metadata.
- `saliva` / `BTO:0001202`: PRIDE sample attribute and paper.
- `colorectal cancer` / `MONDO:0005575`: paper's CRC cohort; accession verified through OLS.
- `normal` / `PATO:0000461`: non-cancer control group; accession verified through OLS.
- `Orbitrap Fusion` / `MS:1002416`: paper Methods and PRIDE metadata.
- Label-free: paper and PRIDE description.
- Trypsin: paper sample-processing protocol; `MS:1001251`.
- DDA: paper Methods; `PRIDE:0000627`.
- HCD at 30% NCE: paper Methods; represented as higher-energy beam-type CID `MS:1002481`.
- Fixed carbamidomethylation and variable methionine oxidation: paper data-processing protocol (`UNIMOD:4`, `UNIMOD:35`).
- Fraction and technical replicate are both `1`: the paper describes single-shot, unfractionated LC-MS/MS and PRIDE exposes one raw file per deposited run.

## Values left unavailable

Precursor and fragment mass tolerances are `not available` because the paper does not report them. Per-sample age, sex, smoking status, and other demographics are omitted: the article discusses demographic effects, but no individual-level source-to-file mapping was available from the PRIDE manifest used here. The four result files are excluded because they are not acquisition runs.
