## Why blocked
No accessible source states which label (light or heavy) the EA-treated cells carried in Fwd or in Rev. The PRIDE protocol only says light and heavy lysates "with and without" EA were mixed. The deposited HSPB1 phosphosite ratios (Fwd H/L ≫ 1, Rev H/L ≪ 1; e.g. S65 9.10 / 0.066) suggest heavy = EA in Fwd. That inference rests only on the abstract's biological direction, and rule 6 forbids using it as the map.

## Unblock
Get the paper's Methods or SI (or an author statement) that gives the label orientation. Then build 104 rows (52 runs × 2 channels) with human + cell-lines, as planned in BLOCKED.md.
