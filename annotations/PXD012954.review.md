### Findings

1. **IMPORTANT — the MC_2580 arm's control channels are DMSO vehicle, not untreated.**
   - **Where:** rows 49-56 (forward light IP), 73-80 (reverse heavy IP), 123-133 (forward light input) and 156-166 (reverse heavy input). Affected columns: `characteristics[treatment]`, `factor value[treatment]`, `source name`, `characteristics[biological replicate]`.
   - **Evidence:**
     - The Fig. 4E schematic (supplement `aax2746-F4.jpg`) labels the Heavy (K8,R10) dish 'LSD1 inhibitor' and the Light (K0,R0) dish **'DMSO'**, each going to LSD1-IP and then Mix 1:1.
     - The Fig. 4G validation used 'cells treated for 24 hours with DMSO, 2 μM MC_2580'.
     - The manifest claim says 'the paper names no vehicle for the IP'. That is false.
   - **Consequence:** these vehicle-treated cultures share the factor groups (anti-LSD1 IP, untreated) and (input, untreated) with the truly untreated basal-arm cultures (bioreps 4-5 and 7-8). Fig. 4A shows no vehicle for the basal arm.
   - **Fix:**
     - Set treatment and factor value for these four channels to the vehicle, e.g. `dimethyl sulfoxide` (the common curated value).
     - Rename the sources accordingly.
     - Renumber the bioreps: IP/DMSO 1-2, input/DMSO 1-2, IP/untreated 1-3, input/untreated 1-6.
     - Rebuild, revalidate and correct the manifest claim. Batch rule 4 only forbids `not applicable` for controls; it does not license calling a vehicle control `untreated`.
2. **minor — two manifest limitations are already resolved by the supplement.**
   - Input↔IP pairing is explicit in SM Table S1, not 'from experiment names only'.
   - Cell treatment of the MC_2580 arm is explicit in Fig. 4E and the Table S2 title, so drop 'assuming cell treatment'.
   - Cite both sources in the manifest.
3. **minor — dose and duration are not encoded.** The documented 2 µM for 24 h (Fig. 4E legend) could go into the treatment value or into licensed dose/time columns.
