## Templates
`ms-proteomics` v1.2.0 + `human` v1.1.0 + `clinical-metadata` v1.0.0 + `oncology-metadata` v1.0.0. `comment[sdrf version]` is v1.1.0 (batch rule 1).
- The samples are patient metastases, so `clinical-metadata` applies (rules 15 and 18). It licenses `treatment status`, `treatment response` and `genotype`.
- These are cancer samples, so `oncology-metadata` applies. It licenses `clinical data` and `clinical history`.
- `cell-lines` is **not** declared. Only the reference channel is cell-line material, and the required `characteristics[cell line]` column would forbid reserved words on the patient rows. The reference lines are named in free text instead (PXD013311 precedent).
- `characteristics[preservation method]` is a custom column, allowed under the spec's "Extending Templates" (PXD002669 precedent).

## Multiplexing check (rule 14)
I extracted MaxQuant 1.6.2.6 `combined/txt/summary.txt` from `combined.zip` (236 GB) by HTTP range reads.
- All 1091 searched runs have `Multiplicity 2`, an empty `Labels0` and `Labels1 Arg10;Lys8`. This is true multiplexed SILAC.
- **Channel map:** light = tumour lysate, heavy = super-SILAC reference.
  - The PRIDE protocol says "heavy labeled super-SILAC standard lysate and the tumor lysates were mixed at a 1:1 ratio". S1 reports values as "log2 ratio tumor/SILAC".
  - The deposited data agree. In S1B, `Intensity L`/`Intensity H` is 72 for HBB, 62 for HBA1, 17 for IGHG1 and 12 for COL1A1. Blood and stroma proteins occur only in tissue. MKI67 is higher in the heavy channel (0.52), as expected for proliferating cell lines.

## Key decisions and evidence

### Sample identity
- `source name` is the MaxQuant `Experiment` name, which equals the S1A `Sample name`.
- **27 deposited files have a different name in their raw-header acquisition path.**
  - `20170322_MH_SA_TIL_84_*` was acquired as `..._PD1_35_*`.
  - TIL_75 and TIL_77 are swapped.
  - TIL_85 and TIL_86 were acquired as TIL_43 and TIL_44.
  - `Animal_20180726_SA_MH_CTLA4_20.raw` was acquired as `..._CTLA4_21`.
- I followed the deposited names. Apart from CTLA20, MaxQuant searched these files under their deposited names, so they are the identities behind the paper's S1B data.
- CTLA20's file was renamed after the search. Its acquisition path equals the summary.txt raw file `Animal_20180726_SA_MH_CTLA4_21`, which MaxQuant assigned to experiment CTLA20. This is the only file with the `SA_MH` naming, so the mapping is unique.

### Clinical values
All clinical values come from individual-level Table S1A rows only (rule 18).
- **age:** `Age at treatment`, floored to whole years (e.g. 44.24 → `44Y`).
- **sex:** from `gender`.
- **organism part:** `Biopsy site` mapped to UBERON. SC is `hypodermis`; its UBERON synonym is "subcutaneous tissue".
- **genotype:** BRAF status.
- **treatment response:** the response to the patient's immunotherapy, as NCIT terms.
- **treatment status:**
  - pre-treatment when the S1A sampling-day cell is empty;
  - on treatment for the 8 samples with a day count;
  - `not available` for the 4 "NA" cells.
  - PRIDE's "except for 12 samples" matches the 12 numeric cells in the full table.
- **developmental stage:** adult (all annotated patients are at least 20 years old).
- **clinical data:** free text holding the immunotherapy group, LDH, overall survival, death, technical set and the low-quality flag.
- **clinical history:** prior MAPKi drug and the initial response to it.
- **individual:** the S1A id. PD58 is written `PD49PD58`, the S1B column name, because `PD49/58` fails the validator pattern.

### Super-SILAC reference (rule 18)
Heavy rows use:
- source `super-SILAC reference mixed with <sample>`
- sample type `reference`
- pooled sample `pooled`
- material type `cell line`
- disease `melanoma` (MONDO:0005105)
- biological replicate, age, individual and sex all `pooled` (the lines' sexes are mixed)

The cell lines are named in clinical data: WM266-4 (CVCL_2765), WM115 (CVCL_0040), A-375 (CVCL_0132), 526-mel (CVCL_8051) and 624-mel (CVCL_8054). The names come from Harel et al. 2019, the protocol that PRIDE cites. PRIDE's text for this deposit only says "five SILAC-labeled melanoma cell lines".

### Factor values
The study compares three variables, so there are three factor columns: `organism part` (metastatic site, the "organ specificity" in the title), `genotype` (BRAF status) and `treatment response` (immunotherapy response). The abstract names all three: "metastatic location, BRAF status, survival, and immunotherapy response". Heavy rows carry `not applicable`.

### Modifications
Modifications come from `summary.txt` and the Andromeda `.apar` file:
- Carbamidomethyl C, Fixed
- Oxidation M, Variable
- Acetyl Protein N-term, Variable
- Arg10 = `Label:13C(6)15N(4)` UNIMOD:267 on R, Variable
- Lys8 = `Label:13C(6)15N(2)` UNIMOD:259 on K, Variable

### Tolerances
Precursor tolerance is **4.5 ppm** (`.apar` "peptide mass tolerance=4.5 ppm"). Fragment tolerance is **20 ppm** (`parameters.txt` "MS/MS tol. (FTMS) 20 ppm").

### Cleavage agents
- The first column is Trypsin on all rows. MaxQuant searched with the Trypsin/P rule (rule 2).
- The second column is Lys-C for technical sets 2–3. The PRIDE protocol uses "Lys-C-Trypsin mix ... and trypsin" with SCX fractionation.
- TIL25 and TIL33 (technical set 1, acquired 2014-04-29) carry `not applicable` in the Lys-C column. Harel 2019's TIL-cohort protocol is FASP with "sequencing grade trypsin" and "six fractions using SAX". TIL33 has exactly 6 runs named by SAX pH (3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 11).

### Fractionation
- SCX for SCX runs.
- SAX for TIL33.
- `not available` for single 240-min runs (TIL25, TIL68/69/71/74/79, CTLA20). These runs show no fractionation, but none is stated either.

### Technical replicates
Re-injections become technical replicate 2 (or 3) at the same fraction. They are identified by:
- the Xcalibur `_yyMMddHHmmss` re-acquisition suffix;
- PD42's second dated series (20170329, fractions 1–3);
- CTLA3's unsearched injections.

TIL68/69/71/74/79 each have two identical 240-min single-shot runs. MaxQuant labelled them "fraction 1" and "fraction 2"; I annotated them as fraction 1, technical replicates 1 and 2.

### Instrument, NCE and MS1 scan range
These are set per file from the raw method text:
- Q Exactive HF (143 files): 27 NCE, 380–1800 m/z
- Q Exactive Plus (195 files): 25 NCE, 300–1800 m/z
- 2014 TIL25/TIL33 runs (QE Plus): 300–1700 m/z
- CTLA20 (QE Plus): 380–1800 m/z

The "Animal_" prefix in file names tracks the QE Plus instrument exactly (all 188 such files). It is a lab naming convention, not an animal sample: S1A lists these samples as patients, and the search used a human-only FASTA.

## Limitations
All are listed in the manifest. The main ones:
- The paper is inaccessible, so its citation of PXD020618 is unverified.
- techsdrf is not installed; raw headers were HTTP-range read instead.
- The response criteria (RECIST or irRC) are unknown.
- The MaxQuant first-search tolerance (in mqpar.xml) is not deposited.
- 8 annotated samples are S1A "low proteomic quality" samples that the paper excluded. They are flagged in clinical data.

## Judgement calls a reviewer should attack hardest
1. **Sample identity for renamed files.** I followed the deposited and MaxQuant names, not the raw-header acquisition names. This affects TIL84 (acquired as PD1_35), the TIL75/TIL77 swap, TIL85/86 (acquired as TIL43/44) and CTLA20 (acquired as CTLA4_21). I also mapped PD58 to S1A "PD49/58", writing the individual as `PD49PD58`.
2. **Per-set protocol split.** TIL25 and TIL33 get trypsin only (Lys-C `not applicable`) and SAX, inferred from Harel 2019's TIL-cohort protocol. Reconcile's BLOCKER on this is refuted. The super-SILAC cell-line identities also come from Harel 2019, not from this deposit's own text.
3. **Factor and replicate design.**
   - Three factors: organism part, BRAF genotype and immunotherapy response (NCIT Remission terms for PR/CR).
   - Re-injections are technical replicates, including TIL68/69/71/74/79's two single-shot runs that MaxQuant called fractions 1 and 2.
   - A scripted fraction-id fix covers 3 re-injection files.
   - Fractionation is `not available` for single-shot runs.
