## Findings (all minor)
1. **Provenance (label claim and limitation):** the manifest says no deposited marker separates AZR3 from AZR4 or COR1 from COR10. The paper's NDRG1 (lower in AZR4 and COR1) and OCRL (lower in AZR4 only) markers do, and the proteinGroups ratios match the PRIDE medium/heavy assignment. *Fix:* add this check to the claim and drop or rephrase the limitation.
2. **Cellosaurus columns on derived lines (996 rows):** these rows carry the parental CVCL_1511 / NCI-H1975. The cell-lines template allows `not available` here. Campaign precedent PXD010683 accepts the parental value, so this does not block. *Fix (optional):* use `not available`, or state in the manifest that the value is the parental's.
3. **pY flow-through runs (12 files, 36 rows: `20180108_Andy_Tapan_pY_*_FT`, `*_ACN_FT`):** they are labelled `enrichment of phosphorylated protein` and share the eluate's source name. The deposit does not say what FT is, and the limitation is already disclosed. *Fix:* split them only if the authors clarify.
4. **Treatment = Dimethyl Sulfoxide (687 rows):** campaign rule 4 and the sample-metadata description say `untreated` for controls. The protocol shows a real 1 h DMSO exposure, so the vehicle value is accurate and validates. Separately, the manifest claim's value string `Dimethyl Sulfoxide (vehicle)` does not match the cell text. *Fix:* have the campaign owner confirm whether vehicle controls keep the vehicle, and fix the manifest value string.
5. **pY fraction identifiers (rows 214-312, 1396-1488):** the 1..n values enumerate different IPs (pY100/pY1000 antibody, 1st/2nd binding, re-injections), not chromatographic fractions. This is deterministic and disclosed. No change needed.
