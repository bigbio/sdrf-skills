## Findings

### IMPORTANT — `comment[precursor mass tolerance]` = `not available` although the deposited data decides it
- **Location:** rows 1-324. Manifest claim "Conflict with no deciding source".
- **Evidence:** in the deposited MaxQuant `evidence.txt`, the largest calibrated |Mass error [ppm]| (non-MULTI) is 4.4989 for 983, 4.4989 for 984, 4.4989 for 136, 4.494 for 139, 4.4537 for R1_96 and 4.4941 for R1_96B. That hard 4.5 ppm cutoff is the main-search tolerance. The PRIDE dataProcessingProtocol also says "After initial recalibration, a mass tolerance of 4.5 ppm". The paper's 6 ppm is contradicted, and deposited search outputs outrank the manuscript for technical parameters. Because the cell is empty, `parse_sdrf convert-openms` fails on the deliverable.
- **Fix:** set `4.5 ppm` on all 324 rows. Cite mq_983, mq_984, mq_136_139 and mq_96 `evidence.txt` plus the PRIDE protocol in the manifest. Drop the limitation and re-run convert-openms.

### minor — shRNA identity in the BioID2 arm
Paper Methods: "cells were infected with pLKO-shKRAS.2 or pLKO-shNRAS.1". The SDRF writes `shKRAS` / `shNRAS` (rows 24-69, 93-138, 162-207, 231-276), while the phospho arm writes `shNRAS.1`. Optional fix: `BioID2-SLC3A2 + shNRAS.1` / `+ shKRAS.2`.

### minor — 983 provenance wording
The RAS/MTOR ratios cited as biological confirmation are weak: direct H/M is +0.18 for KRAS;NRAS and +0.27 for MTOR, the same size as noise elsewhere (136 RAS H/M +0.01, 984 −0.13). Rest the claim on the Fig. 3a identity. Mention the 983 deviation from `File_assignment.txt` in the PXD031662 BLOCKED.md section.

### minor — `characteristics[genetic modification]` licensing
Only clinical-metadata defines this column, and that template is not declared; its validator expects method terms. The column is acceptable as a spec "Extending Templates" custom column, and curated precedent (300+ SDRFs) uses construct text there.
