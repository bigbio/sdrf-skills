## What was wrong with the curated SDRF

The curated file in sdrf-annotated-datasets is byte-identical to `PXD045898_community_annotated.sdrf.tsv`, which is deposited in PRIDE. It says:

- **27 rows, one per RAW file, every one `label free sample` (MS:1002038).**
- Organism part and the only factor: `Epithelial cell` (a cell type).
- Disease, age and sex: `not available`. Biological replicate 1 and fraction identifier 1 on every row.
- No modification parameters, and no cell lines.

So it describes 27 unrelated label-free samples. The data are 3 SILAC mixes of 3 cell lines, each split into 9 high-pH fractions. The file loses the design completely: no conditions, no replicates and no fractions.

The authors' own `sdrf.tsv` (also in PRIDE) has 81 rows with cell lines. It names the channels wrongly, though: HEK293 = `SILAC heavy`, 786-O = `SILAC light`, Caki-1 = `label free sample`.

## Evidence that it is triple SILAC

| RAW headers | Acquisition path `…\Cell line\SILAC_Global\…_3-23%.raw`. LTQ Orbitrap Velos, CID 28% NCE, top 10, FTMS 300-2000, ITMS MS2. |

**Channel map (unchanged from PXD039207):**

| Cell line | Channel | Labels |
|---|---|---|
| HEK293 | SILAC heavy | K8R10 |
| 786-O | SILAC medium | K4R6 |
| Caki-1 | SILAC light | K0R0 |

**Why the deposit texts disagree, and why it does not matter.** Two deposit texts use other names for the same isotope assignment:

- the PRIDE dataProcessingProtocol says "Arg6-Lys4 for light and Arg10-Lys8 for heavy";
- the author SDRF calls 786-O "light" and Caki-1 "label free".

Read as isotopes, both still put K4R6 on 786-O and leave Caki-1 unlabelled. That is what the paper says. In a three-channel MaxQuant run, K4R6 is the medium channel and K0R0 the light channel. An unlabelled channel in a SILAC mix is not a label-free sample.

## Design (as in PXD039207)

- 3 biological replicate mixes (MaxQuant experiments 1st/2nd/3rd, file suffix -1/-2/-3) × 9 high-pH fractions (F0-F8, which become `comment[fraction identifier]` 1-9) = 27 RAW files.
- 27 RAW × 3 channels = 81 rows. There are 9 sources, named `<line>_SILAC_rep<1-3>`, and the factor is `factor value[cell line]`.
- Cellosaurus-derived characteristics (sex, age, developmental stage, ancestry, sampling site, disease), DMEM, -80 °C, and technical values are as in `PXD039207.report.md`.

## Open items and limitations

- **No ratio check of the channel map (rule 6).** No MaxQuant txt output is deposited and no RAW reader is installed. The map rests on Fig. 1A plus the Methods, which agree.
- **Identity rests on sampled bytes.** It uses sizes, an identical mqpar and three sampled 1 MiB blocks. Neither accession has PRIDE checksums.
- **Minor findings carried over on purpose.** The PXD039207 review's minor findings (missing recommended `characteristics[sample type]` and `characteristics[individual]`) are kept, so the twins stay diff-identical. If they are fixed, fix both files together.
- Mix pairing (replicate *i* of each line in mix *i*) is implied by the design, not stated.
- The full list is in the manifest `limitations`.

## Judgement calls the reviewer should attack hardest

1. **Reusing the PXD039207 annotation.** It is valid only if the runs really are the same. Check `file_map.tsv` and the byte samples. A full-file hash of at least one RAW from both FTPs would close this.
2. **Channel names versus the deposit texts.** Both the PRIDE protocol and the author SDRF say "light" for the K4R6 channel. This file follows the paper's Fig. 1A and Methods (medium). The isotope assignment is the same either way, but confirm that this reading of "light" is right.
3. **The same calls inherited from PXD039207:**
   - HEK293 disease `normal`;
   - Caki-1 organism part `kidney` with sampling site `skin of body`;
   - DMEM for the labelled lines;
   - `-80 °C` storage;
   - `late embryonic stage` and `European ancestry` mappings.
