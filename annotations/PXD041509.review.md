## Targeted tests requested by the caller

### 1. Is blocking the SILAC arm justified? — Yes
- **`mqpar_siUSP39_digly.xml`** (MaxQuant 1.5.2.8) has multiplicity 2 and experiments 1/2/3 × fractions 1–6.
  - Parameter group 0 (exp1, exp2) is labelMods `["", "Arg10;Lys8"]`. Parameter group 1 (exp3) is `["Arg10;Lys8", ""]`.
  - That confirms a label swap in exp3. It fixes only the *relative* orientation of the channels; no condition name appears anywhere.
- **Search output:** none was deposited for the diGly search (no summary.txt, GlyGly sites or proteinGroups). No ratio can therefore be checked against a USP39 knockdown signature.
- **Raw-level evidence:** I range-read the first 4 MB of `..._SILAC_exp1_1.raw` and `..._exp3_1.raw`.
  - Both show Orbitrap Exploris 480, method `NSI_110min_TOP15_..._for_SILAC_DiGly_fractionated.meth` and HCD 30.
  - The Study, Client, Sample and Comment fields are empty, so the raws hold no channel annotation.
  - A knockdown test (USP39 peptides lower in one channel) would mean downloading 18 raws of about 1.1 GB each, converting and searching them. No raw reader (ThermoRawFileParser, mono, dotnet) is installed here.
  - USP39 peptides are also unlikely to be well sampled in diGly-enriched fractions.
  - Most importantly, campaign rule 6 requires an *explicit* label source, which data could only corroborate. Data alone could not unblock the arm.
- **Other reachable sources I re-searched:**
  - **Europe PMC:** the Science paper (PMID 39541449) is closed, and no preprint exists.
  - **PMC12851022** (Angew Chem 2026, USP39 PROTAC) is open, but it reports a different experiment: 5/50 nM PROTAC_V1 for 24 h, deposited as PXD065738 (2025 DIA). It contains no TMT or SILAC map for this deposit.
  - **PXD051146** (same paper; TMT ER/insoluble fractions; submitter T. Mosler): no SILAC text.
  - **ZFIN figure page** for the paper: HTTP 503, not bypassed. Per its search snippet the caption reads "si-USP39 and si-Ctrl" volcano plots, which gives conditions only, not a channel orientation.
- **Conclusion:** the block is justified, and keeping rows with unknown conditions would breach rule 7.

  - USP10 is flat (−0.23), and so are its partners G3BP1 (+0.33) and G3BP2 (+0.37).
  - Tri-snRNP partners are enriched: PRPF4 2.43, SNRNP200 2.05, SART1 1.80, PRPF31 1.76.
- The bait is USP39. The file-name discrepancy is disclosed in the manifest limitations.
