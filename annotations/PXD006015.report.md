## Data files: why `.ms2`
The deposit contains no vendor `.raw` file. PRIDE categorises the RawConverter `.ms2` files as **RAW** and the `.mzXML` / `.mgf` as PEAK, so `comment[data file]` lists the `.ms2` files (as instructed for this batch). The original raw names are visible only in the mzXML `parentFile` element.

## Design
One AG03258 (WT) culture per time point (0, 1, 2, 4, 6 days in heavy medium), nuclei split into a detergent/salt **nuclear extract** and the **nuclear pellet**. Each run has two rows: SILAC light = pre-existing proteins, SILAC heavy = proteins made during labelling (same culture, distinct source names, precedent PXD028934). Factors: `sampling time` and `subcellular fraction`. Repeat injections `_2` (0D extract, 0D pellet, 4D extract) = technical replicate 2.

## Channel map (rule 6)
Paper/PRIDE: quiescent cells "switched to media containing stable heavy isotopes of lysine and arginine (13C6-Lysine, 13C6, 15N4-Arginine) for 1 to 6 days"; Fig. 1: light = "old", heavy = "new". Data check from the deposited mzids: the share of passing heavy-search PSMs rises with the file's day (0D ~1%, 1D 27%, 2D 36-37%, 4D 50-53%, 6D 58-61%), as do the Supp Data 1 medians (5.3/28.0/45.9/60.5% at 0/2/4/6 D).
