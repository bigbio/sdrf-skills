## Important: F1, NFX1_2 encoded as biological replicate 2 of Flag-NFX1 is not defensible
The raw headers name three distinct samples: **EV, NFX1 and NFX1_Ub**. No run was acquired as a second plain NFX1 sample, so the deposited set has to contain the NFX1_Ub sample.

- Deposited EV has no bait (0 NFX1 intensity, 0 evidences), so its empty-vector identity is supported by the data.
- That leaves deposited NFX1_2 as the NFX1_Ub sample. It was acquired as "EV" but contains both the bait and USP9X.
- Its ubiquitin profile fits a ubiquitination set-up. The ubiquitin group is 13x higher than in NFX1_1, and ubiquitin peptides are 2.46% of the intensity of NFX1_2_Up, against 0.07–0.12% in the other four runs. The abstract describes NFX1-123 ubiquitination experiments.
- NFX1 itself is 12x lower in NFX1_2 than in NFX1_1.

The name "NFX1_2" is a sample label. It is not an author statement that the sample is an independent replicate under the same condition. The owner decision covers sample identity by deposited name; it does not cover grouping NFX1_2 with NFX1_1 as an n=2 condition. As written, an EV-vs-NFX1 analysis would treat a sample that was probably treated differently as a replicate. The original request says "undocumented arms are blocked, not filled."

**Fix (preferred):** keep rows 1–4 (EV and NFX1_1). Remove the `NFX1_2_Up/Down` runs as an undocumented arm, list them in the manifest limitations, and add them to the BLOCKED.md PXD012871 section until an author statement exists.

**Alternative, only if the owner explicitly wants all 6 rows:** put NFX1_2 in its own group, for example with a second factor column (identical header on all rows) that separates it under an owner-approved label stating its condition is undocumented. Number its biological replicate 1 within that group. Either way, remove the replicate-2 claim and record the change in the manifest.

## Minor
1. The manifest gives the NFX1_2_Up ubiquitin share as 2.44%. My recomputation gives 2.46%, matching on UBB/UBC/RPS27A/UBA52 in evidence.txt Gene names. Either state the matching rule or use 2.46%.
2. The `ubq` source location contains an unexpanded `{S}` placeholder and cites a review file as a cross-check. Expand the path and cite data rather than reviews.
3. claims[0] lists `ubq` as support for the genetic-modification claim, although that source is evidence against the NFX1_2 grouping. Move it to a conflict claim or to the limitations.
4. For EV, the Up=1 / Down=2 fractions are kept although the molecular-weight pattern is inverted. This is disclosed in the limitations; I did not recompute it. No change is needed.
