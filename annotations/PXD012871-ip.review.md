## Findings

### 1. IMPORTANT: the ubiquitin evidence on the conflict is missing

- **Where:** manifest limitations[0], the biological-replicate claim, and SDRF rows 5-6 (`HEK293T_FlagNFX1_2`, replicate 2).
- **What the data show:**
  - In `proteinGroups.txt`, the ubiquitin group (RPS27A;UBB;UBC;UBA52) has intensity 4.06e8 in EV, 6.98e8 in NFX1_1 and 9.03e9 in NFX1_2. NFX1_2 has 13 times the ubiquitin of NFX1_1 although it has 12 times less bait.
  - Ubiquitin's share of summed intensity in `evidence.txt`, by run:

    | Run | Ubiquitin share |
    |---|---|
    | EV_Up | 0.07% |
    | EV_Down | 0.09% |
    | NFX1_1_Up | 0.08% |
    | NFX1_1_Down | 0.12% |
    | NFX1_2_Up | 2.46% |
    | NFX1_2_Down | 0.37% |

  - The excess sits in the high-MW Up gel part, which fits polyubiquitin conjugates.
- **Why it matters:**
  - One acquired name is `NFX1_Ub`, and the abstract says "NFX1-123 could be modified by ubiquitination". This makes it plausible that deposited NFX1_2 is the ubiquitination sample (Flag-NFX1 IP under ubiquitination conditions), not an independent replicate of NFX1_1.
  - The manifest says only that "Data support the deposited names" and frames NFX1_2 as transfection vs repeat IP. That records less than the deposit shows.
- **Fix:** keep the owner decision and the SDRF values. In the manifest:
  1. Add these numbers to the conflict limitation and to the replicate claim.
  2. State that NFX1_2 may be the `NFX1_Ub` sample.
  3. Narrow "data support the deposited names" to "bait/prey data support deposited EV having no bait; the deposited names do not explain the ubiquitin profile of NFX1_2".
  4. Mirror this in the BLOCKED.md open points.

  Then run a fresh review.

### 2. Minor: the renaming predates the authors' search

`txt_IP/parameters.txt` is dated 02/21/2019, and `summary.txt` already uses the deposited names. So the authors renamed the files before their own search. One sentence in limitation 1 would strengthen the provenance.

### 3. Minor: the anti-Flag IP step is not encoded

The anti-Flag IP enrichment step is not in any column. EFO:0009090 has no IP child term, so leaving it out is acceptable. Optionally add a note or a custom column.
