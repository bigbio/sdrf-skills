The data fit an EV/NFX1_Ub swap at acquisition that was corrected at deposition: deposited EV has no bait and low ubiquitin, while NFX1_2 (acquired as "EV") has bait and 13× ubiquitin. This is disclosed in the manifest. An author statement would settle it.
2. **EV Up/Down may be inverted** (rows 1–2, fraction identifier). EV_Up median MW is 43.7 kDa with 18 proteins >150 kDa; EV_Down is 48.8 kDa with 39. For NFX1_1 the pattern is the expected one (72.5 / 32.9 kDa). The fraction IDs correctly follow MaxQuant, and this is disclosed. No change is needed.
