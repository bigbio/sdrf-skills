## Study
No curated SDRF exists for this dataset (`sdrf-tools curated PXD016521` returned an empty list), and it does not appear in DUPLICATES.md or BLOCKED.md.

Protasoni et al., *EMBO J* 2020, 39:e102817 (PMID 31912925, PMC6996572). The study compares 143B-TK⁻ osteosarcoma cybrids that differ only in their mtDNA:
- **WT:** clone #4.1, 100% wild-type mtDNA.
- **Δ4-CYB:** clone #17.3E, homoplasmic 4-bp deletion in MT-CYB.

Both clones are transmitochondrial cybrids: 143B TK⁻ ρ° nuclei repopulated with mitochondria from the **same** heteroplasmic patient (Rana et al 2000). This patient carried the MT-CYB 4-bp deletion and had parkinsonism/MELAS overlap syndrome (de Coo et al 1999).

All samples were SILAC-labelled (light K0/R0; heavy K8/R10) and every experiment was done twice with reciprocal labelling.

## Design and channel map

### Complexome
Mitochondria come from 1:1 SILAC mixtures. Each mixture was solubilized with DDM or digitonin and run on BN-PAGE, and each lane was cut into 64 slices.

| lane (PSM table) | raw files | detergent | WT label | Δ4-CYB label | biological replicate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MultiConsensus_psms.txt | A1–A64 (slices 1–64) | DDM | heavy | light | 1 |
| Multiconsensus_psms_laneC.txt | A65–A128 (slices 71–134) | DDM | light | heavy | 2 |
| Multiconsensus_psms_lane3.txt | A130–A192 + A257 (slices 142–205) | digitonin | heavy | light | 1 |
| Multiconsensus_psms_lane4.txt | A193–A256 (slices 211–274) | digitonin | light | heavy | 2 |

**What the map rests on:**
- **Lane → raw files:** the `Spectrum File` column of each deposited PSM table.
- **Orientation, from the data:** MT-CYB is not translated in Δ4-CYB, and its peptides appear in one channel only (4H/0L, 6L/0H, 17H/0L, 20L/0H). UQCRC1 and UQCRFS1 show the same split (for example UQCRC1 103H/1L, 399L/0H, 429H/0L, 452L/0H).
- **Detergent, from the data:** in the WT channel, the complex III subunit UQCRC1 co-migrates with the complex I subunit NDUFS1 in lanes 3 and 4. This is the supercomplex pattern that digitonin preserves (profile overlap 0.66/0.67). It does not co-migrate in the other two lanes, as expected for DDM (0.06/0.07).
- **Text the map agrees with:**
  - The Fig EV1 legend describes a DDM experiment with Δ4-CYB heavy (laneC) and a digitonin experiment with WT heavy (lane3).
  - The paper and PRIDE say both detergents were used, each with reciprocal labelling.
  - The MS run dates pair the lanes the same way.
- The analysis script is `scripts/orient.py` and its output is `orient_output.txt`.

**Biological replicates:** numbering restarts within each genotype × detergent group: 1 = the WT-heavy mixture, 2 = the reciprocal mixture. Factor values are `genotype` and `detergent`.

**Fraction encoding (campaign rule 16):** each lane is one solubilized SILAC mixture cut into 64 slices that together reconstruct the lane. The slices are not independently prepared samples compared as conditions, so they are encoded as `comment[fraction identifier]` with the slice order preserved.

**Groups:** 4 groups (WT/Δ4-CYB × DDM/digitonin). Each has 2 sources × 64 fractions = 128 rows.

### CYC1-HA IP

| experiment | raw files | WT label | Δ4-CYB label | biological replicate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A (HACYC1A-1..12) | PRO3980A1–A12 | light | heavy | 1 |
| B (HACYC1B-1..12) | PRO3980A25–A36 | heavy | light | 2 |

**What the map rests on:**
- **Explicit source:** the Fig 4H axis titles, "Log2 Ratio H/L Exp A" and "Log2 Ratio L/H Exp B". The complex III subunits, which the paper reports as low in the mutant, lie at negative values on both axes.
- **Cross-check against the deposit:** `proteinGroups_PRO3980.txt` reproduces the plotted values (UQCRC2 −8.51 / −6.00). This means its "Ratio H/L expB" column actually holds the L/H values.
- **Intensity cross-check:** for the complex III subunits that are abundant in WT, the summed light intensity equals the exp A intensity and the heavy intensity equals the exp B intensity (UQCRC2: 3.62e9 vs 3.63e9 and 1.11e9 vs 1.09e9). So WT is light in A and heavy in B, which confirms the labelling is reciprocal.

**Groups:** WT and Δ4-CYB, each with 2 sources × 12 fractions = 24 rows.

## Key decisions: stated vs inferred

**Cell line (revised after review):**
- The cybrids are not in Cellosaurus. `143B cell` / CVCL_2270 is recorded explicitly as the **nuclear parent only**.
- `characteristics[cybrid nuclear parent]` = "143B TK- rho0 (parental 143B, CVCL_2270)". The specific ρ° derivative is not named in the paper; 143B.206 (CVCL_U923) is plausible but unconfirmed.
- `characteristics[mitochondrial DNA donor]` = the heteroplasmic MT-CYB 4-bp deletion patient (de Coo 1999; Rana 2000). The value is the same on WT and Δ4-CYB rows.
- Sex female, age 13Y, `13-year-old stage` (HsapDv:0000107), `European ancestry`, femur and osteosarcoma describe the **nuclear** (143B) donor only. The earlier term `adolescent stage` (HsapDv:0000086) is obsolete in OLS4 and was replaced.
- **Disease:** `osteosarcoma` is kept on all rows. Both clones carry mtDNA from the same patient: the WT clone has the patient's wild-type mtDNA, the Δ4-CYB clone the mutant mtDNA.
  - A MELAS/parkinsonism term on the Δ4-CYB rows only would turn the genotype into a disease contrast that the donors do not support.
  - Putting it on both arms would give a mitochondrial disease to a clone with 100% wild-type mtDNA.
  - The donor's syndrome is therefore stated in the mtDNA-donor column, and the compared variable stays in `genotype`.

**Instruments:** the instrument model was read from the raw headers.
- The IP runs are a Q Exactive Plus: HCD, NCE 28, MS1 400–1600 m/z, Top10. PRIDE lists only "Q Exactive".
- The complexome runs are an LTQ Orbitrap XL: CID, NCE 35, ion-trap MS2.

**Complexome modifications:** read from the Mascot PSM tables. All are written as Variable:
- K8 and R10 labels
- Oxidation (M)
- Propionamide (C)
- Acetyl and Formyl. Their `PP=Protein N-term` position is **inferred** from the full PSM tables against UniProt human reviewed: 15,542 of 15,715 Acetyl PSMs and 5,005 of 5,062 Formyl PSMs map to protein position 1 or 2. The Mascot setting itself is not deposited.

**IP modifications:** only the SILAC labels are listed. No MaxQuant parameter file is deposited, so the other modifications are unknown.

**Cleavage agent:** Trypsin, as stated in the PRIDE protocol.

**Tolerances:** `not available` for both searches, because neither deposited its parameters. The complexome PSM delta masses all lie within about ±10 ppm, but that observation is not a stated parameter.

## Conflicts and limitations
- **Δ4-CYB clone number:** Results say #17.3E was used for all figures, but the Methods complexome paragraph says "#17.1". No clone number is written to the SDRF.
- **IP slice count:** PRIDE says the gel was cut into 11 slices, but 12 raw files per experiment are deposited. All 12 are annotated as fractions 1–12.
- **Lane assignments:** the lane → detergent and lane → orientation assignments are derived from the data and cross-checked against the Fig EV1 text. No deposited table names the lanes.
- **Shared mitochondria preparation:** it is not stated whether the DDM and digitonin lanes with the same orientation share one mitochondria preparation. Replicate numbers therefore make no claim of identity across detergents.
- **Slice 205 re-acquisition:** slice 205 of the digitonin rep1 lane was acquired as PRO4052A257, and there is no A129 in the deposit.
- **MaxQuant re-quantification:** the paper's MaxQuant re-quantification of the DDM complexome (Fig 2A) is not deposited. The SDRF follows the deposited PD/Mascot search.
- **Paper access:** the Europe PMC full-text XML returned HTTP 500, so the paper was read from the PMC Open Access S3 copy.
