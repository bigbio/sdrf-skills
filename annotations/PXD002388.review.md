## Findings

### F1. important (design / omission safety): the heavy reference is numbered as 54 independent biological replicates

- **Location:** `characteristics[biological replicate]` on the 54 rows where `characteristics[sample type]` is `reference`. These are the even rows 2–108, numbered 1..54.
- **Problem:** 54 distinct numbers in one factor-value group say that 54 independent biological samples of the standard exist. No source supports that:
  - The Data Brief says "Quantification of host cell proteins and comparison of the three different groups was accomplished with a **global SILAC standard**".
  - It also says heavy A549 cells "were … collected as spike-in standard for **all groups**".
  - PRIDE says the samples were spiked "with untreated A549 cells grown in medium containing … heavy … arginine and lysine".
  - The producer's own manifest limitation 5 says that whether aliquots share a culture "is not documented".
  - Under README.adoc:330, biological replicate means *independent biological samples*. One standard material split into 54 aliquots is technical splitting, not biological replication.
- **Campaign precedent:** the precedents for a single-cell-line heavy spike-in standard all use one reference source with biological replicate `1`:
  - `PXD003316` (SILAC-HUVEC heavy standard, br 1 on 60 rows)
  - `PXD005854` (heavy MEF spike-in, br 1 on 50 rows)
  - `PXD029216-silac` (heavy reference, br 1 on 222 rows)
  - `pooled` is used only for multi-line super-SILAC mixes (PXD009766, PXD002669, PXD012000, …). A549 alone is not a pool, so `pooled` would be wrong here too.
- **Fix:**
  - Set `characteristics[biological replicate]` = `1` on all 54 reference rows.
  - Optional, recommended: replace the 54 per-aliquot source names with one source name, for example `A549 heavy SILAC global standard (13C6-Arg/13C6-Lys)`, so that source identity matches the single standard. Earlier campaign reviews (PXD003316, PXD029216) raised the per-aliquot split as a minor point.
  - Keep a limitation saying that batching of the standard across the three assays is undocumented.
  - Re-run the build. Do not hand-edit the file (rule 17).

### F2. important (source evidence / technical): runs 105–108 are marked as never searched, but the deposit maps them to search output

- **Location:**
  - rows 105–108 (`120505_OV4_KRIS_HPI_1_1_5h_BR1.raw` and `120505_OV4_KRIS_HPI_8_1_5h_BR2.raw`)
  - the four `comment[modification parameters]` columns, set to `not applicable`
  - `comment[precursor mass tolerance]` and `comment[fragment mass tolerance]`, set to `not applicable`
  - the manifest claim "There is no evidence they went through the database search"
- **Problem:**
  - The deposit's own `README.txt` lists both RAW (file IDs 68 and 75) as sources of the SEARCH files `A549_control_sorted_SILAC_BR1_{peptides,proteins}.xls` (IDs 77 and 86) and `…BR2…` (IDs 78 and 87). So the manifest's "no evidence" statement is false.
  - What the producer saw is real, and I re-checked it. The control BR1 and BR2 tables export conditions `2_5_h…6_5_h` only, while BR3 and all six gfp tables include `1_5_h`.
  - The dataProcessingProtocol explains this as a downstream exclusion, not as an unsearched run: "Host proteins could only be analyzed between 2.5 h and 6.5 h p.i. as the 1.5 h value gave unstable results".
  - Elucidator exports conditions from an experiment that has already been searched. Leaving out a condition column is not evidence that the run skipped SEQUEST.
  - Saying "not searched" therefore claims more than the evidence supports, and it contradicts the submitter's mapping. It also splits the SDRF into two search groups that have no documented basis.
- **Fix:**
  - Give these 2 runs (4 rows) the same values as the other 52: the four modification columns (Carbamidomethyl C Fixed, Oxidation M, Label:13C(6) R and K Variable), and `not available` for both tolerances.
  - Produce the SDRF with a single build, or two builds with identical values.
  - Rewrite the manifest claim and limitation 3. Cite `README.txt` and say that the two runs' 1.5 h conditions are absent from the exported control BR1/BR2 tables, which agrees with the protocol's exclusion of 1.5 h host data.

### F3. minor (provenance): the manifest leaves out `README.txt` and calls stated mappings "inferred"

- **Location:** the manifest source `files` ("72 entries … listing is complete") and limitations 1, 2 and 3.
- **Problem:**

  - Limitation 2 says the mapping from infection batch to table BR is "not stated".

- **Fix:** add `README.txt` as a source. Cite it in the claims for source name, infection status, biological replicate and sampling time. Remove or reword limitations 1–3. The SDRF values do not change.

### F5. minor (ontology): two values carry no ontology term

- **Location:** `characteristics[culture medium]` is `Minimum Essential Medium` with no NCIT term. My own OLS search over ncit, efo, obi and bao returned nothing.
- **Assessment:** writing the stated name is acceptable. The validator only warns.
- **Optional:** `characteristics[infection status]` and `characteristics[infectious agent strain]` are custom free-text columns. This is acceptable under the spec's *Extending Templates* section and matches the PXD014577 precedent. The infectious agent (`Staphylococcus aureus`, NCBITaxon:1280) resolves. No change is required.

- **Note:** the GFP-positive and GFP-negative samples of one time point come from the same infected well or culture. The Data Brief sorts both from one infection. The SDRF does not encode this pairing. The spec has no required column for it, and source names keep the two populations separate. No change is required.
