1. **cell line, rows 1-24.** `HeLa Flp-In T-REx` is not in CLO/BTO/EFO, so `sdrf-tools check` exits 1.
   - No suitable term exists: EFO:0022682 and BTO:0006149 are the HEK293 Flp-In line, and EFO:0022683 is T-REx-HeLa, a different line.
   - **Fix:** keep the Cellosaurus name, as `cellline.md` instructs, and record the accepted warning.
2. **disease, rows 1-24.** A more specific term exists: MONDO:0000554 `endocervical adenocarcinoma` matches NCIt C27677.
   - The manifest's "nearest verified" claim is therefore inaccurate.
   - **Fix:** use MONDO:0000554, or correct the manifest wording.
3. **biological replicate, rows 7-12 vs 19-24.** Two distinct sources share the condition `PLEKHM1-GFP|not available` and both carry replicate 1.
   - **Fix:** number rows 19-24 as 2, or document why both stay 1.
4. **factor value[genetic modification].** There is no matching characteristics column, and the value describes a construct rather than a method.
   - The spec rule here is SHOULD-level, and the factor is documented in the manifest.
5. **sdrf annotation tool.** `manual curation` understates that the agent workflow produced the file.
   - **Fix:** consider `NT=sdrf-skills;VV=v…`.
6. **Recommended columns missing.** `developmental stage` and `passage number` are absent.
   - **Fix:** add them as `not available`, or use `adult` for developmental stage.
