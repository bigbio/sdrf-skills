## Focus points requested by the caller
1. **Heavy reference encoding.** The SDRF has one heavy source per mixture (`SILAC heavy spike-in standard (immortalized WT MEF) in <exp>`), with sample type `reference`, factor value `not applicable` and BR `1`. The PRIDE protocol supports this: "Each unlabeled lysate was mixed at a 1:1 protein ratio with 25µg of total cell lysate of the SILAC labeled cells ... immortalized WT MEF ... lysine 8 ... arginine 10". So does summary.txt: Multiplicity 2, Labels0 empty and Labels1 Arg10;Lys8 on all 50 files. Curated precedents use one source per mixture (PXD009766, PXD002669, PXD001364). For a *single*-line standard they give BR `1` (PXD003316, PXD005581, PXD008327, PXD029216). `pooled` is used for multi-line super-SILAC mixes. Here the standard is one culture of one line, so `1` is correct and `characteristics[pooled sample]` is not needed. Accepted.
2. **Genotype sourcing.** The light source names equal the MaxQuant Experiment names (WT1-5, K1-5; experimentalDesignTemplate.txt), and filename tokens match on all 50 rows. dataProcessingProtocol: "K2, K3, K4, K5 are referred to as Mut1..Mut4". K1 is treated as mutant because it is in the K series. The manifest states that genotype rests only on submitter names and could not be confirmed in the data. Nothing beyond the names is asserted. Accepted.
3. **Disease on mutant rows.** `leukoencephalopathy with vanishing white matter` (MONDO:0800448) on K rows, `normal` on WT and heavy rows. SAMPLE-GUIDELINES.adoc:225 says to use the human disease name for animal disease models. Accepted. See minor finding 2 on specificity.
4. **Precursor tolerance.** The reviewer re-extracted `allSpectra.HCD.FTMS.sil0.apar` and `.sil1.apar`: `peptide mass tolerance=4.5`, `Unit=ppm`, fragment `20 ppm`. sil1 carries the fixed `Arg10,Lys8`. 4.5 ppm (main search) is correct.
5. **WT5/K1 included.** Both are deposited and in the MaxQuant search. The PRIDE note excludes them only from the *published analysis*, so annotating the deposited runs is correct. See minor finding 1: the stated "low IDs" reason does not match summary.txt.
6. **Postbuild Lys-C column.** The PRIDE protocol says "LysC–Trypsin Mix followed by an overnight digestion with ... Trypsin". Under campaign rule 2 that calls for Trypsin plus a second `NT=Lys-C;AC=MS:1001309` column. The post-build step is deterministic and justified in validation.txt (rule 17). validate-sdrf, structure and reconcile all accept it. Accepted.

## Findings
- **minor (provenance):** The PRIDE note gives "low number of identifications" as the reason for omitting WT5/K1, but summary.txt does not support it. WT5 has the *most* MS/MS identified (66786), and K1 has 48786, in the range of the others (40303-66490). Keeping the rows is correct. Add a manifest limitation saying the stated reason cannot be reproduced from the deposited summary.
- **minor (ontology specificity):** MONDO:0957873 "leukoencephalopathy with vanishing white matter 5" (EIF2B5-related) would be more specific for an Eif2b5 model than MONDO:0800448. The general term is defensible.
