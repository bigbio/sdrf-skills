## Study

SLB-msSIM: a spectral-library-based multiplex segmented selected-ion-monitoring platform for
single-cell proteomics. Two sub-experiments, both label-free, both on cultured pancreatic cell lines:

1. **Cell-line heterogeneity** — single cells + bulk lysate from five lines: four pancreatic cancer
   lines (PANC-1, MIA PaCa-2, AsPC-1, HPAF) and one normal immortalized line (HPDE-c7).
2. **EMT time course** — PANC-1 single cells and bulk, TGFβ1-induced epithelial-mesenchymal
   transition (day 3) and its reversal (day 7, TGFβ1 withdrawn after day 3), plus untreated control.

## Key decisions and evidence

**Instrument conflict resolved to Q Exactive HF-X.** PRIDE `instruments` says "Q Exactive HF"; the
paper Methods say "Q Exactive HF-X". I range-read the first 256 KB of two RAW files
(`20230822_SingleCells_PANC_1.raw`, `20230822_Bulk_PANC_1.raw`), decoded UTF-16-LE, and both headers
read **`Q Exactive HF-X - Orbitrap MS`**. Raw data outranks both paper and PRIDE; PRIDE's "HF" is
wrong. `MS:1002877` (verified). This is the HF vs HF-X trap the brief warns about.

**Two acquisition modes in one file — and why `dia-acquisition` is dropped.** Bulk samples were run
DDA (top-25, 1.6 m/z isolation, HCD 28% NCE) to build the Skyline spectral library; single cells were
run by **msSIM** — "t-SIM acquisition mode … wide isolation window of 50 Th … multiplexing degree of
17 for the MS1 range m/z 400–1250 … accumulated in the C-trap … detected in the Orbitrap in one single
scan." msSIM is **MS1-only, no fragmentation**; identification is by MS1 isotope/RT matching against
the library. There is **no PRIDE term for SIM/msSIM** (the four children of `PRIDE:0000659` are DDA,
DIA, PRM, SRM). I mapped single-cell acquisition to **`Data-independent acquisition`** as the
least-wrong bucket: precursor sampling is systematic and abundance-independent (unlike DDA) and tiles
the whole range rather than targeting predefined transitions (unlike PRM/SRM). **This is the judgement
I most want the reviewer to attack** — msSIM is not fragmentation-based DIA, and calling it DIA could
mislead a reprocessing pipeline into expecting MS2 DIA data.
  - Declaring the `dia-acquisition` template is **impossible** for this file: `dia-acquisition/1.1.0`
    overrides `comment[proteomics data acquisition method]` with a validator that admits the single
    literal `Data-independent acquisition` at `error_level: error`. With 38 genuine DDA rows present,
    that override raises `has multiple unique values` and fails. Same dilemma as `PXD044986`. Dropping
    `dia-acquisition` keeps the DDA rows expressible; the cost is losing the `scan window lower/upper`,
    `isolation window width`, and `dia method` columns — so the defining msSIM parameters (**50 Th
    windows, m/z 400–1250, 17-plex, MS1 R=30 000, 250 ms IT**) live here in the report, not the SDRF.

**Five cell lines, each verified — three traps navigated.**

- **HPDE-c7 → HPDE6c7 (CVCL_0P38), not "HPDE" (CVCL_4376).** The paper writes "HPDE-c7". The bare
  "HPDE" in Cellosaurus (CVCL_4376) is a *finite* line; the paper's line is the HPV16-E6/E7
  **immortalized** clone `HPDE6c7` (syn. HPDE6-C7, H6c7), `CVCL_0P38`. It is a *transformed* line, so
  `cell-lines` still applies; and it is **normal** (no cancer) — `disease = normal (PATO:0000461)`,
  matching the paper's "normal HPDE cells".
- **HPAF resolved to HPAF-II (CVCL_0313), the highest-uncertainty call in the file.** The paper writes
  "HPAF", which *literally* matches a distinct Cellosaurus entry `HPAF` / `HPAF-I` (**CVCL_B284**,
  parental). I annotated **HPAF-II** instead because (a) `characteristics[cell line]` requires a
  CLO/BTO/EFO term and **only HPAF-II has one** (`CLO:0003816`; parental HPAF has no cell-line
  ontology term at all), and (b) HPAF-II (ATCC CRL-1997) is the line routinely distributed and called
  "HPAF". Both are PDAC from ascites, same 44Y male donor, so only the CVCL/CLO accessions differ.
  **If the authors truly used parental HPAF, swap to CVCL_B284 and drop the cell-line ontology term.**
- **`MIA PaCa-2` disease is the Cellosaurus-authoritative "undifferentiated pancreatic carcinoma"**
  (MONDO:0006478), not PDAC — the other four cancer lines are PDAC. Per-line Cellosaurus disease is
  used throughout rather than the paper's blanket "pancreatic cancer cell lines".
- **`organism part` follows Cellosaurus `derived-from-site`:** pancreas for PANC-1/MIA/HPDE;
  **ascitic fluid (UBERON:0007795)** for AsPC-1 and HPAF-II, which were established from metastatic
  ascites. The 3-vs-2 split is intentional and true, not an oversight.
- `characteristics[cell type] = epithelial cell (CL:0000066)` on all rows (all five are ductal
  epithelial / carcinoma-of-epithelial-origin). A more specific "pancreatic ductal cell" was
  considered but rejected — MIA PaCa-2 is undifferentiated and the ascites-derived lines are
  metastatic, so the generic term is the safe common denominator.

**Modifications — Carbamidomethyl is correct here.** `Carbamidomethyl` (UNIMOD:4, TA=C, **Fixed**) and
`Oxidation` (UNIMOD:35, TA=M, Variable). The protocol has an explicit alkylation step ("treated with
dithiothreitol (DTT) and iodoacetamide (IDA)"), and the library search set carbamidomethyl fixed /
oxidation variable (Comet). This is the one case where the brief's "never assert Carbamidomethyl
without an alkylation step" caveat is satisfied. PRIDE's obfuscated `modifications`
("monohydroxylated residue" = Oxidation, "iodoacetamide derivatized residue" = Carbamidomethyl)
agrees. No Acetyl (not in the search).

**Cleavage / label / dissociation.** Trypsin (MS:1001251) on all rows — MS-grade trypsin, overnight,
stated for both single-cell and bulk prep. Label free (MS:1002038). Dissociation: bulk = beam-type CID
/ HCD (MS:1000422); single-cell = `not applicable` (msSIM is MS1-only, nothing is fragmented).

**EMT captured in `characteristics[phenotype]` (free text).** No PATO/EFO term exists for the EMT
states, so phenotype carries free text: D3 = "TGF-beta1-induced epithelial-mesenchymal transition
(day 3)", D7 = "epithelial-mesenchymal transition reversal (day 7, TGF-beta1 withdrawn after day 3)",
EMT control = "untreated epithelial control", all 173 heterogeneity rows = `not applicable`. The
TGFβ1 dose (10 ng/mL) is documented here but not as an SDRF column: `characteristics[dose]` restricts
units to a list that excludes `ng/mL`, and `characteristics[compound]` wants a CHEBI/NCIT term that
does not cleanly exist for the TGFβ1 protein — forcing either would be an overclaim.

**Two factor columns for two experiments.** `factor value[cell line]` (populated on all 238 rows —
the heterogeneity axis) and `factor value[phenotype]` (the EMT axis; `not applicable` on the 173
heterogeneity rows). A single global factor cannot represent a dataset that is genuinely two
experiments; this is flagged as defensible rather than clearly canonical.

## Assumptions and open items for the reviewer

1. **Single-cell acquisition = `Data-independent acquisition`** for msSIM — attack this hardest (§ above).
2. **HPAF → HPAF-II / CVCL_0313** rather than parental HPAF / CVCL_B284 — an identity judgement forced
   by ontology coverage and common usage.
3. **`biological replicate` = within-group run index** (1..N per cell-line×phenotype group), with
   `technical replicate = 1` throughout. For single cells this is exact (each cell is one biological
   replicate). For the 4–5 bulk runs per line the paper does not say whether they are separate cultures
   or repeat injections; I treated each as a distinct biological replicate (non-collapsing assumption).
4. **`dia-acquisition` dropped**, so the msSIM window structure (50 Th, m/z 400–1250, 17-plex) is not
   machine-readable in the SDRF — a spec limitation (mixed DDA+DIA in one file cannot also declare
   `dia-acquisition`), documented here for completeness.
5. **HPDE `culture medium`** is free text ("Keratinocyte serum-free medium") — no clean NCIT term; the
   four cancer lines use DMEM (NCIT:C185409, verified).
