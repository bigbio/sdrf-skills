## Study
- **Title:** NSD2 is upregulated by high-risk HPV E6/E7 and modulates epithelial differentiation in HPV-associated Head and Neck Cancer (PRIDE)
- **Paper:** Ghiani L, …, Bonaldi T, Ansarin M, Chiocca S. *J Exp Clin Cancer Res* 2026;45:40, doi:10.1186/s13046-025-03631-0, PMID 41507992, PMC12882316 (CC BY).
- **Design:** histone PTM profiling by super-SILAC. Each sample's histones (light) are mixed 1:1 with heavy-labelled histones from a cancer-cell-line mix, used as an internal standard (heavy). The study reports L/H ratios of %RA from EpiProfile 2.0. There are three arms:
  1. FFPE oropharyngeal SCC tumours: HPV+ vs HPV− (Fig. 1A). **Blocked.**
  2. HNSCC cell lines: HPV+ vs HPV− (Fig. 1B).
  3. Primary human keratinocytes (HK) transduced with an empty vector or HPV16 E6/E7 (Fig. 1C).

## Key decisions and evidence (annotated parts)
1. **Multiplexing verified (batch rule 14).** EpiProfile ran in `histone with SILAC` mode and gives LightR/HeavyR per peptide. The heavy areas are near-constant across runs (a spike-in). The paper states "sample (light channel - L) … internal standard (heavy channel - H)".
2. **Heavy channel = reference (batch rule 18).**
   - Each heavy row is `sample type = reference`, `pooled sample = pooled`, `biological replicate = pooled` and `individual = pooled`.
   - There is one source per spiked aliquot (PXD009868/PXD008327 convention).
   - Cell lines use the breast-cancer cell-line mix; HK uses the "different cancer cells" mix.
   - The mix components are unnamed in the paper, so there is no `SN=` list.
3. **Cell-line identity.** The file token is the line number in the paper's list (VU = 93-VU-147T). The Supplementary Data sheet 2 column names match. Each line's record comes from Cellosaurus:
   - accession, name, sex and age;
   - organism part from "Derived from site", or from the disease-term site for six lines;
   - ancestry: European only where Cellosaurus says "Caucasian";
   - developmental stage: adult (ages 32–68Y).
   - DMEM is from the paper.
   - Shared donors (17A/17B, UPCI 090/152) are stated in the paper Methods.
4. **HK arm per file:** explicit in `Sample_list.txt` (`empty` / `E6/E7`). The treatment wording follows the paper: "transduced with the empty or HPV-16 E6/E7-encoding pLXSN vector". It is free text, as no NCIT/EFO term exists.
5. **Instrument from the raw files.** All 15 cell-line runs and 3 HK runs are `HF*`, and their headers read Q Exactive HF. The 3 `QEP220614` HK runs read Q Exactive Plus. The paper names only the Q Exactive Plus; PRIDE lists both. Raw data wins.
6. **Acquisition settings from the raw method text (all files):** DDA TopN 10, HCD at NCE 28, Orbitrap MS2, MS1 300–1350 m/z. Ref [44] agrees.
7. **Modifications.** No search-parameter file is deposited; EpiProfile is a targeted XIC tool. All modifications are Variable (batch rule 11 and ref44):
   - **HK (PRO-PIC):** Propionyl K (UNIMOD:58) and Phenylisocyanate on any N-term (UNIMOD:411). The HK EpiProfile log path shows `EpiProfile2.0_pr_pic`.
   - **Cell lines (D3-acetylation):** Acetyl:2H(3) K (UNIMOD:56).
   - **Both:** Methyl, Dimethyl, Trimethyl and Acetyl on K, plus Arg10 (Label:13C(6)15N(4), UNIMOD:267) on R.
   - **Arg10 heavy label:** from the lab's histone super-SILAC method (ref19 "Arg-10"; ref44 "Arg10 was selected as heavy label (multiplicity = 2) in … EpiProfile 2"). Lysine is light.
8. **Cleavage agent:** Trypsin (MS:1001251). The digest is Arg-C-like after Lys acylation (batch rule 2).
