## Findings (minor only)
1. **minor / provenance.** File: `07Mar2013_Turnover_cell515_lug2012_04h_A.RAW` (rows with assay `07Mar2013_Turnover_cell515_lug2012_04h_A`).
   - The "lug2012" token (Italian for July 2012) and the acquisition date (07 Mar, while the other cell515 04 h bands are 15 Mar) suggest this band-A run may come from an earlier harvest or culture.
   - It is annotated as technical replicate 1, fraction 1 of the same cell515 4 h source, which is consistent with PD merging it into the 04 h msf. The manifest does not mention the anomaly.
   - **Fix:** add a manifest limitation naming the file. Keep the encoding unless a source says it is a different culture.
2. **minor / source_evidence.** `characteristics[ancestry category]` for the five Nephro subjects.
   - Paper 4.1 says "Five Caucasian T1DM patients with DN", which is a statement about every DN subject, not a summary statistic.
   - `not available` is a defensible conservative choice: mapping "Caucasian" to a HANCESTRO term is itself an inference, and rule 18 is strict.
   - **Fix (optional):** keep the value, and note in the manifest that it was a deliberate choice.
3. **minor / design.** `comment[technical replicate]` = 2 for the exclusion-list runs.
   - These are complementary re-acquisitions with a different method (a static exclusion list), not pure repeats.
   - The encoding is the best SDRF can do, and it is documented in the manifest.
   - **Fix:** none required. Optionally add `comment[acquisition date]` or a method-name note to separate the passes.
4. **minor / provenance.** The `disease` claim depends on a data-derived identity match.
   - It is now reproduced three times: producer, round-1 reviewer, and this round with a different estimator and an exact sqlite3 extraction cross-check.

   - **Fix:** none required for approval.
