## Findings

### IMPORTANT 1: failed injections annotated as searched technical replicate 1
- **Location:** data rows 100, 105, 112, 117 (`Data081416_04.wiff`, `Data081416_15.wiff`) and rows 123–126 (`Data081416_05.wiff`, `Data081416_16.wiff`)
- **Problem:** `Data081416_04` and `_15` are failed injections: their `.wiff.scan` files are 44 bytes, so there are no spectra. Inside `T618I12.5minRep1combinedsearchfile.zip`, the `.group` lists exactly 12 runs: `_02, _05, _07, _09, _11, _13, _16, _19, _21, _23, _25, _27`. `_04` and `_15` were therefore never searched, yet they carry 0.05 Da / 0.1 Da and the search mods. That breaks "Unsearched runs: tolerances `not applicable`". The runs that were searched as fractions 2 and 7 (2b/7b) are technical replicate 2.
- **Effect on the design:** in `experimental_design.tsv`, fraction group 6 holds `_02/_04/_15` and fraction group 7 holds only `_05/_16`. One 12-fraction searched set is split in two.
- **Fix:**
  - Drop `_04`/`_15`, as the Blanks were dropped, and list them in manifest limitations as failed injections that were not searched.
  - Set `_05`/`_16` to technical replicate 1 (fractions 2 and 7).
  - Make the change in samples.tsv and rebuild, not as another post-build edit.
  - Update the manifest technical-replicate claim and limitation 4.
- **Not affected:** `Data030416_04` (Q741x 12.5 Rep 2, frac 3b) is a full run, and both 3 and 3b were in that combined search (13 runs). Keeping it as technical replicate 2 is defensible.

### Minor
- The manifest `zipcd` source says 155 .wiff.scan; there are 152 (3 Blanks have no .scan).
- `RPMI 1640` is inferred from "RPMI medium". The inference is disclosed, and NCIT has no generic RPMI term.
- Frac 3b re-injection is a single-fraction technical-replicate group (see above). Optionally note it in limitations.
