## Study
"Probing the HIV-Host Interactome in Infected Cells" — Luo Y, Jacobs EY, Greco TM, Mohammed KD, Tong T,
Keegan S, Binley JM, Cristea IM, Fenyö D, Rout MP, Chait BT, Muesing MA. *HIV–host interactome revealed
directly from infected cells.* Nat Microbiol 2016;1(7):16068 (PMID 27375898, PMC4928716).

Replication-competent HIV-1 R7/3 derivatives carrying a 3xFLAG insertion in *env* or in *vif* were used to
infect human T-cell lines (CEM-SS for Env, MT-2 for Vif). Anti-FLAG affinity isolates were compared with the
isolate from a companion culture infected with the untagged parental virus, the two cultures being
differentially SILAC-labelled and mixed 1:1 before lysis — the I-DIRT design, in which a specific interactor
sits at ratio ~1 in the tagged-virus channel and a nonspecific binder at ~0.5. Both label orientations were
run (forward and reverse). A third experiment measured the off-rate of Vif interactors: heavy Vif-3xFLAG
complexes on beads were exposed to a light untagged-infection lysate for 5, 15 or 60 min.

**Map source (campaign rule 6).** Explicit statements, not inference:
- Paper Methods, *Cell culture for I-DIRT and reverse I-DIRT analyses*: cells infected with "the untagged,
  parental control virus, R7/3" were grown in medium "supplemented with heavy isotope 13C6 labelled Lys and
  Arg", while "Cells infected with the 3xFLAG-tagged viruses were cultured in media supplemented with Lys and
  Arg with natural isotopic abundances"; "For reverse I-DIRT, the isotopic characteristics of the RPMI media
  were switched".
- Paper, *Quantification of viral–host interactomes via differential isotopic labeling*: "Light CEM (or MT-2)
  cells were then infected with the Env- or Vif-3xFLAG-tagged virus … the companion heavy CEM (or MT-2)
  culture was infected with the untagged WT virus."
- Paper, *Competitive isotopic exchange assay*: Vif complexes were affinity-purified "from the heavy labelled
  Vif-3xF infected cells", then "an equal volume of the light labelled lysate prepared from the untagged
  control infection was added to the beads" and split into 5, 15 and 60 min aliquots (Fig. 4c legend agrees).
- PRIDE data-processing protocol: "Isotope ratios were calculated as light/total for forward I-DIRT
  experiments or heavy/total for reverse I-DIRT, Vif isotope exchange, and Env SILAC experiments."
- The file letters F/R match the `.msf` names `*_For` / `*_Rev`.

## Encoding choices
- **Infection (campaign lesson).** Every channel is an HIV-1 infection, so `characteristics[infection status]`
  = `infected` on all rows, `characteristics[infectious agent]` = `Human immunodeficiency virus 1`
  (NCBITaxon:11676) and `characteristics[infectious agent strain]` names the virus actually used
  (`R7/3 Env-3xFLAG`, `R7/3 Vif-3xFLAG`, `R7/3 (untagged parental)`). There is no uninfected MS run in the
  deposit, so no uninfected arm is asserted. The search database the deposit names (and deposits) is the
  human SwissProt subset plus HIV-1 sequences — the pathogen proteome is included exactly as stated.
- **Bait and control.** `characteristics[bait]` (custom; precedent `annotations/PXD004666-apms.sdrf.tsv`)
  carries `HIV-1 Env-3xFLAG`, `HIV-1 Vif-3xFLAG`, or the explicit `no bait (…untagged HIV-1 R7/3)` for the
  comparator channel, which also gets `characteristics[sample type]` = `negative control` (PRIDE:0000905);
  the tagged channel is `study sample` (PRIDE:0001013).
- **Exchange time.** `characteristics[isotope exchange time]` / `factor value[isotope exchange time]` =
  `5 minute` / `15 minute` / `60 minute`, `not applicable` on the I-DIRT rows.
- **Factors.** `factor value[bait]`, `factor value[cell line]`, `factor value[isotope exchange time]`: what
  the study compares is tagged-bait vs untagged control, in two cell lines, over the exchange series.
- **Biological replicates (campaign rule 3, spec README.adoc:330).** Numbered 1..n inside each
  (cell line, bait, exchange time) group: the forward and reverse mixtures are separate infections of
  separately labelled cultures, so each gives replicate 1 and 2. The three exchange time points are aliquots
  of one heavy purification and one light lysate, so each time-point group holds one culture, numbered 1 —
  the time point is the factor, not a replicate. The manifest states that these aliquots come from one
  physical preparation.
- **Fractions.** The gel-lane fractions are a technical division of one injection series of one sample, so
  they are `comment[fraction identifier]` 1..n with `comment[fractionation method]` SDS PAGE
  (PRIDE:0000568) — not separate samples (campaign rule 16 does not apply: the fractions are not compared).
