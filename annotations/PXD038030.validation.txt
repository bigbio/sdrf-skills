Remaining warnings, each deliberate:
- 'untreated' (characteristics[treatment], POT rows): campaign rule 4 prescribes 'untreated' for
  controls; it is not an NCIT/EFO label.
- 'estrogen deprivation' (characteristics[treatment], AI/-E2 rows): the paper's own term for culture in
  phenol red-free DMEM + charcoal-stripped FCS (mimics aromatase-inhibitor therapy, no drug added).
  OLS (NCIT, EFO; fuzzy search across ontologies) has no term for it; 'Aromatase Inhibitor' (NCIT) would
  falsely assert a drug.
- 'super-SILAC mix' (characteristics[cell line], heavy reference rows): the heavy channel is a
  spiked-in super-SILAC histone standard whose component cell lines are not named by the paper or the
  deposit. The cell-lines template forbids not available / not applicable here (allow_not_available /
  allow_not_applicable = false), and TERMS.tsv marks cell line allow_pooled = false. CORRECTION (review
  F3): parse_sdrf and sdrf-tools structure do NOT enforce allow_pooled, so 'pooled' would also pass
  validation; the earlier wording that the column "forbids pooled" was true only of TERMS.tsv, not of
  the validators. 'super-SILAC mix' is kept (see round 3).
