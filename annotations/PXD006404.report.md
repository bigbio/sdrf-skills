# PXD006404 — Proteomics analysis of ALK signaling in neuroblastoma cells

**Status: REVIEW_PENDING_EXTERNAL** (round 2). The round-1 external review (`PXD006404.review.md`) failed on one important finding. Deamidation (NQ) had been searched but was not annotated. This version fixes it; a new review is needed.

**PARTIAL SCOPE.** Only the 36 triple-SILAC raws (ALK interactome and ALK phosphoproteome) are annotated. The 140 label-free raws are out of scope for this SILAC campaign and excluded: 112 `PeptidePulldown` raws (pTyr-peptide interactome) and 28 `NB1proteome` raws (label-free proteome); MaxQuant summary.txt gives Multiplicity 1 for all of them. They are listed in the manifest limitations and in `annotations/BLOCKED.md` (excluded runs, not a blocker).

Publication: Emdal KB et al., *Sci Signal* 2018;11(557):eaap9752 (PMID 30459283, PMC7618099).

## The file

- `PXD006404.sdrf.tsv`: 108 rows × 51 columns. sha256 `e90c8beae7383185df1a8bc68ff06ce73106e4e0a2a024f2ab6d5d9c5f2c48cf`. Round 1 was `1a661eec…`, 50 columns.
- Templates: ms-proteomics v1.2.0, human v1.1.0 and cell-lines v1.1.0. The samples are a human cancer cell line, with no patients, so clinical-metadata is not used. sdrf version v1.1.0.
- Build: `scripts/make_tables.py` writes the tables. `sdrf-tools build` runs three times, once each for the IP arm, phospho series 1 and phospho series 2; the headers are identical. ## Design

The cell line is NB1 (Cellosaurus CVCL_1440, label EFO "NB-1"): human neuroblastoma, male, 2Y3M, Japanese, from a metastatic cervical lymph node. Cells were grown in SILAC RPMI with Lys0/Arg0 (light), Lys4/Arg6 (medium) or Lys8/Arg10 (heavy), then treated for 30 minutes.

| Experiment | SILAC light | SILAC medium | SILAC heavy |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | crizotinib 500 nM | TAE684 100 nM | DMSO 0.1 % |
| E2 | TAE684 100 nM | LDK378 (ceritinib) 250 nM | DMSO 0.1 % |
| E3 | LDK378 250 nM | crizotinib 500 nM | DMSO 0.1 % |

Source of the channel map: the PRIDE projectDescription ("Experiment 1: Light: 500 nM crizotinib, Medium: 100 nM TAE684, Heavy: 0.1% DMSO …"). It is matched to the MaxQuant experiments ALK-IP-E1..E3 (raw suffixes A/B/C) and ALK-Phospho-E1..E3 (MIX1/2/3) by their numbers.

What the deposited ratios confirm: heavy is the DMSO vehicle, in both arms.

- **Phospho arm:** in all three experiments, every quantified ALK pY site (Y1078, Y1092, Y1096, Y1507, Y1584, Y1604) and IRS2 pY632 is lower in both light and medium than in heavy.
- **IP arm:** GRB2, PTPN11, IRS2 and SHC1 are enriched in heavy over light and medium (log2 about 2.2–3.9) in E1–E3, while the ALK bait stays flat.
- **Inhibitor order:** the IRS2 IP medium/light ratios give one consistent order, TAE684 < crizotinib < LDK378. This fits the stated map but does not prove it. The phosphosite ratios cannot tell the inhibitors apart, because the correlations follow the label rather than the drug. So light vs medium identity rests mainly on the PRIDE statement (see limitations).

- **ALK interactome:** rows 1–54. 3 experiments × 3 channels. Each experiment is one SDS-PAGE lane cut into 6 slices (`ALK_IP_1..5<X>`, `ALK_IP_IGG_<X>`). In MaxQuant these are fractions 201–206 of a single experiment, so IGG is the sixth slice, not an IgG control IP. Instrument: Q Exactive.
- **ALK phosphoproteome:** rows 55–108. 3 experiments × 3 channels × 3 enrichment fractions (TiO2 1st = 1, TiO2 2nd = 2, pTyr IP = 3), injected twice:
  - Technical replicate 1 is the 20140311 QE3 "50cm" series, on a Q Exactive (raw header).
  - Technical replicate 2 is the 20140812 QE5 series, on a Q Exactive Plus (raw header).
- **Factor:** `factor value[treatment]`, with values Crizotinib, ALK Inhibitor TAE684, Ceritinib and Dimethyl Sulfoxide (NCIT labels). The DMSO control is written as `Dimethyl Sulfoxide`, not rule 4's `untreated`, because the cells received 0.1 % DMSO vehicle. This deviation is deliberate.
- **Biological replicates** are numbered within each treatment group: each inhibitor has 1–2 and DMSO has 1–3, one number per experiment's culture. The same numbering is used in both arms.
- **Groups:** 18 sources (9 per arm). Per arm: crizotinib 2, TAE684 2, ceritinib 2, DMSO 3.

## Search and technical values (from the deposited MaxQuant 1.5.3.33 txt)

- **Fixed modification:** Carbamidomethyl (C).
- **Variable modifications:** Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term), Gln->pyro-Glu and Phospho (STY), for both arms. Also Deamidated (N,Q; UNIMOD:7), which summary.txt lists under `Multi modifications` for all 36 SILAC raws. It was added in round 2; `sdrf-tools search-params` ignores that column.
- **SILAC labels:** Lys4 (UNIMOD:481), Arg6 (188), Lys8 (259) and Arg10 (267), all with MT=Variable.
- **Fragment tolerance:** 20 ppm (FTMS). Precursor tolerance is `not available`: no mqpar.xml was deposited.
- **Digestion:**
  - IP arm: in-gel trypsin.
  - Phospho arm: Lys-C followed by trypsin, with DTT reduction and chloroacetamide (CAA) alkylation.
  - MaxQuant used the Trypsin/P rule.
- **Acquisition:** DDA, HCD fragmentation, Orbitrap MS2.

## Open items / limitations

- The ratios do not separate the light vs medium inhibitors, so their identity rests on the PRIDE statement. Fig. S1C was not retrieved.
- The instruments conflict. The paper says Q Exactive Plus for both arms, but the raw headers show Q Exactive for 27 of the 36 raws. The annotation follows the raw headers.
- It is not stated whether the IP and phospho arms used the same cultures. They are annotated as separate sources.
- Precursor tolerance is `not available`.
- Values marked `not available`:
  - cell type, individual;
  - enrichment process (IP rows);
  - reduction and alkylation reagent (IP rows);
  - precursor mass tolerance.
- Developmental stage `child stage (1-4 yo)` (HsapDv:0000265) gives a validator warning, because the term is not in EFO.
- Inhibitor concentrations and the 30-minute duration are recorded only in the manifest.
- The 140 label-free raws are excluded. They could be annotated as a separate label-free SDRF, since the bait/lysate map is documented in PRIDE.
