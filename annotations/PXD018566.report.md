**Where the channel map comes from:**
- Fig. 1A shows the media assignment: Light = LMP2A KO, Medium = EBV WT, Heavy = LMP2A KO with 5' α-BCR. This covers the proteome, the GPome and the pYome.
- The Methods "Global Proteome Analysis" section states the same assignment.
- Fig. 1D gives the second pYome its EBV WT + 5' BCR heavy channel.

**Checks against the deposited ratio CSVs (`Data_upload_for_LMP2A_manuscript.zip`):**
- In the GPome, the H/L ratios show the BCR signature: ERK T185/Y187 is 7–13×.
- The M/L ratios show RB1 hypophosphorylation and lower RB1 protein, as the paper reports for LMP2A.
- The GPome Spearman correlation between M/L and H/L is 0.37, against r=0.38 in Fig. 1B.
- The pYome-B Spearman is 0.90, against r=0.94 in Fig. 1D. The pYome-A Spearman is 0.20.
- The points labelled in Fig. 1D match pYome-B: MAPK9 Y185 has log2 M/L −0.51 and H/L +0.63; CD79A Y210 has M/L −1.72 and H/L −1.01.

**Which pYome file set is which:** no source states this. The assignment rests on acquisition dates from the raw headers and file names, compared with the dates in the CSV file names. pYome-B was acquired in Feb 2018, after the Oct 2017 CSV and before the Mar 2018 `bcr_stim` CSV.

**Technical replicates:** in each pYome set the replicate columns pair as r1/r4, r2/r5 and r3/r6. These pairs have the highest correlation (0.96–0.97) and almost identical site counts. So the rep1/rep2 files are two injections of one sample (technical replicates 1 and 2).

**Biological replicates:** they are numbered within each genotype × treatment group. The numbering runs across the sub-experiments because each was prepared on a different date:
- KO unstimulated and WT unstimulated: proteome 1–5, GPome 6–9, pYome-A 10–12, pYome-B 13–15.
- KO + BCR: 1–12, in the same order.
- WT + BCR: 1–3.

**Factor values:** genotype (LMP2A wild type / LMP2A knockout) and treatment ("BCR stimulation with anti-human Ig F(ab')2, 5 min" / untreated).

**Infection:** encoded as `characteristics[infectious agent]` = human gammaherpesvirus 4 (NCBITaxon:10376) and `characteristics[infectious agent strain]` = EBV wild type / EBV LMP2A-knockout. The search used a human-only UniProt database. `characteristics[disease]` is `normal`: the paper says the B cells came from healthy donors. `characteristics[organism part]` is `blood` (UBERON:0000178). Source: the LCLs "were generated by infecting healthy human peripheral blood B cells".

**Search parameters (paper Methods, MaxQuant 1.5.5.1):**
- Tolerances are 6 ppm for precursors and 20 ppm for fragments.
- Carbamidomethyl is fixed. Oxidation, protein N-terminal acetylation and the SILAC labels are variable.
- Phospho (STY) is set only in the GPome and pYome rows. The proteome rows have it as `not applicable`, because the proteome search excluded it.

**Instrument settings (raw headers):** Q Exactive HF, HCD at NCE 28, Orbitrap MS2, MS1 scan range 350–1600 m/z.

## Open items / limitations
- **Blocked mouse arm (36 raws):**
  - `YLJI_310717_040817_FISH_GP9_TiO2_Phos_frac{5..17,54}{a,b}.raw` (28 files)
  - `J_Corso_291116_LMP2A_{6,7}_pY_{1,2}.raw` and `J_Corso_161317_pY_MYC_{8,9}_1_{1,2}.raw` (8 files)
  
  No source states their channel map. They are in the manifest and in `BLOCKED.md`, with a ratio-based hint for whoever unblocks them.
- **Stimulation time conflict:** some Methods sections and the PRIDE protocol say 3 h of BCR stimulation for the proteome and pYome. The cell-culture Methods section ("5 min for all proteome analyses") and Fig. 1 say 5 min, which is what the SDRF encodes. The data also fit 5 min: there is no protein-level induction in the proteome H/L ratios.
- The pYome samples 195/198/201 and 773/776/779 are treated as independent replicates. No source states whether they came from independent cultures.
- Donor details of the LCLs are `not available`: age, sex, ancestry and individual. The cell lines have no Cellosaurus entry.
- **Donor confounding:** the EBV-WT and LMP2A-KO LCLs almost certainly come from different donors, so genotype is confounded with donor.
  - HLA allele groups in the proteome have M/L of 0.02–0.04 or 26–53×.
  - H/L stays around 1.
  - The SDRF cannot encode the two donors because no donor IDs are published.
- **Proteome replicates:** all five proteome replicates share one sample ID and prep date in the file names (`TO_K_Fish_220517_*_GPome_192_L2_rep1-5`). The paper calls them "five biological replicates". They are numbered 1–5, as the paper says.
- The pYome reduction and alkylation reagents are `not available`; the samples were prepared with the CST kit.
- `techsdrf` is unavailable. Only 5 raw headers were read.
