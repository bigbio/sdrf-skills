### Important (must fix before approval)
1. **`comment[precursor mass tolerance]` = 7 ppm is contradicted by the deposited search output.**
   - In MaxQuant `evidence.txt`, the calibrated `Mass Error [ppm]` never exceeds 4.5 ppm in any run. Per-run maxima: 4.358, 4.425, 4.477, 4.472, 4.479 ppm, across 98,187 rows. That hard cap is the MaxQuant 1.5.5.1 default main-search tolerance of 4.5 ppm.
   - The paper's 7 ppm most likely copies the "MS/MS deisotoping tolerance 7 ppm" in parameters.txt.
   - The manifest says the value is "not recorded" and does not mention the conflict.
   - **Fix:** set the column to `4.5 ppm`, cite the `mq` evidence.txt cap in the claim, and record the conflict with the paper's 7 ppm in the manifest limitations and validation.txt.

### Minor
- `characteristics[sample type]` is missing. Upstream base 1.1.0 marks it recommended, with default `study sample`. Adding it is optional.
- `organism part` = lymph node (the metastasis site in Cellosaurus) is defensible. Repo precedent is mixed, though, and `sdrf-tools cellline lookup` gives Pancreas. Consider adding `characteristics[sampling site]` = lymph node (the PXD027268 / PXD004941 pattern).
- The genetic-modification value says "(CRISPR-Cas9 knockout)", while the deposit and the Methods say "knockdown"/"depletion" for this experiment. This is acceptable because the Methods also call lentiCRISPRv2 use "KOs". Optional wording change.
