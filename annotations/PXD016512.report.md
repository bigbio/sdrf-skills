## Search coverage and excluded runs
For every bait and direction, the raw count equals the MaxQuant experiment count, with one exception: **PRKCA REV has 10 raws against 8 `KCAREV` experiments.**
- `LDY1357-S24-A-PRKCA-R-4_010.raw` is excluded. It is a 39,754-byte stub (header only, no scans), the aborted first acquisition of slot S24 / vial BE8. The deposited `R-4` is the complete acquisition.
- `ALI1442-S18-A-KCA-R.raw` **was searched** and is annotated (round 1 wrongly excluded it). KCAREV1 has a summed intensity of 4.68e11, the Q Exactive Plus scale, like KCAFOR1 (4.65e11, ALI1442 KCA-F) and the single-QE experiments of PRKCE/PRKCG (3.4–4.3e11). KCAREV2–8 are ≤2.2e10.
- That leaves 8 Velos raws against 7 Velos experiments. One Velos PRKCA REV run is unaccounted for (unsearched, or merged), and which one cannot be determined without mqpar.xml. All 8 are annotated with the search parameters.

Other baits are fine:
- YWHAE (14-3-3 / 1433E names) matches with 8/8.
- SRC is in two proteinGroups files (6 and 7 experiments); the 7 raws match the larger.
- PDPK1 has experiments here but no raws in this deposit, so it is out of scope.
