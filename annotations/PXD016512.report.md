## Relation to PXD016505

- it has the same PRIDE title (with a leading ": "), the same protocol text and the same two design files;
- its baits are different: CRK, PAK1, PRKAR1A, PRKCA/B/E/G/I/Z, PRKD1, RAB5A, RALB, RASA1, RIN1, SH2D3C, SH3GL3, SHC1, SRC, VAV1 and YWHAE, plus RAC1;

## Search coverage and excluded runs
For every bait and direction, the raw count equals the MaxQuant experiment count, with one exception: **PRKCA REV has 10 raws against 8 `KCAREV` experiments.** Two runs are excluded for it:
- `LDY1357-S24-A-PRKCA-R-4_010.raw`: its header name is `…R-4.raw`, so it is the superseded first acquisition of vial BE8. The deposited `R-4` is the re-acquisition.
- `ALI1442-S18-A-KCA-R.raw`: this is an inference. It is the only single-injection pool of the arm, and the KCAREV correlation matrix has four pair blocks and no singleton.

If the second exclusion is wrong, one searched run is missing.

Other baits are fine:
- YWHAE (14-3-3 / 1433E names) matches with 8/8.
- SRC is in two proteinGroups files (6 and 7 experiments); the 7 raws match the larger.
- PDPK1 has experiments here but no raws in this deposit, so it is out of scope.
