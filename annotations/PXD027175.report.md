## Study

- **Full text:** NCBI efetch (numeric id 9110368, title checked). The Europe PMC fullTextXML returned HTTP 500.

- **Design:** MDA-MB-468 SILAC cells (light Lys0/Arg0, heavy Lys8/Arg10) with 50 nM methotrexate (MTX) against an untreated control.
- **Sample prep:** the two channels were mixed 1:1 (50 µg + 50 µg) and digested with Lys-C for 4 h, then trypsin overnight.

## Key decisions and evidence
1. **SILAC channel → condition (SCR arm).**
   - **Stated orientation:** the PRIDE protocol and the paper Methods both say "Cells in light channel were treated with 50 nM MTX … heavy channel was used as control".
   - **Swap and control series:** `TreatSwap` (from the raw names) is the label swap. `Ctrl` is an untreated light/heavy mix.
   - **Data check (rule 6), median-centred log2 H/L in `marker_ratios.txt`.** I used the MTX targets the paper names, DHFR, TYMS and TK1, plus SOD2 and RRM2:
     - In **all 16** SCR normal experiments the light channel is up: DHFR −0.4 to −3.1, TYMS 0 to −1.5, TK1 −0.7 to −2.0.
     - In **all 8** SCR TreatSwap experiments the heavy channel is up: DHFR +0.9 to +2.8, TYMS +0.9 to +1.2.
     - Both SCR Ctrl experiments sit near 0: DHFR +0.17/+0.38, TYMS +0.11/+0.06.
   - So the stated map and the data agree for this arm.
2. **Time points.** The values 6, 24, 48 and 72 h come from the raw and MaxQuant names, and the Fig. S2F legend says "different MTX treatment durations". The Methods mention only 24 h and 72 h. I used the deposited names.
3. **Replicates.**
   - The normal-series name `<t>-<b>-<tr>` gives biological replicate `b` (1 or 2) and technical replicate `tr`. The protocol says "biological duplicates … each sample analyzed twice as technical replicates".
   - TreatSwap `-1/-2` and Ctrl `-1/-2` are technical injections of one mixture. Within each pair the per-experiment median raw H/L, which is the mixing ratio, is identical: −0.81/−0.81, −1.30/−1.32, −0.52/−0.53. This is *inferred*.
   - The TreatSwap MTX culture is biological replicate 3 of its time point.
4. **Untreated controls.**
   - Sampling time is `not available`. The record does not say whether each control was harvested alongside its MTX time point.
   - I wrote one source per mixture channel (14 in total) and numbered them 1-14 within the single untreated group (rule 3).
5. **Factor values.** `treatment` (methotrexate / untreated) and `sampling time` are the two variables the study compares (the MTX time course). Cell line is constant here, so it is not a factor.
6. **Cleavage agents.** Trypsin, plus a second `comment[cleavage agent details]` = Lys-C (rule 2). `build` cannot emit a second value, so the column is inserted by the post-build script, which asserts its preconditions. The same was done for PXD008327.
7. **Technical values from the RAW headers** (all 54 methods are identical):
   - Q Exactive HF.
   - Top-12 DDA.
   - **NCE 25** (the paper says 28; the RAW wins).
   - MS1 375-1500 m/z at 60,000 resolution (the paper says 70,000).
   - Isolation window 1.2 m/z (the paper says 1.4).
8. **Modifications and tolerances.** No search parameter file was deposited, so these come from the paper and PRIDE Methods:
   - Fixed: Carbamidomethyl C.
   - Variable: Oxidation M, Acetyl Protein N-term, Deamidated N,Q, and the SILAC labels Lys8 (UNIMOD:259) and Arg10 (UNIMOD:267), with `MT=Variable` per rule 11.
   - proteinGroups.txt has `Oxidation (M)` and `Deamidation (NQ)` site columns, which is consistent.
   - Precursor tolerance 10 ppm, fragment tolerance 20 ppm. All 26 runs are in the search.
9. **Sample characteristics from Cellosaurus CVCL_0419:**
   - female, 51Y → developmental stage adult;
   - ancestry African American (HANCESTRO:0568);
   - disease breast adenocarcinoma (MONDO:0004988);
   - organism part breast; sampling site pleural effusion (UBERON:0000175).

## Blocked arm (28 RAW): MTHFD2(?) KO series
- **Files:** QHF_00014373-14375, 14378-14383, 14393, 14394, 14397, 14398, 14403-14406, 14411-14414, 14503-14506 and 14508-14510.
- **Not described anywhere:** neither the paper nor PRIDE describes a KO/SCR SILAC experiment or names the knocked-out gene. The paper has both an MTHFD2 CRISPR KO with a scramble gRNA control and an MTHFD1L KO.
- **The stated map fails the data check:**
  - In every `KO_<t>` experiment the MTX signature is in **heavy**: DHFR +0.1 to +2.6, TYMS +0.2 to +1.1, SOD2 +0.4 to +1.8.
  - In every `KO_TreatSwap` it is in **light**.
  - `KO_Ctrl` is not flat: TK1 −1.0 to −1.8.
- **The channel genotype is unclear:** MTHFD2 is lower in heavy in 22 of 25 quantified KO experiments, whatever the treatment.
- **Not done:** flipping the orientation to fit the data, or inferring the genotype. Both would assert identities that no source states (rules 6-8).
- **Unblocked by:** author confirmation of the KO arm's channel orientation, the genotype of each channel and the gene knocked out, and which of QHF_00014380 or QHF_00014503 was searched as `KO_48h-1-2`.

## Stated vs inferred
- **Stated:** the cell line, the MTX dose, the light-MTX/heavy-control orientation, the digestion, reduction and alkylation, the modifications and the tolerances.
- **From the RAW headers or search output:**
  - the run ↔ sample name map;
  - the time points;
  - the replicate digits;
  - the instrument and method values.
- **Inferred:**
  - that the TreatSwap and Ctrl pairs are technical replicates (identical mixing ratio);
  - that the 14 untreated sources are independent cultures;
  - the Ctrl untreated status, from the name plus flat marker ratios.

## Judgement calls for the reviewer to attack
1. **Excluding the whole KO arm (28 files).** Its orientation is contradicted by the data rather than missing. The alternative is to annotate it with the data-derived orientation and an unknown genotype. Rules 6-8 argue against that.
2. **Untreated controls:** sampling time `not available` with 14 separate biological replicates. The alternative is to give each control its paired time point, or to treat the heavy controls as one shared culture.
3. **Technical-replicate status of TreatSwap/Ctrl `-1/-2`.** It is inferred from identical mixing ratios, not stated. If these were independent mixtures, the TreatSwap MTX source would be two biological replicates (3 and 4).

Other open items are the culture medium (`not available` because no ontology term exists) and that no genotype column was written for the "SCR" cells. All limitations are listed in the manifest.
