## Study
Mulder C, Prust N, van Doorn S, Reinecke M, Kuster B, van Bergen en Henegouwen P, Lemeer S. *Adaptive Resistance to EGFR-Targeted Therapy by Calcium Signaling in NSCLC Cells.* Mol Cancer Res 2018;16(11):1773–1784, doi:10.1158/1541-7786.MCR-18-0212, PMID 29967110.
- The paper is not in PMC. The publisher-version PDF was read from the Utrecht University repository.
- The Supplementary Methods and Tables are at aacrjournals.org, which is behind a Cloudflare bot check (not bypassed).
- The PhD thesis of the first author (Utrecht 2019, chapter 5) reproduces the Supplementary Figure legends.

PC9 cells (EGFR ΔE746–A750) were treated with 10 nM afatinib for 1, 3, 5 and 7 days. Full proteome: SILAC Arg6/Lys4, high-pH fractionation into 5 fractions, Q Exactive HF, MaxQuant 1.5.6.0.

## SILAC verification (rule 14)
`SEARCHProteome.zip` (read by HTTP range) contains `summary.txt` with Multiplicity 2 and Labels1 `Arg6;Lys4`. Its `experimentalDesignTemplate.txt` lists exactly the 60 SILAC raws, grouped into 12 experiments (24h/3D/5D/7D × R1–R3, 5 fractions each).

## Design / channel map
| source pattern | label | treatment | sampling time | dose | biol. rep |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PC-9_afatinib_{24h,3D,5D,7D}_R{1-3} (12) | SILAC heavy | afatinib | 24 hour / 3 day / 5 day / 7 day | 10 nM | 1–3 per time point |
| PC-9_DMSO_in_mix_{time}_R{1-3} (12) | SILAC light | untreated (DMSO vehicle) | not available | not applicable | 1–12 |

The factor values are treatment and sampling time.

Evidence for heavy = afatinib, light = DMSO (rule 6):
1. **Design.** The thesis Supp Fig S2A legend says the full-proteome PCA "showed separation of ratios of time points versus DMSO". So each mixture is one afatinib time point against DMSO.
2. **Label description.** Only the heavy label (Arg6/Lys4) is described.
3. **Check against the deposited data.** The deposited log2 Ratio H/L agrees with the paper's reported directions at 3D–7D:
   - CAPN2 is down (−0.22 to −0.36 at 3D/5D).
   - ATP2C1, MARCKS, MARCKSL1 and CNN2 are up (+0.3 to +1.2).
   - The cell-cycle signature expected under EGFR inhibition is down: MKI67 −0.7 to −1.4, and MCM2/3/7, PCNA and CCNB1 are all negative.
4. **No label swap.** All 12 experiments correlate positively (Pearson 0.16–0.80, 6,339 protein groups).

**Caveat:** no sentence explicitly says "heavy = treated". The map is a data-checked inference from the explicit treated-vs-DMSO design, recorded as a limitation.

## Technical values
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive HF (PRIDE and raw header).
- **Acquisition:** DDA Top12, HCD at 27 NCE, Orbitrap MS2, MS1 scan range 375–1600 m/z (raw method).
- **Cleavage:** trypsin + Lys-C, from the PRIDE statement "For full-proteome analysis ... Trypsin/LysC". The paper mentions only trypsin. MaxQuant used the Trypsin/P rule.
- **Fractionation:** high-pH reversed-phase.
- **Reduction / alkylation:** TCEP / chloroacetamide.
- **Modifications:**
  - Carbamidomethyl (C), fixed;
  - Oxidation (M), variable;
  - Label:13C(6) R (UNIMOD:188);
  - Label:2H(4) K (UNIMOD:481), `MT=Variable`.
- **Tolerances:** fragment 20 ppm (FTMS). Precursor is `not available` because it is not recorded in the MaxQuant txt files and no mqpar.xml was deposited.
