## Design and channel maps

### PMP (`-pmp`): HaCaT, plasma membrane profiling at 6 hpi, MOI 10
**Channel map source.** The docx (Experiment 4) gives "Light Mock, Medium HSV-1, Heavy HSV-1 ΔUL56". The MaxQuant raw names carry `SILAC_LMH_Mock_WT_dUL56`.

**Check against the data:**
- Light is mock: viral protein intensity is about 10× lower in light than in medium or heavy (MaxQuant experiments 4 and 5).
- Medium is WT and heavy is ΔUL56. The paper's Fig. 6 proteins are lost from the surface with WT and rescued by ΔUL56:

  | protein | M/L | H/M |
  |---|---|---|
  | TLR2 | 0.37 | 2.0 |
  | IL18R1 | 0.19 | 3.7 |
  | DUOX1 | 0.32–0.41 | 2.7–3.7 |

- Controls behave as expected: NECTIN-1 is down in both infections; EGFR is about 1.

**Two mixtures.** The deposited raws were renamed. Original names from the raw headers and the MaxQuant evidence show:
- `SILAC_PMP_UL56_WT_F1..F6` = `MPW_CD_HSV_pmp_{10,25,40,60,90,150}_080218`, the tip-SCX KCl steps (8 Feb 2018). These are MaxQuant experiment 5, "Exp2_2018_Fraction1-6".
- `SILAC_PMP_UL56_WT_Unfrac` = `MPW_CD_HSV_PMP_130817` (13 Aug 2017). It uses a 120K-MS1, 180-min method. In the evidence it matches only "Exp1_2018_singleshot" (MaxQuant experiment 4): RT up to 192 min and median resolution 78K, against about 39K for the experiment-5 runs.

So the Unfrac file is a separate mixture (biological replicate 1). The F1–F6 fractions are mixture 2 (biological replicate 2). One more raw in the MaxQuant search, "Exp2_2018_singleshot", was not deposited.

| source | label | biol. rep | files |
|---|---|---|---|
| HaCaT_PMP_exp1_mock / _HSV1-WT / _HSV1-dUL56 | light / medium / heavy | 1 | Unfrac (fraction 1) |
| HaCaT_PMP_exp2_mock / _HSV1-WT / _HSV1-dUL56 | light / medium / heavy | 2 | F1..F6 (fractions 1–6, SCX) |

The pathogen is HSV-1 strain KOS (NCBITaxon:10306), from the paper: all viruses were reconstituted from the KOS BAC. ΔUL56 has stop codons after residue 21. The searched database was human plus HSV-1 KOS, as the deposit states.

### IP (`-ip`): HEK293T GFP-Trap IP, 3 biological replicates
**Channel map source.** The docx (Experiment 2) gives "Light GFP, Medium pUL56(1-207)-GFP, Heavy GFP-pUL56(1-234)". The raws `SILAC_IP_Exp1..3` are the original `090316_Exp1..3` (MaxQuant experiments 1–3).

**Check against the data.** In replicates 1/2/3, the light GFP control is depleted relative to both baits:

| protein | M/L | H/L |
|---|---|---|
| pUL56 | 12.9 / 1.9 / 5.5 | 12.3 / 9.6 / 9.6 |
| GOPC | 5.5 / 2.8 / 5.4 | 9.1 / 6.9 / 9.2 |
| ITCH | 8.6 / 7.8 / 12.4 | — |

The data cannot tell the two baits apart; which construct is medium and which is heavy rests on the docx alone.

Rows: 3 replicates × 3 channels. `biological replicate` is 1–3 within each bait group, and `factor value[bait]` is set.
