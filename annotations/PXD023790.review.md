   - The decisive evidence is acquisition order. The round-2 cells-t0 sequence is W2, W3, H1 (the 2X files, 2018-06-13 10:02-14:04), then H2 (16:06), H (18:07), C1-C3. So H sits in the H3 slot of the W1-3/H1-3/C1-3 order used in every block.
