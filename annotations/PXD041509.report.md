## Key decisions and evidence

### Group identities
They come from the MaxQuant experiment names:
- `mqpar.xml`: IGG1-3, USP391-3.
- `mqpar-PRPF4.xml`: p41-3, p4si391-3.

The deposited Perseus tables use the same names.

### Bait checks against deposited data (`bait_check.txt`)
- **USP39 arm.** USP39 log2 LFQ is 20.9–22.1 in the IgG IPs and 29.7–29.8 in the USP39 IPs (Δ +8.43, top hit). USP10 is flat (Δ −0.23), and so is its partner G3BP1 (Δ +0.33).
  - The raw files named "USP10" are therefore USP39 IPs. The MaxQuant names, the table name and the data all agree.
  - The file-name mismatch is recorded as a limitation.
- **PRPF4 arm.** PRPF4 is at bait level (log2 ~28.1–28.3) in all six IPs. The arm has no IgG control.

### Treatment
- USP39 arm: the files say `unt`.
- PRPF4 arm: `si39` = USP39 siRNA. The control (`PC_c`, MaxQuant `p4`) is written `untreated` (rule 4). Whether it received a non-targeting siRNA is undocumented.
- The knockdown cannot be checked: USP39 is absent from the filtered PRPF4 table.
