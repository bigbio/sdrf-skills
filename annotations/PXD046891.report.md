## Study and publication
- **PRIDE record:** "Loss of p14 reduces immunogenicity in melanoma by affecting the peptide surface density via non-canonical Wnt signaling" (Schlosser lab, Würzburg). Submitted 2023-11-13, public 2025-05-06.
- **Paper:** Wohlfarth J et al., *Mol Oncol* 2024 18(10):2449–2470, PMID 38807304, PMC11459041, doi:10.1002/1878-0261.13660 (CC BY). The full text came from the Europe PMC fullTextXML, and I verified the title. The Data Availability section names PXD046891 as "the mass spectra files of pSILAC experiments".
- **Experiment:** this is pulsed SILAC immunopeptidomics in Ma-Mel-51 melanoma cells. The cells carry either a dox-inducible scrambled shRNA (scr) or a dox-inducible shRNA against p14ARF (CDKN2A).
  - Dox-treated cells were pulsed in heavy medium (Arg10/Lys8/Leu6) with dox.
  - Untreated cells were pulsed in medium-heavy medium (Arg6/Lys4/Leu3).
  - After 24 h or 48 h the two cultures were mixed 1:1, HLA-I peptides were immunoaffinity-purified, and the samples were run on an Orbitrap Fusion.
  - Peptides were identified with PEAKS Xpro and Peptide-PRISM. MaxQuant 2.0 ("no digestion" mode, multiplicity 3) was then run against the identified-peptide database to obtain H/M ratios.
- **Step 0.5:** the dataset is not in bigbio/sdrf-annotated-datasets, and PRIDE has no deposited SDRF. This is a new annotation.

## Channel → condition map (key decision)
- **Explicit statement:** paper section 2.13 says "dox-treated cells were kept in RPMI SILAC medium supplemented with heavy-labeled amino acids (Arg10, Lys8, Leu6) and dox, whereas untreated cells were kept in medium with medium-heavy-labeled amino acids (Arg6, Lys4, Leu3)". sample_overview.xlsx lists "scr +/- dox" and "p14 +/- dox".
- **Checked against the deposited ratios** (MQ_peptides.txt H/M, joined to the Peptide-PRISM gene symbols):

  | Measure | scr 24 h | scr 48 h | p14 24 h | p14 48 h |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Median log2 H/M, all HLA peptides | 0.10 | 0.09 | 1.09 | 1.44 |
  | PMEL, median log2 H/M | 0.26 | −0.14 | −0.97 | −0.51 |
  | DCT, median log2 H/M | 0.44 | 0.70 | −2.09 | −2.52 |

  - In the p14 samples, heavy carries the global HLA-I increase that the paper reports for p14 knockdown.
  - In the p14 samples, the melanoma differentiation antigens PMEL and DCT go down in heavy, as Fig. 4C reports for p14 kd.
  - In the scr samples, the ratios stay close to 1.
  - So heavy = dox-induced knockdown is confirmed by the data. A swap inside the scr samples would not show in the ratios; that part rests on the stated design.
- **SILAC light is not annotated.** In this pulsed design, the light signal is pre-existing, unlabelled material from both mixed cultures. It is not a sample, and the authors use only H/M ratios. A light row would need a pooled source with no single condition, which rule 7 warns against. The same lab's curated pSILAC immunopeptidomics SDRFs (PXD054097, PXD051190) also carry only medium and heavy rows.

## Conflicts found and how resolved
1. **Fraction vs technical replicate.** Round 1 used technical replicates 1/2. The external review showed that the runs partition disjoint charge populations (z = 2 vs z = 1/3), so they are not repeated measurements. MaxQuant combined them as Fraction 1xx/2xx, and the same-lab PXD054097 encodes them as fractions. Round 2 uses fraction 1/2 with technical replicate 1.
2. **Carbamidomethyl: fixed in the proteome prose, variable in the immunopeptide search.** The search output and the paper's immunopeptide paragraph both say variable, so I used variable.
3. **The paper's MS settings paragraph (45 min gradient, 3 s cycle) describes the proteome runs.** The RAW headers show different HLA methods (80 min, 1.5 s cycle), so I used the RAW headers.
4. **PRIDE `organismParts` (cell culture, melanocyte) vs Cellosaurus.** Neither PRIDE value is an anatomical origin. I used the Cellosaurus derived-from site, lymph node.

## Judgement calls a reviewer should attack
1. **Light channel omitted (16 rows, not 24).** The light signal in a pulsed SILAC mix is the pre-pulse protein pool of both cultures, so I did not treat it as a sample. The same-lab precedents do the same. The alternative is a pooled light source per run.
2. **Fractions without a fractionation method.** The charge-state runs are fraction 1/2 (technical replicate 1), set by an asserting post-build script together with the second dissociation column (ETD) and the per-fraction MS1 scan range. `comment[fractionation method]` is omitted because no term describes this acquisition split.
3. **Inferred or convention values:**
   - unspecific cleavage (MS:1001956) where no enzyme was used
   - MaxQuant-default tolerances (4.5 ppm / 20 ppm) where no parameter file exists
   - clinical-metadata declared only to license genotype and dose
