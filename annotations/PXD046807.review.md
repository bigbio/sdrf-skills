### Findings
1. **IMPORTANT — genotype on adjacent-normal rows.** `characteristics[genotype]` and `factor value[genotype]` say `EGFR mutant` on rows 353–396 and 529–572 (MT-E-N, MT-L-N) and `EGFR wild type` on rows 1–44 and 177–220 (WT-E-N, WT-L-N).
   - **Problem:** WT/MT is the somatic EGFR status of the patient's tumour. Normal lung was not genotyped and does not carry the tumour mutation. The manifest admits the value "applies to the patient group (tumour EGFR status), also for the adjacent normal part".
   - **Fix:** encode the grouping as tumour status, for example `EGFR-mutant tumour` / `EGFR wild-type tumour`. Put it in a column named for that (check OLS for a term), or keep characteristics[genotype] only on T rows, set N rows to `not available`, and carry the patient's tumour status in the factor value and clinical data. N rows must stay in their comparison groups. Rebuild with `sdrf-tools build`.
2. **IMPORTANT — non-ontology custom column.** The headers `characteristics[cancer stage group]` and `factor value[cancer stage group]` are not ontology terms (OLS4 exact search: 0 hits).
   - **Problem:** this violates spec README.adoc:659 ("Column names MUST be valid ontology terms"). oncology-metadata already licenses `characteristics[disease staging]` (EFO:0000410). Its ontology validator is warning-level, so `early stage` / `late stage` are usable (SNOMED:234438000 / SNOMED:236040000 exist).
   - **Fix:** rename both columns to `disease staging`, keep the values, record the warning in validation.txt and rebuild.
3. minor — add `characteristics[sampling site]` = `tumor` / `normal tissue adjacent to tumor`. clinical-metadata documentation calls it important for matched tissue designs.
4. minor — `characteristics[developmental stage]` (human template) is absent and score flags it. Use `not available`, since there are no ages.
5. minor — sex and smoking status are acceptable as described above. Confirm them against the paper's patient table if the full text becomes accessible: PMC12419241 is not OA, the Europe PMC supplementaryFiles endpoint says "not open access", and no bot check was bypassed.
