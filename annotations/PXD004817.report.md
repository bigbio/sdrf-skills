## Study
Winter M *et al.* "Deciphering the acute cellular phosphoproteome response to irradiation with X-rays, protons and carbon ions." *Mol Cell Proteomics* 2017;16(5):855-872. PMID 28302921, PMC5417826, doi:10.1074/mcp.M116.066597.

A549 lung adenocarcinoma cells, SILAC duplex (Arg10/Lys8). Light = sham-irradiated, heavy = irradiated with 6 Gy X-rays, 3.5 Gy protons or 2 Gy carbon ions (HIT Heidelberg); harvested 2 h later, L+H lysates mixed 1:1. Three arms × 3 biological replicates. Three sub-experiments on one LTQ Orbitrap XL:
- **Proteome**: SDS-PAGE, 27 gel slices per lane → 9 × 27 = 243 runs (MaxQuant 1.5.3.8)
- **Phosphoproteome**: in-solution digest, IMAC + TiO2 → 2 phosphopeptide fractions → 9 × 2 = 18 runs (MaxQuant 1.5.3.8)
- **Targeted spike-in validation**: enriched phospho samples spiked with 27 isotope-labelled synthetic phosphopeptides, inclusion-list DDA → 8 runs deposited (Skyline)

## Limitations / open items (also in the manifest)
1. Paper full text not retrievable (CC-BY hybrid, but Europe PMC XML HTTP 500, PDF HTTP 403, PMC HTML/PDF reCAPTCHA, efetch front matter only, BioC/PMC OA S3 absent). Abstract only; Methods came from PRIDE protocol text + deposited outputs.
2. The per-search SDRF is a concatenation of three `sdrf-tools build` outputs (headers asserted identical). The concatenation is outside `build`. Missed cleavages (2 vs 4) not encoded.
3. The targeted runs were not database-searched in the deposit. Their modification set comes from the Skyline document. The spike amount is unknown, and the phosphosite and label of each standard cannot be expressed in PS=.
8. Individual, passage number, culture medium and storage temperature are `not available` (paper Methods unreadable).
4. **Dose confound**: modality and dose co-vary (6 Gy X-ray, 3.5 Gy proton, 2 Gy carbon ion, chosen as biologically equivalent doses per PRIDE). `factor value[treatment]` therefore encodes modality and dose jointly. No licensed column holds dose (the clinical-metadata dose units exclude Gy), and the NCIT terms are clinical-radiotherapy terms used for in-vitro irradiation.
9. `convert-openms` fails on the targeted rows' reserved-word tolerances (see review round 2). `characteristics[mass]` is not licensed by the declared templates.
5. techsdrf not installed; raw verification = 3 headers (one per sub-experiment).
6. `sdrf-tools fetch` failed twice (Europe PMC HTTP 500); record gathered manually.
7. Proteome label direction is protocol-stated, not data-checked.
