## Study
"Effect of puromycin on newly synthesized proteome in pancreatic cancer cells" is the puromycin control experiment for the QuaNCAT (SILAC + AHA) method in Liu S, … Gozani O, Mazur PK, *Methylated eEF1A selectively decodes mRNA to shape cancer proteomes*, Nat Cell Biol 2026, doi:10.1038/s41556-026-02085-6, PMID 42957783 (Extended Data Fig. 4a,b; Supplementary Table 2c). The paper's Data availability section names PXD062045 for the puromycin experiment and PXD062091 for the METTL13 knockdown. PXD062091 is a separate dataset, not a duplicate.

Design: SILAC-labelled T3M4 pancreatic cancer cells (light, or heavy Lys8/Arg10) were serum-starved for 18 h and Met-depleted for 1 h. They were then pulsed for 3 h with 100 nM AHA, ± 5 µg/ml puromycin. Lysates were mixed 1:1, nascent proteins were captured on alkyne agarose (click chemistry) and digested on-resin with trypsin. Samples ran on an Orbitrap Fusion Lumos (HCD 30%, Orbitrap MS2) and were searched with MaxQuant 1.5.5.1.

## Technical values
- **Instrument:** Orbitrap Fusion Lumos, from the raw headers and the paper. The PRIDE dropdown says "Orbitrap Fusion", which is overridden.
- **Acquisition and fragmentation:** DDA; HCD at 30 NCE; Orbitrap MS2; MS1 range 340–1500 m/z.
- **Enzyme:** trypsin. The search rule was Trypsin/P with 2 missed cleavages.
- **Reduction and alkylation:** DTT and iodoacetamide.
- **Modifications:** Carbamidomethyl (C) fixed. Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (Protein N-term) variable. Label:13C(6)15N(4) R (UNIMOD:267) and Label:13C(6)15N(2) K (UNIMOD:259) are variable, following rule 11.

## Open items / limitations
- **Precursor tolerance:** 7 ppm comes from the paper and PRIDE text only. MaxQuant 1.5.5.1 parameters.txt does not record it and no mqpar.xml was deposited. The text also says "default settings", whose main-search default is 4.5 ppm.
- **Enrichment process:** `characteristics[enrichment process]` = generic `sample enrichment` (EFO:0009106). No EFO term exists for AHA/click-chemistry nascent-protein capture. The details are in the manifest.
- **Not encoded as columns:** the AHA pulse and the puromycin dose and duration (5 µg/ml, 3 h).
- **`not available`:** cell type, passage number, biorepository, sample storage temperature and individual.
- **Organism part:** `lymph node`, the derived-from site of this metastasis-derived line (Cellosaurus). Disease is PDAC (MONDO:0005184).
- **Expected validator warnings:** `untreated` and `T3M-4` are not in the ontology lists. `untreated` is the value the template prescribes for controls, and the Cellosaurus accession column carries the cell-line identity.
- **Paper access:** the paper is not OA in Europe PMC. It was read from the publisher page (HTTP 200).
- **Raw-file checks:** techsdrf is unavailable, so raw checks were header range reads only.
- **Review:** the independent review is still pending.
