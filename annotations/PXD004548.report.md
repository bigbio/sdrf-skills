## Study
"Transcriptional control of lysosomal homeostasis by the disease-related CLN3 membrane protein" (PRIDE title). Paper: Schmidtke C et al., "Lysosomal proteome analysis reveals that CLN3-defective cells have multiple enzyme deficiencies associated with changes in intracellular trafficking", *J Biol Chem* 2019;294(24):9592-9604, PMID 31040178, PMC6579452 (not linked in PRIDE; found by Europe PMC search). Immortalized mouse cerebellar cells, wild type and Cln3 Δex7/8 knock-in (CbCln3 lines; Fossale et al. 2004), were Lys0/Lys6 SILAC-labelled and loaded with magnetite. Light and heavy cells were mixed before homogenization, and lysosomes were isolated on a MACS column. The eluate was run on SDS-PAGE and cut into 10 slices, which were digested in-gel with trypsin and measured on an LTQ Orbitrap Velos (CID, Top25). Data were searched with Proteome Discoverer 1.4 / Mascot 2.4 and analysed as cln3 ki/wt (L/H) ratios with limma.

SILAC check (rule 14): the pep.xml quantification node is `SILAC 2plex (Lys6 only)`, with dynamic `Label:13C(6) (K)`, so multiplicity is 2. This is a single deposit with no duplicate.

## Limitations / uncertain values

- That these are the Fossale 2004 CbCln3 lines is an inference: the paper cites Fossale 2004, Cotman is a co-author, and the cells were under G418 selection. The zygosity of the KI line is not asserted.
