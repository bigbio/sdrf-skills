## Main question: is the trans-SILAC channel modelling sound?

**Yes.** Light = the acceptor K-562 proteome. Heavy = proteins derived from the HS-5 donor, measured inside the sorted K-562 cells. Each channel has its own source name, and the heavy rows carry the donor cell-line metadata.

1. **Label direction is explicit.**
   - Methods (Trans-SILAC): "Donor cells were labeled with heavy isotopologues of lysine and arginine: l-lysine:2HCL (13C6 … 15N2) and l-arginine (13C6 … 15N4)".
   - The donor is HS-5 (Results: "Donor stromal cells…", "HS-5 cells possessing DiD-tracked vesicles were co-cultured with CML cells expressing GFP").
   - `mqpar.xml` group 1 has labelMods `['', 'Arg10;Lys8']`, which matches Lys8 = UNIMOD:259 and Arg10 = UNIMOD:267.
2. **The deposited data agree.** In `proteinGroups.txt` the light channel carries 98.4–99.5% of L+H intensity in every experiment. The heavy share is higher in plus_ (1.62% / 1.20%) than in minus_ (0.55% / 0.57%).
3. **The plus/minus = DiD+/DiD− map is confirmed by a source the producer did not use.** Supplementary Table 1 (ESM MOESM11, "Raw TRANS-SILAC data") gives per-population intensities. Their column totals match the deposited combined search almost exactly:

   | Supp. Table 1 | L total | H total | Deposited experiment | L total | H total |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | DID− A | 7.436e13 | 4.04e11 | minus_01 | 7.446e13 | 4.20e11 |
   | DID− B | 7.503e13 | 4.29e11 | minus_02 | 7.514e13 | 4.36e11 |
   | DID+ A | 8.482e12 | 1.35e11 | plus_01 | 8.491e12 | 1.39e11 |
   | DID+ B | 9.885e12 | — | plus_02 | 9.894e12 | 1.17e11 |

   So plus = DiD+ and minus = DiD−, and replicate A/B = _01/_02. This fixes both the factor value and the biological-replicate numbering.
4. **A curated precedent exists with the same encoding.** `sdrf-annotated-datasets/datasets/PXD012833` (trogocytosis: SILAC-heavy NALM-6 protein transferred to sorted CAR-T cells) uses the same pattern:
   - heavy rows are named "NALM-6-derived 13C6-Lys protein in … sorted CAR T cells";
   - they carry the donor cell line's Cellosaurus accession, disease, age and sex;
   - light rows carry the acceptor's metadata;
   - both channels share the sort factor value.

   PXD011013 follows that pattern. The manifest's statement that "there is no curated precedent" is wrong (minor finding F1), but this supports the modelling rather than weakening it.
5. **Spec fit.** Two source names per lysate is legitimate here. The channels describe material of different cellular origin. Each (source name, data file, label) coordinate is unique. The validator, `structure` and `convert-openms` all accept the layout.

Caveat (minor finding F2): the heavy channel is "material carrying the donor's label", not purely HS-5-synthesised protein. Heavy signal appears on acceptor-specific proteins in DiD+ cells only:
- HBE1: H 138e6 in plus_01 and 68e6 in plus_02, 0 in minus;
- HBG1: also heavy in all four experiments.

HS-5 stroma does not express embryonic or fetal globins, so this points to recycling of heavy amino acids or to mis-assigned heavy features. The unlabelled single-shot `zar` runs also show about 1% "heavy" background. The protein-origin value is the authors' interpretation and is acceptable. The manifest should say this.

## Findings (all minor)

- **F1 (minor, provenance).** The manifest limitation says "There is no curated precedent for trans-SILAC in sdrf-annotated-datasets". PXD012833 is one: a heavy-labelled donor protein measured inside sorted acceptor cells, with the same per-channel source names and donor metadata on heavy rows.
  - Fix: cite PXD012833 as precedent in the manifest.
- **F2 (minor, source_evidence).** Heavy signal is not exclusively HS-5-synthesised protein. Acceptor-specific HBE1 is heavy only in DiD+ (plus_01 138e6, plus_02 68e6; minus 0), HBG1 is heavy in all four, and unlabelled `zar` runs show about 1% heavy background. This points to label recycling or false heavy features.
  - Fix: add a manifest limitation that `characteristics[protein origin]` for the heavy channel follows the authors' interpretation.
- **F3 (minor, source_evidence).** Supplementary Tables 1–3 (Springer ESM MOESM11–13) are not cited.
  - Table 1 independently confirms plus/minus = DiD+/DiD− and A/B = _01/_02 (intensity totals within 0.2%).
  - Table 2 reports SILAC incorporation for **both** K562 and HS-5 (96.8/98.9% and 95.0/98.6% at 7/9 days). This is relevant to the blocked `zar` arm. The heavy share in the deposited zar k562/HS5 runs is about 1%, so those runs cannot be the labelled incorporation samples, which supports keeping them blocked.
  - `samplenames.xls` puts the "not discussed in this manuscript" note only on the four +IM runs, so the claim in BLOCKED.md that "the paper never describes the other four" is too strong.
  - Fix: add the supplementary as a source, use Table 1 in the channel-map claim, and mention Table 2 and the samplenames note in BLOCKED.md.
- **F4 (minor, omission).** The Sample preparation paragraph of the Trans-SILAC methods begins "Cell pellets were lysed… Protein lysate (10 μg) … reduced with 5 mM TCEP, and blocked with 5 mM iodoacetamide" before the DiD in-gel step. Keeping `not available` is defensible because the in-gel protocol is not described and the search had Carbamidomethyl and mmts as variable modifications.
  - Fix: record that reading in the manifest (already partly done); no SDRF change required.
- **F5 (minor, omission).** The optional cell-lines columns `characteristics[biorepository]` (ATCC CCL-243 / CRL-11882) and `characteristics[cell line authentication]` (paper: ATCC STR authentication) have evidence and could be added. The K-562 GFP expression is only free text in the co-culture column.
  - Fix: optional.
- **F6 (minor, design).** Both channels share `factor value[sorted cell population]`, so quantms or MSstats groups the light K-562 and heavy HS-5 "samples" into one condition. This matches PXD012833 and is acceptable.
  - Fix: optionally add `factor value[protein origin]` if downstream heavy-versus-population comparisons are wanted.
