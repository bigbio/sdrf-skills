## Study
Shenoy A, Belugali Nataraj N, Perry G, … Dadiani M, Geiger T. *Proteomic patterns associated with response to breast cancer neoadjuvant treatment.* Mol Syst Biol 2020;16:e9443.
- PMID 32960509, PMC7507992, doi:10.15252/msb.20209443.
- Open access. I read the full text, Datasets EV1–EV5, the Appendix and the Review Process File.
- The PRIDE title ("Clinical proteomics of invasive breast carcinoma reveals a major role for proline metabolism…") is the pre-publication title.

**Cohort:** 113 FFPE samples.
- 35 women with partial response to neoadjuvant AC-T (one woman had bilateral cancer, analysed as tumours 27a and 27b). Each has a matched pre-treatment core-needle biopsy, a post-treatment residual tumour and post-treatment tumour-adjacent normal ducts.
- 5 healthy breast-reduction donors.

**Workflow:**
- Each lysate was mixed 1:1 with a heavy (Arg10/Lys8) breast super-SILAC mix.
- LysC-Trypsin mix plus trypsin digestion, SCX fractionation.
- EASY-nLC 1000 on a Q Exactive Plus or a Q Exactive HF, DDA.
- MaxQuant 1.5.5.1.

**Factor values: the four variables the paper compares.**

**Channel map.**

## Conflicts resolved
- **PRIDE instrument "Q Exactive"** vs the raw headers, which show QE Plus and QE HF. The raw files win.
- **reconcile MAJOR `instrument_contradicted`.** It is a false positive: the protocol names both QE Plus and QE-HF, and each row carries the model from its own raw header. Refuted in validation.txt.
- **Patient-1 file naming (`*_CS_21*`).** The MaxQuant design wins over the file name.
- **Paper text "all tumour samples were grade 2 or 3"** vs EV1 diagnosis grade 1 for patients 10 and 16. The Cohort section says "33 patients had grade 2 and 3 tumors", which is consistent with EV1. I used the per-patient EV1 values.
- **EV1 patient 15:** the surgery date is before the diagnosis date. This is one reason age is not recomputed from dates.
