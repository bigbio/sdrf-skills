### Findings (all minor)

1. **minor / completeness:** two recommended columns are missing, `characteristics[sample storage temperature]` and `characteristics[sample type]`.
   - Evidence: `sample storage temperature` is recommended in cell-lines 1.1.0 (allow_not_available true). `sample type` is recommended in base 1.1.0 with `default: study sample`, and reserved words are not allowed there. Batch rule 9 says to add licensed recommended columns, using `not available` where evidence is absent. Campaign precedent PXD018961 carries storage temperature.
   - Fix: add `characteristics[sample storage temperature]` = `not available` and `characteristics[sample type]` = `study sample` through the build inputs, then rebuild.
2. **minor / provenance:** the manifest's `files` source says "55 entries = 51 .raw + 4 MaxQuant txt zips".
   - Evidence: PRIDE v3 `/files` returns 54 entries, 50 .raw and 4 .zip.
   - Fix: correct the count to 54 / 50.
3. **minor / consistency:** control rows have `characteristics[dose]` = `not applicable` while `characteristics[compound]` = `not available`.
   - Evidence: the paper says the controls were vehicle-treated with an unnamed vehicle. A vehicle was given, so its dose is unknown rather than inapplicable.
   - Fix: `not available` would be more accurate, or keep it and say why in the manifest.
4. **minor / evidence strength:** the deposited ratios confirm light = control but cannot independently separate medium (NTRC) from heavy (sgLCK). LCK total protein has no SILAC ratio, and LCK Y192 is down in both channels. The M/H assignment rests on the explicit Methods statement, which is acceptable under rule 6, plus only circumstantial data support.
   - Fix: say in the manifest claim that the data check is confirmatory for light, and only circumstantial for M vs H.
