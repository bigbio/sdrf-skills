Data check (`channel_check.txt`, from proteinGroups.txt):
- **Infection:** in all 12 mixes, the summed intensity of 350 Chlamydia-only protein groups is highest, by 6–15×, in exactly the
  channel marked Ctr-infected.
- **Genotype:** in all 6 triplex mixes, CPPED1 is highest and LCP1 is lowest in exactly the pWPI channel. The paper reports CPPED1
  strongly down and LCP1 up in E6E7 cells.
- No conflicts were found. The HPV E6/E7 protein group itself is too weakly quantified to use as a marker, because its ratios come from requantified isotope patterns.

## Design
- Factors: `genetic modification` (HPV16 E6 and E7 expression (lentiviral pWPI vector) / empty lentiviral vector pWPI),
  `infection` (Chlamydia trachomatis, NCBITaxon:813 / uninfected) and `sampling time` (24 hour / 48 hour). There are 8 groups.
- Biological replicate numbers restart within each group, one per culture:
  - each E6E7 group has 3 (1–3);
  - each pWPI + Ctr group has 3 (7a–c or 8a–c);
  - pWPI uninfected has **6 per time point**: mixes 1, 2, 3, 7a, 7b, 7c = 1–6, and 4, 5, 6, 8a, 8b, 8c = 1–6.
- Source names have the form `NOK-<pWPI|E6E7>_<uninfected|Ctr>_<24h|48h>_sample<mix>`. Each is one labelled culture in one mix, 30 sources in total.
- Fraction identifier 1–11 is MaxQuant fraction 3–13. Fraction 11 is the pooled hpRP fractions 1+2+13 (file suffix `fr1-2-13`).

## Sample values
- Homo sapiens; oral epithelium (UBERON:0002424, inferred from "normal oral keratinocytes"); keratinocyte (CL:0000312).
  Disease is normal (PATO:0000461): these are spontaneously immortalized normal keratinocytes. The PRIDE record's "cervix carcinoma" is the study's
  motivation and is not applied to the rows. Material type: cell line.
- Sex is female (paper: "Immortalized female human oral keratinocytes").
- Cell line: `NOK (normal oral keratinocytes, Piboonniyom et al. 2002)`, from paper ref 29 (PMID 11854066). **Cellosaurus has no record for this line.**
  `id:NOK` returns only the mouse hybridomas NOK-1…5. NOK-SI (CVCL_BW57) is a different line: male, a HaCaT derivative. So the
  name is disambiguated with its origin, and the cellosaurus accession and name are `not available`.

## Technical values
- Q Exactive (MS:1001911), confirmed from the raw headers of both run dates. DDA Top10, HCD at 27 NCE, Orbitrap MS2, MS1 350–1650 m/z
  (raw method + paper).
- High-pH RP fractionation (PRIDE:0000564). Trypsin (MS:1001251); the MaxQuant rule was Trypsin/P, 2 missed cleavages. DTT / iodoacetamide.
- Modifications come from parameters.txt and summary.txt:
  - Carbamidomethyl C, Fixed;
  - Oxidation M and Acetyl Protein N-term (PP=), Variable;
  - labels Arg6 UNIMOD:188, Lys4 (D4) UNIMOD:481, Arg10 UNIMOD:267 and Lys8 UNIMOD:259, all Variable.
- Precursor tolerance is 4.5 ppm, the MaxQuant main search (first search 20 ppm), taken from the Methods text. Fragment tolerance is 20 ppm (parameters.txt).
- Search database: human + HPV16/18 + C. trachomatis UniProt proteomes, as stated by the deposit. It is a manifest note only, because the SDRF has no column for it.

## Values left `not available` and why
- **culture medium:** the medium is FAD (75% Ham's F-12 / 25% DMEM SILAC). NCIT has no term for it, and a free-text value failed validation in round 1.
- **cellosaurus accession and name:** no Cellosaurus record exists for this line.
- **age, developmental stage, ancestry category, individual and passage number:** none is stated in the paper. The passage is given only as "within 10 passages after selection".

## Judgement calls for the reviewer to attack
1. Replicates 4–6 of pWPI uninfected (part 2: 7a–c and 8a–c) are assumed to be cultures separate from part 1. PRIDE calls part 2 a separate
   experiment, but no source says outright that new cultures were grown.
2. `sampling time` (24 h or 48 h after infection) is applied to the uninfected channels of the same mix as their harvest time.
3. The organism part "oral epithelium" is inferred.
4. The C. trachomatis strain, serovar and MOI are not stated anywhere, so only the species is encoded.
5. For the precursor tolerance, the main-search 4.5 ppm was chosen over the first-search 20 ppm.
