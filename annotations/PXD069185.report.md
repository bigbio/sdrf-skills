## Channel → condition map (key decision)
- **Source:** the paper states it explicitly, in the Results text and the Fig. 5B legend. Light = BP-only/no H2O2, medium = APEX2 −IR, heavy = APEX2 +IR. The label isotopes come from mqpar `labelMods` (light: none; medium: Arg6;Lys4; heavy: Arg10;Lys8). These agree with the Methods: Lys-D4 + Arg-13C6 is medium, and Lys-13C6 15N2 + Arg-13C6 15N4 is heavy.
- **Label swaps:** the paper describes none. All 18 files share one MaxQuant parameter group, so one label assignment covers all experiments.
- **Cross-check against the deposited proteinGroups.txt:** the bait UFC1 has log2 M/L of 4.28, 4.66 and 4.64 and log2 H/L of 5.04, 3.03 and 3.07 in experiments 1, 2 and 3. Median protein log2 M/L is 1.9 to 2.5 and H/L is 1.6 to 2.5. This fits light being the non-labelled control in every replicate, so a light swap is excluded. A medium↔heavy swap could not be detected from the ratios, because both are active-labelling channels. That part rests on the paper's single stated design.
