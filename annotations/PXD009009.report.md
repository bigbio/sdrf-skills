# PXD009009: SDRF annotation report

**Status: BLOCKED. No SDRF was built.** The proteome arm is genuine duplex SILAC: the Proteome Discoverer 1.4 quantification method is "SILAC 2plex (Arg6, Lys6)" with channels `Light` and `Heavy`. No accessible source says whether the hypoxic or the normoxic HCT116 cells carried the heavy label in either SILAC mixture (Exp1 and Exp2). Campaign rules 6–7 therefore block all 132 SILAC runs. The 44 secretome runs are dimethyl-labelled, not SILAC, so they are out of scope. Details are in `annotations/BLOCKED.md`, section "PXD009009".

## Study
Chen JT, Liu CC, Yu JS, Li HH, Lai MC. "Integrated omics profiling identifies hypoxia-regulated genes in HCT116 colon cancer cells." *J Proteomics* 2018, doi:10.1016/j.jprot.2018.02.031, PMID 29524648.

HCT116 colon carcinoma cells were cultured under hypoxia or normoxia. The methods below come from the PRIDE protocols:
- **Proteome:** SILAC, light Lys0/Arg0 vs heavy [13C6]Lys/[13C6]Arg. The two lysates were mixed in equal amounts and run by online 2D SCX–RP LC-MS/MS, with 66 SCX fractions per mixture.
- **Secretome:** conditioned serum-free medium, labelled by reductive dimethylation (light 12CH2O vs heavy 13CD2O), with 22 SCX fractions per mixture.
- **Search:** Proteome Discoverer 1.4.0.288 with Mascot 2.2, against Swiss-Prot 2014 human.

## Why blocked (rules 6–7)
- No explicit source gives the SILAC label of the hypoxic and normoxic cells in Exp1 or Exp2. None says whether Exp2 is a label swap or a replicate in the same orientation. The PD channel names are generic.
- The HIF-target ratios above point to a label swap, but rule 6 forbids a map built from data alone.

## Out of scope (not SILAC)
The 44 secretome raws and their 2 msfs are labelled by reductive dimethylation (PD "Dimethylation 2plex"): `20151211-hypoxia-12_5ug-s01..s22.RAW`, `20151214-hypoxia-12_5ug-s01..s22.RAW`, `20151211-hypoxia-12.5ug_Hypoxia_L.msf` and `20151214-hypoxia-12.5ug_Hypoxia_heavy.msf`. They are excluded from this SILAC campaign and listed in BLOCKED.md. For a later non-SILAC pass: the msf file names `_Hypoxia_heavy` and `_Hypoxia_L` are the authors' own statement of the dimethyl label of the hypoxic sample in each mixture.

## What would unblock it
The SILAC label of the hypoxic and normoxic cells in Exp1 and Exp2. It could come from the Methods, Fig. 1 or supplementary tables of the J Proteomics 2018 paper, or from the authors. It should then be checked against the marker ratios above.

The planned SDRF would have 264 rows (132 runs × 2 channels) and use the ms-proteomics v1.2.0, human and cell-lines templates:
- HCT116 (CVCL_0291), colon carcinoma;
- factor value[treatment]: hypoxia vs the normoxia control;
- biological replicate 1–2 within each condition (Exp1, Exp2);
- comment[fraction identifier] 1–66 (online SCX);
- Orbitrap Elite, CID, ion-trap MS2;
- precursor tolerance 10 ppm, fragment tolerance 0.6 Da;
- cleavage agent Trypsin;
- Carbamidomethyl fixed; Acetyl (Protein N-term), Gln->pyro-Glu, Oxidation and Label:13C(6) on K and R (UNIMOD:188, MT=Variable) variable.
