### Key question: the SILAC light channel

**What I searched for the light-channel identity.** I fetched everything myself into `scratchpad/rev-PXD072695/`:
- **Paper (PLOS JATS):** the Methods say "labeled with either … (Arg6, Lys4) or … (Lys8, Arg10)" and "Cell pellets from control and transfected cells". The Results describe "a tRF control oligo (scrambled oligos) or Leishmania tRFs". The Fig 1 legend says "a mixture of two lysates". No light / Arg0 / Lys0 / untransfected / mock sample is described anywhere.
- **S1 Fig:** I read the image, not just the legend. Both panels show only two boxes, "K4, R6 (Medium) Scrambled tRF" and "K8, R10 (Heavy) Ld-tRF-Asp/Leu". The legend says "cultured in medium and heavy SILAC media".
- **S1 Table (13 sheets):** the raw MaxQuant export sheets include Ratio M/L and H/L columns, but no sheet names the light sample. Every analysis sheet uses H/M only.
- **S2–S5:** these hold oligo/RNAhybrid material and raw blots. There is nothing on SILAC there.
- **PRIDE:** the description says nothing. The sample protocol says "3-plex SILAC labelled set". The data protocol says "3-plex labels … (K4, R6, K8, and R10)", which names only medium and heavy.
- **References 18/19:** these are Moradimotlagh 2023/2024, earlier SILAC studies from the same lab. They describe other experiments, so they cannot identify this light sample.

**The data.** `summary.txt` has Multiplicity 3, Labels0 empty, Labels1 Arg6;Lys4 and Labels2 Arg10;Lys8. I recomputed the summed protein-group intensity per experiment: light 2.6–4.4e9, medium 1.6–2.5e9, heavy 1.5–2.6e9. So the light channel is a real, populated sample, not an empty or unused channel. Nothing identifies it.

**Judgement: omitting the light rows is acceptable, and is the correct encoding.**
1. **Spec:** nothing requires a row for every label in a multiplexed file. The MUST-unique key is source name + assay name + label, and the validator passes.
2. **Campaign rule 7 and the request** ("undocumented arms are blocked, not filled"):
   - Light rows with `not available` treatment would pool 9 unknown samples into one fake condition. Rule 7 forbids that.
   - Rows labelled `reference` or `untreated` would be invented.
   - Blocking the whole runs would throw away the fully documented and data-verified medium/heavy channels, with no gain in safety.
   - Rule 7's "leave those runs out" assumes the undocumented arm occupies whole runs. Here it is one channel inside documented runs, and dropping that channel's rows applies the same rule at that granularity.
3. **Downstream:** `convert-openms` keeps the 3-plex numbering when `SILAC medium` is present. I checked: the design has labels 2/3 and 12 samples, so no index shift. `convert-maxquant` would emit a 2-plex medium/heavy design, which degrades gracefully.
4. **Disclosure:** the omission is recorded in the manifest limitations (PARTIAL SCOPE, with numbers) and in the BLOCKED.md PXD072695 section. That section lists all 9 files, gives the unblock path, and says why the channel is blocked.
