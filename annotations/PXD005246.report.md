## Study
- **PRIDE title:** *SILAC phosphoproteomics of 293-F10 cells [induced (H) vs uninduced (L)]*.
- **Paper:** Greseth MD, Carter DC, Terhune SS, Traktman P. *Proteomic Screen for Cellular Targets of the Vaccinia Virus F10 Protein Kinase…* Mol Cell Proteomics 2017;16(4 Suppl 1):S124–S143. doi:10.1074/mcp.M116.065003, PMID 28183815, PMC5393388.
  - The full text was read from the PMC HTML render. Europe PMC reports no full text, and efetch returns the front matter only.
- **Design:**
  - The FLP-In T-REx 293 c.o.-3xFLAG-F10 cells ("293-F10") were SILAC-labelled: light, or heavy with Arg10/Lys8.
  - The heavy cells were induced with doxycycline (50 ng/ml, 10 h) to express the vaccinia F10 kinase.
  - Heavy and light cells were mixed 1:1. Post-nuclear supernatant proteins were digested with trypsin, and phosphopeptides were enriched with TiO2.
  - There are 3 biological replicates (separate cultures and inductions), each injected 3× on an LTQ Orbitrap Velos.
- **Step 0.5:** the dataset is not in `sdrf-annotated-datasets`, and PRIDE has no deposited SDRF.

## Channel → condition map
1. **The map is stated explicitly.**
   - Paper section (A) and the PRIDE protocol: "Cells grown in the presence of heavy media were then induced with 50 ng/ml doxycycline for 10 h … mixed 1:1 (heavy/light)."
   - Experimental Design: "H-labeled cells were treated with doxycycline … mixed with uninduced, L-labeled cells."
   - The PRIDE title says "induced (H) vs uninduced (L)".
2. **The search agrees.** MaxQuant multiplicity is 2: Labels0 is empty (light) and Labels1 is `Arg10;Lys8` (heavy).
3. **The deposited data confirm the direction.**
   - The paper reports increased phosphorylation of mDia (DIAPH1) at Ser22 on F10 induction.
   - In the deposited `Phospho (STY)Sites.txt`, DIAPH1 S22 has normalized H/L of **14.7 / 16.5 / 12.8** in BR1 / BR2 / BR3.
   - So heavy is the induced channel in every replicate, with 0 discrepancies (`channel_direction_check.txt`).
   - There is no label swap in any replicate.

## Key technical decisions (deposited search > paper > PRIDE)
- **Instrument:** `LTQ Orbitrap Velos` (MS:1001742). All 9 raw headers say "LTQ Orbitrap Velos MS", not Velos Pro.
- **Raw method:** Top10 DDA.
  - MS1 is FTMS at 60k resolution over m/z 375–2000.
  - MS2 is CID in the ion trap (ITMS) at 35 NCE.
  - Encoded as dissociation `collision-induced dissociation` (MS:1000133), MS2 analyzer `linear ion trap` (MS:1000291) and collision energy `35 NCE`.
- **Modifications** come from the mqpar and `parameters.txt` of MaxQuant 1.3.0.5:
  - Fixed: Carbamidomethyl C.
  - Variable: Oxidation M; Acetyl at Protein N-term (`PP=`); Phospho STY.
  - SILAC: Arg10 is written as `Label:13C(6)15N(4)` (UNIMOD:267, R), and Lys8 as `Label:13C(6)15N(2)` (UNIMOD:259, K). Both use `MT=Variable` (batch rule 11).
  - The heavy masses match the paper's 13C6,15N4-Arg and 13C6,15N2-Lys.
  - The `isobaricLabels` iTRAQ list in the mqpar is MaxQuant's unused default.
- **Tolerances:**
  - Precursor is **6 ppm**, the mqpar `mainSearchTol`. The paper's "20 ppm" is the `firstSearchTol`.
  - Fragment is **0.5 Da** (ITMS).
- **Cleavage agent:** `Trypsin` (MS:1001251), the trypsin gold used in the digest. The search rule was Trypsin/P with 2 missed cleavages; that is in the manifest only (batch rule 2).
- **Reduction / alkylation:** DTT / iodoacetamide, from the paper and the PRIDE protocol.
- **Fractions:** 1 per file. The experimental design has no fraction numbers, and the three files per biological replicate are replicate injections (`comment[technical replicate]` 1–3).
