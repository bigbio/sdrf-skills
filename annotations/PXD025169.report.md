## Study
"Analysis of the nascent proteome during murine norovirus infection". Brocard M, Lu J, Hall B, et al., Locker N. *Murine Norovirus Infection Results in Anti-inflammatory Response Downstream of Amino Acid Depletion in Macrophages.* J Virol 2021;95(20):e0113421, doi:10.1128/JVI.01134-21, PMID 34346771, PMC8475529.

Design: RAW264.7 mouse macrophages were fully SILAC-labelled for 5 passages (light R0K0, medium R6K4, heavy R10K8). Cells were then mock-infected, infected with UV-inactivated MNV (MNV(UVi)) or infected with MNV-1 (strain CW1), all at MOI 10 TCID50/cell. Nascent proteins were pulse-labelled with L-azidohomoalanine (AHA) for 30 min before harvest at 6.5 h or 10.5 h p.i. They were then click-biotinylated, mixed 1:1:1 and enriched on streptavidin. Each mix ran on one SDS-PAGE lane, cut into 10 slices. The instrument was an Orbitrap Fusion (CID, ion-trap MS2) and the search used MaxQuant 1.6.1.0.

**What is pulsed:** the pulse is the AHA label, not SILAC. SILAC labelling was complete, so the SILAC channels encode the infection condition, not new versus pre-existing protein. The AHA pulse and enrichment are recorded in a custom `characteristics[nascent protein labeling]` column.

The other 6 files are not data runs:

No runs were excluded, so nothing was added to BLOCKED.md.

SILAC multiplexing is verified (rule 14): `summary.txt` gives Multiplicity 3, Labels1 `Arg6;Lys4` and Labels2 `Arg10;Lys8` for all 60 files.

## Technical values
- **Instrument.** Orbitrap Fusion (MS:1002416), from the raw headers ("Orbitrap Fusion", no Lumos) and the paper ("Orbitrap Fusion Tribrid"). The PRIDE dropdown says "Orbitrap Fusion Lumos"; it is overridden.
- **Acquisition, fragmentation and scan range.** DDA; CID (MS:1000133) at 35 NCE; ion-trap MS2 (MS:1000264); MS1 range 350–1550 m/z.
- **Fractionation.** SDS PAGE (PRIDE:0000568). The fraction identifier is the slice number, which equals the MaxQuant Fraction.
- **Cleavage agent.** Trypsin (MS:1001251), from "in-gel tryptic digestion". The MaxQuant rule was Trypsin/P with 2 missed cleavages (rule 2).
- **Tolerances.** Precursor 20 ppm and fragment 0.5 Da, from the paper and PRIDE; `parameters.txt` gives ITMS 0.5 Da.
- **Modifications.** These were read from the deposited search output, identical in both searches:

  | modification | site | type | source |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Carbamidomethyl (UNIMOD:4) | C | Fixed | 10 h `parameters.txt`; 6 h peptide masses +57.0215 per Cys |
  | Oxidation (UNIMOD:35) | M | Variable | `summary.txt` |
  | Acetyl (UNIMOD:1) | Protein N-term | Variable | `summary.txt` |
  | Met->Aha (UNIMOD:896, −4.9863) | M | Variable | `summary.txt`, paper |
  | Met->Triazole-PEG4-biotin (+452.238) | M | Variable | `summary.txt`, paper |
  | Label:13C(6) (UNIMOD:188) | R | Variable | SILAC labels |
  | Label:2H(4) (UNIMOD:481) | K | Variable | SILAC labels |
  | Label:13C(6)15N(4) (UNIMOD:267) | R | Variable | SILAC labels |
  | Label:13C(6)15N(2) (UNIMOD:259) | K | Variable | SILAC labels |

  - **Met->Triazole-PEG4-biotin has no Unimod record.** I checked OLS and the unimod.xml shipped with sdrf-pipelines; the nearest mass is Sulfo-NHS-LC-LC-Biotin at 452.2457, which is a different chemistry. It is written as `NT=Met->Triazole-PEG4-biotin;TA=M;MT=Variable;MM=452.238`. The mass equals AHA plus biotin-PEG4-alkyne (C21H35N3O6S, 457.2247 Da).
  - **SILAC masses** follow the paper's stated shifts.
  - **PRIDE's "No PTMs"** entry is ignored.
- **Not available:** reduction and alkylation reagents (not stated), and passage number.

## Validation summary (details and sha256 in `PXD025169.validation.txt`)
- **`parse_sdrf validate-sdrf`** with `-t ms-proteomics -t vertebrates -t cell-lines`, full ontology: exit 0, warnings only. The only warning is that `BALB/c` is not in NCBITaxon. That column is free text, and the same warning appears on the passed precedent.
- **`reconcile`** reports one MINOR finding, `cleavage_agent_unsupported`. It is refuted: the record's sample protocol says "in-gel tryptic digestion", and the paper and the MaxQuant Enzyme field confirm trypsin.

The modification is kept because rule 5 requires the modifications the files were actually searched with. Reprocessing with quantms would need this modification defined by hand.

## Open items / judgement calls for the reviewer
1. **Mock/UVi channel identity.** It rests on the Latin-square statement, the Fig. 6A schematic and reproduction of the figure coordinates; there is no tabulated per-replicate map. The coordinates were read from images, to a precision of about 0.05 log2.
2. **Sampling time.** The SDRF uses the 6.5/10.5 h harvest times; the paper labels the arms 6/10 h.
3. **Disease of the cell line.** `leukemia` is the origin of the line, not the infection status.
4. **Custom columns.** `characteristics[infection]`, `[multiplicity of infection]` and `[nascent protein labeling]` are custom columns (spec "Extending Templates").
5. **No AC on the Triazole-PEG4-biotin modification**, so `convert-openms` fails.
6. **Replicate pairing across time points.** Nothing says whether replicate n at 6 h and at 10 h came from the same experiment day; they are separate dishes either way, so separate source names.
