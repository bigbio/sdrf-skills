## Is it multiplexed SILAC? (rule 14)
**Yes, for the 24 `_H_` runs.** These are dynamic (pulse) SILAC:
- On day 5 of differentiation, half of the cultures were switched to heavy Lys8/Arg10 SILAC DMEM. Cells were harvested on day 7 or day 15.
- MaxQuant used "a standard multiplicity with two labels … Lys8/Arg10 … heavy channel".

The other **24 `_L_` runs** come from cultures that stayed in light medium ("non-SILAC samples to evaluate changes in total protein abundance"). They are label-free, so per rule 12 they go in a separate file, `-lfq`.

The only deposited search result is a Proteome Discoverer 2.4.1.15 protein export (`…48SILAC…xlsx`). It searched all 48 runs as a 2-plex. Per-run median H/L (`/tmp/claude-0/-home-user/a98b4c07-45f2-5d08-aa74-0c95b1e5e538/scratchpad/b6-PXD063206/ratio_check.txt`):

## Open items / limitations (also in the manifests)
1. **Search parameters:** the deposited search is a PD 2.4 export without the pdResult, so its parameters are unknown. The SDRF uses the parameters the paper and PRIDE text give for MaxQuant 1.6.14. The MaxQuant outputs are not deposited.
2. **Modification conflict:** the paper's variable "cysteine N-ethylmaleimide" conflicts with iodoacetamide alkylation, and no fixed Carbamidomethyl is stated. The SDRF encodes what was stated.
3. **Phospho search not encoded:** the FragPipe LFQ-phospho search of the light-only runs has no reported parameters, so it is not encoded.
4. **Cell line not registered:** i11w-mNC has no Cellosaurus entry. This causes the validator warning and the `check` exit 1.
5. **Replicate batches:** it is not stated whether replicate k is shared across groups or arms (the same differentiation batch).
