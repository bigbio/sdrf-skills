## Study
Alhalabi et al., Cancer Lett 2024;605:217265 (PMID 39332586, PMC12768573). Glioblastoma stem-cell (GSC) lines NCH711d, NCH705 (mesenchymal) and NCH421k, NCH644 (proneural) were SILAC-labelled; heavy cells got 1 µM dasatinib, light cells got DMSO vehicle. Samples were taken at day 4 and day 12, with 3 biological replicates. MaxQuant 1.6.0.16 ran one search over all files (Multiplicity 2, Arg10;Lys8).

## Design
- Channel map: SILAC light = untreated (DMSO vehicle), SILAC heavy = dasatinib 1 µM. Sources: the PRIDE sample protocol, the paper's Methods, and the data processing note "heavy/light ratio i.e. Dasatinib treated/control". Checked against the deposited phosphosite ratios: RB1 S807/S811/S780 phosphorylation drops strongly in heavy, and RB1-T373 and CDK1-T161 drop more in MES than PN lines, as the paper reports.
- 24 SILAC mixtures (4 lines × 2 time points × 3 replicates), from the deposited `setup.txt`. Each mixture was measured twice:
  - runs 01–24: total proteome (rows 1–48)
  - runs 25-3 and 26–48: phospho-enriched (rows 49–96). Phospho evidences make up 40–80% of each run, against 0.4–0.5% in the proteome runs. In summary.txt these runs map to the same experiments Sample01–24 as Fraction 11.
- Factor values: treatment, sampling time (4 day / 12 day), cell line → 16 conditions, 6 rows each. Biological replicates are numbered 1–3 within each group.

## Open items / limitations
- The paper says "Arg-10 and Lys-6", but PRIDE and the MaxQuant search say Lys8. The annotation uses Lys8 (UNIMOD:259): 83–97% of K-only peptides carry a ratio under the Lys8 search.
- The phospho-enrichment method is not described anywhere.
- The deposited mqpar.xml is from an earlier configuration (it lists the undeposited files 25 and 25-2). The file→experiment map comes from summary.txt instead. The precursor tolerance of 4.5 ppm comes from that mqpar.xml.
- NCH711d and NCH705 have no Cellosaurus entry. For them, cellosaurus accession and name, age, sex, ancestry and developmental stage are `not available`.
- individual and passage number are `not available` for every row.
- DMEM/F12 has no NCIT term. characteristics[dose] is a custom column.
- Paper supplementary tables were not retrieved because Europe PMC was unavailable.
