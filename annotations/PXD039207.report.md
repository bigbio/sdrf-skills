## The key finding: PRIDE links the wrong paper; the design comes from a duplicate deposit

- **The PRIDE reference for PXD039207 is wrong.** It points to Park et al. 2023, *Cancer Genomics Proteomics* 20:592 (PMID 37889066, PMC10614069). That paper is a **TMT6plex study of pooled patient tissue**, and its Data Availability names **PXD039204**. It has no SILAC or cell-line content (grep of the full text: 0 hits). PXD039207's own files are SILAC runs of cell lines: the raw path is `...\Kidney_cancer\Cell line\SILAC_Global\`.
- **PXD045898 holds the same data.** A PRIDE search found PXD045898, "LC-MS/MS proteomic profiling study of ccRCC cell-lines", from the same lab. It holds the same 27 RAW files plus the same mqpar, and the evidence is:
  - all 28 file sizes are identical;
  - the first 1 MiB and a 1 MiB block at offset 300 MB of F0-1 are byte-identical;
  - `mqpar.xml` is byte-identical to `mqpar_SILAC_Global.xml`.

  Its paper is Park et al. 2024, *Cancer Genomics Proteomics* 21:645 (PMID 39467626, PMC11534031, open access). That paper describes exactly this experiment, so it is the design source.
- **Duplicate deposition.** A corpus must not include both PXD039207 and PXD045898. The curated `sdrf-annotated-datasets/datasets/PXD045898/PXD045898.sdrf.tsv` annotates these runs as **label free with organism part "Epithelial cell"**. That is wrong: mqpar shows multiplicity 3. The curated file was not touched.

## Design

- **Multiplexing is verified (rule 14).** mqpar has `multiplicity` 3 and `labelMods` = `""` / `Arg6;Lys4` / `Arg10;Lys8`.
- **Channel map, explicit in Fig. 1A of PMC11534031:**

  | Cell line | Channel | Labels |
  |---|---|---|
  | HEK-293 | Heavy | K8R10 |
  | 786-O | Medium | K4R6 |
  | Caki-1 | Light | K0R0 |

  Each line has n=3, and the lysates were "mixed equally". The Methods agree: "HEK-293 and 786-O cell lines were grown in SILAC medium", and "normal DMEM (K0R0) was used for cells grown in light".
- **Runs:** 3 mixes (biological triplicates; MaxQuant experiments 1st/2nd/3rd = file suffix -1/-2/-3) × 9 high-pH fractions (F0-F8 → `comment[fraction identifier]` 1-9) = 27 RAW files × 3 channels = **81 rows**.
- **Samples:** 9 sources, `<line>_SILAC_rep<1-3>`. The factor is `factor value[cell line]`, with 27 rows per line.
- **Scope:** 27 of 27 deposited RAW files are annotated. The only other deposited file is `mqpar.xml`.

## Values and where they come from

- **Stated: instrument.** LTQ Orbitrap Velos (MS:1001742), from the RAW header and the paper. PRIDE says the generic "LTQ Orbitrap"; the raw file wins.
- **Stated: acquisition and fragmentation.** Top-10 DDA. CID at 28% NCE. MS2 in the linear ion trap (ITMS), so the fragment tolerance is 0.5 Da (mqpar ITMS). MS1 range 300-2000 m/z (RAW and paper).
- **Stated: modifications (mqpar).**
  - Carbamidomethyl C: Fixed.
  - Oxidation M and Acetyl Protein N-term: Variable.
  - SILAC labels as Variable (rule 11), with UNIMOD accessions from the mass shifts: Arg6 → UNIMOD:188, Lys4 → UNIMOD:481, Arg10 → UNIMOD:267, Lys8 → UNIMOD:259.
- **Stated: digestion and search settings.** Cleavage agent is Trypsin (MS:1001251), the digestion enzyme; the search rule was Trypsin/P (rule 2). Precursor tolerance is 4.5 ppm, the mqpar main search. The paper's "20 ppm" is the first-search tolerance.
- **Stated: reagents.** DTT and IAA, from the paper and the PRIDE protocol.
- **Fractionation.** High-pH reversed-phase (PRIDE:0000564), from Fig. 1A ("High-pH fractionation") and 9 fractions in mqpar. The Methods text does not describe the fractionation further.

## Open items and limitations

- **No data check of the channel map (rule 6).** Neither deposit contains MaxQuant txt output, and no RAW reader is installed. The map rests on Fig. 1A plus the Methods text, which agree with each other. The author SDRF agrees on HEK293 = heavy.
- **Mix pairing is implied, not stated.** That replicate *i* of each line went into mix *i* follows from the design but no source says it.
- **Duplicate check is partial.** Identity with PXD045898 rests on sizes, partial byte comparison and an identical mqpar. Full checksums are unavailable for PXD045898.
- **No techsdrf.** Technical values come from 4 RAW headers (all 4 show the identical method), not every file.
- The full list of limitations is in the manifest `limitations`.

## Judgement calls the reviewer should attack hardest

1. **The design source.** Annotating PXD039207 from the paper of a *different* accession (PXD045898). This rests on byte-level identity of the files, not on any statement in PXD039207 itself. The SILAC channel map was not checked against quantitative data.
2. **Disease and organism part.**
   - HEK293 disease = `normal`: the paper calls it the normal group, but the line is adenovirus-transformed and Cellosaurus lists no disease.
   - 786-O = `renal cell carcinoma` (Cellosaurus) rather than the paper's "ccRCC".
   - Caki-1 organism part = `kidney`, with sampling site `skin of body`. The cells came from a skin metastasis.
3. **Recommended columns filled from indirect evidence.**
   - DMEM for the labelled lines, whose "SILAC medium" formulation is not named.
   - `-80 °C` storage temperature: stated for the pellets, while the column describes the cell line.
   - `late embryonic stage` for HEK293's "Fetus".
   - `European ancestry` for Cellosaurus "Caucasian".
