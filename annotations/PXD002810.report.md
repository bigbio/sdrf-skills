- **Cells:** the mouse B-lymphoma line #2567, derived from a λ-MYC transgenic mouse.
- **Transduction:** cells received a GFP retrovirus, either pMIG-miR-17-19b or the empty pMIG vector, and were GFP-sorted.

- **Conflict.** For the Rev experiments, no text in the paper states the swap. The annotation rests on the deposited "Rev"/"Rev1bis" names and the inverted ratios. This conflict is recorded in the manifest.
- **Ele.** The paper's Methods describe a "triplicate SILAC proteome of WT:WT cells", and Supp Fig 4a says "combining three technical replicates". The deposited names read "HL1to1", the search is called "combined_onetoone", the run set is 3 × 15 fractions, and the deposited H/L is about 1 (median log2 −0.014).

## Values to note (open items)
- **Not available:**
  - organism part, developmental stage and sex are not reported for the mouse tumour;
  - cellosaurus accession and name: #2567 is an in-house line;
  - culture medium: the SILAC DMEM/Ham's F12 has no NCIT term;
  - precursor mass tolerance: MaxQuant 1.2.2.5 txt does not record it, and no mqpar.xml was deposited.
- **Biological replicates 1–8** treat each MaxQuant mixture as an independent culture. The paper's two sets, #2567-1 and #2567-2, cannot be assigned to experiments. The "b"/"bis" sets may be re-preparations of the same cultures.
- **WT:WT genotype "wild type"** follows the Methods wording. Supp Fig 4 calls these cells "control cells".
- **Custom columns.** `characteristics[genotype]` and `factor value[genotype]` are custom; the compared variable is the vector.
- **Cleavage.** A second `comment[cleavage agent details]` column (Lys-C) was inserted by the deterministic `postbuild.py` (rule 2). MaxQuant used the Trypsin/P rule.
- **Fraction identifier.** It is the position of the OFFGEL well file in well order. For B/E/F/G/I, MaxQuant's own fraction numbers merge wells 17+18 and 19+20; `filemap.tsv` lists both numberings.
