## Findings

1. **important, characteristics[disease], rows 1-170:** `not applicable` should be `normal`.
   - `vertebrates/1.1.0/vertebrates.yaml` (upstream 7da1747) says: "Use `normal` for healthy animals".
   - SAMPLE-GUIDELINES.adoc:186 says `normal` (PATO:0000461) is the REQUIRED term for healthy samples. `not applicable` is reserved for concepts that do not apply.
   - The manifest itself says no disease is modelled.
   - Curated precedent: 226 mouse SDRFs with a genotype column use `normal`.
   - **Fix:** set disease = `normal` for all 6 sources in samples.tsv, rebuild, rerun postbuild.py, rerun the validation log, and update the manifest claim.
2. **important, manifest claim on characteristics[biological replicate] and limitation 4:** the manifest misreads the paper and leaves out a conflict.
   - The manifest says the paper's "Exp. 1-3" numbering "refers to the separate gel-LC protein expression experiment". This is false. The Fig. 3A legend covers the acetylated sites (this dataset) and says "Exp. 2 corresponds to the experiment in which the SILAC label swap was introduced". Fig. 1B says the same.
   - Methods (and the PRIDE SPP) say "in the third experiment, a label swap was used", so the paper is internally inconsistent.
   - Three things support Exp1 = II_29, Exp2 = II_53_Reverse, Exp3 = II_68:
     - acquisition order (II_29 May 2013, II_53 June 2013, II_68 July 2013);
     - notebook IDs;
     - "first replicate analyzed three times" (II_29 = 36 runs).
   - The SDRF has II_68 = 2 and II_53 = 3.
   - **Fix:** either renumber II_53 -> 2 and II_68 -> 3 (rows 73-170) to match the figures, or keep the MaxQuant order. Either way, correct the claim and record the Methods-vs-figure conflict in the limitations.
3. **minor, comment[label] claim:** add the Pearson vs Spearman figures and the outlier explanation above, so that the weak positive Pearson is not later read as a failed label swap.
4. **minor, comment[technical replicate] claim:** record the PRIDE SPP "2 technical replicates / 72 analyses" conflict with the paper (84) and the deposit (85). Cite the `_repeat` raw-header original filename. Optionally add `comment[acquisition date]`.
5. **minor, missing characteristics[sample type]:** upstream base 1.1.0 lists it as recommended, with default `study sample`. The local `sdrf-tools contract` (older nested templates) lists it as optional. Add it if build supports it (batch rule 9).
