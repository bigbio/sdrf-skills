## Key probe: IP medium/heavy bait assignment (FAILS for SILAC_IP_Exp1)

The producer says the medium/heavy constructs "rest on the details sheet only", that the data "cannot discriminate" them, and that the map is the same in all three replicates. Both statements are wrong.

1. **The data do discriminate the constructs.** The HSV-1 pUL56 sequence (AFE62885.1, 234 aa) has the tryptic peptide `QGCCGVVWR` at residues 206-214. Residues 208-214 are absent from the pUL56(1-207) construct, so the peptide can only come from the 1-234 construct. Per-channel intensities in the deposited evidence table:
   - Exp1 (`090316_Exp1`): L 3.7e6, **M 3.2e7, H 6.8e5**. Ratio H/M = 0.0096 and 0.0077 in two MS/MS events, so the 1-234 construct is in **medium**.
   - Exp2: L 8.5e6, M 2.6e5, **H 9.5e7** (H/M = 332 and 318), so 1-234 is in **heavy**.
   - Exp3: L 5.6e6, M 3.2e5, **H 5.6e7** (H/M = 137), so 1-234 is in **heavy**.
2. **The paper's own Table S4 states the swap.** In mmc5.xlsx, the tab "UL56(1-207)-GFP" has the columns `Exp 1 log2 (H/L)`, `Exp2 log2 (M/L)` and `Exp3 log2 (M/L)`. The tab "GFP-UL56(1-234)" has `Exp 1 log2 (M/L)`, `Exp2 log2 (H/L)` and `Exp3 log2 (H/L)`. The values match the deposited proteinGroups exactly. For example, TRAPPC10 in the 1-207 tab is 4.235 / 3.511 / 3.569, and proteinGroups gives H/L1 = 4.235, M/L2 = 3.511 and M/L3 = 3.569. Interactors specific to the full-length construct follow the swap too: KPNB1 has M/L1 +5.41 but H/L2 +3.69 and H/L3 +4.18, and PHB2 shows the same pattern.
3. Light = GFP-only is supported in all three replicates: pUL56 and viral intensity are lowest in light, and the bait column agrees.

So the deposited `Detailsofsamplesandrawfiles.docx` map (Medium = pUL56(1-207)-GFP, Heavy = GFP-pUL56(1-234)) is correct for Exp2 and Exp3 but **inverted for Exp1**, which was a label swap. Rows 1-3 of the IP SDRF (`SILAC_IP_Exp1.raw`) currently give medium = pUL56(1-207)-GFP and heavy = GFP-pUL56(1-234). Batch rule 6 applies: an explicit label statement plus the data beat the docx, and the conflict must be recorded.
