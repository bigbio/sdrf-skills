# PXD002454: SDRF annotation report

**Status: BLOCKED. No SDRF was built.** The dataset is genuine triplex SILAC: 3 biological replicates × 5 SCX fractions on a TripleTOF 5600, searched with ProteinPilot 5.0. No accessible source says which SILAC channel (light, Lys4/Arg6 medium, or Lys8/Arg10 heavy) holds mock, EV71 8 hpi or EV71 20 hpi. Campaign rules 6–7 therefore block all 15 runs. The details are in `annotations/BLOCKED.md`, section "PXD002454".

## Study
Zhang LK, Lin T, Zhu SL, Xianyu LZ, Lu SY. "Global quantitative proteomic analysis of human glioma cells profiled host protein expression in response to enterovirus type 71 infection." *Proteomics* 2015;15(22):3784-3796, doi:10.1002/pmic.201500134, PMID 26350028. In this study, U251 glioma cells were mock-treated or infected with EV71, harvested at 8 and 20 hpi, and mixed 1:1:1.

## Evidence examined
- **PRIDE v3 project record** and the paginated file list. There are 33 files: 15 `.wiff`, 15 `.wiff.scan` (`20140418_{A,B,C}N{1-5}`) and 3 ProteinPilot FDR reports (`20140418_{A,B,C}N1__FDR.xlsx`). The sample protocol names the conditions and the 1:1:1 mix but not the labels.
- **Paper: closed access.** Europe PMC has no PMCID and no supplement. Unpaywall and Semantic Scholar both report it as closed. Wiley returned a 403 bot check, which I did not bypass. I read only the abstract.
- **The three FDR reports**, downloaded in full and then deleted. Their sha256 values are in `scratchpad/b10-PXD002454/xlsx.sha256`.
  - The Search Summary gives Sample Type `SILAC (Lys+4D, Arg+6)(Lys+8, Arg+10)`, trypsin, iodoacetamide, urea denaturation and the TripleTOF 5600.
  - The database is `Uniprot_Human(reviewed)_201412.fasta` (20188 entries), which includes one EV71 polyprotein (`gi|530307006`).
  - The Protein Summary has `M:L` and `H:L` columns, so light is the denominator. This confirms multiplicity 3 (rule 14).
  - No condition names appear anywhere in the reports.
- **WIFF header of `20140418_AN1.wiff`.** The sample name is just `20140418_AN1`, with no description. I deleted the file after reading it.
- **Viral-peptide labels**, from the distinct-peptide sheets. Of the 16 EV71 peptide identifications, 10 are medium, 4 are heavy and 2 light. These are only hints (see `scratchpad/b10-PXD002454/viral_peptides.txt`). They suggest that light is mock, but they are not decisive, and they cannot tell 8 hpi from 20 hpi. Rule 6 forbids a map built from data alone.
- I checked whether the dataset is a duplicate or already annotated: it is in neither `annotations/` nor `sdrf-annotated-datasets/datasets/`.

## What would unblock it
The channel labels for mock, 8 hpi and 20 hpi, from the paper's Methods or Fig. 1 or from the authors. The map should then be checked against the viral-peptide labels and against the reported TRAF2/TRAF6 up-regulation. The planned SDRF would have 45 rows (15 runs × 3 channels) and use the human and cell-lines templates. It would carry a custom `characteristics[infection]` column for Enterovirus A71 (NCBITaxon:39054, OLS4-verified), with factor values for infection status and time post infection.

I ran no validation, because no SDRF exists. No `.sdrf.tsv`, `.evidence.json` or `.validation.txt` was written, following the precedent of the fully blocked PXD035841.
