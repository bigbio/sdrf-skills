## Study
Siebert A, Gattringer V, Weishaupt JH, Behrends C. *ALS-linked loss of Cyclin-F function affects HSP90.* Life Sci Alliance 2022;5(12):e202101359, PMID 36114006, PMC9481933 (open access; full text and Tables S1–S4 retrieved).

The study looks for SCF(Cyclin-F) substrates with three kinds of experiment:
- SILAC diGly ubiquitinome profiling of CCNF-KO versus WT Neuro-2a and SH-SY5Y cells, and of ALS-patient LCLs carrying CCNF V335M or D628V versus control LCLs;
- label-free FLAG-HA-CCNF interaction proteomics, with TBC1D7 as the control bait;
- label-free APEX2-CCNF proximity proteomics, with and without biotin-phenol.

All runs were acquired on a Q Exactive HF and searched in MaxQuant 1.6.0.1, in 7 searches whose summary/parameters/mqpar files are deposited.

## SILAC verification (rule 14)

## Key design decisions
- **Sequential diGly IPs.** Each SILAC mixture was immunoprecipitated twice in sequence, giving 2 raw files per MaxQuant experiment. These are encoded as `comment[fraction identifier]` 1/2 of one sample, which matches how MaxQuant summed them. Fraction 1 is assigned to the earlier acquisition (the lower AS number block). That order is inferred.
- **Biological replicates.** Numbers restart per (cell line, genotype) factor group. Each mutant LCL is numbered 1–8, because it was labelled in 4 mixtures with each of two controls. The paper reports "eight biological replicates" for the LCLs.
- **Factor values.**
  - digly-human: `factor value[cell line]` + `factor value[genotype]`;
  - digly-mouse: `factor value[genotype]`;
  - lfq: `factor value[bait]` + `factor value[treatment]`.
- **Modifications** come from mqpar.xml:
  - Carbamidomethyl C, Fixed;
  - Oxidation M, Acetyl Protein N-term, Variable;
  - diGly files only: GG (UNIMOD:121, "stringent GlyGly (K)") and Lys8 (UNIMOD:259, MT=Variable).
  - The LCL experiment names say "K8/R10", but the search and the paper's medium description use Lys8 only. The SDRF follows the search.
- **Tolerances.** Precursor 4.5 ppm (mqpar mainSearchTol) and fragment 20 ppm (FTMS). The paper's "main search 0.5 D" is the ITMS MS/MS setting.
- **Enzymes.** diGly runs: Trypsin + Lys-C, as two `comment[cleavage agent details]` columns; the Lys-C column is inserted by `scripts/postbuild.py --lysc`. LFQ runs: Trypsin only. MaxQuant used the Trypsin/P rule.
- **Reduction and alkylation.**
  - diGly: DTT / chloroacetamide;
  - APEX2: TCEP / iodoacetamide;
  - HA-IP: `not available`. HA-IP and APEX2 were built separately with identical headers and concatenated.
- **Cell lines.**
  - SH-SY5Y (CVCL_0019): bone marrow (metastatic site), neuroblastoma, female, 4Y, European ancestry.
  - Neuro-2a (CVCL_0470): peripheral nervous system, neural crest derived neuroblast, neuroblastoma, A/J, male.
  - LCLs: study codes as cell line and individual; `cellosaurus accession`/`cellosaurus name` = `not available`; blood; B-lymphoblastoid cell line; sex from the paper's reagent table; developmental stage adult (patient onset at 47 and 62 years, controls age-matched); age `not available`.
- **Culture medium.** DMEM for the diGly cells (SILAC DMEM). Not stated for the IP and APEX2 cells.

## Open items / limitations
- The order of the two sequential diGly IPs (which file is fraction 1) is inferred from acquisition order.
- Each mutant LCL is assumed to have had an independent culture per mixture (biological replicates 1–8).
- LCL age, ancestry and passage number are `not available`. Developmental stage `adult` is inferred from the patients' ages at onset and the age-matched controls.
- `comment[collision energy]` and `comment[ms1 scan range]` are omitted: only one raw header per search was read, and the values differ between searches.
- Culture medium for the HA-IP and APEX2 cells is not stated. Whether H2O2 was applied to the no-biotin-phenol controls is not stated either.
- The paper says interaction proteomics was done "in quadruplicates", but only triplicates are deposited and searched. Table S4's APEX2-CCNF vs APEX2-TBC1D7 comparison has no deposited runs.
