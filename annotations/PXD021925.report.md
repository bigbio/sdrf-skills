## Study
"LPS Tolerance Inhibits Cellular Respiration and Induces Global Changes in the Macrophage Secretome" — Gillen J, Ondee T, Gurusamy D, Issara-Amphorn J, Manes NP, Yoon SH, Leelahavanichkul A, Nita-Lazar A. *Biomolecules* 2021;11(2):164, doi:10.3390/biom11020164, PMID 33513762, PMC7918892 (open access; full text read). Triple-SILAC secretome of RAW 264.7 mouse macrophages: untreated (Con), LPS-responsive (LR, one stimulation) and LPS-tolerant (LT, two stimulations 24 h apart). The conditioned media were mixed 1:1:1 (v/v), run on an SDS-PAGE gel, digested in-gel with 5 sections per lane recombined into one sample, and measured on a Q Exactive HF.

## Key decisions and evidence
### Channel map (rule 6)
- **Explicit source.** Both the paper (section 2.4) and the PRIDE protocol give the map: Con = Arg0/Lys0 (light), LR = Arg+6/Lys+4 (medium), LT = Arg+10/Lys+8 (heavy).
- **Ratio check** (from `proteinGroups.txt`):
  - Tnf: log2 M/L is +5.0 to +5.7 and H/M is -3.7 to -4.1 in the four 070318 runs. Tnf was not quantified in the 111918 runs.
  - Ccl4: log2 M/L is +2.4 to +3.6 and H/M is -3.0 to -3.7 in all 8 runs.
  - So the cytokine burst is in the medium (LR) channel and is suppressed in the heavy (LT) channel. This matches the paper's LRU group (TNF and Ccl4 higher than in both Con and LT).
- **Conclusion.** The map is confirmed and the sources do not conflict.

### Replicates
- The paper says the experiment was "repeated twice with two biological replicates each time to produce four biological replicates" and that each was "analyzed twice to create two technical replicates".
- In the files, Mix1/Mix2 are the biological replicates and the A/B suffix is the injection. The A/B pairs agree to within 0.1 log2, while Mix1 and Mix2 differ.
- Which preparation round each mixture belongs to (070318 vs 111918) is inferred from the dates in the file names and from the different LC methods. It does not affect the numbering.

### Treatment
- `characteristics[treatment]` and `factor value[treatment]` take three values: `untreated` / `LPS-responsive (single LPS stimulation)` / `LPS-tolerant (repeated LPS stimulation)`.
- The dose and timing are in the custom column `characteristics[treatment protocol]`: 100 ng/mL *Salmonella minnesota* R595 LPS.
- No infection column: the cells received purified LPS, not a pathogen.
- My first draft put long free-text treatment values in the factor column. The validator's OLS searches on those strings stalled, so that run was aborted (exit 143) and the values were shortened.
