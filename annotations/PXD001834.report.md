## Study

The full text was read from the PMC article page; the publisher PDF returned 403.

- **Samples:** primary cortical astrocytes from C57BL/6J wild-type and GCLM(−/−) littermates, taken at 1–2 days old.

- **Sample preparation:** Lys-C then trypsin digestion, then 12 SCX fractions, then anti-acetyl-K immunoprecipitation of each fraction.
- **Acquisition:** Orbitrap Elite, CID in the ion trap.

**Where the channel map comes from (rule 6).** The paper and the PRIDE protocol state it explicitly: WT is light and GCLM(−/−) is heavy in two experiments, and the third is a label swap. MaxQuant names its experiments FirstForw, SecForw and Reverse.

**Check against the deposited ratios.** Values are log2 H/L normalized, in the order FirstForw / SecForw / Reverse:
- Nrf2 targets reported as up in KO:
  - G6pdx +0.42 / +0.35 / −0.38
  - Gclc +0.52 / +0.40 / −0.35
  - Cat NaN / +0.28 / −0.39
  - Gstm1 +0.25 / +0.19 / −0.17
- Idh1 K243 acetyl site (paper: 3.03-fold up in KO): SecForw +1.49, Reverse −1.71.
- Regulated acetyl sites: 55 sites have |log2| > 0.585 with the same sign in both forward experiments, and 49 of them flip sign in Reverse.

The map is confirmed, with no conflict between sources.

The global forward-vs-reverse Pearson correlation of acetyl sites is +0.25 / +0.29, which could look like a failed swap. It is not:
- Spearman correlation is −0.28 / −0.27.
- Pearson without the 5 most extreme sites is −0.57.
- The positive Pearson comes from three label-invariant saturated sites: Cdh17 K406, Klhl30 K359 and Kcnj11 K5. The paper removes this kind of artefact.
