## Findings

1. **IMPORTANT — light-pool source names (240 light rows).**
   - Problem: each experiment has one physical light standard. The paper says "all light-labeled cell fractions of one experiment were combined … spiked … into the mixed compartment fractions". The SDRF splits it into 4 sources, `…_spiked_into_<compartment>_mix`. That gives 72 sources where there are 54, and four nominal reference samples share one replicate number in the same group.
   - Rule: spec README.adoc:297 says the same sample used several times keeps one source name.
   - Fix: give all 40 light rows of each (cell line, experiment) one source name in make_tables.py, rebuild with `sdrf-tools build`, and update the manifest claim.
2. minor — The manifest says the Calu-1 slice-numbering convention is "not stated". Its own MW check settles it: MaxQuant numbering follows gel order.
3. minor — `characteristics[sampling time]` is 24 hour on infected rows but not available on the matched controls. This is the infection duration, not a time-course point. Consider dropping the column or using a "time post infection" characteristic.
4. minor — The claim that PXD007809 does not overlap comes from an earlier listing and was not fetched again. The rows here are not affected.
