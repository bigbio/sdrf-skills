### Key verifications
- **Channel map.** The PRIDE sample protocol states the map explicitly:
  - L = A549/TAP;
  - M = TAP-SUMO, K4R6;
  - H = TAP-SUMO, K8R10;
  - in the IAV experiments, L and H were infected and M was mock-infected;
  - in the heat-shock experiment, H was held at 43 °C for 30 min.

  The paper's Fig. 5A legend agrees. The deposited MaxQuant ratios confirm the map:
  - The viral proteins are in L and H, not in M. IAV_SUMO1 M1 has crude log2 M/L −4.32 and H/M +4.09. The IAV_SUMO2 viral peptides have median crude M/L −3.29 and H/L −0.07.
  - The SUMO bait is in M and H. RANGAP1 purified M/L is +3.73, +4.03 and +3.05 across the three experiments.
  - Heat shock shows in HS H. Purified median H/M is +0.42, and SUMO2 H/M is +1.81.
- **Files.** The six RAW zips are >4 GiB and have no zip64 records. I read their central directories by HTTP range and recomputed the wrapped offsets. Each archive has exactly 13 `.raw` members, 78 in total. That set is identical to `comment[data file]`, and each archive's runs map to the matching samples.
- **Technical values.** The raw header of `20140708_MT_FG_pur01.raw` gives Q Exactive, NCE 25.0, scan range 300–1800 and TopN 10. `parameters.txt` and `summary.txt` give FTMS MS/MS tolerance 20 ppm, fixed Carbamidomethyl, variable Ox/Acetyl/GlyGly, and SILAC labels Arg6;Lys4 and Arg10;Lys8 (multiplicity 3) in all three searches.
- **Cell line.** Cellosaurus CVCL_0023 confirms A-549, male, 58Y, Caucasian, lung, lung adenocarcinoma.
- **Deterministic checks:**
  - `validate-sdrf` (ms-proteomics + human + cell-lines, full ontology): exit 0 with 2 warnings (`untreated`, `heat shock`);
  - `structure`, `check` (CLEAN) and `reconcile`: exit 0;
  - `score`: 100/100;
  - the manifest passes schema-check and coverage;
  - `convert-openms` fails on `precursor mass tolerance = not available`. That value is documented and the sentinel is legitimate.
- **Ontology.** OLS4 term endpoints resolved all 16 controlled terms, none obsolete. The UNIMOD accessions match the MaxQuant modification names.

### Findings (all minor)
1. **Data files are zip members.** `comment[data file]` names `.raw` members inside multi-run zips, and the SDRF has no `comment[file uri]`. The repo convention allows member names. Keep the member→archive map in the report and manifest; it is already in limitation 3.
2. **`heat shock` is free text.** No NCIT/EFO treatment term exists for it; the closest, NCIT:C15415 "Hyperthermia Treatment", is a therapeutic procedure. It is acceptable as is.
3. **Crude vs purified has no factor value.** The two are told apart only by source name and `characteristics[enrichment process]`. This follows the PXD004650 precedent. Adding `factor value[enrichment process]` is optional.
4. **Absolute path in the manifest.** The manifest's `artifact` is an absolute path. Use `annotations/PXD002943.sdrf.tsv` instead.
5. **Precursor tolerance is unrecorded.** It appears in no deposited file (MaxQuant 1.3.0.5, no mqpar.xml) and not in Document S1, so the field stays `not available`. As a result, convert-openms cannot run as is.

No blocker or important findings.
