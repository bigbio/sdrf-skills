## Study
**Paper.** Cintas C et al., *Mol Cancer Ther* 2021;20(12):2433-2445. doi:10.1158/1535-7163.MCT-20-0981, PMID 34552006.
- The text comes from the HAL accepted manuscript (hal-03223423).
- The supplementary files come from AACR figshare.

**Design.** SILAC (Lys8/Arg10) phosphoproteomics of Capan-1 (CVCL_0237), TiO2-enriched.
- **Light cells:** serum-starved, pre-treated for 1 h with an inhibitor or vehicle (DMSO), then stimulated with 2% dialysed FBS for 10 min or 24 h.
- **Heavy cells:** the vehicle reference.
- **Replicates:** 4 biological replicates (RB1-RB4).
- **Instrument and search:** LTQ Orbitrap Velos; Proteome Discoverer 1.4.1.14 with Mascot. Every .msf uses "SILAC 2plex (Arg10, Lys8)".
