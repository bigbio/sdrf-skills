## Study
**Paper.** Cintas C et al., "Phosphoproteomics Identifies PI3K Inhibitor-selective Adaptive Responses in Pancreatic Cancer Cell Therapy and Resistance", *Mol Cancer Ther* 2021;20(12):2433-2445. doi:10.1158/1535-7163.MCT-20-0981, PMID 34552006.
- PMC has no copy. The text comes from the HAL accepted manuscript (hal-03223423).
- The supplementary files come from AACR figshare (Tables S1-S3, Supplementary Methods, Supplementary Figures).

**Design.** SILAC (Lys8/Arg10) phosphoproteomics of the human pancreatic cancer cell line Capan-1, TiO2-enriched.
- **Light cells:** serum-starved for 16 h, then pre-treated for 1 h with one of:
  - vehicle (DMSO);
  - LY294002 10 µM (pan-PI3K);
  - A66 5 µM (PI3Kα);
  - TGX-221 500 nM (PI3Kβ);
  - AS-252424 5 µM (PI3Kγ).

  They were then stimulated with 2% dialysed FBS for 10 min or 24 h.
- **Heavy cells:** the vehicle reference, mixed 1:1 into every run.
- **Replicates:** 4 biological replicates (RB1-RB4, done in four different weeks).
- **Runs:** 5 conditions × 2 times × 4 replicates = 40 runs, one injection each, on an LTQ Orbitrap Velos.
- **Search:** Proteome Discoverer 1.4.1.14 with Mascot, then Percolator and phosphoRS. There is one .msf file per raw file.

**SILAC check (rule 14).** Every .msf uses the quantification method "SILAC 2plex (Arg10, Lys8)", channel 1 Light and channel 2 Heavy.

**Light/heavy.** Light = condition and heavy = vehicle, per:
- the Fig. 1 legend ("Light and heavy … pre-treated respectively with [inhibitors] or … vehicle (DMSO)"; "heavy vehicle condition is a technical replicate that is added in each run");
- the Supplementary Methods;
- the PRIDE text ("heavy samples (starved untreated cells)"; ratios are Light/Heavy).

**ISO1/2/3.** Neither the paper nor the deposit decodes these labels in words. Supplementary Table 2 groups peptides under the headings "PI3Kalpha / PI3Kbeta / PI3Kgamma / pan-PI3K selective peptides". Applying the authors' 0.7/1.4 thresholds to each group:

| Table S2 group | changed only in | 10 min | 24 h |
|---|---|---|---|
| α-selective | ISO1 | 56/58 | 35/35 |
| β-selective | ISO2 | 25/25 | 30/31 |
| γ-selective | ISO3 | 82/86 | 70/71 |
| pan-selective | LY | 75/76 | 47/48 |

Hence ISO1 = A66, ISO2 = TGX-221 and ISO3 = AS-252424. This is also the α, β, γ order the paper uses throughout.

**Check against the deposited data.** Light/heavy areas were read from 10 .msf files (replicate RB2 at 10 min and RB1 at 24 h) by HTTP-range SQLite. For each inhibitor run, I took log2(L/H inhibitor) − log2(L/H FBS) and correlated it with log2 of each Table S2 column:
- 30 of the 32 correlations are positive. A swapped light/heavy map would make them negative.
- The matching column correlates best in 7 of 8 runs (10 min: ISO1 +0.26, ISO3 +0.32; 24 h: LY/ISO1/ISO2/ISO3 +0.15/+0.12/+0.12/+0.15).
- The correlations are weak because each is a single replicate compared with a 4-replicate average.

## Technical values
All taken from the 40 .msf files; the search settings are identical in all of them (`msf_compare.txt`):
- **Instrument:** LTQ Orbitrap Velos (MS:1001742) per the raw headers and the paper. PRIDE lists the generic "LTQ Orbitrap".
- **Acquisition and fragmentation:** DDA top 20; CID at 30 NCE; MS2 in the linear ion trap; MS1 350-1800 m/z (raw method).
- **Enzyme:** trypsin. The search rule was Trypsin/P with 2 missed cleavages (rule 2).
- **Tolerances:** 5 ppm precursor, 0.6 Da fragment.
- **Reagents:** DTT and chloroacetamide.
- **Modifications:** all variable — Carbamidomethyl C, Oxidation M, Phospho S/T/Y, Acetyl Protein N-term, Label:13C(6)15N(2) K (UNIMOD:259), Label:13C(6)15N(4) R (UNIMOD:267). Carbamidomethyl was searched as a dynamic modification, even though the PRIDE text says "static".
- **convert-openms works:** it gives 11 conditions with no failure.

## Open items / limitations
1. **ISO→inhibitor mapping.** It rests on the Table S2 group structure plus the paper's listing order. The data check supports it only weakly. No sentence in any source states it.
2. **Heavy reference as one material.** The Fig. 1 legend supports this. The Supplementary Methods wording (heavy cells lysed after 10 min and 24 h; replicates in four weeks) leaves open separate heavy lysates per week or time point.
3. **`not available` values:**
   - DMSO dose;
   - heavy sampling time and serum stimulation;
   - passage number;
   - cell type;
   - sample storage temperature.
4. **Papers not fetched.** The published AACR text and the bioRxiv JATS were blocked (Cloudflare / HTTP 429) and were not bypassed. The HAL manuscript and the figshare supplements were used instead.
