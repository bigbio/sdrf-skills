## Study
This is the proteome arm ("– proteome dataset") of Fabrik I et al., *Mol Cell Proteomics* 2018;17(1):81-94 (doi:10.1074/mcp.RA117.000160, PMID 29046388, PMC5750852). The phosphoproteome arm is PXD005747. The paper says: "Aliquots of 6 digests (60 min p.i./two bacterial strains/triplicate) were also subjected to proteome analysis."

- **Samples**: SILAC (Arg6/Lys6) bone-marrow-derived dendritic cells from female C57BL/6 mice. They were infected for 60 min with *F. tularensis* subsp. *holarctica* FSC200, either wild type or ΔdsbA, against mock controls.
- **Fractionation**: high-pH reversed-phase into 10 fractions, with fractions 1+9 and 2+10 pooled, giving 8 injections per digest.
- **Acquisition and search**: Q Exactive, HCD; MaxQuant 1.5.2.8 without phospho.

## Companion / duplicate check
This deposit is a companion of PXD005747, not a duplicate:
- **Runs**: 48 raw files (`*_ML_IF_*`, 2016-09/10). None of them is among the 358 phospho runs of PXD005747.
- **Search**: this deposit has its own MQ_output.ZIP and design template.
- **Samples**: they are the same physical digests as the 60 min phospho samples. The source names are therefore identical to the 60 min sources in `PXD005747.sdrf.tsv`, and they name the same lysates.

Nothing was added to DUPLICATES.md.

## Key values
- **Sample columns**: same as PXD005747 (organism, cell type, strain, sex, age, infection columns, `disease = normal`, sample type), with sampling time 60 minute.
- **Enrichment column**: none, since this is the unenriched proteome.
- **Fractionation**: hpHRP (PRIDE:0000564).
- **comment[fraction identifier]**: the deposited MaxQuant fractions 1, 3-8 and 10, set by a scripted post-build edit. Fr1a9 maps to 1 and Fr2a10 to 10.

## Conflicts

## Judgement calls a reviewer should attack
1. **Mock replicate numbering (1-6)**: each mock lysate is treated as its own culture.
2. **Mock sampling time**: 60 minute, inferred from "the respective mock-treated BMDCs".
3. **Disease**: `normal`, although PRIDE tags tularemia.
4. **Shared source names**: the names are the same as in PXD005747, because the samples are aliquots of the same digests.
5. **Channel map**: it rests on the explicit statements plus the MaxQuant labels, without a marker check.
