## Study
"Intronic CRISPR repair in LZTR1-deficient iPSC-cardiomyocytes from Noonan syndrome patients reveals new treatment options".
- Paper: Hanses U … Cyganek L, *Circulation* 2020;142:1059-1076, doi:10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.119.044794, PMID 32623905.
- Design: super-SILAC proteomics of iPSC-derived cardiomyocytes (iPSC-CMs) from two siblings with recessive LZTR1 Noonan syndrome (NS), from wild-type (WT) lines, and from CRISPR-repaired patient lines.
- Labelling: each light (unlabelled) sample was mixed 1:1 with a heavy (Arg10/Lys8) super-SILAC standard. The standard pools the 4 NS and 3 WT lines.
- Acquisition: 12 or 15 fractions per sample, with two LC-MS series (R1 and R2). Searched with MaxQuant 1.6.3.4 in three combined searches.
- **The primary paper could not be read.** It is not in PMC; ahajournals returned 403; the Göttingen green-OA copy returned 403 at a bot check, which was not bypassed. Sample identity therefore comes from:
  - the PRIDE protocol;
  - the MaxQuant experiment names in the deposited `summary.txt`;
  - the same group's 2024 open-access follow-up (PMC10851011).

## Channel map and group identity (rule 6)
- **Explicit source.** The PRIDE protocol says the heavy (13C6,15N2-Lys / 13C6,15N4-Arg) cultures of the 4 patient-derived and 3 WT lines were pooled as the spike-in standard. Each light sample was mixed 1:1 with this standard.
- **Group identity.** The MaxQuant experiment names give the groups:
  - H1/H2 × clone A/B × differentiation 1/2: the 4 patient lines (two siblings).
  - WT1–3 × differentiation 1/2: the 3 WT lines.
  - Corr*: the "NS-corr" group of the PRIDE data-processing protocol.
- **Data check** (`ratios.txt`): LZTR1 loss causes RAS accumulation, so RAS GTPases should be higher in the light NS samples. log2 H/L normalized:

## Open items / limitations (unattended)
1. The primary paper and its supplement were not retrievable. This is a 403 / bot check, which was not bypassed.
2. **Cellosaurus identities are not mapped.** The follow-up paper names the patient lines UMGi030-A and UMGi031-A and the repaired clones UMGi030-A-1 and UMGi031-A-1. The deposit's labels (H1-A/B, H2-A/B, Corr02, Corr4, WT1–3) cannot be tied to them, so `characteristics[cell line]` holds the depositors' labels. This is the source of the validate-sdrf warnings.
3. **Corr02A/Corr02B are treated as two differentiations of one line "Corr02".** They could be two clones. Which patient each repaired line comes from is not stated.
4. The 2019 runs are assumed to use the same super-SILAC standard; the protocol describes only one.
5. Treating R1/R2 as technical replicates is inferred from the run names.
6. Precursor tolerance is `not available`, so convert-openms exits 1.
7. PRIDE's instrument (Q Exactive only), MaxQuant version and FASTA disagree with the deposited files; the deposited files are used.
8. The reconcile MINOR finding on the cleavage agent is refuted in validation.txt.
