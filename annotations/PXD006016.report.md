## Data files: why `.ms2`
The deposit contains no vendor `.raw` file. PRIDE categorises the RawConverter `.ms2` files as **RAW** and the `.mzXML` / `.mgf` as PEAK, so `comment[data file]` lists the `.ms2` files (as instructed for this batch). The original raw names are visible only in the mzXML `parentFile` element.

## Scope: deposited vs annotated
30 files: 10 RAW-category `.ms2` (all annotated), 10 `.mzXML` (PEAK), 10 `.mzid` (SEARCH). 10 runs x 2 channels = 20 rows.

SILAC multiplexing verified (rule 14): every mzid holds a light protocol and a heavy protocol (`H`-prefixed spectra) with fixed +6.0201 K / +10.0083 R.

## Design
One AG11498 (HGPS) culture per time point (0, 1, 2, 4, 6 days), nuclear extract and nuclear pellet, one run each (no repeat injections). Light = pre-existing, heavy = newly synthesised; factors `sampling time` and `subcellular fraction`.
