## Findings (minor only)
1. **Phospho fraction encoding (rows 55-114).** F1–F10 are sequential TiO2 enrichment rounds, not chromatographic fractions. They are encoded as `comment[fraction identifier]` 1–10 with fractionation method "not applicable". Per-sample summation comes out right and the manifest documents the choice, so no change is required.
2. **Cell line authentication value.** `characteristics[cell line authentication]` = "Microsynth AG" names the provider, not the method. The paper gives no method, and precedents also use provider names, so this is acceptable as is.
3. **Optional addition to `BLOCKED.md`.** The Table S3.1 intensities do not match the deposited proteinGroups (RPS9 BR1 L: 4.93e9 in the table against 1.30e9 deposited). The table came from a different processing, which strengthens the block.
