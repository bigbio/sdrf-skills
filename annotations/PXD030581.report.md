## Study
"HMG20B stabilizes association of LSD1 with GFI1 on chromatin to confer transcription repression and leukemia cell differentiation block". Maiques-Diaz A, …, Whetton AD, Somervaille TCP. *Oncogene* 2022;41(44):4841-4854, doi:10.1038/s41388-022-02471-y, PMID 36171271, PMC7613766. Design: SILAC (Arg6/Lys8) LSD1 co-immunoprecipitation in THP-1 AML cells. Heavy cells got the LSD1 inhibitor OG86 (250 nM, 48 h) and light cells got DMSO. The lysates were combined, then pulled down with anti-LSD1 or with control rabbit IgG. Each pull-down was run on SDS-PAGE and cut into slices, on an LTQ Orbitrap Velos, and searched with MaxQuant 1.5.1.2.

Not a duplicate: a PRIDE search (OG86, HMG20B, Somervaille LSD1) finds no other deposit of this experiment. PXD012954, an LSD1 interactome in NB4 cells from another lab, is a different study. sdrf-annotated-datasets has no PXD030581 folder.

## Key decisions and evidence
### Channel map (rule 6)
- **Explicit source.** Supplementary Methods: "Heavy-labeled cells were exposed to 250nM of OG86 and the light-labeled to DMSO control for 48 hours … The lysates from heavy-OG86 or light-DMSO were combined and incubated with the anti-LSD1 or anti-IgG rabbit antibodies". The paper Results say the same.
- **Check against the deposited data.** In txt2 `proteinGroups.txt`, the Intensity L / Intensity H values of KDM1A (351800000 / 192530000), HMG20B (13691000 / 3929200), GSE1 and RREB1 are *identical* to the "Intensity DMSO" / "Intensity OG86" columns of Table S1. So L = DMSO and H = OG86.
- KDM1A raw H/L is 0.56, matching the supplement's statement "0.55". HMG20B normalized H/L is 0.47, the reduced value the paper reports.
- **Conclusion.** The map is confirmed, with no conflict.

### Source names and replicates
- **Two pull-downs.** The anti-LSD1 and IgG pull-downs are two physical pull-downs of one combined lysate. Each (pull-down, channel) therefore has its own source name.
- **One culture per channel.** The light channel of both pull-downs comes from the same DMSO culture, and the heavy channel from the same OG86 culture.
- **Replicates.** There is one SILAC experiment, so each factor group (immunoprecipitation × treatment) holds one culture and `biological replicate` = 1. Neither the deposit nor the paper describes a replicate experiment.

### Factors
- `factor value[treatment]` (OG86 vs DMSO vehicle) and `factor value[immunoprecipitation]` (anti-LSD1 vs IgG control) use a custom `characteristics[immunoprecipitation]` column, as in precedent PXD012954.
- **DMSO deviates from rule 4.** It is encoded as `Dimethyl Sulfoxide` (NCIT:C437), not `untreated`, because the control was a vehicle. This is the same justified deviation as in PXD012954 and PXD008454.
- **Sample type.** IgG rows are `control sample` and LSD1 IP rows are `study sample`.

### Fraction identifier (post-build edit)
- **The edit.** `scripts/fix_fraction.py` sets `comment[fraction identifier]` to the slice number in the file name.
- **Why.** `build` numbers fractions by their position in the file list. With LSD1_4 and IgG_3 missing, that would shift the later slices.
- **Logged.** The edit is deterministic and justified in validation.txt. It changed 30 cells.

### Technical values
- **Modifications** (`parameters.txt` + `summary.txt`, identical in both searches):
  - fixed: Carbamidomethyl (C);
  - variable: Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (Protein N-term);
  - SILAC labels: Arg6 = UNIMOD:188 and Lys8 = UNIMOD:259, `MT=Variable`;
  - summary.txt has no "Multi modifications" column.
- **Cleavage agent**: Trypsin MS:1001251. The MaxQuant search rule was Trypsin/P (rule 2).
- **From the raw headers** (LSD1_1 and IgG_1, range read):
  - instrument: LTQ Orbitrap Velos;
  - acquisition: Top20 DDA (`120mins_Top20_CID_wilf.meth`);
  - full MS: FTMS, 60k resolution, m/z 300–1700;
  - MS2: ITMS CID at NCE 35.
- **Fragment tolerance**: 0.8 Da (MaxQuant ITMS). The MS2 analyzer is an ion trap.
- **Fractionation**: SDS PAGE.
- **PRIDE's Mascot settings** (10 ppm / 0.6 Da) belong to the non-deposited label-free experiment and are not used.

## Conflicts and open items
- **Labelling amino acids.** The supplement says "heavy methionine and arginine". The MaxQuant searches use Arg6;Lys8, and Lys8 pairs are present in evidence.txt. The search setting is annotated.
- **Number of gel pieces.** The supplement says each gel lane was cut into 10 pieces, but 11 files per lane were searched.
- **Culture medium.** RPMI comes from the general THP-1 culture statement. The base of the SILAC medium is not named separately.
- **OG86.** It has no ontology term (validator warning). Its dose and duration are recorded in the manifest only.
- **HsapDv term.** It is not in the EFO list the validator searches (validator warning; same as precedent PXD072695).
