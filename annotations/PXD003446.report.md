## SDRF: `annotations/PXD003446.sdrf.tsv`
- 18 rows × 46 columns. sha256 `fcc821ba93c13866809326d8f2d486e586f83a9ef47dfb4f76fa41e1ae0b7c1c`.
- Design: 3 experiments (Q18e1, Q18e2, Q18e3) × 3 fractions × 2 SILAC channels.

| channel | source | condition (factor value[treatment]) | biological replicate |
|---|---|---|---|
| SILAC light | `N2a_uninduced_GFP-IP_Q18e{1,2,3}` | untreated (uninduced, no HttEx1Q18-eGFP) | 1, 2, 3 |
| SILAC heavy | `N2a_HttEx1Q18-eGFP_GFP-IP_Q18e{1,2,3}` | induction of HttEx1Q18-eGFP expression | 1, 2, 3 |

**How the channel map was settled.**
- The PRIDE dataProcessingProtocol names both conditions: "The Q18 interactome was analyzed using uninduced (without Q18 expression) cells as the background control".
- The bait in the deposited `proteinGroupsQ18RR_IP.txt` fixes which label is which. EGFP heavy/light intensity is 4.9e10/4.5e8, 1.8e10/5.4e8 and 3.0e9/1.0e8 (raw H/L 13.9, 40.6, 26.6), against a proteome-wide normalized H/L median of 0.70. The Htt protein group is also heavy-enriched.
- So heavy is the Q18-expressing culture in all three experiments; there is no label swap.
- No source states this orientation in words.

## Other values and where they come from
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive, read from all 9 RAW headers and `summary.txt`.
- **Acquisition:** DDA Top10, HCD at 25 NCE, Orbitrap MS2, MS1 scan range 300–1750 m/z, from the RAW method headers.
- **Search (MaxQuant 1.4.1.10):** Carbamidomethyl (C) fixed; Acetyl (Protein N-term), Oxidation (M), Arg10 (UNIMOD:267) and Lys8 (UNIMOD:259) variable; fragment tolerance 20 ppm (FTMS).
- **Cleavage agent:** encoded as Trypsin (MS:1001251). The search used the Trypsin/P rule.
- **Cell line:** Neuro-2a, CVCL_0470. Cellosaurus gives male sex, A/J strain, derived from the peripheral nervous system, disease neuroblastoma (MONDO:0005072).
- **Disease:** the project's "Huntington disease" is **not** put on the rows. It is the disease being modelled, and the light channel expresses no huntingtin construct.
- **Enrichment process:** sample enrichment (anti-GFP MicroBeads).
