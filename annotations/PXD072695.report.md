- **Channel map source.** The Fig 1 legend says: "The 'Heavy' cell population was transfected with either Ld-tRF-Asp or Ld-tRF-Leu mimic, while the 'Medium' cell population was transfected with either scrambled Ld-tRF-Asp or scrambled Ld-tRF-Leu (control)". The Methods give medium = Arg6/Lys4 and heavy = Lys8/Arg10, which matches summary.txt.
- **Data check.** The paper's fold changes are the deposited H/M normalized ratios:
  - CD109: 1.33 in the paper; 1.325, 1.332 and 1.343 deposited.
  - CPT1A: 0.69 in the paper; 0.680 and 0.706 deposited.
  - ACSL4: 0.77 in the paper; 0.781 and 0.762 deposited.
  - Asp 26S protease regulatory subunit 6B: 1.78 in the paper; 1.53 and 2.03 deposited.

  So treated/control = H/M, which is consistent with heavy = mimic. There is no bait-type marker that could orient the map independently.
- **Biological replicates.** Replicates are numbered 1–3 within each of the 4 treatment groups, one number per independently transfected culture (the experiment number).
- **Technical replicates.** Each Leu "-redo" run is technical replicate 2 of the same pooled mixture. MaxQuant groups each redo with its original run in one experiment, and the originals have about 10× fewer IDs. The MaxQuant "Fraction" values on these runs are not fractions.
- **Treatment.** `characteristics[treatment]` and `factor value[treatment]` are free text. The control is a scrambled-oligo transfection, so `untreated` would be false.
- **Infection.** `characteristics[infection]` = `uninfected` (EFO:0001460). The SILAC arm used synthetic oligos only. L. donovani infection appears only in the Ago pull-down experiments, which are not part of this deposit.
- **Cell line.** THP-1, CVCL_0006: Homo sapiens, blood, macrophage (PMA-differentiated), childhood acute monocytic leukemia (EFO:1001944), Japanese, 1Y, male, child stage (1-4 yo) (HsapDv:0000265), RPMI-1640 (SILAC formulation).
- **Templates.** ms-proteomics v1.2.0, human v1.1.0 and cell-lines v1.1.0. `comment[sdrf version]` is v1.1.0.

## Technical values
- Instrument: impact II (MS:1002666), DDA, CID, TOF MS2.
- MS1 scan range: 200–2000 m/z.
- Digestion: Trypsin plus Lys-C, in two `cleavage agent details` columns. The Lys-C column was inserted by `scripts/postbuild.py`, which can be reversed exactly. The MaxQuant search rule was Trypsin/P with 2 missed cleavages.
- Fixed modification: Carbamidomethyl C.
- Variable modifications: Oxidation M, Acetyl Protein N-term and Deamidated N,Q (summary.txt; the paper omits Deamidation). The labels are UNIMOD 188, 481, 267 and 259, set to Variable.
- Precursor tolerance: 10 ppm, from the protocol text.
- Fragment tolerance: 40 ppm, from parameters.txt TOF. The paper says 0.05 Da.
- Reduction and alkylation reagents: not available.
- Search database: human only, as deposited.

## Limitations
These are listed in full in `PXD072695.evidence.json`. In short:
- The light channel is blocked.
- The Fig 2B legend conflicts with the rest of the paper: it says the Leu-arm control was scrambled Ld-tRF-Asp. The majority reading is annotated.
- Re-acquisition of the Leu runs is an inference.
- `uninfected` rests on the absence of any infection step in the protocol.
- The paper's MaxQuant version (1.5.1.0) and fragment tolerance differ from the deposit. The deposited values are used.
- The raw archives were not opened.
