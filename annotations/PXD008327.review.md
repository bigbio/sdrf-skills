## Findings

No blocker or important findings.

- **m1 (minor, spec_compliance)**: columns 21–22 hold two `comment[cleavage agent details]` columns (Trypsin, Lys-C). The ms-proteomics 1.2.0 template does not mark this column `cardinality: multiple`, and the default is single (TEMPLATES.adoc:437; sdrf-template.schema.json:214–217). The paper and the PRIDE protocol fully support Lys-C. The spec's own examples at dc9be283 (PXD013923, PXD002137, PXD042173) use the same pattern, and parse_sdrf accepts it. *Fix:* none needed now. If maintainers make single cardinality strict, drop column 22 and record Lys-C as a manifest limitation.
- **m2 (minor, design)**: the heavy rows' sampling time `0 minute` means the time of metaphase arrest, not time after release. This is disclosed in the manifest. *Optional:* `not applicable` for the heavy sampling time.
- **m3 (minor, specificity)**: the organism is species-level NCBITaxon:4932. A strain-level term exists (NCBITaxon:580240 "Saccharomyces cerevisiae W303"), and the paper says W303. *Optional* refinement; the species level matches PRIDE.
