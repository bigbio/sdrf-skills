## Study
*Upregulation of CD73 Confers Acquired Radioresistance and is Required for Maintaining Irradiation-selected
Pancreatic Cancer Cells in a Mesenchymal State.* Nguyen AM et al., Mol Cell Proteomics 2020;
PMID 31879272, PMC7000112, doi:10.1074/mcp.RA119.001779. Full text read from the PMC HTML render
(Europe PMC fullTextXML gave HTTP 500; `sdrf-tools fetch` failed for the same reason).

Design: a 2-plex SILAC comparison of the irradiation-selected radioresistant line MIA PaCa-2-R
(**light**) against parental MIA PaCa-2 (**heavy**, Arg-13C6 / Lys-13C6-15N2). Each population was split
into soluble and membrane proteins; the soluble mix and the membrane mix were each run on one SDS-PAGE
lane cut into 10 slices, giving 10 + 10 RAW files on an LTQ Orbitrap XL (DDA, CID, ion-trap MS2).
No biological replicates (stated explicitly in the paper), and no label swap.

- **Instrument** `LTQ Orbitrap XL` (MS:1000556), from the paper. PRIDE lists the generic "LTQ Orbitrap"; RAW header range-reads show only "LTQ Orbitrap MS"/"Thermo Finnigan LTQ" (no contradiction, model block not reached).

- **Cleavage** Trypsin (MS:1001251); **fractionation** SDS PAGE (PRIDE:0000568, descendant of PRIDE:0000550).
- **Modifications** (no mqpar.xml deposited; from paper/PRIDE protocol): Carbamidomethyl C Fixed (UNIMOD:4), Oxidation M Variable (UNIMOD:35; also confirmed by "Oxidation (M) site IDs" in the deposited tables), Acetyl PP=Any N-term Variable (UNIMOD:1), and the SILAC heavy labels Label:13C(6) on R (UNIMOD:188, Arg6) and Label:13C(6)15N(2) on K (UNIMOD:259, Lys8), both `MT=Variable` (convention used in curated SILAC SDRFs, e.g. PXD000228).
- **Precursor tolerance** 4.5 ppm (paper; MaxQuant main search).
- **Sample**: Homo sapiens; pancreas (Cellosaurus derived-from site, UBERON:0001264); disease `undifferentiated pancreatic carcinoma` (MONDO:0006478 ← Cellosaurus NCIt C5722); age 65Y, sex male (Cellosaurus donor); ancestry category `European ancestry` (HANCESTRO:0005, from Cellosaurus "Population: Caucasian"); developmental stage `adult` (EFO:0001272, from donor age 65Y); cell line MIA PaCa-2 / CVCL_0428; culture medium DMEM (NCIT:C185409); biological replicate 1.
- **Factor**: `factor value[treatment]` = `Gamma Radiation` (NCIT:C44386) for the radioresistant rows and `untreated` for the parental rows (the control value the template prescribes). **"Gamma Radiation" describes how the resistant clone was selected** (60 Gy of fractionated γ-irradiation over 6 weeks, after which the surviving clones were expanded and maintained like the parental cells). The resistant cells were not irradiated just before harvest. Do not read this column as freshly irradiated vs untreated. It marks the stably radioresistant derivative line vs its parent. The paper says the radioresistance "was stable and was retained after more than 30 passages". So **Gamma Radiation records how the resistant line was selected**: 60 Gy over 6 weeks, then more than 30 passages in culture. It does not record an exposure before harvest.
