## Findings — PASS (minor only)

1. **Provenance gap in the manifest.** The PRIDE data-processing protocol says limma was "applied on the protein heavy to light ratios", which conflicts with the xlsx "(Light/Heavy)". The manifest does not record this conflict (rule 6). The PSM-level check above resolves it in favour of the xlsx.
   *Fix:* add a manifest limitation that records the conflict and how it was resolved.
2. **Secondary searches not encoded** (`comment[modification parameters]`). Only the primary Mascot search is encoded. The semi-tryptic second round (10 ppm; Phospho ST/Y, Propionamide C, dynamic Carbamidomethyl C) and the cRAP search are left out. This is disclosed.
   *Optional:* add `Propionamide C Variable`, given the acrylamide alkylation.
3. **convert-openms fails on rows 61–64** because of their `not applicable` tolerances. This follows the campaign convention and is disclosed. A reprocessing run should drop those rows.
4. **Cell type and zygosity.** Fossale calls these "neuronal precursor" lines and made both heterozygous and homozygous KI lines. The SDRF's 'cerebellar granule cell' rests on the PRIDE wording, and zygosity is not asserted.
   *Optional:* note in the manifest that the cell type follows the depositor's wording.
5. **Disclosed inferences.** The gel-slice order comes from run numbers, and the independence of the replicate cultures is assumed. Both are disclosed, and neither affects the genotype comparison.
