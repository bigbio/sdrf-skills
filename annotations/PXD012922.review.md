## Findings

No blocker or important findings.

1. **minor** (technical-metadata). comment[fragment mass tolerance], rows 1-40: Value 'not available' is spec-legal but makes parse_sdrf convert-openms fail, so the SDRF cannot be fed to quantms/OpenMS without a manual edit.  
   Evidence and fix: RAW Membrane-1 method: MS2 = 'ITMS + c norm ... Dep MS/MS'; paper states 'MS/MS error tolerance 20 ppm' (MaxQuant FTMS setting, not applied to ITMS spectra); no mqpar.xml/parameters.txt deposited. Fix only if evidence appears (e.g. author-confirmed ITMS tolerance); otherwise keep and document, as done.

2. **minor** (semantics). characteristics[treatment] / factor value[treatment], rows 1-20: 'Gamma Radiation' encodes the selection history of an irradiation-selected clone (expanded and passaged >30 times without IR), not an acute treatment; a reader may take it as irradiated-vs-unirradiated cells.  
   Evidence and fix: Paper: clones surviving 60 Gy 'were expanded and maintained in the same manner as the parental cells'; 'radioresistance was stable ... after more than 30 passages'. No template-licensed phenotype/derived-line column exists for cell-lines (characteristics[phenotype] is clinical-metadata only) and Cellosaurus has no MIA PaCa-2-R entry, so this is the best available encoding; source name carries the 'MIA PaCa-2-R' identity.

3. **minor** (completeness). file (recommended columns): Recommended columns absent: characteristics[individual] (human), characteristics[passage number], characteristics[sample storage temperature] (cell-lines), characteristics[sample type] (base 1.1.0).  
   Evidence and fix: Template YAMLs at sdrf-templates 7da1747 mark these 'recommended'; validator does not require them. Could be added as 'not available' (passage number: paper gives only '>30 passages' for the resistant line), or sample type 'study sample' after OLS check.

4. **minor** (provenance). characteristics[cell type], rows 1-40: 'epithelial cell' rests only on the PRIDE submitter tag; Cellosaurus gives no cell type and the paper describes MIA PaCa-2-R as mesenchymal-like.  
   Evidence and fix: PRIDE organismParts CL:0000066; Cellosaurus CVCL_0428 has no cell-type annotation; PRIDE description 'mesenchymal-like cells'. Value describes epithelial origin; acceptable, limitation already recorded in manifest.

5. **minor** (provenance). comment[modification parameters] (Acetyl, 3rd), rows 1-40: PP=Any N-term follows the paper wording 'peptide N terminus'; MaxQuant's usual setting is Acetyl (Protein N-term) and no mqpar.xml confirms which was used.  
   Evidence and fix: Paper/PRIDE dataProcessingProtocol wording; deposited tables have no Acetyl site columns. Keep unless the search parameters become available.

6. **minor** (design). comment[label] vs factor value[treatment]: Label is fully confounded with condition (no label swap, n=1 per fraction); inherent to the deposited study, not an annotation error.  
   Evidence and fix: Paper: 'Instead of performing biological replicates ... we fractionated cellular proteins into soluble and membrane fractions'.
