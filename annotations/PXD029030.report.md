## Study
- **Paper.** Wang MJ, …, Burgering BM. "Metabolic rewiring in keratinocytes by miR-31-5p identifies therapeutic intervention for psoriasis". *EMBO Mol Med* 2023;15(4):e15674, doi:10.15252/emmm.202215674, PMID 36855912, PMC10086589 (CC BY).
- **Design.** The paper runs SILAC proteomics in HEK 293T and HaCaT cells transfected with a miR-31 mimic or a control mimic (30 nM, 48 h). ## Key decisions and evidence

### Channel map (rule 6)
- **Explicit design statement.** The paper and the PRIDE protocol state the generic design: the miRNA lysate from heavy medium plus the control (SCR) lysate from light medium is "reverse"; the opposite pairing is "forward".
- **Which experiment is which: from the deposited data.**
  - Experiment d log2 H/L vs Dataset EV1 "HEK 293T Log fc (miR-31/Ctrl)": r = +0.96 (n = 1033). So d has miR-31 heavy.
  - Experiment b vs the same values: r = −0.95. So b has miR-31 light.
  - The deposited Perseus lists `L31up.txt` / `L31down.txt` agree: their genes have b < 0 and d > 0 for up, and the reverse for down.
  - Marker genes: TACC1 b +1.84 / d −2.18 (EV1 −1.95); POMP b −1.75 / d +2.20 (EV1 +2.04).
- **Cell line.** HEK293T is derived the same way. The match to the EV1 HEK 293T columns (0.96) is clearly better than to the HaCaT columns (0.80). No deposited text names the cell line of these files: this is a limitation.

### Treatment encoding
- The control is a transfected negative-control mimic, so it is written `control miRNA mimic`, not `untreated`: `untreated` would be false for this sample (a deliberate deviation from rule 4).
- The values are free text, since no NCIT/EFO term exists for a miRNA mimic. validate-sdrf gives warnings only.

### Technical values (from mqpar.xml / parameters.txt / summary.txt of the SCX search)
- **Modifications:** fixed Carbamidomethyl (C); variable Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term) and Deamidated (N, Q).
- **SILAC labels:** Arg10 = UNIMOD:267 and Lys8 = UNIMOD:259, `MT=Variable` (rule 11).
- **Conflict on the heavy label:** the paper/PRIDE text says 13C6-Arg / "3C6"-Lys, but the deposited search, which quantified about 6,000 protein groups with ratios, used Arg10/Lys8. The SDRF follows the search.
- **Tolerances:** precursor 2 ppm (mainSearchTol); fragment 0.5 Da (ITMS, because MS2 was acquired in the ion trap).
- **Cleavage agent:** Trypsin (digestion enzyme; MaxQuant used the Trypsin/P rule, rule 2).
- **Acquisition:** DDA (Top Speed).
- **Instrument settings (raw headers):** Orbitrap Fusion; HCD at 30 NCE; MS2 in the linear ion trap; MS1 400–1500 m/z.
- **Fractionation:** SCX, taken from the file names. The paper says the UPLC fractionation was "based on molecular mass".
- **Reagents:** reduction with TCEP, alkylation with chloroacetamide (lysis buffer).

### Biological replicates
The miR-31 culture of b and that of d are separate cultures, and so are the two control cultures. They are numbered 1 and 2 within each factor-value group.
