The flipped orientation gives the opposite sign for the anchor proteins (SCARB2, GBA, SNCA and MVP) in every experiment, so the published heatmap does not admit it.

The result does not depend on which column is which experiment. All six heatmap columns show SCARB2 and GBA up in KO. Each pair's raw ratios have opposite signs: H/M is +1.58 / −1.65, and H/L is +1.09 / −1.31. So in each pair, one mix must have KO heavy and the other KO in the lighter channel. Without being told to, SNCA (the paper's central finding, confirmed by Western blot and ELISA in Fig. 3B/C) comes out positive in all four experiments under the SDRF orientation: +0.60, +0.20, +0.80 and +0.87.

**Conflict with Fig. 2A.** The SDRF contradicts the Fig. 2A schematic, which draws mix 1 as KO in Arg6Lys4 and every mix as Arg6Lys4 vs Arg10Lys8. The schematic is not tied to file names, and the deposited data already disprove it as a literal key: the #899 mixes use light (Arg0Lys0), not medium. The author-published per-replicate KO/WT values (Fig. 2B) are the stronger source, and the deposited data reproduce them. Following them satisfies batch rule 6: there is an explicit source (Fig. 2B), the map was checked against deposited ratios, and the conflict is recorded in the manifest's `comment[label]` claim and in limitation 2. The SDRF does not assert clone identity (KO-1/WT-1 and so on), which is correct.

## Findings (all minor)

- **m1 — `characteristics[disease]` = Fabry disease on KO rows** (rows with `GLA knockout`). These are CRISPR-engineered subclones of a line from a healthy 3-year-old donor. The paper does call them "Fabry clones/podocytes", so the value is defensible as an in vitro disease model. It does, however, duplicate the genotype factor.
  - Fix (optional): keep it, and add a manifest note that the disease value is the modelled disease, not the donor's.
- **m2 — The genome-wide replicate concordance is weak.** Under the SDRF orientation, the oriented KO/WT log2 ratios correlate as follows: #899_s1 vs #899_s2 r = −0.64 (n = 2538), and #867_s1 vs #867_s2 r = +0.06. The raw H/L of the two #899 mixes therefore correlate at +0.64, which is a label-specific bias typical of label swaps. The orientation rests on the strong, sign-flipping lysosomal proteins of Fig. 2B, not on whole-proteome agreement.
  - Fix (optional): add this caveat to manifest limitation 2.
- **m3 — `characteristics[developmental stage]` = juvenile stage** for a 3-year-old donor. The term is valid, but a human-specific term (for example HsapDv "child stage") would be more precise. The column is optional in cell-lines.
- **m4 — `comment[precursor mass tolerance]` = not available**, so `convert-openms` fails. MaxQuant 1.4.1.2 `parameters.txt` does not record the value and no mqpar.xml was deposited. The value is accepted under campaign precedent, and the reviewer did not want the MaxQuant default inferred.
- **m5 — Manifest residual channel shares** (L 3.4% / 4.4%, M 3.8% / 3.6%) differ from the reviewer's recomputation over every complete row (L 5.8% / 7.6%). The channel-use conclusion is unaffected.
