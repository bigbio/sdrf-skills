## Questions the caller asked me to test

### 1. Is the scaffold donor → disease mapping (SAL = IPF, FL/Lures = normal) acceptable, or should these runs be blocked?

**Verdict: acceptable. Do not block.** The map rests on four independent pieces of evidence, and only one step is an inference.

- **Donor identity per file is explicit.** Each raw header carries a sample ID and a vial. I re-read all 47 S1803 headers. All 94 rows match their file's sample ID and vial, with 0 mismatches. There are 8 codes. Seven have 6 runs each. FL11 has 5 runs, because `Emma_S1803_214` (vial A5) is in the MaxQuant search but was not deposited.
- **Group of origin is explicit.** Paper section 4.2 states that the healthy donor lungs came from Lund University hospital and the IPF lung samples from Sahlgrenska University hospital. The codes split 4 SAL against 4 FL/Lures. Paper section 2.3 states that four patients per group were each cultured in duplicate.
- **The one inference.** Reading "SAL" as Sahlgrenska is an abbreviation reading. The manifest says so openly in its limitations ("No source spells out the code-to-group key").
- **Data check in both directions.** I recomputed it from `txt.repopulated/proteinGroups.txt`, light-channel intensity per MaxQuant experiment, excluding contaminants and reverse hits. The paper's Results (§2.2, not the abstract) report that laminin β3/γ2/α3 and collagen IV α3/α4 decrease in IPF, and that nidogen-2 and collagens increase.
  - LAMB3 separates the groups completely: the SAL maximum is 1.67e8, below the FL/Lures minimum of 3.29e8.
  - For LAMB3, LAMC2 and COL4A4, the SAL medians are 0, while the FL/Lures medians are around 1e9.
  - In the opposite direction, NID2 and COL1A1 are both about 1.8× higher in SAL (median ratio).
  - The swapped assignment (SAL = healthy) is contradicted by every marker.

This meets campaign rule 6. There is an explicit textual source (Methods 4.2), an explicit per-file identity (the raw header) and a data check against the deposited quantities. The label is not derived from the abstract's biological direction alone. The light/heavy channel meaning is itself stated explicitly in paper §2.4 and §4.6 and in the PRIDE description, and the data confirm it (Labels1 Arg6;Lys8, heavy share 2–21% in every run). The manifest overstates part of the data check; see minor finding 1.

### 2. The compared variable is in custom `scaffold disease` columns, while heavy rows carry disease `normal`

**Correct by design.**

- The heavy channel holds proteins made by fibroblasts from one healthy donor (paper §4.6). Putting IPF in `characteristics[disease]` on those rows would therefore be false.
- Putting the factor on `factor value[disease]` would pool heavy proteins from IPF scaffolds with the healthy group.
- `characteristics[scaffold disease]` and `factor value[scaffold disease]` are identical on both channels of every run, with 48 IPF rows and 46 normal rows. `convert-openms -t2` therefore yields the two intended conditions.
- `characteristics[scaffold individual]` carries the donor on both channels. `characteristics[individual]` is `not available` on the heavy rows, because the fibroblast donor is unidentified.

The column names are not ontology terms (minor finding 2), but no template column expresses "disease of the culture substrate donor".

### 3. Replicate numbering

Biological replicates are numbered 1–4 per donor within each scaffold-disease group. All 6 cultures of a donor share the number, and both channels of a run share it too. The paper defines the replicates explicitly in §4.6 and in the legends of Supplements 3 and 4: "biological replicates per group n = 4, with technical replicates n = 2". The duplicate scaffold pieces from one donor are therefore not independent biological samples.

Campaign rule 3 aims to keep independent cultures from merging. That is still respected, because every culture has its own source name (with the vial in it) and its own raw file. `comment[technical replicate]` = 1 is correct: each culture was injected once.

Row identity is unique. No (source name, biological replicate, technical replicate, fraction) key repeats, and no (file, label) pair repeats.

### 4. Sampling time is `not available`

**Correct; it must not be filled.**

- The paper and the supplementary file give no per-file or per-vial day. The Supplement 3 correlation map is labelled `HL_1_day_1 … IPF_4_day_9`. It uses the paper's HL/IPF IDs, not the deposit codes, and shows means only.
- The three vial blocks (A1–B4, B5–C8, C9–D12) each contain every donor twice; I verified this. Any block-to-day assignment would still be a guess.
- I recomputed the median heavy share per block: 0.044, 0.037 and 0.045. That is not monotonic, so the data do not settle the order.
- Both disease groups are balanced within every block. Time is therefore an unannotated but balanced nuisance variable for the disease comparison. It is not a pooled fake group in the sense of campaign rule 7.
- The limitation is recorded in the manifest and in BLOCKED.md.

### 5. The 12 blocked decellularized-scaffold runs (`Emma_S1801_*`)

**Correctly blocked.**

- The FTP listing has exactly 59 RAW files: 47 S1803 and 12 S1801.
- In MaxQuant, all 12 S1801 runs belong to `txt.scaffold` Experiment 1 (Multiplicity 2, Labels1 Lys6). Their pooled heavy share is 0.09% excluding contaminants (BLOCKED.md says 0.15%, which is immaterial). They are label-free in substance.
- Their raw sample IDs are `SILAC_sample_1` to `SILAC_sample_12`. Nothing maps these to HL 1/HL 2/IPF 1/IPF 2 or to a replicate. MaxQuant pooled them, so it gives no per-file identity either.
- Including them would require guessing the donor and disease. They would also need their own label-free SDRF under campaign rule 12.
- BLOCKED.md lists exactly these 12 files and what would unblock them.

### 6. Arg6/Lys8 labels

**Correct.**

- Paper §4.6 states 13C6 L-arginine (Thermo 88210) and 13C6 15N2 L-lysine (Thermo 88209).
- MaxQuant `summary.txt` shows Labels0 empty and Labels1 `Arg6;Lys8` on all 55 searched runs.
- The SDRF uses `NT=Label:13C(6);AC=UNIMOD:188;TA=R;MT=Variable` and `NT=Label:13C(6)15N(2);AC=UNIMOD:259;TA=K;MT=Variable`. OLS confirms both labels and accessions.
- `SILAC light` / `SILAC heavy` are set on the odd and even rows respectively.
- The other modifications match the deposited search (parameters.txt and summary.txt): Carbamidomethyl C fixed; Oxidation M and Acetyl Protein N-term (with `PP=`) variable.

## Minor findings

1. **Manifest overstates the data check** (claim at `characteristics[scaffold disease] … light rows`). The manifest says LAMB3/LAMC2/COL4A3/COL4A4 are about 0 in all SAL runs and 1e8–5e9 in all FL/Lures runs, "with no overlap". That holds for LAMB3 only.
   - SAL24 has COL4A3 2.93e8 and COL4A4 1.47e8/1.93e8 (vials D7/D8). These exceed FL10 B8 COL4A3 (1.95e8) and FL11 A6 COL4A4 (8.0e7).
   - SAL24 D7 LAMC2 is 9.35e7, against an FL/Lures minimum of 9.37e7.
   - **Fix:** say that LAMB3 separates the groups completely; the other three separate by medians (SAL 0 vs about 1e9). Add NID2 and COL1A1 (about 1.8× higher in SAL) as the opposite-direction check.
2. **Custom column names are not ontology terms.** Spec "Extending Templates" says column names MUST be ontology terms, and `scaffold disease` / `scaffold individual` are not. This is acceptable, because no template term fits and the values themselves are ontology terms. Curated precedent instead uses `characteristics[growth substrate]` for decellularized scaffolds (PXD072354). Optionally, note that alternative in the manifest.
3. **Optional `characteristics[smoking status]` (clinical-metadata).** Table 1 gives smoking history "Yes" for all four IPF donors, which is group-uniform, so it would hold for every SAL light row without the ID key. The healthy donors are mixed. Leaving it out is safe, since "Yes" does not say whether the donor is a current or former smoker. Record it in the manifest as considered and left out.
4. **BLOCKED.md wording.** It gives the S1801 heavy share as 0.15%; I get 0.09% excluding contaminants. Both support "label-free in substance". State the basis of the figure.
