## Study
Elowsson Rendin L, …, Westergren-Thorsson G. "Matrisome Properties of Scaffolds Direct Fibroblasts in Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis." *Int J Mol Sci* 2019;20(16):4013, doi:10.3390/ijms20164013, PMID 31426504, PMC6719040 (open access).

The study has two MS parts:
1. **Decellularized scaffolds.** Healthy and IPF scaffolds, 2 donors × 3 replicates per group (Fig. 2, Fig. S2).
2. **Repopulated scaffolds.** Primary lung fibroblasts from one healthy donor were labelled with heavy Arg (13C6) and Lys (13C6 15N2). They were seeded on scaffolds from 4 healthy and 4 IPF donors, in duplicate, and cultured for 1, 3 or 9 days.

Not in sdrf-annotated-datasets.

## Design and channel map
Each raw file is one repopulated scaffold culture.

| label | source name pattern | meaning | rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| SILAC light | `<donor>_scaffold_<vial>_preexisting-scaffold-proteins` | pre-existing scaffold (ECM) proteins of the scaffold donor | odd rows |
| SILAC heavy | `<donor>_scaffold_<vial>_fibroblast-newly-synthesized-proteins` | proteins newly synthesized by the heavy-labelled healthy fibroblasts | even rows |

**Channel map (rule 6).**
- **Stated in the sources.** Paper Methods 4.6/4.7 and the PRIDE description: heavy medium was used "to differentiate between preexisting scaffold proteins and newly synthesized proteins".
- **Search output.** `summary.txt` gives Labels1 Arg6;Lys8.
- **Data check.** The light channel carries the donor's scaffold signature (below).

**Scaffold donor and disease.**
- Each raw header has a sample ID and a vial position:
  - FL9, FL10, FL11 and Lures18 (Lund) on 23 runs;
  - SAL23, SAL24, SAL25 and SAL26 (Sahlgrenska) on 24 runs.
- Paper 4.2: healthy lungs came from Lund University hospital, and IPF samples came from Sahlgrenska University hospital. So SAL is IPF and FL/Lures is normal.
- **Cross-check (`datacheck.tsv`).** The paper reports laminin γ2/β3/α3 and collagen IV α3/α4 as decreased in IPF scaffolds.
  - In the light channel, LAMB3, LAMC2, COL4A3 and COL4A4 are about 0 in all 24 SAL runs.
  - They are 1e8–5e9 in all 23 FL/Lures runs.
  - The two groups do not overlap.

**Factor.** `factor value[scaffold disease]` is `idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis` (MONDO:0800504; MONDO:0008345 is obsolete) or `normal`. It is on both channels, because the compared variable is the scaffold. `characteristics[disease]` describes the material itself:
- light rows: the scaffold donor's disease;
- heavy rows: `normal` (healthy fibroblast donor).

**Biological replicate.** The scaffold donor, numbered 1–4 within each group:
- normal: FL9=1, FL10=2, FL11=3, Lures18=4;
- IPF: SAL23=1, SAL24=2, SAL25=3, SAL26=4.

The paper defines its biological replicates the same way ("n = 4 per group, technical replicates n = 2"). Each culture has its own source name. `comment[technical replicate]` is 1.

**Other sample values.**
- **Organism part:** lung.
- **Light rows:** material type `tissue`, cell type `not applicable`, `characteristics[individual]` = the scaffold donor code, developmental stage `adult` (Table 1: all 8 donors are 41–86 Y).
- **Heavy rows:** material type `cell`, cell type `fibroblast of lung` (CL:0002553), individual `not available` (the fibroblast donor is unnamed).

## Values left `not available` and why
- **`characteristics[sampling time]`, all rows.** The day of each culture is undocumented.
  - The runs form three vial blocks of 16 (A1–B4, B5–C8, C9–D12), and each block holds every donor twice.
  - No source says which block is which day.
  - The heavy share does not rise monotonically across the blocks.
- **`characteristics[age]` / `characteristics[sex]`, all rows.** Table 1 is keyed HL 1–4 / IPF 1–4, and no source maps those IDs to the deposited codes. The fibroblast donor's demographics are not stated.
- **`characteristics[ancestry category]`:** not reported.
- **Heavy rows:** `characteristics[individual]` and developmental stage.

## Open items / points for the reviewer
1. **Inference: scaffold disease.** No source spells out the SAL = IPF / FL+Lures = healthy key. It rests on the hospital names and is cross-validated by the paper's own IPF basement-membrane signature.
2. **Encoding choice: heavy channel.** The heavy channel is encoded as fibroblast material (`disease` normal, cell type fibroblast), and the comparison is carried by the custom scaffold columns.
3. **Light channel purity.** The light channel is not pure ECM: vimentin heavy share is 3–73%. This fits incomplete labelling after 5 days of pre-culture. The channel meaning follows the paper.
4. **Not used: Lures18 = HL 4.** Lures18 is probably HL 4 (the only resection sample; Table 1 lists squamous cell carcinoma, tissue taken from an unaffected area). This is an inference and was not used.
5. **MaxQuant version conflict.** parameters.txt says 1.5.2.8; the paper and PRIDE say 1.5.3.30.
6. **Blocked items:** the 12 S1801 runs and the time-point attribute (BLOCKED.md).
7. **Review:** not performed here (external hand-off).
