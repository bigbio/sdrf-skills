## Study
Lucena ACR, Amorim JC, de Paula Lima CV, Batista M, Krieger MA, de Godoy LMF, Marchini FK. *Quantitative phosphoproteome and proteome analyses emphasize the influence of phosphorylation events during the nutritional stress of Trypanosoma cruzi: the initial moments of in vitro metacyclogenesis.* Cell Stress Chaperones 2019. doi:10.1007/s12192-019-01018-7, PMID 31368045, PMC6717228.

*T. cruzi* Dm28c epimastigotes were grown in LIT medium with 10% FBS for 5 days. The ST-0min sample is the parasites at early stationary phase. The stressed samples were washed into TAU and held there for 5 min, 15 min, 1 h or 2 h (nutritional stress, NS).

The super-SILAC standard was made from Dm28c epimastigotes grown in LM-14B with Lys-8 and Arg-10. Cells were taken after 3 and 5 days of culture and after 5 min, 15 min, 1 h and 2 h of NS, then pooled in equal proportions. The standard was mixed 1:1 by cell number with each time-point sample.

Each mix was digested by FASP and split into 4 high-pH RP fractions (ACN 10, 14, 18 and 60%). About 50 µg went to proteome analysis, which was injected twice. The remainder was TiO2 phospho-enriched and injected once. Runs were acquired on an LTQ Orbitrap XL ETD (DDA top-10, CID in the ion trap) and searched with MaxQuant 1.5.2.8, with separate proteome and phospho searches.

## Key decisions with evidence
1. **Multiplexing verified (rule 14).** In summary.txt and summary_phospho.txt, all 120 raws have Multiplicity 2, an empty Labels0 and Labels1 = Arg10;Lys8.
2. **Channel map.** Light = the time-point sample; heavy = the super-SILAC standard. This is **stated** in the PRIDE protocol (the Lys-8/Arg-10 culture is the standard) and consistent with three things:
   - the MaxQuant label set;
   - the fact that only the standard was grown in labelled medium (the time-point parasites were in LIT/TAU, which is unlabelled);
   - proteinGroups reporting "Ratio H/L <experiment>".

   A data-level direction check, like the blood-protein check in PXD009868, is not possible here. Both channels are the same organism and stage, and the standard pools every time point.
3. **Super-SILAC encoding (rule 18, precedent PXD009868).** Each mix has its own heavy source: "super-SILAC standard (…composition…), aliquot spiked into <timepoint> R<n> (<portion>)". These rows have sample type `reference`, biological replicate `pooled`, pooled sample `pooled`, and sampling time, treatment and factor `not applicable`. `SN=` is not used because the pool components are not SDRF sources; the source name lists them instead.
4. **Proteome vs phospho portions.** Both come from one digest, so they share the time-point/replicate identity. They have distinct source names (suffix "total proteome" / "phosphopeptide-enriched") and differ in `characteristics[enrichment process]`, with `enrichment of phosphorylated protein` EFO:0010806 on the phospho rows. Without distinct names the row coordinate (source, biological replicate, technical replicate, fraction) would collide.
5. **Modifications from the deposited searches (rule 5).** The file was built twice with identical headers and then concatenated by a script.
   - Both builds: Carbamidomethyl C Fixed (parameters*.txt); Oxidation M, Acetyl Protein N-term (PP=), Arg10 UNIMOD:267 and Lys8 UNIMOD:259, all Variable (summary*.txt, rule 11).
   - Phospho STY (UNIMOD:21) only on the 80 phospho rows. On proteome rows that column is `not applicable`.
6. **Factor.** The study compares time in nutritional stress, so the only factor is `factor value[sampling time]` = 0/5/15/60/120 minute. Minutes are used throughout so the values sort. `characteristics[treatment]` says whether stress was applied: `untreated` for ST-0min (rule 4), `starvation` EFO:0022074 otherwise. Treatment is not a second factor because it is collinear with time.
7. **Organism and stage.** Organism is `Trypanosoma cruzi Dm28c` (NCBITaxon:1416333, OLS label). Cell type is `epimastigote` (BTO:0000409, crithidial stage of trypanosomatids; the column permits BTO). Organism part is `not applicable` (unicellular). Material type is `whole organism`.
8. **Technical values.**
   - Instrument: LTQ Orbitrap XL ETD (MS:1000639). The raw Finnigan header says "LTQ Orbitrap ETD" and the method says "LTQ Orbitrap XL MS"; the PRIDE protocol says the same.
   - Dissociation: CID (MS:1000133). MS2 analyser: radial ejection linear ion trap (MS:1000083).
   - Fractionation: hpHRP (PRIDE:0000564), fractions 1–4 = F10/F14/F18/F60, matching MaxQuant Fraction.
   - Cleavage agent: Trypsin (MS:1001251). This is the digestion enzyme; the MaxQuant search rule was Trypsin/P (rule 2).
   - Tolerances: precursor 4.5 ppm (main search, PRIDE data protocol); fragment 0.5 Da (MaxQuant ITMS).

## Conflicts found and how they were resolved
- **PRIDE disease vs samples.** PRIDE lists Chagas disease (DOID:12140), but the samples are axenic parasite cultures. Disease is set to `not applicable`; the project-level disease is not broadcast onto the rows.
- **PRIDE instruments.** PRIDE lists both LTQ Orbitrap XL ETD and the generic LTQ Orbitrap. The raw files and the protocol give the XL ETD.
- **reconcile false positive.** reconcile reported "protocol names 'LTQ Orbitrap XL'", but the protocol text is literally "LTQ Orbitrap XL ETD". The refutation is in validation.txt.

## Stated vs inferred
- **Stated** (PRIDE protocol or MaxQuant files): organism and strain, stage, time points, standard composition, label assignment, fractions, injections, enrichment, instrument, CID/ion trap, tolerances, modifications.
- **Inferred**: that R1/R2 are biological replicates, and the mapping of material type and treatment terms.

## Judgement calls a reviewer should attack hardest
1. **R1/R2 as biological replicates.** The alternative is replicate processing of one culture, which would make them technical replicates. Evidence: R is a separate MaxQuant experiment in both searches and sits above the injection-duplicate level. No accessible text says "independent cultures".
2. **Template choice and organism-level encoding.** Declaring only ms-proteomics with no organism layer. Encoding the parasite as cell type `epimastigote` (BTO) with material type `whole organism` and organism part `not applicable`.
3. **Factor and treatment encoding.** The factor is `factor value[sampling time]` (minutes in TAU), with `starvation` EFO:0022074 for TAU incubation and `untreated` for ST-0min. ST-0min is harvested at early stationary phase without TAU, which is itself mildly nutrient-limited. Also contestable: separate proteome and phospho source names for the same digest, and `not applicable` factor on the reference rows.
