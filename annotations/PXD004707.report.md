## Design
There are four independent experiments (thesis #1–#4 = deposit SILAC1–SILAC4). In each one, HCV-infected (Jc1 NS5AB reporter virus) and uninfected Huh7.5 SILAC cultures were mixed 1:1. Lipid droplets were isolated, separated by SDS-PAGE, and the gel bands were digested and measured.
