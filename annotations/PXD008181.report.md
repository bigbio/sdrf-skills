## Study
"Quantitative proteome analysis of Merkel cell carcinoma cell lines using SILAC", by Kotowski U … Mitulović G. *Clin Proteomics* 2019;16:42, doi:10.1186/s12014-019-9263-z, PMID 31889939, PMC6921584 (open access; full text and supplementary files read).

Each MCC cell line was grown with 13C6-Lys (heavy). The keratinocyte line HaCaT, the reference, was grown with light Lys. Arg was light in both. Lysates were mixed 1:1 per MCC line, digested with trypsin and injected unfractionated as technical replicates. The instrument was a Bruker maXis impact Q-TOF (DDA, CID). Identification used ProteinScape 3.1.5 with Mascot 2.6 (SwissProt human); quantification used WARP-LC 1.3.

## Scope
84 files are deposited: 14 RAW `.baf`, 14 Mascot `.dat`, 14 `.mzid.gz`, 14 `.mgf`, plus PRIDE-generated mgf.gz and mzTab files. All 14 `.baf` runs are annotated, 2 channel rows per run, giving 28 rows. Nothing is blocked.

**Partial scope against the paper.** The paper names six MCC lines. No BroLi run was deposited. MCC13 has only 2 of the 3 stated technical replicates.

- the paper states 13C6-Lys heavy vs light Lys and reports H/L ratios;
- all 14 Mascot `.dat` files carry Label:13C(6) (K).

How the table was set up:
- **Technical replicate order:** the run in the 2015 folder (`150531silac`) comes first, then the runs in the 2016 folder (`160817`) in ascending order. Folders come from the mgf headers.
- **File chain:** each `.baf` was linked to its mgf, `.dat` and mzid through the mgf header and the mzid SpectraData. The full chain is in the manifest.
- **Factor:** `factor value[cell line]`. HaCaT is a real level here, because it is the comparator; convert-openms finds 6 conditions.
- **Biological replicate:** 1 everywhere, with `pooled sample` = pooled. The paper says: "Three biological replicates were cultured per each cell line, and these were merged into one sample".
- **Sample type:** HaCaT rows are `reference` and MCC rows are `study sample`. This follows precedents PXD005854 and PXD009766.

## Channel map (rule 6)
- **Explicit source.** Methods: "13C6 l-Lysine-2HCl (heavy) … or l-Lysine-2HCl (light)". The Fig. 1 legend says: "MCC cells were cultured in medium supplemented with 13C6 l-Lysine-2HCl (heavy) and HaCaT cells … l-Lysine-2HCl (light)". The line in each run comes from the run-file prefix.
- **Data check** (`orientation.txt`, `scripts/markers.py`). The Mascot search was heavy-only, with Label:13C(6) K fixed, so any identified K-containing peptide is heavy. Results:
  - heavy chromogranin A PSMs appear in the MKL-1, MKL-2 and WaGa runs;
  - heavy TBB3 appears in MKL-2 and heavy ENO2 in MCC13 (both MCC/neuroendocrine markers);
  - the keratinocyte marker KRT5 is absent from MKL-1, MKL-2, PeTa and WaGa. In MCC13 it is seen almost only through K-free peptides, which carry no label (1 heavy vs 13 K-free PSMs).
- **Conclusion.** The data are consistent with the stated map and nothing conflicts with it. The check is weak: only 107–390 PSMs pass per mzid, and no H/L ratio table was deposited.
