## Findings

- **minor** [design] characteristics[biological replicate], mock rows (sources *_mock): Mock replicates are numbered 1-6 per time point on the assumption that the WT-paired and DsbA-paired mock lysates of one replicate are separate cultures. The paper only says 'the respective mock-treated BMDCs' (Fig. S1 not available). If they were one split culture, n is overstated (3, not 6). Evidence: Proteome-arm protein ratio correlations do not settle it: WT1-DsbA1 0.69 vs WT1-DsbA3 0.67. The manifest already records this limitation. Fix: Keep as is unless supplemental Fig. S1 or the authors show shared mock cultures; if they do, renumber WT/DsbA mock pairs to 1-3 and reconcile with PXD006759.
- **minor** [provenance] characteristics[age] all rows: '6W-8W' is a range at bone-marrow harvest. The spec allows ranges (README line 1382), but sdrf-tools score flags it as non-standard. Evidence: sdrf-tools score consistency 83/100 for this single item. Fix: None needed. Optionally note in the report that the cells were cultured 9 more days.
- **minor** [omission] file: The culture condition 'SILAC medium with 10% dialyzed FBS' (RPMI-based, GM-CSF) is documented but not encoded. No declared template licenses characteristics[culture medium], so it is not required. Evidence: PMC5750852 Infections and Treatments paragraph. Fix: Optional custom characteristics[culture medium] column.
