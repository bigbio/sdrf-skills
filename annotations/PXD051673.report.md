## Study
Belur NR, Bustos BI, Lubbe SJ, Mazzulli JR. "Nuclear aggregates of NONO/SFPQ and A-to-I-edited RNA in Parkinson's disease and dementia with Lewy bodies." *Neuron* 2024;112(15):2558-2580.e13. doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2024.05.003, PMID 38761794, PMC11309915.

The study used iPSC-derived midbrain dopaminergic neurons from a PD patient with heterozygous SNCA A53T, and the isogenic gene-corrected line. These are the Jaenisch lines WIBR-iPS-SNCA A53T / A53T-Corr (Soldner 2011, as identified in Cuddy 2019). The neurons were SILAC-labelled from day 40 (heavy Lys8/Arg10) and harvested at day 60 and day 90. Each culture was extracted sequentially into a Triton X-100-soluble fraction and an SDS-soluble (Triton-insoluble) fraction. Heavy and light extracts of the same fraction were mixed 1:1 and given a short gel run. Runs were on a Q Exactive HF and searched with MaxQuant 1.6.0.16. There are 4 culture wells per condition.

## Channel map (rule 6)
- **Text source.** The paper's Methods say results are "expressed as log fold change of heavy over light", and all results are reported as A53T relative to Corr (Fig 1B: "SILAC labeled A53T iPSn relative to corrected lines"). Together these give heavy = A53T. There is no single explicit "heavy = A53T" sentence in the accessible text. The supplementary tables are behind reCAPTCHA or Cloudflare and were not retrieved.
- **Data check.** Values are log2 normalized H/L from proteinGroups (`orientation.txt`):
  - NONO and SFPQ are +1.6 to +2.1 (batch 18-1234) and +0.5 to +1.2 (batch 18-1310) in every SDS run, and negative in every Triton run. FUS and PSPC1 follow the same pattern. This matches the paper's insoluble NONO/SFPQ/FUS/PSPC1 increase in A53T.
  - CYFIP2, CADPS, PRKAR2A and MAPT are negative in all Triton runs of both batches. This matches the reported decreases in PD iPSn (Fig 4E, Fig 6I).
- **Result.** No run has the opposite sign, so there is no label swap. Sources do not conflict.

## Key decisions
- **Disease.** A53T rows: `autosomal dominant Parkinson disease 1` (MONDO:0008200). Corr rows: `normal`, because the corrected isogenic line is the control. The project-level disease is not broadcast to it.
- **Genotype and solubility fraction** are characteristics and factor values. The fraction is a measured sample (rule 16), so it uses a custom `characteristics[protein solubility fraction]` column.
- **Biological replicate** is numbered 1-8 within each (genotype, fraction) group, one number per culture: batch 18-1234 wells 1-4, then batch 18-1310 wells 1-4. The S and T extracts of the same well share a number.
- **Donor data.** Age, sex, ancestry, developmental stage and Cellosaurus accession are `not available`. Cellosaurus has no entry for the WIBR-iPS-SNCA lines. CVCL_E3C7 "A53T" (Female, 49Y, Caucasian Italian, Contursi kindred) is a line the Khurana lab re-derived, probably from the same donor. No individual-level source ties it to these lines, so its values were not copied (task rule: patient values only from individual-level sources).
- **Instrument** is Q Exactive HF, from all 16 raw headers and the paper. PRIDE's "LTQ Orbitrap Elite" is wrong and was overridden. Other values from the raw method: HCD at NCE 30, Orbitrap MS2, MS1 300-2000 m/z.
- **Modifications** come from MaxQuant parameters.txt and summary.txt, which are identical for both searches:
  - fixed: Carbamidomethyl C;
  - variable: Oxidation M, Acetyl Protein N-term, and Pro6, written as Label:13C(5)15N(1) on P (UNIMOD:268) for arginine-to-proline conversion;
  - SILAC labels: Arg10 (UNIMOD:267) and Lys8 (UNIMOD:259), `MT=Variable`.
- **Tolerances.** Fragment tolerance is 20 ppm (FTMS). Precursor tolerance is `not available`: it is not recorded and no mqpar.xml was deposited.
- **Cleavage agent** is Trypsin, from the MaxQuant Trypsin/P search rule. Neither the paper nor PRIDE names the protease.
