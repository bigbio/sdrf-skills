## Answers to the specific test points

**(a) Using RawConverter .ms2 files as comment[data file].** Acceptable, minor note. None of the five deposits has a vendor .raw. PRIDE puts the .ms2 files in the RAW category, and curated SDRFs from the same IP2 pipeline (PXD005667, PXD006384) also use .ms2. However, every .ms2 header says `ScanType MS2`: the files hold no MS1 scans, so SILAC MS1 quantification cannot be redone from them. The mzid SpectraData and the MS1 scans live in the .mzXML copies. Recording the paired .mzXML per row is recommended.

**(b) Abundance channel map heavy = WT, light = HGPS.** Verified. The supplementary data descriptions say it explicitly: Supp Data 2 'WT cells (AG3258) were completely labeled with heavy isotope while HGPS cells (AG11498) were maintained in light ... %heavy <50% indicates enrichment in HGPS', and Supp Data 3 says the same for PRF168/PRF167. Methods, the Fig. 4a legend and the PRIDE protocol agree. A scripted check found the label map correct on every row of 006012, 006013 and 006014.

**(c) Pulse map and the two-rows-per-run encoding.** Verified. Light = pre-existing ('old' 12C) proteins and heavy = proteins newly synthesised during the 1–6 day pulse (Methods, Fig. 1 legend, Supp Data 1). The two channels of each run share the culture and biological replicate 1 but get distinct source names, as in curated PXD028934. A scripted check found label, sampling time, fraction and source name consistent on all 46 rows. Minor: the paper calls 0D 'no label', so the 0D heavy rows are a background channel and the manifest should say so.

**(d) _1/_2 in 006013/006014 as technical replicates.** Accepted. Each pair was searched jointly in one mzid (SpectraData lists both mzXML), feeds one supplementary table, and has similar scan counts. The producer discloses that this is an inference.

**(e) Per-timepoint modification sets and the inferred WT 1D-pellet search.** Re-derived from the protocol sections of all 20 pulse mzids. 0D, 2D and 6D: carbamidomethyl fixed + Phospho STY variable, missedCleavages -1. 1D and 4D: empty light-search mods and labels only, missedCleavages 1. All use 50/600 ppm. The SDRF matches on every row. WT_1D_pellet.mzid really is a copy of WT_1D_extract.mzid (same size, identical protocol md5, SpectraData MHAB-3_MP). The parameters for that row are therefore inferred; this is disclosed, so it is a minor finding.

**(f) Unannotated WT MHAB-1_MP / MHAB-2_MP in 006015.** Leaving them out is defensible: they are PEAK-only, have no .ms2, are not referenced by any mzid, and used a different clean-up. But the exclusion is **not recorded in annotations/BLOCKED.md** (it appears only in the manifest limitations). That breaks rule 7 and the PXD029216 precedent, so it is an **important** finding and the reason PXD006015 FAILs. The manifest also calls their identity 'undocumented'. That is inaccurate: the MHAB-N numbering the producer used to map the other 18 files (WT 1–10 / HGPS 11–20 = 0D,1D,2D,4D,6D × extract,pellet) identifies them as the WT 0D extract and pellet, re-run with MP on 20160302 alongside HGPS 0D MHAB-11_MP/12_MP.

**(g) Instrument: Orbitrap Fusion vs PRIDE 'LTQ Orbitrap'.** The SDRF is right and PRIDE is wrong. The mzXML msModel is 'Orbitrap Fusion' in every Fusion-deposit file checked (006013, 006014, 006015 ×3, 006016 ×2), and 'Q Exactive Orbitrap' for 006012. Methods and the PRIDE protocol agree. The MS2 analyzer was taken per deposit from the raw heads: FTMS → orbitrap for 006013, ITMS → linear ion trap for 006014/15/16, and FTMS with HCD 25 for 006012. All of these are verified.
