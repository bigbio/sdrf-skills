**What would unblock it:** any copy of the CC-BY paper's Methods or proteomics figure legend that names the light and heavy arms, or the authors' MaxQuant `experimentalDesign`/`mqpar.xml`. **Data-derived hint, deliberately NOT applied** (for a curator with access to the Methods):
- Orientation is the same in all three replicates — no label swap: log2 normalized H/L correlates r = 0.82 / 0.83 / 0.89 between experiment pairs (n ≈ 1190–1250 proteins).
- CTGF normalized H/L = 2.66 / 1.85 / 1.51 and COL1A1 = 2.54 / 2.31 / 1.86 (COL1A2 1.69/1.40/1.23). With the abstract's stated proteomic result ("baicalein decreased the expression levels of CTGF") this would put **baicalein in the light channel, TGF β1 alone in heavy**.
- Counter-evidence: ACTA2 (0.31–0.41) and TAGLN (0.41) H/L < 1, i.e. the light channel would show *higher* α-SMA/transgelin under baicalein, against the authors' earlier report that baicalein inhibits fibroblast-to-myofibroblast differentiation. The composition of the two arms (TGF β1 in both? vehicle?) is also unstated.
If the Methods confirm "heavy = TGF β1, light = TGF β1 + baicalein", the fix is two values in `samples.tsv` (scratch) + rebuild.
