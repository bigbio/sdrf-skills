## 1. Study and publication

- **Title:** *Impact of intermittent lead exposure on hominid brain evolution* (Joannes-Boyau, de Souza, Muotri et al.).
- **Publication:** *Sci Adv* 2025;11(42):eadr1524. PMID 41091888 / PMC12527068 / DOI 10.1126/sciadv.adr1524. Open access; full text read via Europe PMC.
- **This PXD is only the single-cell proteomics (SCP) slice** of a multi-omics study. The paper's headline concerns fossil-teeth lead mapping (LA-ICP-MS, not in this PXD) and iPSC-derived brain organoids carrying modern vs archaic *NOVA1* variants exposed to lead. The deposited MS data is exclusively the SCP arm on the organoids.
- **Biology:** human iPSC-derived cortical brain organoids, isogenic for the *NOVA1* locus: modern-human `NOVA1 hu/hu` vs archaic/Neanderthal-like `NOVA1 ar/ar`. Organoids were treated at day 45 with 10 or 30 µM lead(II) acetate for 10 days, washed out, collected day 60, dissociated to single cells, and analysed by label-free DIA SCP.

HeLa's cell-line identity is documented here in the report instead of forcing the trap.

## 5. Values left `not available` / `not applicable`, and why

- **`characteristics[single cell isolation protocol]` = `not applicable` (all organoid cells).** **Corrected post-review (Correction §C1).** The paper states cells were "injected directly from a 384-well multiwell plate" but **never states how single cells were placed into wells** (FACS / cellenONE / other) — confirmed against the paper full text ("Single-cell proteomics analytical method"), the deposited acquisition CSV, and the supplement. The earlier draft used `not available` here, which is **illegal** for this column: the resolved `single-cell` template sets `allow_not_available: false`, `allow_not_applicable: true` for `single cell isolation protocol`. `not applicable` is therefore the only spec-legal reserved word. HeLa rows already carry `not applicable` (a bulk standard, no single-cell isolation) and were left unchanged.
- **`characteristics[age]` / `characteristics[sex]` = `not available`.** The donor age/sex of the iPSC lines are not reported in this paper. (`age` disallows `not applicable`, so `not available` is the only legal reserved word.)
- **`comment[precursor mass tolerance]` / `comment[fragment mass tolerance]` = `not available`.** The DIA search parameters are not described in the paper (only "peptide filtering with quality thresholds").
- **`comment[modification parameters]` = `not available`.** PRIDE reports "No PTMs are included in the dataset," and **no reduction/alkylation step is documented** for the SCP prep. Per the brief I did **not** add reflex Carbamidomethyl (there is no alkylation evidence) or Oxidation. The search modification set was simply not reported → `not available`.
- **`comment[dia method]` = `not available`.** No PRIDE ontology term cleanly names the scheme; the actual scheme is non-overlapping fixed 20 m/z windows over 400–1000 m/z (captured via the scan-window and isolation-window columns).
- **HeLa QC rows** carry `not applicable` for organism part / cell type / disease / genotype / compound / dose / cell identifier / cells per well: it is a 200 pg whole-proteome **standard**, not a biological sample or a single cell (`characteristics[sample type] = standard`, PRIDE:0000908; `cells per well = not applicable` per the "mass-not-a-count" rule).

## 6. Inferred vs stated

**Stated** (paper/PRIDE/raw): Orbitrap Astral; DIA; label-free; trypsin; HCD 25% NCE; 400–1000 m/z; 20 m/z windows; NOVA1 hu/hu = modern, ar/ar = archaic; lead(II) acetate 10 & 30 µM; single cells from dissociated brain organoids; 226 deposited raws.

**Inferred (flagged):**
1. **Two distinct lead-acetate dose groups, specific µM unverified (`dose level 1`/`dose level 2`).** **Corrected post-review (Correction §C2).** The earlier draft asserted `NeanTreat1 = 10 µM` / `NeanTreat2 = 30 µM` purely from filename-token order. No primary source fixes this direction (evidence searched: paper full text, all supplementary tables S3–S18 and the supplement PDF, the deposited Xcalibur acquisition CSV, Europe PMC supplementary files, PRIDE metadata). The SDRF now encodes the two groups as distinct-but-unquantified dose levels; `characteristics[compound] = lead diacetate (CHEBI:31767)` is retained on both (certain).
2. **Cortical organoids.** The SCP arm is on cortical organoids (COs; paper Fig. 7A and the SCP results section refer to COs). **Corrected post-review:** an earlier draft claimed "thalamic received only 10 µM" — this is contradicted by the paper Methods ("Lead Treatment Protocol"), which states **both cortical and thalamic organoids were treated at 10 µM *or* 30 µM**. That erroneous premise is removed; it does not affect the annotation, which sets `organism part = brain` (UBERON:0000955, generic modelled organ) rather than "cerebral cortex" to avoid over-committing.
3. **`characteristics[cell type] = neural cell` (CL:0002319, generic).** SCP was on *unsorted* dissociated organoid cells — a mixed neural population (progenitors + neurons). A specific CL neuron subtype would be over-specific and unsupported, so I used the generic neural-cell term.
4. **Re-injections (`_reinject10`) = technical replicate 2** of the same cell (shared `source name`, shared `cell identifier`, shared biological-replicate index; `technical replicate` = 2). The 7 HeLa files are 7 injections of one standard.
5. **`organism part = brain`** for an iPSC-derived organoid is a modelled-organ choice, not dissected tissue.

## 7. Conflicts found and how resolved

- **FAIMS.** Every deposited raw filename contains `FAIMS_CV-50`, i.e. FAIMS was used at CV −50 V. The paper Methods **never mention FAIMS**. Following the raw-data-over-Methods precedence, FAIMS was used — but SDRF/TERMS has **no column for FAIMS compensation voltage**, so it could not be encoded. Recorded here as a raw-vs-Methods discrepancy.
- **PRIDE `modifications` = "No PTMs"** — consistent with the absence of a documented alkylation step; I did not override it with fabricated mods.
- **iPSC clone identity unresolved.** The paper names four clones (isogenic pairs `ASC`/`C136` = line 1, `NOVA1-C15`/`NOVA1-C27` = line 2; ASC & NOVA1-C27 = hu/hu, C136 & NOVA1-C15 = ar/ar) but does **not** say which clone the SCP arm used. Only the genotype-level identity (hu/hu vs ar/ar, from the `Homo`/`Nean` filename tokens) is defensible, so that is what `characteristics[genotype]` records; no Cellosaurus/CLO accession was asserted.

## Correction (post-review)

This artifact failed an independent review. Two defects were repaired from primary sources; two factual errors in this report were also corrected. Only `annotations/PXD067623.*` were touched; the spec submodule was not modified. Dimensions unchanged: **226 rows × 43 columns**.

### C1 — BLOCKER (mechanical): illegal reserved word in `single cell isolation protocol`
- **Defect:** 219 organoid single-cell rows carried `characteristics[single cell isolation protocol] = not available`. The resolved `single-cell` template sets **`allow_not_available: false`, `allow_not_applicable: true`** for this column (verified via `spec/scripts/resolve_templates.py`), so `not available` is illegal here. `parse_sdrf` does **not** enforce the flag (it exited 0 both before and after), so this was a silent, spec-illegal value.
- **Investigation:** the well-loading method (FACS / cellenONE / manual) is genuinely undocumented. Confirmed against the paper's "Single-cell proteomics analytical method" section (only "injected directly from a 384-well multiwell plate"), the sole deposited non-raw file (the Xcalibur acquisition CSV — `Comment` and `Sample Name` columns entirely empty), and the supplement. No isolation method is recoverable.
- **Fix:** replaced `not available` → **`not applicable`** on all 219 affected organoid rows (the only spec-legal reserved word for this column). The 7 HeLa-standard rows already carried `not applicable` and were left unchanged. Post-fix: all 226 rows = `not applicable`.

### C2 — IMPORTANT: unverified dose direction
- **Defect:** `NeanTreat1 → 10 µM` (50 rows) and `NeanTreat2 → 30 µM` (71 rows) were assigned **purely from filename-token order**, asserting a specific µM that could be inverted.
- **Evidence searched (every available primary source):**
  - **Paper full text (PMC12527068):** the SCP results describe both a `NOVA1 ar/ar` 10 µM group and a 30 µM group (Fig. 7, C and D), and the "Lead Treatment Protocol" Methods state organoids were treated at "10 μM or 30 μM." **No plate map, sample key, or token→dose crosswalk exists in the text.**
  - **Deposited files (PRIDE `files/all`, 227 entries, every `projectAccessions` asserted to contain PXD067623):** the only non-raw file is the Xcalibur acquisition CSV. Its `Comment` and `Sample Name` columns are empty; it carries only plate positions (NeanTreat1 = rows I, J; NeanTreat2 = rows M, N) with **no dose annotation**. No DIA-NN/Spectronaut report or sample sheet was deposited.
  - **Supplementary tables S3–S18 + supplement PDF (via Europe PMC `supplementaryFiles`):** all SCP references use biological labels ("NOVA1 ar/ar 10 µM / 30 µM"). The strings `NeanTreat`, `plate`, and `Astral` appear **0 times**; there is no crosswalk from the deposited filename tokens (or plate rows I/J/M/N) to a specific concentration.
  - PRIDE project metadata: no map.
- **Conclusion:** the token→dose direction is **genuinely undeterminable**. Both doses exist and the two-group contrast is real, but which deposited group is 10 vs 30 µM cannot be established.
- **Fix (honest downgrade):** kept the two groups **distinct** and `characteristics[compound] = lead diacetate (CHEBI:31767)` on both (certain), but set `characteristics[dose]` and `factor value[dose]` to neutral, unquantified **`dose level 1`** (NeanTreat1, 50 rows) / **`dose level 2`** (NeanTreat2, 71 rows). The "1"/"2" labels are bound to the deposited filename tokens (NeanTreat1/NeanTreat2) and are **not** a concentration ranking — no claim is made that level 1 < level 2 in µM. This preserves the dose-response contrast the study rests on while asserting no unverified µM. Trade-off: it produces two `parse_sdrf` pattern WARNINGs (the dose column prefers `number + unit`), accepted deliberately over fabricating a concentration; there are no ERRORs.
