**Status: PARTIAL (fix round 1 applied after external review FAIL; see end). Super-SILAC arm annotated (72 of 96 RAW files). TMT10plex arm blocked (24 RAW) because its channel map is incomplete; see BLOCKED.md. Review status: REVIEW_PENDING_EXTERNAL.**

## Study
- **Title:** Quantitative mass spectrometry to interrogate proteomic heterogeneity in metastatic lung adenocarcinoma and validate a novel somatic mutation CDK12-G879V.
- **Paper:** Zhang X *et al.*, Mol Cell Proteomics 2019;18(4):622–641. PMID 30617155, PMC6442362, doi:10.1074/mcp.RA118.001266.
  - Full text came from the PMC HTML render; Europe PMC and PMC OA give only the abstract.
  - Supplementary Tables S1–S5 sit behind an NCBI bot check, which was not bypassed. The publisher site returns 403.
- **Design:**
  - Samples come from one patient: an African American male never smoker, diagnosed at 50 with metastatic, HER2-amplified lung adenocarcinoma.
  - **Super-SILAC:** three tumors were each mixed 1:1 with a heavy (13C6-Arg/13C6-Lys) reference made from 12 lung cell lines:
    - LN1: right cervical lymph node, 5/2011
    - Lung: left lower lobe wedge resection, 11/2011
    - LN2: right cervical lymph node, 11/2013
  - Each mix was fractionated by high-pH RP into 12 fractions. Each fraction was injected twice.
  - **TMT10plex:** a separate experiment on 9 tumors (LN1, Lung, LN2, PDX, MET1–5) plus a pool, at proteome and TiO2-phosphopeptide level.
- Step 0.5: the dataset is not in sdrf-annotated-datasets, and PRIDE has no SDRF deposited.

## Key decisions and evidence
1. **SILAC is real (rule 14):** mqpar shows multiplicity 2 and labelMods `["", "Arg6;Lys6"]`.
2. **Channel and sample identity (rule 6).**
   - Light = tumor and heavy = reference is stated in the paper (heavy-labelled cell lines, unlabelled tissue).
   - The MaxQuant experiments #1_LN, #2_Lung and #3_LN map to LN1, Lung and LN2. This was cross-checked against the deposited proteinGroups:
     - Proteins with L/H < 0.5: 18.8% for #1_LN, 8.6% for #2_Lung and 9.3% for #3_LN. Fig. 2C reports 19% for LN1 and 8–9% for Lung and LN2.
     - ERBB2 H/L: 0.022 in #2_Lung, so ERBB2 is about 45× higher in the lung tumor, as the paper reports.
3. **Super-SILAC reference (rule 18).**
   - Heavy rows use `sample type` = reference and `material type` = cell line.
   - `pooled sample` = the SN= list of the 12 lines, by Cellosaurus name (CVCL accessions are in the manifest).
   - `individual`, `biological replicate`, `age` and `sex` = `pooled` (spec README.adoc:365 TIP; the 12 lines come from donors of both sexes). Disease, organism part, metastasis site, ancestry and smoking status are `not applicable`, because normal and tumor lines are mixed.
   - There is one reference source name per mix, because each tumor got its own aliquot. This keeps the row coordinate unique.
4. **R1 / R2 are technical replicates 1 and 2.**
   - All 72 raw headers were checked. R1 used `top15HCD_360min.meth`, acquired 2014-09-09. R2 used `top15HCD_80min.meth`, acquired 2015-01-08.
   - MaxQuant assigns both injections of a fraction to the same experiment and fraction.
   - The different run times are recorded in `comment[chromatography duration]`: 21600 s for R1, 4800 s for R2 (the raw "MS Run Time").
5. **Fractions** come from the design template (F1–F12). `1_LN_super-SILAC_F7_R2_150110215046.raw` is fraction 7 of R2.
6. **Clinical values (rule 18)** come only from statements about this individual in the paper:
   - Ancestry, sex and smoking status from "A 50-year old African American never smoker…" and "his".
   - Site and date for each sample, and the regimen before each excision, from Table I.
   - `individual` = `index_patient`. The paper's only name for him is the "Index patient", and no ID is published.
7. **Technical values follow raw and search output, ahead of the paper.**
   - Instrument: Orbitrap Elite. Acquisition: DDA. Dissociation: HCD. MS2 analyzer: orbitrap. Collision energy: **32 NCE** (from raw; the paper says 40%).
   - Precursor tolerance **4.5 ppm** (mqpar main search; the paper says 7 ppm). Fragment tolerance 20 ppm (FTMS).
   - Modifications are read from mqpar:
     - Fixed: Carbamidomethyl C.
     - Variable: Oxidation M, Acetyl Protein N-term, Phospho STY, and Label:13C(6) (UNIMOD:188) on R and on K.
     - **Deamidation is omitted.** The paper lists it, but the super-SILAC search does not use it.
   - Trypsin (digestion; the search rule is Trypsin/P). Fractionation: high-pH RP (PRIDE:0000564). Reduction: DTT. Alkylation: IAA.
8. **Factor:** `factor value[organism part]` (lung vs lymph node, the paper's main comparison). Heavy rows are `not applicable`. LN1 and LN2 are two independent tumors in the same factor group, so they have biological replicates 1 and 2 (spec README.adoc:330, rule 3).
9. **Metastasis site** (oncology-metadata): cervical lymph node for LN1 and LN2, lower lobe of left lung for Lung (Table I).

## Judgement calls a reviewer should attack
1. **Factor = organism part.** It separates lung from lymph node. LN1 and LN2 share 'cervical lymph node' and are separated by biological replicate 1/2, source name and clinical history. A time-point factor would be the alternative.
2. **Per-patient clinical columns from a single-case narrative.** Ancestry, sex, smoking status, adult stage and the clinical-history text all come from paper prose about one named index patient (individual-level, rule 18). Age was left `not available` rather than derived from the age at diagnosis.
3. **R1/R2 as technical replicates, plus the mqpar 4.5 ppm tolerance and the raw 32 NCE over the paper's values.** R1 and R2 have different gradients and were acquired months apart, and MaxQuant pooled them per fraction. The choice not to declare `cell-lines` is also open to challenge.
