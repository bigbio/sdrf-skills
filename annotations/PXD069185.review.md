1. **minor: ontology.** `characteristics[treatment]` and `factor value[treatment]` (all rows) are free text, which gives 3 parse_sdrf warnings. No NCIT/EFO term exists for APEX2 BP/H2O2 labelling with or without IR, and the validator only warns. *Fix (optional):* none required. 2. **minor: completeness.** The recommended human column `characteristics[individual]` is absent. *Fix (optional):* add a constant donor identifier for the single U2OS donor, or leave the column out.
3. **minor: omission.** Neutravidin affinity enrichment and transient UFC1‑2HA‑APEX2 expression are not encoded. I checked in OLS: no EFO:0009090 descendant fits, and no genotype column is licensed by this template union. The manifest discloses both. *Fix:* none required now.
4. **minor: technical.** `comment[cleavage agent details]` = Trypsin (MS:1001251), which is the wet-lab enzyme. The deposited search used Trypsin/P (mqpar.xml, summary.txt). This is defensible against TERMS.tsv and documented. Anyone reprocessing to reproduce the deposited search needs Trypsin/P.
5. **minor: technical.** `comment[precursor mass tolerance]` = 4.5 ppm (mqpar mainSearchTol), while the protocol text says 6 ppm. The deposited setting takes precedence, and the discrepancy is documented.
6. **minor: provenance.** `characteristics[ancestry category]` = European ancestry is mapped from Cellosaurus "Population: Caucasian". This mapping is conventional but inferred.
