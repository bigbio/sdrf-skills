## Study

- **PRIDE:** PXD041679, "Transcription-coupled DNA-Protein Crosslink repair by CSB and CRL4(CSA)-mediated
  degradation". Submitted 2023-04-20, public 2024-02-09. Quantification is SILAC (PRIDE:0000315).
- **Publication:** van Sluis M *et al.*, *Nat Cell Biol* 2024 26(5):770-783, PMID 38600236, PMC11098752,
  doi:10.1038/s41556-024-01394-y. It is open access. I read the full text through NCBI efetch with the
  numeric id 11098752 and checked that the returned title matches. The paper's Data availability says:
  "SILAC-based Pol II quantitative interaction data have been deposited … PXD041679".
- **Design the paper describes** (Fig. 3a legend and Methods, "SILAC–mass spectrometry"):
  - pSer2-RPB1 (elongating Pol II) immunoprecipitation from mock-treated cells vs cells treated with
    300 µM formaldehyde (FA) for 45 min.
  - Two independent experiments, one of them a label swap.
  - Light = [12C6]-Lys / [12C6,14N4]-Arg. Heavy = [13C6]-Lys / [13C6,15N4]-Arg.
  - IP eluates were run on SDS–PAGE, the gel lanes were cut into slices, and each slice was digested
    in-gel (DTT, iodoacetamide, trypsin).
  - LC-MS/MS: EASY-nLC 1200 with an Orbitrap Fusion Lumos, DDA, HCD. Search: MaxQuant 1.6.3.3.

The **channel → condition** map *within* each experiment is therefore known, label swaps included. What
is missing is the map from **raw file → experiment**:

- The table has `Fraction 1`…`Fraction 10` columns with peptide counts spread smoothly over all ten. So
  each gel lane was searched as up to 10 slice-fractions. Eight experiments × up to 10 slices is far
  more than the 15 deposited raw files. **The deposit is a subset of the search**, and nothing says which
  experiment(s) or slices the 15 files are.
- Only the two Pol2/FA experiments appear in the paper (Fig. 3a; Supplementary Table 1, columns
  "forward"/"reverse"). The two Pol2/UV and four CSB-IP experiments are not described anywhere. Their
  cell line and the meaning of "CSB-KI" (the HCT116 CSB-K538R ATPase knock-in? CSB–mScarlet-I KI?) are
  not stated.
- The 15 files were acquired consecutively: Xcalibur sequence rows 135–149, one instrument method
  (`120min_FTOT_2sec_75um_trap_reprosil_8ul.meth`). The plausible reading is "the two Pol2/FA lanes",
  but 15 does not split into two equal lanes, and nothing ties any file to a lane. **I did not use this
  assumption.**

## Conflicts noted (PRIDE vs paper vs raw)

- **Gradient:** the PRIDE protocol says a "linear gradient … over 90 min". The paper says 120 min, and
  the raw instrument method name is `120min_FTOT…`. Raw and paper agree on 120 min.
- **Database:** the PRIDE text says UniProt "release June 2017". The paper says "release 2018".
  `parameters.txt` uses `UP000005640_9606.fasta` (human reference proteome, release not stated).
- **PRIDE `organisms`** includes *Caenorhabditis elegans*. Worms appear only in the paper's non-MS
  experiments; every MS search is human-only.
- **PRIDE `organismParts` = Fibroblast** is unsupported by any MS-specific statement.
- **Search scope:** the deposited proteinGroups covers 8 experiments, 6 of which the paper never
  reports. PRIDE's description covers only the FA/Pol II result.

3. Fixed modifications and the precursor tolerance are not in the deposited MaxQuant 1.6 output.

5. Europe PMC `fullTextXML` (HTTP 500) and `supplementaryFiles` (error bean) failed; I used the NCBI

6. I could not list the paper's GitHub repository (API 403; attaching the repo was denied).
7. `sdrf-tools fetch` failed twice (Europe PMC HTTP 500), so I gathered the record by hand from the
   PRIDE v3 REST API. All working files are in the scratch directory `PXD041679/`, under
   `evidence/`, `dl/`, `raw_hdr/` and `supp/`.

## Judgement calls a reviewer should attack

1. **BLOCK vs a technical-only SDRF.** A 30-row SDRF (15 files × light/heavy) with correct
   label/mods/instrument is constructible. But every IP target, treatment and cell line would be
   `not available`, and the factor value would carry no information. I chose to block, following the
   BLOCKED.md precedent of blocking when the biological factor is missing.
2. **Not assuming the 15 files = the two Pol2/FA lanes.** It is plausible: the paper's Data
   availability calls the deposit "Pol II quantitative interaction data", and the files are 15
   consecutive runs. But proteinGroups shows CSB-IP experiments with more protein IDs than the Pol2
   ones, and 15 is not two equal lanes. Without a file list the assumption is not evidence.
3. **Not reconstructing the map forensically from spectra.** This could be attempted (per-file H/L of
   FA-induced interactors such as CSB/ERCC6, CSA/ERCC8). It would be inference, and it still could not
   tell slices of different lanes apart reliably.
