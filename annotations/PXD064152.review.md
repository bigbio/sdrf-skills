## Scope and the FFPE block

Deposit: 43 `.raw` files. 15 are cell-line runs (HF190703_RN_HN_*), 6 are keratinocyte runs (HF220822_RN_Lavinia_33–35 and QEP220614_RN_Lavinia_15/17/18), and 22 are FFPE runs. 15 + 6 + 22 = 43, so every deposited run is either annotated or listed in BLOCKED.md. The partial scope is stated in BLOCKED.md and in both manifests' `limitations`.

**The FFPE block is justified.** These are the sources I checked independently:

- **`Sample_list.txt`.** It lists the 29 FFPE entries in two unlabelled blocks, `#1–#10` and `#1–#19`, with no HPV, patient or clinical field. Only 3 of the 10 block-A entries have a deposited raw file (FFPE_15T, 21T, 22T); QEP230822_RN_Lavinia_1, 8, 11, 12, 19, 20 and 21 are not deposited.
- **Supplementary Data sheet 1.** It has merged headers `B5:J5` = HPV− over columns `#1–#9` and `K5:AC5` = HPV+ over `#1–#19`, and no file names.
- **EpiProfile outputs.** The FFPE2208.zip and FFPE2308.zip central directories, and the `histone_ratios_SILAC.xls` and `histone_logs.txt` headers, key the data by raw file name only. They contain no HPV or patient label.

Block B has 19 entries and the paper has 19 HPV+ cases, so block B = HPV+ is a plausible hypothesis. But that is a count and numbering pattern, not an explicit key, and block A (10 entries) does not match the paper's 9 HPV− cases. Under the request's rule, these runs must stay unannotated until the submitters provide a key. The unblock criterion in BLOCKED.md asks for exactly that key.

## Key independent verifications

### Cell lines

- **File → cell line.** The `Sample name` column of `Sample_list.txt` is blank for all 15 cell-line runs. The explicit key is the token in each deposited file name (4, 6, 10A, 17A, 17B, 18, 19, 23, 47, 104, 2, 90, 152, 154, VU). Each token matches one cell line in the paper's list (Methods), and the study has no UM-SCC-2, so `HN_2` can only be UD-SCC-2. Cells1907.zip contains the same 15 runs.
- **HPV status.** Supplementary Data sheet 2 has merged headers `B5:I5` = HPV− over UM-SCC-4, 6, 10A, 18, 19, 23, 17A and 17B, and `J5:O5` = HPV+ over UD-SCC-2, UM-SCC-104, 93-VU147-T and UPCI:SCC-90/152/154. This matches the SDRF row for row. For UM-SCC-47, the paper's Fig. 6 legend says HPV+ and Cellosaurus lists it as an HPV16 transformant.
- **Cellosaurus metadata.** Age, sex, site, ancestry (Caucasian → European ancestry, on 4 lines only) and the `OI` donor links (17A↔17B, 090↔152) all match the SDRF.
- **Methods.** The paper says DMEM for all lines, D3-acetic anhydride for the cell lines, and a breast cancer heavy mix for the cell lines. The SDRF matches each.

### Keratinocytes

- **File → arm.** `Sample_list.txt` names the arm explicitly: files 33, 15 and 17 are `empty`, and 34, 35 and 18 are `E6/E7`. The SDRF matches.
- **Pairing and donors.** The SDRF asserts no donor pairing, which is correct: no source keys the files to donors.
- **Methods.** The paper says "Adult human epidermal keratinocytes" and propionic anhydride + phenyl isocyanate derivatisation. HK.zip was processed with `EpiProfile2.0_pr_pic`. The SDRF matches.
- **Heavy rows.** These are the "different cancer cells" super-SILAC mix: sample type `reference`, pooled, factor `not applicable`.
