## Minor findings
1. **Precursor tolerance 0.07 Da**: this is MaxQuant's QTOF *initial* window (Beck 2015). The authors do not report it. Note it as the first-search window, or revert to `not available` if conversion is not needed.
2. **Ref dose**: the no-PK Ref light/heavy rows could carry `0 mg/kg` so the PK dose series is complete.
3. **mg/kg unit**: here it encodes the PK:lysate w/w ratio. The arithmetic is correct, but a reader may mistake it for a body-weight dose.
4. **LiP fractionation**: `no fractionation` is supported ("A single fraction was analyzed per dilution").
5. **Annotation tool version**: v0.1.0 matches pyproject, but plugin.json says 0.2.0.
