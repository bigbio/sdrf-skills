## Requested stress tests

- **Channel map, D4S8 / D8S4.** Confirmed: D4S8 means DMSO in medium (Lys4/Arg6) and FM247 in heavy (Lys8/Arg10); D8S4 is the reverse. The evidence comes from four independent places:
  - The PRIDE protocol describes the label swap ("pair of heavy and medium cells and vice versa for label-switch"). It also says the MaxQuant identifiers were reversed for the swapped files so that every ratio is probe/DMSO.
  - In summary.txt, all 30 files have Multiplicity 2. The D4S8 files have Labels0 = Arg6;Lys4 and Labels1 = Arg10;Lys8; the D8S4 files have them reversed.
  - Reviewer-recomputed PARK7 (DJ-1) log2 normalized H/L is about +2 to +6 in every quantified experiment of both orientations, in all three cell lines. GAPDH is about 0.
  - The file-name mnemonic reads D(MSO)<Lys label> S(ubstance)<Lys label>.
- **Medium/heavy-only labels.** There is no light channel. `SILAC medium` and `SILAC heavy` are valid spec label values (README.adoc:1107; precedent PXD013923). The four label mods carry UNIMOD accessions that match their mass shifts. convert-openms resolves 6 conditions.
- **A549 rounds I–III.** These are encoded as technical replicates 1–3 of one source per culture. The evidence supports that choice:
  - The MaxQuant experiment names share the replicate index (D4S8_k_I/_II/_III).
  - The same autosampler vials (RA1–RA6) were injected in round I (2016-05-27) and round II (2016-06-16). Round III used RB1–RB6. All rounds used the same method.
  - PARK7 is unquantified for D4S8_2 in all three rounds, which fits one shared sample measured three times.

  If the rounds were in fact independent cultures, this encoding only under-counts n, so it is the conservative choice. The ambiguity is disclosed in the manifest limitations.
- **Dissertation as the design source.** Acceptable. It is by the first author, from the same lab, open access, and its methods text is near-verbatim the PRIDE protocol. The raw-file dates (2016–2018) are earlier than the dissertation.
  - The channel-critical facts do not depend on it: they come from PRIDE, MaxQuant and the deposited data.
  - The values that come only from the thesis are the 10 µM dose, the culture media, the medium/heavy amino-acid definition (which is also obvious from the masses), and the H2O2 concentrations (which are also in the file names).
  - The paper and its Supporting Information are closed access; the producer correctly did not bypass the 403.
- **Instrument conflicts.** Resolved correctly from the raw headers:
  - All 18 A549 files are "LTQ Orbitrap XL MS".
  - All 12 HeLa and SH-SY5Y SILAC files are "Orbitrap Fusion" (HCD 30 %, ion-trap detection).
  - All 9 H2O2 files are "Q Exactive Plus – Orbitrap MS" (HCD NCE 27). PRIDE lists the generic term "Q Exactive", and the thesis heading reads "QE Plus (HeLa)"; both are superseded by the raw headers.
  - The reconcile MAJOR finding (instrument_contradicted) is refuted, because the PRIDE protocol names three instruments and reconcile reads only the QE Plus paragraph.
- **Post-build Lys-C and join step.** Rerunning `postbuild.py` on the scratch build outputs reproduces both SDRF hashes byte for byte. The script asserts identical headers and a single Trypsin column before inserting `NT=Lys-C;AC=MS:1001309`. The PRIDE protocol and thesis 4.8 support the Lys-C (2.5 h) then trypsin (overnight) digestion, and thesis 4.8 says the LFQ samples went through the same processing.
- **Cell type `not available`.** Acceptable. The column is recommended and allows `not available`. No source states a cell type: the thesis Table 4 "Cell type" column actually holds diseases (and swaps HeLa and A549), and the Cellosaurus entries have no cell-type field.
