## Focus questions

### 1. Is the genotype per file good enough under rules 6 and 7? Yes. These runs should not be blocked.
- **Explicit source.** The submitter's deposited names are `CHIP_WT_1-3` and `CHIP_KO_1-3`, and the PRIDE protocol describes "CHIP WT and CHIP KO each in biological triplicate". Rule 7 applies only when the map is *undocumented*, and here it is documented.
- **The conflict cannot be the genotype.** The instrument-recorded names are `CHIP_WT_A`–`F`. I confirmed them myself in the raw headers of KO_1 (`180404_SP007_CHIP_WT_D.raw`) and WT_3 (`180328_SP008_CHIP_WT_C_4ul.raw`), and in the FileInfos table of KO_2.msf (`..._CHIP_WT_E.raw`). If these names were the genotype, all six runs would be WT, which contradicts the deposited 3 + 3 design. So they are a series label, and the manifest records the conflict as rule 6 asks.
- **Data check (rule 6), one marker.** VGF has 17/15/20 PSMs in KO_1-3 and none in WT_1-3. In the deposited SWATH t-tests VGF is about 10× higher in the group with low STUB1.
- **Data check, independent and proteome-wide (my test).** I took SWATH proteins with p<0.01 and |log2FC|>0.585 and compared their SWATH KO/WT ratio with the SILAC KO/WT log2 ratio (L+H):
  - biorep1: Pearson 0.66, 21/22 with the same sign
  - biorep2: Pearson 0.44, 19/31 with the same sign
  - Swapped labels would give a negative correlation.
- **Residual risk.** Genotype is confounded with acquisition batch (WT 27–28 Mar 2018, KO 4 Apr 2018) and with peptide yield (KO 8.6–9.6k vs WT 6.8–7.2k peptides). Batch cannot plausibly create a 0-vs-~17-PSM VGF switch or the sign concordance, but the confound is real and disclosed (minor 6).

### 2. Pulse direction is inferred and the duration is unknown. Acceptable.
- **Wording.** The protocol says "pulse SILAC" and quantifies "peptides with the heavy isotope labels". The keywords include "Pulse silac".
- **My data check.** The per-protein H/(L+H) distribution is right-skewed: q05/q50/q95 = 0.33/0.41/0.55 in WT_1. Ribosomal proteins have median 0.39, below the global median, so long-lived proteins carry less heavy label. This fits light → heavy pulse (heavy = newly synthesised) and argues against a heavy → light chase.
- **Duration.** No time is stated anywhere, and none is invented. No sampling-time column is needed.

### 3. Two rows per run. Correct.
One row per channel, light = pre-existing and heavy = newly synthesised. Both rows carry the culture's genotype and replicate number and share the assay name. This matches the reviewed campaign precedents PXD028934 and PXD004880. convert-openms gives 2 conditions with 6 file-rows each.

### 4. Search parameters from the .msf vs the PRIDE text (rule 5). Correct.
- **What I confirmed in CHIP_KO_2.msf:**
  - Proteome Discoverer 1.4.1.14 (SchemaInfo), Mascot + Percolator
  - 10 ppm / 0.5 Da, Trypsin, 2 missed cleavages
  - Static CAM(C); dynamic Ox(M) and Acetyl(protein N-term)
  - Quant method "SILAC 2 plex (Arg6, Lys4) (Custom)"
- **Why the PRIDE text loses.** It describes PD 2.2 / Sequest HT / 0.6 Da / static labels / Met-loss+Acetyl, and no PD 2.2 output is deposited. The SDRF describes the deposited search, and the conflict is recorded.
- **Nuance (minor 3).** The labels were not Mascot dynamic mods. No labelled PSMs exist, and the heavy partners come from the quantifier; Lys4 is a custom PD mod with no Unimod accession. Listing UNIMOD:188/481 as MT=Variable is the rule-11 convention and correct.

### 5. Disease: neuroblastoma, not PRIDE's SCA31. Correct.
SH-SY5Y is a neuroblastoma line (Cellosaurus CVCL_0019, NCIt C3270 → MONDO:0005072). PRIDE's DOID:0050980 SCA31 describes a disease the KO models, not the samples, and STUB1 is linked to SCAR16/SCA48 rather than SCA31. Organism part = bone marrow follows the Cellosaurus metastatic site.

### 6. Genotype/batch confounding disclosure. Adequate, could be more visible.
It is in the manifest limitations and the genotype claim. Minor 6 suggests surfacing it at the top of the report or in an optional column.

## Minor findings
1. The manifest `files` source says "15 SWATH pairs". The deposit has 12 SWATH + 2 IDA.
2. CHIP_WT_1.msf searched `uprot_human_18_3_sp`, the other five `uprot_human_2017_07`, so the manifest should not say "identical in all six". No SDRF change.
3. Add a manifest note that the SILAC labels come from the PD quant method (custom `Label: 2D(4)`) and were not Mascot dynamic mods.
4. Add the proteome-wide SWATH concordance to the genotype claim, so the check does not rest on VGF alone.
5. Add the heavy-fraction-distribution check to the pulse-direction claim.
6. Make the genotype ↔ acquisition-batch and yield confound more visible to SDRF consumers.
7. `characteristics[cell type]` could be `neuroblast` (CL:0000031, given by the submitter in PRIDE). `not available` is also acceptable.
8. The culture medium for maintenance is DMEM + 10% FBS (paper), but the SILAC medium is unstated. Keep `not available`.
