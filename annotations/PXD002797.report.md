## Study
Drabovich AP, Pavlou MP, Schiza C, Diamandis EP. *Mol Cell Proteomics* 2016;15(6):2093–2107, doi:10.1074/mcp.M115.057257, PMID 27067054, PMC5083091. MCF-7 cells were SILAC-labelled. Heavy cells (Arg6 = L-13C6-Arg, Lys8 = L-13C6,15N2-Lys) got 10 nM 17β-estradiol, and light cells got the 0.1% ethanol vehicle, for 6 h or 36 h. There were three biological replicates per time point. Equal amounts of heavy and light lysate were mixed and split into 18 SCX fractions. Each fraction was injected twice on an LTQ Orbitrap XL, and the data were searched with MaxQuant 1.1.1.25 against IPI human 3.71.

**Not pulsed SILAC.** This is a standard 2-plex SILAC design in which the treatment is carried by the heavy channel. MaxQuant multiplicity is 2 (Labels1 `Arg6;Lys8` for all 216 runs), so campaign rule 14 is satisfied. The study has not been annotated before (`sdrf-tools curated`: none; no sdrf-annotated-datasets folder). It is not a duplicate: Europe PMC "PXD002797" returns only this paper.

## Technical values
Search output, raw headers and the paper agree on the following:
- **Modifications:** Carbamidomethyl C (fixed); Oxidation M, Acetyl Protein N-term, Arg6 (UNIMOD:188) and Lys8 (UNIMOD:259) (variable).
- **Digestion and preparation:** trypsin, DTT and iodoacetamide.
- **Acquisition:** CID at 35 NCE with ion-trap MS2, MS1 450–1450 m/z, top-6 DDA.
- **Tolerances:** precursor 6 ppm and fragment 0.5 Da, from the deposited Andromeda parameter files `combined/allSpectra.CID.{iso,sil0,sil1}.apar` in both MaxQuant zips.
- **Sample additions (round 2):** `characteristics[biorepository]` = ATCC (paper: purchased from ATCC; Cellosaurus ATCC HTB-22). `characteristics[individual]` = `MCF-7_donor`, a placeholder for the single cell-line donor, not a published ID.

## Open items / limitations
- The `-1`/`-2` suffixes are read as duplicate injections. This is an inference: MaxQuant gives both the same slice and experiment, and the raw files use the identical method. No source states it.
- Control rows have treatment = `untreated` (rule 4), but the cells actually received the ethanol vehicle. validate-sdrf warns that `untreated` is not an NCIT/EFO term.
- Precursor tolerance conflict: the paper and the PRIDE text say 20 ppm, but the deposited .apar search parameters say 6 ppm. The SDRF uses 6 ppm because it must describe the actual search. The first draft used 20 ppm and wrongly said the main-search tolerance was not deposited; the external review caught this and it is fixed in round 2.
- The paper and the deposit disagree on two settings. The paper states 1 missed cleavage and a minimum peptide length of 7, while the deposited files have 2 and 6; neither setting has an SDRF column. The PRIDE text also says "Lys6 and Arg8", which is contradicted by the paper and the search output.
- Cell type and passage number are `not available`.
- The paper was read from the PMC HTML. Europe PMC XML returned 500 and the PDF returned 403; neither was bypassed. The supplementary files were not consulted.
