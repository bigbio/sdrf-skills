## Findings
1. **important — `comment[ms2 scan range]` rows 1-36 = `200m/z-2000m/z` is contradicted.**
   - The dd-MS2 method sets `Fixed first mass 110.0 m/z` next to the `Scan range 200 to 2000 m/z` field. Fixed first mass sets the start of each MS2 scan.
   - Deposited PSMs carry matched fragment peaks below 200 m/z in 18,136 of 18,340 sampled PSMs, down to the 110 m/z bin.
   - *Fix:* set the column to `not available`, or to `110m/z-2000m/z` with the method text cited. Rebuild with sdrf-tools build and update the manifest claim.
2. **important — the manifest's channel-map claim says the light channel is "empty"; BLOCKED.md says "light unused". This is false.**
   - Light-state PSMs are 2,001–2,677 per raw (~10%). proteinGroups Intensity L sums to 3.8e12 (rep1) and 4.1e12 (rep2).
   - In pAHA-pSILAC the light signal is unpulsed, pre-existing protein from both pooled cultures. Leaving out light rows is defensible, but only for that reason.
   - *Fix:* correct the claim, the limitations and BLOCKED.md to state that reason.
3. minor — validation.txt records only the offline `sdrf-tools check`. The online check now completes and flags UNIMOD:896 "not found", an OLS-outage false positive. Record it and its refutation (rule 13).
4. minor — the AHA click enrichment (Click-iT) and the 3 h pulse are not encoded. Both are optional; a custom characteristics column could carry them.
5. minor — the 10p rows are technical replicate 2, fraction 1, of fractionated sources. This is acceptable, but keep the limitation text, because downstream replicate pairing will see an unbalanced design.
