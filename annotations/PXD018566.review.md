## Findings

### IMPORTANT 1: organism part is left out although the paper states it
- Location: `characteristics[organism part]` rows 1–549 = `not available`. Manifest limitation 6 also says the B-cell source "is not given in the paper (refers to ref. 7)".
- Evidence: the paper's Results say: *"LCLs ... were generated by infecting healthy human peripheral blood B cells with either wild-type (EBV-WT) or LMP2A-knockout (LMP2A-KO) EBV."* `UBERON:0000178` "blood" resolves on OLS4. Batch rule 9 says to fill a value when evidence exists.
- Fix: set organism part = `blood` on all rows (samples table, then rebuild). Correct the limitation and add a manifest claim that cites the sentence.

### Minor
1. **pYome-B medium/heavy evidence is non-discriminating.** The manifest cites Spearman(M/L, H/L) = 0.90. That value would be the same if medium and heavy were swapped: Spearman between the pYome-A BCR effect and pYome-B log H/M is 0.03. The map is still correct, because the labelled Fig. 1D points decide it. MAPK9 Y185 has M/L log2 −0.50 and H/L +0.63, and the figure shows x≈−0.6, y≈+0.6. CD79A Y210 has −1.71/−1.01, and the figure shows x≈−1.7, y≈−1.0. So x = medium (EBV-WT) and y = heavy (EBV-WT + 5′ BCR). Fix: replace the manifest evidence with this.
2. **Disease justification is understated.** The manifest says "no donor disease stated", but the paper says "healthy" donors, and the cell-lines 1.1.0 docs say to use `normal` for non-disease-derived lines. The value `normal` (PATO:0000461) is correct. Fix: cite both.
3. **The donor confound is stated too weakly.** In fc170923_proteome_lcl.csv, allele-specific HLA protein groups have M/L 0.007–0.04 (HLA-DRB3, HLA-A, HLA-B) and 23–106× (HLA-DRB1/DRB5, HLA-B, HLA-C), consistent across r1–r5. H/L, which compares KO with KO, stays at about 1. So EBV-WT and LMP2A-KO almost certainly come from different donors, and genotype is confounded with donor. `individual` can stay `not available`. Fix: state the confound plainly in the limitations.
4. **Proteome replicate naming is not documented.** All five proteome replicates share one sample id and one prep date (`TO_K_Fish_220517_*_GPome_192_L2_rep1..5`), and the names say "GPome". The SDRF follows the paper's explicit "five biological replicates", which is acceptable. Fix: add a limitation about the conflict with the file names.

## Points tested and upheld
- **Channel map, Fig. 1A** (proteome, GPome, pYome-A): L = LMP2A-KO, M = EBV-WT, H = LMP2A-KO + 5′ anti-BCR. This matches Fig. 1A and the Methods. The data agree. In pYome-A, PRKCD Y334 has M/L −2.36 and H/L +1.48, and CD79A Y210 has −0.94/+1.6, matching the Fig. 1C positions with x = M and y = H. The GPome BCR signature is in H/L.
- **Channel map, Fig. 1D** (pYome-B): L = KO is supported, since pYome-B M/L agrees with pYome-A M/L (Spearman 0.74). M = WT and H = WT + BCR are decided by the Fig. 1D points (minor 1).
- **pYome-A vs pYome-B files:** the raw FileHeader times are 2017-07-17 for `TO_K_Fish_210417_140717_*` (method `TOP20_120K30_118min-pho_pYome`) and 2018-02-15 for `TO_K_Fish_071217_140218_*`. Only set A predates fc171012_pyome_lcl.csv (Oct 2017), so set B must be fc180322_pyome_lcl_bcr_stim.csv. The assignment is consistent.
- **pYome technical pairing:** in fc171012, M/L Pearson is 0.96 for r1/r4, 0.96 for r2/r5 and 0.97 for r3/r6, against 0.88–0.93 for all other pairs. Site counts pair the same way: 425/424, 224/221, 342/350. In pYome-B the correlations are flat (0.91–0.96), but the counts pair the same way (471/490, 399/407, 459/457). File names encode sample + rep1/rep2, so tech rep 1/2 within one source is justified.
- **Biological replicate numbering:** numbers restart per factor-value group and are unique per SILAC mix: KO-unstim and WT-unstim 1–15, KO-BCR 1–12, WT-BCR 1–3. The four sub-experiments have distinct prep-date prefixes (220517, 080817, 210417, 071217), so the SDRF does not double-count a reused lysate. Whether the three pYome IPs in a set came from independent cultures is unknown, and the limitations already say so. This follows rule 3 and is acceptable.
- **Stimulation time 5 min vs 3 h:** 5 min is supported by the Results ("5-min BCR-stimulated"; "stimulated for 5 min by BCR cross-linking"), Fig. 1A/1D ("5′") and the Methods Cell Culture section ("5 min (for all proteome analyses) or 3 h (for RNA-seq)"). The "3 h" in the proteome and pYome Methods and in the PRIDE protocol looks like a copy from the RNA-seq section. The proteome H/L shows no induction of immediate-early proteins (JUNB H/L 0.96). The conflict is recorded in the limitations. Upheld.
- **Infection:** `characteristics[infectious agent]` = `human gammaherpesvirus 4` (NCBITaxon:10376, label verified). A strain column separates EBV wild type from EBV LMP2A-knockout. The search used a human-only database, so no EBV proteome is implied. These custom characteristics are licensed by the spec's extension rules. Acceptable; the WT strain (e.g. B95-8) is not named in this paper, so leaving it out is correct.
- **Disease `normal`:** justified, because the donors were healthy and the cell-lines template says to use `normal` for non-disease-derived lines. In-vitro EBV immortalisation is encoded as infection, not as a donor disease.
- **Mouse block (36 raws):** the files are 28 `YLJI_310717_040817_FISH_GP9_TiO2_*` (= fc180214_gpome_mouse.csv: 2 replicates × 14 fractions) and 8 `J_Corso_*` (fc171021_pyome_mouse.csv has only 4 replicate columns, so 8 runs cannot be mapped). The paper names the three conditions but assigns no SILAC channel to them. Europe PMC supplementaryFiles says "not open access", the pnas.org SI returns 403, PMC bin is behind a bot check, and no preprint exists. The block is correct under rules 6 and 7, and the files are listed in BLOCKED.md and the manifest.
- **Technical values:** Q Exactive HF, DDA, HCD NCE 28, Orbitrap MS2 and scan range 350–1600 are confirmed from my own raw headers. Trypsin follows rule 2. Tolerances 6/20 ppm (Methods; the proteome search used "identical" settings). Phospho is present only in the GPome/pYome rows. SILAC UNIMOD 481/188/259/267 match medium Lys D4/Arg 13C6 and heavy Lys8/Arg10. Fractions frac5..17 and frac54 map to 1..14. DTT (proteome) and TCEP (GPome) are correct, and `not available` for the pYome kit is correct.
- **File mapping:** PRIDE lists 220 entries (219 raw + zip). The SDRF uses 183 raws, all present. The other 36 are exactly the mouse set.
