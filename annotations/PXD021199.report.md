## What changed in round 2

1. **The DH design now follows the evidence.**
   - The deposited `proteinGroups_interactome.txt` shows a bait-vs-background IP. PLEKHM1 has H/L 9.55 (12.4 normalized), against a median of about 0.99 over all groups. Interactors are enriched in heavy: MTOR 2.55, VPS39 4.09, VPS41 3.54, RPTOR 1.98.
   - The heavy channel is therefore annotated as the PLEKHM1-GFP pulldown.
   - The light source name no longer asserts the bait, and its bait status is `not available`.
   - Bait status has no licensed characteristics column. `genetic modification` and `genotype` are licensed only by `clinical-metadata`, a patient-study template. So bait status goes in the source names and in `factor value[genetic modification]`.
2. **Treatment is limited to what the protocol states.**
   - Light = `starvation` (EFO:0022074). The protocol says the light cells were "treated in EBSS for 4 hours to starve cells of amino acids".
   - Heavy = `not available`. Its treatment is never stated, so "control" is no longer inferred.
   - In DH, starvation is **confounded** with bait status, and the deposit cannot separate the two.
3. `comment[sdrf version]` changed from v1.2.0 to **v1.1.0**.
4. **Cleavage agent** = `NT=Trypsin;AC=MS:1001251`, the digestion enzyme (coordinator correction). The MaxQuant search rule `Trypsin/P` (MS:1001313) is recorded as a manifest note only.
5. **Cell line.** The protocol names the cells "HeLa FlpIn TREx", so `HeLa Flp-In T-REx` / **CVCL_C4ET** is used (Cellosaurus record checked live; its parent is CVCL_0030). The donor data are unchanged: 30Y6M, female, African American, uterine cervix, cervical adenocarcinoma.
6. **Culture medium added:** `Dulbecco's Modified Eagle Medium` (NCIT:C185409). The protocol says "cultured in DMEM supplemented with …" the SILAC amino acids.
7. **Manifest corrections.** The `sample preparation batch` claim now matches the SDRF. The DH batch label no longer asserts PLEKHM1-GFP. The DGM caveats were added to the limitations.

## Limitations / open items

The manifest `limitations` list has the full text.

1. The paper is unreachable. The channel design rests on the PRIDE protocol and the deposited MaxQuant outputs.
2. **DH:** starvation is confounded with bait status, and the light channel's bait status and the heavy channel's treatment are both unstated.
3. **DGM:** light = EBSS rests on one shared protocol sentence. Three signals do not fit it, though none is conclusive:
   - the processFolder is named `PLEK KUp` (possibly the mTOR inhibitor KU-0063794);
   - MTOR normalized H/L is 0.30;
   - PLEKHM1 S432 normalized H/L is 0.72 and S443 is 0.54, against 0.86 for the protein.
4. **DH4–6** are not in the deposited interactome search, which is depleted of low-MW proteins. They are inferred to be bands 4–6 of the same mix.
5. CVCL_C4ET is documented as the Harper-lab line. Its identity with this lab's stock is assumed from the name.
6. techsdrf is not installed. Values come from a range read of the first 1 MiB of each raw file.
7. `search-params` missed the MaxQuant 1.3 variable mods and labels, so they were read from the mqpar by hand.
