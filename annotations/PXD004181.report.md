**Checks against the deposited ratios:**
- **DGII heavy channels** show the heat-shock response expected from HSP90 inhibition: log2 H/L HSPA1A 3.7 and 2.9, DNAJB1 about 2. They also lose BCR-proximal pY sites (SYK, LYN, BTK).
- **DG_TelSyk heavy channel** shows the strongest BCR-pY induction (CD79A pY210 +3.2, SYK pY525 +5.2). The medium channel shows a weaker, overlapping induction.
- **DG_TelSyk medium and heavy channels** show no heat-shock response.

**Replicates:**
- Each `-1`/`-2` pair is two injections from one autosampler vial, according to the raw headers. They are encoded as technical replicates 1 and 2.
- Biological replicates are numbered within each factor group: untreated/wild type is 1 (DGII-1), 2 (DGII-2) and 3 (TelSyk); HSP90 inhibitor is 1 and 2; TEL-SYK is 1; BCR stimulation is 1.
- The proteome and pY-enriched preparations, and the two TelSyk pY immunoprecipitations, each have their own source name.

**Factor groups (convert-openms):** untreated/wild type 14 files, HSP90 inhibitor 8, TEL-SYK 6, BCR stimulation 6.

**Technical values:**
- Instrument from the raw headers: Q Exactive HF for 28 rows; Orbitrap Fusion Lumos with HCD and ion-trap MS2 for the TelSyk proteome (6 rows). PRIDE's instrument field ("Q Exactive") is wrong.
- Tolerances: precursor 6 ppm (paper). Fragment 20 ppm for Orbitrap MS2 and 0.5 Da for ion-trap MS2 (`parameters.txt` FTMS/ITMS settings).
- Modifications are read from `summary.txt`/`parameters.txt`: Carbamidomethyl fixed; Oxidation, Acetyl (protein N-term), Deamidated (N) and Phospho (STY) variable; plus the SILAC labels. The DGII rows have `not applicable` in the Arg6/Lys4 columns because that search had no medium label.
- Cleavage agent is Trypsin (the search rule was Trypsin/P).

## Open items (`not available` values and uncertain points)
- **DGII compound:** whether each DGII heavy channel got AT13387 or STA-9090 is undocumented. `characteristics[compound]` is `not available`, so both heavy channels share the factor value `HSP90 Inhibitor` even though they may be different drugs.
- **TelSyk pY samples:** it is undocumented whether pY 1 and pY 2 are independent cultures. They are encoded as one experiment (biological replicate 1) with distinct source names.
- **Genetic modification inferred:** `wild type` for the DGII rows and the TelSyk heavy channel is inferred, because no construct is described for them.
- **Missing reagents:** the BCR stimulation reagent for the MS samples and the reduction/alkylation reagents are not stated.
- **Precursor tolerance:** 6 ppm is the paper's stated value. The main-search tolerance per parameter group is not deposited.
- **Not available:** passage number, reduction reagent, alkylation reagent, and the compound for the HSP90-inhibitor rows.
- **Reconcile finding:** reconcile reports one MINOR `cleavage_agent_unsupported`. I rejected it: `summary.txt` says Enzyme = Trypsin/P, which is consistent with a trypsin digest. See `validation.txt`.
- **Supplement unavailable:** supplemental Fig. 1C / the supplemental Methods could not be retrieved. ashpublications returns a Cloudflare check (not bypassed), PMC has no supplement, and Europe PMC says the article is not open access.

## What would unblock the rest
The Blood data supplement, or an author statement, giving the L/M/H conditions for `DD`, `DGI_w`, `DGI_X` and `DGI_Y`, and the inhibitor used in each of `DGII_1` and `DGII_2`.
