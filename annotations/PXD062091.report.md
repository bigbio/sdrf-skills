Where the map comes from:
- The paper Methods describe a label swap: "light control + heavy sgMETTL13; reverse heavy control + light sgMETTL13".
- Supplementary Table 2A headers say this explicitly: `H/L sgControl-H_1..3` and `L/H sgControl-L_1, sgControl-L_2`.
- I checked the map against the deposited data. Every protein's Table 2A value equals proteinGroups `Ratio H/L WT-H_n`, or its reciprocal for WT-L (100% agreement on 1577–2169 proteins per run). Supp `sgControl-L_2` matches deposited WT-L_3.
- The dDENPs MRPS36, MRPL14, RALA and SSR4 have H/L > 1 in every WT-H run and < 1 in every WT-L run, which fits their reported loss after METTL13 depletion.

Groups:
- sgControl: 5 rows. sgMETTL13: 5 rows.
- Biological replicate is numbered 1–5 within each group, one number per independent experiment (n = 5, Fig. 1b/c).
- Factor: `factor value[genetic modification]`.
