## Study
"Global Interactome Mapping Reveals Pro-tumorigenic Interactions of NF-κB in Breast Cancer". Lapcik P et al., *Mol Cell Proteomics* 2024;23:100744, doi:10.1016/j.mcpro.2024.100744, PMID 38417630, PMC10988130 (OA text read).

The authors used SEC-PCP-SILAC on MCF-7 cells. NF-κB was inhibited by transient transfection of the FLAG-ΔN-IκBα super-repressor (pCMV4-FLAG-ΔN IκBα); controls got the empty pCMV4 vector. Equal volumes of the H and M lysates were mixed and separated by SEC into 80 fractions. The L lysate (untransfected cells) was SEC-separated into 20 fractions, which were pooled. The pool was spread evenly over the 80 H/M fractions as an internal standard. Each fraction was measured once (DDA, Orbitrap Fusion Lumos, HCD) and all were searched together in MaxQuant 2.0.3.0.

No PXD040125 folder exists in sdrf-annotated-datasets. No duplicate deposit was found.
