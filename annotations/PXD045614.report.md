## Templates

`ms-proteomics v1.1.0`, `vertebrates v1.1.0`, `single-cell v1.0.0`.

`vertebrates`, not `human` — mouse. It is the template that makes
`characteristics[developmental stage]` **required** and licenses `strain or breed` and `sex`.

`single-cell` licenses `sample type`, `cells per well`, `cell identifier`,
`single cell isolation protocol`, and the carrier/reference channel columns — all load-bearing here,
because the central problem of this deposition is separating single oocytes from bulk.

### `dia-acquisition` was deliberately NOT declared — and this is the key template decision

The screen row said `acquisition=both`, and it is right: this deposition mixes DDA, BoxCar, and DIA.
`dia-acquisition v1.1.0` hard-restricts `comment[proteomics data acquisition method]` with a
`single_cardinality_validator` **plus** a `values` validator whose allowed list is exactly
`[Data-independent acquisition]`. Declaring it on a mixed deposition forces one of two frauds:

1. relabel the 28 DDA/BoxCar/library/wash runs as DIA, or
2. drop those 28 runs to make the file DIA-pure.

Both were rejected. `ms-proteomics` places no such restriction on the acquisition column, so DDA and
DIA coexist legally there. This was caught by validation, not by reading — the first build declared
`dia-acquisition` and `parse_sdrf` refused it. The template is built for DIA-**only** depositions.

Consequence: the two DIA-only columns worth carrying (`comment[scan window lower/upper limit]`) are
unlicensed without that template. The same fact — a 350–1500 m/z MS1 range, confirmed in every raw
header — is carried instead by `comment[ms min mz]` / `comment[ms max mz]` (`350 m/z` / `1500 m/z`),
which `ms-proteomics` licenses.

## Scope: 67 deposited → 67 annotated (100%, nothing excluded)

The deposition contains **70 files**: 67 `.raw`, plus `Search.zip` (73 GB), `PDV-2.1.0.zip`, and
`Visualize_phosphorylation_spectra.zip`. All 67 raw files are annotated. The file list came from
`https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v3/projects/PXD045614/files/all`, and the SDRF's
`comment[data file]` set was **programmatically reconciled against it — 67/67, zero missing, zero
invented**, in both directions.

> That reconciliation earned its keep: an early build *constructed* the library filenames from a date
> pattern and produced `GV-MII-Zygote-HPRP-library-F10_20211226.raw`. The real file is
> `...F10_20211225.raw`. The generator now reads all 12 library names out of `files/all` and asserts
> F1–F12 are present. This is the "NEVER guess file names" rule paying out.

**42 single oocytes, 22 bulk, 3 blanks = 67.**

The screen's `~27` is the POA/melatonin arm only (the paper's headline: "Nine biological replicates
of each group were analyzed... a total of 27 samples"). It undercounts the 9 MG132-study oocytes and
the 6 method-comparison oocytes, which are equally single cells.

**The 10 ng runs are bulk and are annotated as such.** The paper is explicit: "we evaluated the
performance of BoxCar and DIA compared with DDA... **using 10 ng mouse oocyte protein, whose protein
amount is similar to one single mouse oocyte**". Protein-amount-equivalent to one oocyte is not one
oocyte. Their source is the pooled 1000 GV + 1000 MII + 1000 zygote lysate. Writing
`cells per well = 1` there was the trap; `PRIDE:0000907 bulk control` is defined as "a bulk sample
used as a control in single-cell proteomics experiments to benchmark single-cell..." — an exact fit.

**No runs were excluded.** The brief permits dropping sampleless blanks, but PRIDE has an `empty`
term (`PRIDE:0000903`) and `annotations/PXD014256.sdrf.tsv` sets the precedent (`cells per well = 0`,
biological replicate `1`). Annotating them beats excluding them, and gives 100% deposition coverage.

### Group → treatment/time mapping, and how `Rescue` was resolved

| files | group | `treatment` | `sampling time` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `..._Fresh_R1..9`, `SingleMII_Fresh_R1..3`, `SingleMII-{DDA,DIA}-R1..3` | fresh | `not applicable` | `0 hour` |
| `..._Aging_R1..9`, `SingleMII_Aging_R1..3` | POA / aged | Dimethyl Sulfoxide `NCIT:C437` (vehicle) | `16 hour` |
| `..._Rescue_R1..9` | melatonin | Melatonin `NCIT:C2267` | `16 hour` |
| `SingleMII_MG132_R1..3` | MG132 | Proteasome Inhibitor MG-132 `NCIT:C128633` | `16 hour` |

`Rescue` = melatonin **by elimination, not by guess** — the same move as `MII`=`IVO` in PXD023366.
The paper: "nine fresh oocytes, nine aged oocytes, and nine melatonin-treated oocytes were collected
to profile the single-cell DIA-based proteome and phosphoproteome." Three groups of nine; the
deposition has exactly three groups of nine; `Fresh`=fresh and `Aging`=aged; therefore
`Rescue`=melatonin. Melatonin is also the paper's rescue agent throughout ("protein changes can be
rescued by melatonin treatment"). No `Melatonin`-named files exist.

`sampling time` is **duration of post-ovulatory culture**, not clock time: fresh oocytes were "directly
used" (`0 hour`); aged/melatonin/MG132 were "cultured for 16 h in a droplet of M2 medium". All
oocytes were collected 14 h post-hCG, so `0 hour` = 14 h post-hCG and `16 hour` = 30 h post-hCG.

## Why `characteristics[developmental stage]` is `adult` and NOT the oocyte's maturation stage

This is the PXD023366 lesson applied. TERMS.tsv defines the column as "Developmental stage of **the
organism or donor**". The donors are ICR females **6–8 weeks old** — sexually mature → `adult`
(`EFO:0001272`), cohort-level evidence covering every row, exactly the case Step 1.4 of the skill
permits. It is *not* the GV/MII/zygote stage of the oocyte, however much the column name invites it.

The oocyte's own identity is carried by `characteristics[cell type]` instead, and the study's variable
by `treatment` + `sampling time`.

Unlike PXD023366, **no free-text `phenotype` column was needed**, so `clinical-metadata` is not
declared — which is right, since these are mice, not patients. The factor here is a real drug
treatment with real NCIT terms, so it lives in the columns designed for it.

## Three `characteristics[cell type]` columns — the pooled library is genuinely three cell types

`characteristics[cell type]` has `cardinality: multiple` and `allow_pooled: false`. The bulk lysate is
"1000 germinal vesicle oocytes, 1000 MII oocytes, and 1000 zygotes" — one pool, three cell types, and
no CL term for that mixture. The spec-sanctioned mechanism is multiple columns:

| column | single oocytes & blanks | pooled bulk (22 rows) |
|---|---|---|
| `cell type` #1 | `secondary oocyte` CL:0000655 / `not applicable` | `secondary oocyte` CL:0000655 |
| `cell type` #2 | `not applicable` | `primary oocyte` CL:0000654 |
| `cell type` #3 | `not applicable` | `zygote` CL:0010017 |

GV oocytes are arrested in prophase I → **primary** oocyte (CL:0000654, "has not completed meiosis
I"). All the single cells here are ovulated MII oocytes — meiosis I complete → **secondary** oocyte
(CL:0000655). Column #1 is `secondary oocyte` on every biological row because every sample contains
MII material.

## Key decisions and evidence

**Every run's acquisition mode was verified independently from its own raw header**, not inferred from
its filename. HTTP-range-read the first 512 KB, decoded UTF-16-LE, parsed the Xcalibur method summary:

| group | header evidence | annotated |
|---|---|---|
| `10ng-DDA`, `HPRP-library`, `phospho_library`, `SingleMII-DDA` | `Data Dependent`, no MSX | DDA `PRIDE:0000627` |
| `10ng-Boxcar-3MSX` | `Data Dependent` **+** `MSX`/`Multiplex`, scan ranges 350–1500 *and* 350–2000 | DDA `PRIDE:0000627` |
| `10ng-DIA`, `SingleMII-DIA`, `InVitroAging*`, `SingleMII_*` | `tMSn` + `MSX`/`Multiplex` | DIA `PRIDE:0000450` |
| `R{1,2,3}-Wash` | `Data Dependent`, 24-min method | DDA `PRIDE:0000627` |

**BoxCar is an ontology gap, and is annotated as DDA on purpose.** `Proteomics data acquisition
method` (PRIDE:0000659) has exactly **four** children — DIA, DDA, PRM, SRM. **No BoxCar term exists**
in PRIDE or MS (searched both). BoxCar is a multiplexed-**MS1** scheme whose MS2 selection is
data-dependent; the raw header confirms this precisely (`Data Dependent` *and* `MSX` together, unlike
the DIA files which show `tMSn`), and the authors processed BoxCar with MaxQuant exactly as they did
DDA. `Data-dependent acquisition` is therefore true at the MS2 level rather than a compromise. The
BoxCar specificity survives in `comment[data file]` (`...-Boxcar-3MSX-...`, and "3MSX" matches the
paper's "The scan was set as 3 and the box was set as 10"). **Flagged as a genuine ontology gap.**

**`getChildren(PRIDE:0000895)` returns an incomplete picture — do not trust it as the value list.**
It reports only 5 direct children (Quality Control Sample, single cell, multiplex sample, standard,
environmental sample), which would have forced `sample type = not applicable` on all 25 non-single-cell
rows. But `pooled` (PRIDE:0000902), `empty` (PRIDE:0000903), and `bulk control` (PRIDE:0000907) all
exist and all resolve as *descendants* of PRIDE:0000895 — verified via the OLS `/ancestors` endpoint
(`pooled`/`empty` sit under `multiplex sample`; `bulk control` under `Quality Control Sample`). They
were found only because `annotations/PXD014256.sdrf.tsv` used two of them. Direct-children queries
under-report; check ancestors.

**The `20210108` in six filenames is a typo for `20220108`.** `GV-MII-Zygote-10ng-Boxcar-3MSX-R*` and
`GV-MII-Zygote-10ng-DDA-R*` are named as if acquired 2021-01-08 — a year before the library
(2021-12) and before the `10ng-DIA` counterpart (2022-01-08). Their raw headers show the instrument
method was **last modified 01/02/2022**; a run cannot execute a method modified 11 months after it.
So the real date is 2022-01-08 and the whole bulk arm (library 2021-12-16→26, 10 ng 2022-01-08,
SingleMII-DDA 2022-01-02) is contemporaneous, consistent with one pooled collection.
`comment[acquisition date]` is therefore **omitted**: filename dates are demonstrably unreliable here
and the headers do not expose a parseable acquisition timestamp in the first 4 KB.

**Search settings are per-run, because two different engines were used.** The paper: DDA and BoxCar →
MaxQuant 1.6.5.0; DIA → FragPipe `DIA_DIA-Umpire_SpecLib_Quant`.

| rows | engine | precursor | fragment |
|---|---|---|---|
| DDA + BoxCar (12: 6 bulk, 3 SingleMII-DDA, 3 washes n/a) | MaxQuant | **4.5 ppm** (main search, not the 20 ppm first search) | 20 ppm |
| DIA (39) + library DDA (13) | FragPipe / MSFragger | 20 ppm | 20 ppm |

The 4.5 ppm main-search choice follows the PXD023366 precedent. DIA-Umpire's own 10 ppm pseudo-spectrum
extraction tolerance is not the database-search tolerance and is not annotated.

Modifications (all four on every biological row, per the stated global search settings):
`Carbamidomethyl` UNIMOD:**4** fixed on C; `Oxidation` UNIMOD:**35** variable on M; `Acetyl`
UNIMOD:**1** variable on `PP=Protein N-term` (not the UNIMOD:21 swap; positional value in `PP=`, not
`TA=`); `Phospho` UNIMOD:**21** variable on S,T,Y. Phospho belongs on the single-cell DIA rows because
the phosphoproteome was read from **the same DIA raw files** searched against a separate phospho
library — there is no separate single-cell phospho acquisition ("without phosphopeptide enrichment").

**One `source name` for all 22 bulk rows** (`pool-GV-MII-zygote`). The paper describes a single
collection serving library construction; all 22 filenames share the `GV-MII-Zygote` prefix; and the
date analysis above puts them all in 2021-12/2022-01. The 12 HPRP fractions, the IMAC phospho library,
and the 10 ng aliquots are processing variants of that one biological pool, distinguished by
`comment[fractionation method]`, `characteristics[enrichment process]`, and `sample type` — not by
inventing separate source names. *Residual uncertainty:* the paper never states the 10 ng aliquots came
from the identical physical digest as the library. If a reviewer disagrees, the fix is splitting the
source name, not changing any other cell.

## Values deliberately left `not available` / `not applicable`

| column | rows | value | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `characteristics[disease]` | all bio | `normal` PATO:0000461 | healthy ICR mice, no disease model |
| `characteristics[sex]` | 22 bulk | `pooled` | oocytes are female by definition, but the pool contains **zygotes of undetermined sex**. `allow_pooled=true`. `female` would overclaim. |
| `characteristics[cells per well]` | 22 bulk | `not applicable` | defined as "cells per well/reaction (use 1 for true single cells)". A 10 ng aliquot / a 1-of-12 fraction of a 3000-oocyte lysate has no well-based integer cell count. `1` would be the fabrication. |
| `comment[reduction reagent]` | 22 bulk | `not available` | single oocytes state TCEP → `PRIDE:0000609`. The bulk protocol says only "reduced, alkylated" — reagent never named. |
| `characteristics[organism]` etc. | 3 washes | `not applicable` | blanks have no sample |

**`comment[alkylation reagent]` is omitted, and this is a paper defect worth flagging.** Methods say
each oocyte was lysed in "25 mM **cyanoacetamide**" — but cyanoacetamide does not produce
Carbamidomethyl (+57.021), which is exactly what the authors set as their fixed modification.
25 mM CAA + 10 mM TCEP is the classic **chloroacetamide** one-pot, and "CAA" is the standard
abbreviation for chloroacetamide; `Chloroacetamide (ClAA)` PRIDE:0000600 exists. The paper has almost
certainly mis-expanded its own abbreviation. **Asserting chloroacetamide would be an inference against
the paper's literal text, so no reagent is asserted at all** — the paper's text and its own search
settings contradict each other, and no row can carry a defensible term (the bulk rows never name one
either). Recorded here rather than laundered into a cell.

**Column-inclusion rule used:** include a column when ≥1 row carries a real value; omit when no row
can. Hence `reduction reagent` in (TCEP on 42 rows), `alkylation reagent` out.
`comment[carrier channel]` / `comment[reference channel]` are the deliberate exception — kept at
`not applicable` on all 67 rows, because *absence of a carrier* is itself the finding in a label-free
SCP study (and both are `recommended` by `single-cell` precisely for that reason). This corroborates
the screen's `carrier=no`.

**`comment[isolation window width]` and `comment[dia method]` are unrepresentable and were dropped
with `dia-acquisition`.** The DIA scheme is variable-width — **50 Da over 350–450 m/z, 15 Da over
450–960 m/z, 100 Da over 960–1500 m/z** — and the column's grammar is a single `numeric m/z`; no
single number is true. `dia method` has no PRIDE term for plain variable-window Orbitrap DIA (the
candidates are SWATH-MS, MSE, HDMSE, PAcIFIC, SONAR, diaPASEF — none apply). The scheme is recorded
here in full instead.

## Values inferred rather than stated

- **`characteristics[single cell isolation protocol] = manual picking`** (42 single-cell rows). The
  column is `required`. The proteomics Methods never state the transfer method; the scRNA-seq Methods
  say "Single oocytes in different groups were chosen and **aspirated using pipettes under a
  microscope**", cumulus was removed with hyaluronidase, and no FACS / microfluidics / nanoPOTS /
  cellenONE appears anywhere. Same inference, same evidence class, as PXD023366. Matches the screen's
  `isolation=manual picking`.
- **`characteristics[organism part] = ovary`** (UBERON:0000992). Follows PXD023366 for
  cross-dataset integration. Note the collection sites differ: MII oocytes were flushed from **oviduct
  ampullae** post-ovulation, and the pool's zygotes were made by IVF *in vitro*. `ovary` is the
  normalized anatomical origin of the oocyte lineage, not the collection site.
- **`comment[ms2 mass analyzer] = orbitrap`** — every mode detects MS2 in the Orbitrap (DDA/DIA 30 K,
  BoxCar 120 K, library 15 K).
- **`developmental stage = adult` on the pooled rows**, which contain zygotes. A zygote is arguably an
  organism at zygote stage; the column is defined as the *donor's* stage and every donor (6–8 wk
  females, 10–20 wk males) was adult. The zygote material is carried by `cell type` #3.

## Design note (recorded, not a defect)

The MG132 arm (n=3/group, 2023) and the melatonin arm (n=9/group, 2022) are **separate experiments
with their own fresh and aged controls**, run ~13 months apart on the same instrument. They are not
one four-arm design and must not be pooled across `fresh`/`aged`: batch is fully confounded with arm.
Source names (`oocyte-POA-*` vs `oocyte-MG132expt-*`) keep them separable; the SDRF records the
structure faithfully and `factor value[treatment]` × `factor value[sampling time]` uniquely identifies
the four groups **within** each arm.

## What a reviewer should attack first

1. `sample type = bulk control` on the 10 ng runs vs. `pooled` on the library runs — both are pooled
   lysate; the distinction rests on `bulk control`'s definition naming single-cell benchmarking.
2. The single `source name` for all 22 bulk rows (identity of the 10 ng digest and the library digest
   is inferred, not stated).
3. `cells per well = not applicable` vs. `3000` for the library rows.
4. Omitting `comment[alkylation reagent]` rather than recording `not available` on all rows.
5. `developmental stage = adult` on rows whose `cell type` #3 is `zygote`.
