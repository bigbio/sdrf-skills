## Templates

`ms-proteomics v1.1.0`, `human v1.1.0`, `single-cell v1.0.0`.

**`cell-lines` was deliberately NOT declared, and this is the central decision in this file.**

`characteristics[cell line]` is `required` in `cell-lines/1.1.0` with **both** `allow_not_applicable`
and `allow_not_available` set to `false`. This deposition is a **mixed cohort**: only 3 of 12 runs
are a cell line. The 6 primary neurons (LCM from post-mortem spinal cord) and 3 blanks have no cell
line and **no legal value exists for them** under that template. Declaring `cell-lines` would
therefore require asserting a cell line for 9 rows that have none.

The alternative — declaring `cell-lines` and annotating only the 3 HeLa runs — would discard the
study's entire biological result (the MN vs IN comparison, Fig. 4). Rejected.

**What this costs:** `characteristics[cell line]`, `[cellosaurus accession]`, and `[cellosaurus name]`
are licensed *only* by `cell-lines`, so HeLa's `CVCL_0030` is not recoverable from this file as a
dedicated column. Mitigation below. Recorded in `annotations/BLOCKED.md` as a spec limitation.

## Key decisions and evidence

**HeLa identity preserved via CLO, not via `cell line`.** With `cell-lines` undeclarable, the only
spec-legal machine-readable home for HeLa identity is `characteristics[cell type]`, whose resolved
validator explicitly allows **`clo`** alongside `cl`/`bto`. CLO contains nothing *but* cell lines,
so the spec sanctioning CLO here is the spec sanctioning cell-line terms here. HeLa rows therefore
carry `NT=HeLa cell;AC=CLO:0003684`.

This **deliberately diverges from `PXD052416`**, which used `epithelial cell` (CL:0000066) for cell
type — correct *there*, because that file could declare `cell-lines` and put HeLa in
`characteristics[cell line]`. Here the same choice would erase HeLa from a HeLa dataset entirely.
Flagged for the reviewer as the most contestable call in the file.

**The HeLa OLS trap was hit and avoided.** `mode="fuzzy"` on `HeLa` in EFO returns
`EFO:0001185` (HeLa) first — with **`HEp-2` (EFO:0006438) in the same result list**, which is what
smart mode returns as a confident single hit. Parental HeLa / `CVCL_0030` confirmed against the
live Cellosaurus API.

**HeLa donor demographics are Cellosaurus-sourced, not paper-sourced.** `age=30Y6M`, `sex=female`,
`ancestry=African American` (HANCESTRO:0568) come from the live Cellosaurus record for `CVCL_0030`
(`age`/`sex` fields; `Population` comment). The paper states none. These columns are licensed by
`human`, not by `cell-lines`, so dropping `cell-lines` does not un-license them. `organism part`
= `uterine cervix` (UBERON:0000002) is Cellosaurus's own `derived-from-site` xref for CVCL_0030,
and matches `PXD052416`.

**Disease.** HeLa → `cervical adenocarcinoma` (MONDO:0005153), matching `PXD052416`. Cellosaurus
asserts the finer `NCIT:C27677` "Human papillomavirus-related endocervical adenocarcinoma", and the
resolved validator *does* allow `ncit` — but TERMS.tsv's `values` field lists `MONDO, EFO, DOID, PATO`
in preference order, so MONDO wins. MONDO has no HPV-related cervical adenocarcinoma term (verified,
0 hits). The HPV sub-claim is dropped rather than smuggled in.

**Cleavage agents — partly inferred.** `Trypsin` (MS:1001251) + `Lys-C` (MS:1001309) on all 12 rows.
*Stated:* the paper's Material section lists "MS-grade trypsin and Lys-C were from Promega" among the
reagents for this work. *Inferred:* the paper never writes "digested with Lys-C and trypsin" — it
defers to the cited nanoPOTS one-pot protocol (refs 6, 16), which uses both. The search used tryptic
(plus semi-tryptic second-stage) specificity, but this column describes **digestion**, not the search.
Flagged as the weakest technical claim in the file.

**Modifications** follow the paper's stated search settings (identical in PRIDE's protocol text):
`Carbamidomethyl` UNIMOD:4 (TA=C, Fixed), `Oxidation` UNIMOD:35 (TA=M, Variable), `Acetyl` UNIMOD:1
(`PP=Protein N-term`, Variable). UNIMOD:1/UNIMOD:21 not swapped — Acetyl verified live. The
N-terminal position is in `PP=`, not `TA=`. PRIDE's `modifications` field lists the same three in
obfuscated PSI-MOD phrasing ("monohydroxylated residue", "acetylated residue", "iodoacetamide
derivatized residue") and does not conflict.

**Instrument — no PRIDE/paper conflict.** PRIDE says `Orbitrap Eclipse`; the paper says "Orbitrap
Eclipse Tribrid". `MS:1003029` "Orbitrap Eclipse" is the only MS term (verified) and covers both.
Raw headers were not range-fetched: there is nothing to adjudicate.

**Acquisition detail** all from the paper's LC-MS section: DDA; HCD at 30% NCE →
`beam-type collision-induced dissociation` (MS:1000422); MS2 in the ion trap →
`radial ejection linear ion trap` (MS:1000083) — corroborated by every filename carrying
`OTIT_HCD` (OT MS1 / IT MS2) and `2CV`. Precursor tolerance recorded as `5 ppm` (paper: "<5 ppm");
fragment `20 ppm`.

**Replicate structure.** Each single cell is its own biological replicate (1–3 within each group);
`technical replicate = 1` and `fraction identifier = 1` throughout — no fractionation, no repeat
injection.

**Donor structure — a real confounder, now explicit.** `characteristics[individual]` records that
all **6** neurons come from **one** spinal cord donor (`spinal_donor_1`; the paper says "human spinal
tissue", singular, from ProteoGenex). The headline MN-vs-IN comparison is therefore n=1 donor,
n=3 cells per group. That is visible in the SDRF rather than buried.

**Anatomy deliberately left at `spinal cord` (UBERON:0002240) for all 6 neurons.** The paper localizes
MNs to the *ventral horn* and INs to the *intermediate zone*. `UBERON:0002257` "ventral horn of spinal
cord" exists, but **no UBERON term exists for the spinal intermediate zone** (searched; the near
misses — `spinal cord lateral horn` UBERON:0004676, `intermediolateral nucleus` UBERON:0002175 — are
different structures and would be wrong). Annotating MNs at ventral-horn specificity while INs fall
back to `spinal cord` would imply anatomy was the annotation axis. The MN/IN distinction is carried
by `characteristics[cell type]` — `spinal cord motor neuron` (CL:0011001) vs `spinal cord interneuron`
(CL:0005000), both verified — which is the paper's actual claim.

**Factor.** `factor value[cell type]` — the study's comparison is between cell types (MN vs IN;
HeLa vs blank for the workflow arm). It mirrors `characteristics[cell type]`.

## Values deliberately left `not available` / `not applicable`

| column | rows | value | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `disease`, `age`, `sex`, `ancestry category` | 6 neurons | `not available` | The spinal cord donor's demographics and health status are **never stated**. The tissue is "deidentified ... exempt from human subjects regulations (45 CFR 46)" and commercially sourced. `normal` (PATO:0000461) is **not** asserted: the paper never says the donor was healthy, and the study motivates itself with ALS/SMA. Information exists but was not provided → `not available`, not `not applicable`. |
| all sample columns | 3 blanks | `not applicable` | Cell-free supernatant has no organism, cell type, disease, donor, or cell identity. |
| `characteristics[age]` | 3 blanks | `not available` | **Forced.** `age` is `required` with `allow_not_applicable: false` / `allow_not_available: true` — the semantically correct `not applicable` is illegal, so the only legal token is the less accurate `not available`. A spec wart, not a judgement. |
| `characteristics[organism]` | 3 blanks | `not applicable` | The blank is cell-free culture supernatant — a negative control with no organism sample. It does contain human-secreted and bovine media protein (the paper filters *Bos taurus* contaminants), so it is a mixture, not a human sample. `allow_not_available` is `false` here, so the choice was `Homo sapiens` or `not applicable`; the latter is the honest one. |
| `comment[lcm microscope model]` | HeLa, blanks | `not applicable` | Not LCM-isolated. |
| `comment[carrier|reference channel]` | all | `not applicable` | Label-free; no multiplexing. |

`characteristics[cells per well]` = `0` for blanks (pattern `^\d+$` permits it) — true and more
informative than a reserved word.
