## Study
Iglesias-Gato D, Thysell E, … Wikström P. *The proteome of prostate cancer bone metastasis reveals heterogeneity with prognostic implications.* Clin Cancer Res 2018;24(21):5433-44 (PMID 30042207, doi:10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-18-1229). Closed access: only the abstract was readable (Unpaywall closed, AACR behind Cloudflare 403, no PMC copy, DiVA unreachable).

FFPE tissue was mixed 1:1 with a heavy (Arg10/Lys8) super-SILAC standard made from four prostate-derived cell lines (LNCaP, PC-3, 22Rv1, WPMY-1 at 27:27:27:19). The mix was digested by FASP, split into SAX fractions and run on a Q Exactive (DDA, HCD). The data were searched with MaxQuant 1.3.7.1.

- **PRIDE "five fractions"** — the deposit and design template have 6 per sample. The data wins.
- **PRIDE disease DOID:2526 prostate adenocarcinoma** — this is the submitter dropdown. The broader "metastatic prostate carcinoma" is used because per-sample histology is unavailable.

## Judgement calls for the reviewer to attack
1. **Inferring PC31–PC52 = the 22 bone metastases by exclusion.** The other 36 experiments are in the six localized-PCa PXDs, and the count matches n=22, but no document names them.
2. **Super-SILAC heavy rows as per-run reference sources.** They are encoded as sample type reference, biological replicate/age/individual pooled, `SN=` with the cell-line names, organism part prostate gland, and disease not applicable. They follow the PXD002882 pattern, and `factor value[disease]` contrasts patient tissue with the reference.
3. **`characteristics[individual]` = PCnn and the omission of treatment-status columns.** The sample code is used as the patient ID on the basis of the abstract's 22 patients / 22 tumours. The treatment-status columns are left out rather than set to `not available`. The disease term is the metastatic parent (MONDO:0004956) rather than PRIDE's prostate adenocarcinoma.
