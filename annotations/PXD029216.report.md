## Key evidence and decisions
- **Channel maps.** These come from the deposited `SWOG_NPM1_TMT_label_key.xls` (4 sheets). They were cross-checked against the search outputs:
  - **SWOG27:** RPS4Y1 reporter intensity separates the Table S13 males (27-61) from the females (3-16) on **27/27 channels**.
  - **NPM1 arm:** 7/7 patients that also appear in Table S13 agree.
  - **Gr08 reshoot:** its channel-intensity profile matches Gr08 channel for channel. The key also lists both injections for every Gr08 channel, so the reshoot is technical replicate 2.
- **The SILAC arm is really multiplexed (rule 14).** MaxQuant reports Multiplicity 2 and Labels1 Arg10;Lys8.
  - The patient is the light channel. The patient material is FACS-sorted, cryopreserved primary cells lysed without culture, so it cannot carry a metabolic label.
  - Data check: haemoglobin (HBB) has Intensity L ≫ H in all 38 experiments (about 100×), so blood-derived material is in L. MPO is mostly L > H.
  - Heavy rows are encoded as a `reference` of **unreported composition** (rule 18). No cell lines are named, because no source names them.
- **Undocumented channels left out (the reason for PARTIAL).** These channels carry reporter signal, but the label key leaves them blank. They are not annotated, so no identity is invented:
  - SWOG27 TMT131 in groups A/B/C. Its signal is 0.86-0.97 of the strongest channel. Its RPS4Y1 level is intermediate, which fits a pooled reference, but nothing states that.
  - E2/E3 TMT131N/131C. In both plexes their reporter heights in the `.msf` are comparable to channels 1-9.
  - Total: 168 (run, channel) pairs.
- **Clinical data (rule 18).** Age, sex, treatment status, treatment response (CR → NCIT:C4870 Complete Remission; No CR → NCIT:C70622 Primary Induction Failure), developmental stage and a clinical-data string come **only from Table S13**. That table covers 27 SWOG27 + 3 FHCC/UW-HDR3 patients. The clinical-data string holds the other mutations, cytogenetic risk, karyotype, ELN 2017, protocol and induction regimen.
  - These values were propagated by patient ID to the same patients in the NPM1 arm: AML1050, AML039, AML044, AML058, AML158, AML179, AML185.
  - Every other patient has `not available`. The NPM1 sheet's Age_Group gives `adult` only.
- **The sorted population is per sample, not per patient.** AML044, AML058 and AML185 were sorted into different fractions in SWOG27 and in NPM1. Source names therefore carry an arm suffix (`_SWOG27`, `_NPM1`, `_FHUW-E2/E3`, `_SILAC`), and `characteristics[individual]` links the same patient across arms.
- **Factors.** The factors are the variables the study compares (refinement b):
  - TMT file: `factor value[disease]` (NPM1-mutant AML vs normal controls), `factor value[genotype]` (FLT3-ITD/NPM1 status; the paper compares mutant vs non-mutant) and `factor value[phenotype]` (sorted population: MNC vs differentiated vs undifferentiated VLBs in E2; CD34+ vs CD34− in Tables S2/S6/S7).
  - SILAC file: `factor value[genotype]`.
- **Instrument conflicts.**
  - PRIDE says Orbitrap Fusion only. The Supplemental Methods say Orbitrap Eclipse for SWOG27, but those runs were acquired in March 2018.
  - Raw headers: Fusion for the SILAC and SWOG27 arms, Eclipse (with FAIMS) for the NPM1 arm and E2/E3. The raw headers win.
- **Modifications** were read from the search outputs (rule 5; parts built separately with identical headers):
  - MaxQuant arms: Carbamidomethyl C fixed and Oxidation M variable. The TMT arms add TMT6plex K/N-term fixed, and the SILAC arm adds Arg10 (UNIMOD:267) and Lys8 (UNIMOD:259) as Variable (rule 11).
  - PD (E2/E3): additionally Acetyl, Met-loss and Met-loss+Acetyl at the protein N-terminus. PRIDE's text omits these. The `.msf` WorkflowXML was read by HTTP-range SQLite and wins.
- **Tolerances.**
  - PD: 10 ppm / 0.6 Da.
  - MaxQuant fragment: FTMS 20 ppm (TMT), ITMS 0.5 Da (SILAC).
  - MaxQuant precursor: 20 ppm, as stated by PRIDE/Supplement. MaxQuant's main-search default is 4.5 ppm, and the deposited output does not record the value (limitation).
- **Cleavage.** Trypsin in every arm. E2/E3 also get a second `comment[cleavage agent details]` = Lys-C (rLys-C then trypsin; rule 2), inserted by a scripted post-build because the column is single-valued in the contract. SWOG27 is trypsin-only (stated). NPM1 is not described, so its second column is `not applicable`.
- **Reduction/alkylation.**
  - SWOG27 and E2: TCEP / iodoacetamide (stated).
  - E3: inferred from E2 (same JN092820 prep batch, same acquisition date, identical PD workflow).
  - NPM1 and SILAC: `not available`. The searches set Carbamidomethyl fixed, so alkylation happened but the reagent is unstated.
- **Cell lines in the NPM1 plexes.** MOLM-14 (label "Molm14.L1"), OCI-AML-3, KG-1a, ME-1, Kasumi-1 and PL-21 were verified live in Cellosaurus. The `sdrf-tools cellline` fuzzy match KG-1a → KG-1-C was wrong and was overridden. `cell-lines` is **not** declared, because its required `characteristics[cell line]` forbids both reserved words and the patient, control and pool channels of the same plex would need one. Cell-line identity is in `characteristics[individual]` and `characteristics[phenotype]` (name + CVCL), and donor age/sex come from Cellosaurus.
- **NPM1 plexes, other channels.**
  - 10 channels without a patient ID (8 pediatric, 2 adult) are annotated as unidentified NPM1-mutant AML. Their sample sheet gives cohort, age group and sort gate.
  - NPM1_Pool and Control_Pool are `pooled`.
  - The NL### donors are `normal`.

## Limitations (also in both manifests)
- techsdrf is not installed. Instrument, CE and MS2 detector were read from 9 raw headers (one per run group).
- The MaxQuant precursor tolerance is stated in prose only.
- 168 (run, channel) pairs are omitted as undocumented.
- `convert-openms` fails on the full SILAC file because of the rule-5 `not applicable` tolerances.
- The SILAC and NPM1 arms and plex E3 are not described in the publication.
- Table S13 AML179 says sex F but karyotype 46,XY. Proteomics supports female.
- Deposited raw files were renamed from the original acquisition names (e.g. RO50479 → AML1027_R2). The name→patient mapping is the submitter's.
- **SILAC raw-header names (all 228 headers scanned in review round 1).** Four groups disagree with their deposited names:
  - AML1005: F01–F05 are named F02–F06 of RO50063, and F06 is named as a yeast QC run.
  - AML1016: F01–F05 are named F02–F06, and F06 is named as a blank.
  - AML1029: F06 is named RO50145, which is AML1012's specimen code.
  - AML1038: F01–F05 are named F02–F06, and F06 is named F06b.
  - Resolution: a per-run L/H profile correlation (reviewer-supplied, reproduced in `fix-PXD029216/silac_f06_profile.tsv`) ranks the deposited patient first for every F06 (r 0.856, 0.893, 0.839, 0.887). Deposited names and fraction numbers are kept. AML1038 F06 is only marginally ahead of AML1005 (0.8871 vs 0.8868).
- **NPM1 Gr07/Gr08 header names are scrambled.** Deposited Gr08 F01–F08 carry Gr07 F17–F23 names, and Gr07 F09–F24 carry Gr07 F01–F16 names. Timestamped duplicate names also appear in Gr01, Gr04 and Gr09. The reviewer's reporter-profile and peptide-overlap checks support the deposited names, which are kept (manifest limitation).
- **Pool encoding assumption.** NPM1_Pool and Control_Pool are encoded as one source per plex (11 each). Nothing deposited says whether one bridging pool was reused across plexes.
- **FAIMS.** All 336 NPM1/E2/E3 raws were acquired with FAIMS CV −40/−60/−80 V (raw headers; MaxQuant splits each NPM1 raw into `_-40/_-60/_-80`). This is recorded in a new `comment[FAIMS compensation voltage]` column (`-40 V,-60 V,-80 V`; SWOG27 `not applicable`), following curated precedent PXD045616.

## Judgement calls the reviewer should attack hardest
1. **Omitting the 5 undocumented-but-occupied channels** (SWOG27 TMT131; E2/E3 TMT131N/C). The alternative is to block whole arms, as precedent PXD031662 did with 9/11 unknown channels. Is a channel-level omission acceptable here, with 1-2 channels unknown per plex and every other channel documented and cross-validated?
2. **SILAC encoding:**
   - light = patient and heavy = reference of unknown composition, with no explicit statement of the label direction (biology + HBB L≫H data instead).
   - Keeping AML1027_R2 (not in the deposited search, but documented by the label key) with tolerances `not applicable` but the SILAC label mods kept. AML1036 was removed in review round 1.
3. **Propagating Table S13 clinical data across arms by patient ID**, and the fields derived from it:
   - NPM1-arm overlaps.
   - `Primary Induction Failure` for "no CR within 12 months".
   - `pre-treatment` from the paper's "previously untreated" for the S13 patients.
   - Choosing `factor value[phenotype]` (sorted population) and `factor value[disease]` alongside genotype as the compared variables.
