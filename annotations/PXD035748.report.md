## Study
- Goyal A, Bauer J, Hey J, Papageorgiou DN, … Krijgsveld J, Walz JS, Plass C. *DNMT and HDAC inhibition induces immunogenic neoantigens from human endogenous retroviral element-derived transcripts.* Nat Commun 2023;14:6731. doi:10.1038/s41467-023-42417-w, PMID 37872136, PMC10593957 (open-access full text).
- A Europe PMC search for PXD035748 finds only this paper, with no data descriptor. The companion immunopeptidomics data are a different deposit, PXD035750, so this is not a duplicate. `sdrf-tools curated PXD035748` returns nothing.
- Design of the annotated arm:
  - NCI-H1299 cells were treated with DMSO or with DAC + SB939 (500 nM decitabine for 72 h plus 500 nM SB939/pracinostat for 18 h).
  - Cells were depleted of Met/Lys/Arg for 30 min, then pulse-labelled for 4 h with 0.1 mM AHA plus SILAC amino acids: medium (Arg6/Lys4) for DMSO, heavy (Arg10/Lys8) for the inhibitors.
  - The two extracts were mixed 1:1. Newly synthesised proteins were enriched by click chemistry and digested on-bead with Trypsin/LysC.
  - Peptides were HpH-fractionated (40 fractions concatenated into 8) and run on a Q Exactive HF (DDA, HCD 26%).
  - There are 7 biological replicates.
- SILAC check (rule 14): `summary.txt` gives Multiplicity 3, Labels1 Arg6;Lys4 and Labels2 Arg10;Lys8 for all 54 files in both deposited MaxQuant searches. These are human UniProt (MaxQuant 1.6.8.0, `txt_SBDAC_23_10_2019.zip`) and the TINPAT ORF database (MaxQuant 2.0.3.0, `TINATs_16_03_2022.zip`).

Where the map comes from:
- **Explicit:** the PRIDE protocol says "For the control condition the cells were cultured in medium SILAC conditions whereas for the inhibitors in heavy". The paper Methods name heavy = [13C6,15N4]Arg + Lys8 and intermediate = [13C6]Arg + D4-Lys, and the analysis used "normalized SILAC ratios for the H/M".
- **Data check** (`orientation.txt`): log2 normalised H/M of each replicate correlates with Supplementary Data 5 "DAC + SB939 vs DMSO" logFC at r = 0.85–0.93 (5,404–6,616 proteins). The DAC-induced cancer-testis proteins PAGE2 and DAZL are heavy-high in every replicate where they are quantified. Map and data agree.
- **Light channel:** it was searched but is not a study condition. It is encoded as one `pre-existing-proteome-pool` source per mix, with `characteristics[pooled sample]` = SN= of that mix's two cultures and treatment `not applicable`. This follows precedent PXD016211. Each mix gets its own light source name, so no name is reused across mixes.
- **Groups:** untreated (DMSO) 54 rows, decitabine and pracinostat 54 rows, light pool 54 rows. Biological replicate is numbered 1–7 within each group, one number per independent mix. The factor is `factor value[treatment]`.

## Key values
- **Cell line:** NCI-H1299, CVCL_0060, ATCC CRL-5803. Cellosaurus gives male, 43Y (so adult), Caucasian (so European ancestry), lung large cell carcinoma (MONDO:0003050), organism part lung, and sampling site lymph node (metastatic). Authentication was by Multiplexion. Culture medium is RPMI 1640 (NCIT:C178973).
- **Instrument:** Q Exactive HF (MS:1002523). The raw headers say "Q Exactive HF - Orbitrap MS". PRIDE's Orbitrap Fusion belongs to the blocked FS1 runs.
- **Acquisition:** DDA, HCD 26 NCE, Orbitrap MS2, MS1 350–1500 m/z. Fractionation is hpHRP (PRIDE:0000564).
- **Digestion:** Trypsin (MS:1001251) plus a second cleavage column for Lys-C (MS:1001309). The paper says "Trypsin/LysC ... 50:1". The MaxQuant search rule was Trypsin/P.
- **Reduction / alkylation:** TCEP / chloroacetamide, from the on-bead step. 55 mM iodoacetamide was also used before the click reaction. This is noted in the manifest, because the column takes one value.
- **Modifications** (summary.txt, both searches):
  - Fixed: Carbamidomethyl C.
  - Variable: Oxidation M, Acetyl Protein N-term, Deamidated N,Q.
  - Multi modifications: empty.
  - SILAC labels (MT=Variable): UNIMOD:188 R, 481 K, 267 R, 259 K.
  - AHA was not searched.
- **Tolerances:**
  - Fragment: 20 ppm (parameters.txt FTMS).
  - Precursor: 4.5 ppm. The paper says MaxQuant defaults were used. The largest calibrated mass error in TINPAT evidence.txt is 4.498 ppm, with 0 of 15,604 rows above 4.5.
- **Custom columns:** `characteristics[treatment protocol]` (dose, duration, vehicle) and `characteristics[nascent protein labeling]` (AHA pulse; precedents PXD025169, PXD062091).
- **Post-build edits** (scripted in `postbuild.py`, justified in validation.txt):

## Blocked / excluded runs (see BLOCKED.md)
- **48 FS1 Orbitrap Fusion "STABLE" runs** (SB939 A/B, DAC A/B, SB_DAC A/B; MaxQuant 1.6.8.0, Multiplicity 2, light vs Arg10/Lys8). No source states which label carried which treatment, or what the A and B series are. The data anti-correlate with Supplementary Data 5 (r −0.34 to −0.54), which suggests light = treated. That is inference only, so these runs are blocked.
- **9 unfractionated QE-HF runs** (TINPAT experiments SBDAC_NF_R1–R9: Translatome_1/2, SBDAC_R2_Unfractionated, 4h_R1/R2, C3T3_2mio_4h_Unfrac, CT_08_4h_Unfrac, _Unfrac_2, _Unfrac_3). The paper does not mention them, and nothing says whether they are aliquots of mixes R1–R7 or separate cultures.
