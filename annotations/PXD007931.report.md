## Study
Hu HF, Xu WW, Wang Y, Zheng CC, Zhang WX, Li B, He QY. "Comparative proteomics analysis identifies Cdc42-Cdc42BPA signaling as prognostic biomarker and therapeutic target for colon cancer invasion." *J Proteome Res* 2018;17(1):265-275, doi:10.1021/acs.jproteome.7b00550, PMID 29072916.

- The paper is closed access. Only the abstract and the PRIDE protocols could be read.
- Design: the highly invasive subline HCT116-I8 was selected from HCT116 by eight rounds of Matrigel invasion. It was SILAC-compared with the parental line: heavy Arg10/Lys8 for HCT116-I8, light for HCT116.
- Three mixtures were made, with 16 fractions each, run on a SCIEX TripleTOF 5600+ and searched with MaxQuant 1.5.2.8.
- The abstract also names RKO-I8, but the deposit has no RKO runs.

## Key findings and decisions
- **Channel map.**
  - The PRIDE protocol says: "heavy-labeled HCT116-I8 and light-labeled HCT116 cells were mixed". Every file is named `H-HCT116-I8-n`.
  - The deposited data agree. CDC42BPA, the paper's headline protein (up in I8), has log2 H/L of +0.87, +1.05 and +1.25 in the three searches.
  - The searches correlate positively with each other (r 0.73–0.77), so no mixture is label-swapped.
  - One conflict is recorded: CDH1 is +0.5 in the data, but the abstract reports reduced E-cadherin by western blot.
- **Instrument.**
  - Every `.wiff` header reads "Component ID: TripleTOF 5600+, AB Sciex", and MaxQuant reports the mass analyzer as TOF.
  - The PRIDE instrument field (LTQ Orbitrap) and its MS paragraph (350–1500 m/z, 35K resolution, "middle vs light") are copied from another study. They were rejected.
  - Annotated as: dissociation = beam-type CID; MS2 analyzer = TOF; fragment tolerance = 40 ppm (MaxQuant "MS/MS tol. (TOF)"); precursor tolerance = `not available` (not recorded).
- **The zip labels and the search labels are swapped** for experiments 2 and 3. This was found from the acquisition names embedded in the `.wiff` files (`run_match.tsv`). Source names follow the zips, and the manifest records the mapping.
- **The 2ndexperiment-MS.zip files are out of fraction order.**
  - The 2017-09-06/08 series is missing fractions 2, 3, 6, 7, 9 and 13. Those were re-run on 2017-09-13/14 under the names I 8_2_n.
  - In 8 of the 16 files, the peptide content matches a different fraction than the file number. Assignment uses peptide-set Jaccard against the other two mixtures: best match ≈0.42–0.52, the file number's own value ≤0.18.
  - Fraction identifiers in this zip therefore follow content: deposited files 1, 2, 13, 4, 5, 3, 6, 8, 7, 10, 11, 12, 9, 15, 16, 14 are fractions 1–16.
  - In the other two zips, the fraction identifier is the file number.
- **Same basenames in all three zips.** Each zip uses the basenames `H-HCT116-I8-1..16.wiff` for different files (the CRCs differ).
  - The annotation is therefore split into one SDRF per zip (review B1). Within each file the data files are unique and map 1:1 to that zip's members.
  - Assay name = file stem. There is no post-build edit.
  - Biological replicate 1/2/3 = 1st/2nd/3rd zip, numbered within each phenotype group across the three files.
- **The six re-runs in the 2nd zip are ambiguous.** These are the 2017-09-13/14 runs named `I 8_2_{2,3,6,7,9,13}`. They carry the prefix of the 3rd-zip mixture.
  - Assigning them to exp2 follows the zip packaging and the MaxQuant grouping: they are in the same search as the `I 8_n` runs and fill its six acquisition-time gaps.
  - Their content is slightly closer to the `I 8_2` mixture (not decisive). This is recorded as a limitation.
- **Companion files.** `comment[associated data file]` is not in the resolved contract, so the companion files are noted in the manifest limitations rather than annotated.
  - The 1st and 2nd zips, and members 1–2 of the 3rd zip, use `.scan`; the other 3rd-zip members use `.wiff.scan`.
  - Readers expect `.wiff.scan`, so `.scan` files must be renamed after extraction.
- **Modifications** (from the MaxQuant `parameters.txt` and `summary.txt`, identical in all three searches):
  - fixed: Carbamidomethyl (C);
  - variable: Acetyl (Protein N-term), Oxidation (M), Gln->pyro-Glu;
  - SILAC labels: Arg10 (UNIMOD:267) and Lys8 (UNIMOD:259), `MT=Variable`;
  - `summary.txt` has no Multi modifications column.
- **Cleavage agent** = Trypsin, from the MaxQuant enzyme setting ("Specific"). `reconcile` flags it because the record never names a digestion enzyme. The finding is refuted with search-output evidence in `validation.txt`.
- **Cell line.** HCT116-I8 has no Cellosaurus entry. Both populations are annotated as HCT116 (EFO label), CVCL_0291 / "HCT 116", with Cellosaurus metadata: 48Y, male, adult, European ancestry, colon, colon carcinoma. The subline is carried in a custom `characteristics[phenotype]` column, which is also the factor value.
