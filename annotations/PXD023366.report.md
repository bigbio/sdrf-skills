## Why this dataset was annotatable

Supplementary Table S1 (`mmc2.xlsx`, sheet `Patient_info`, via the Europe PMC supplementaryFiles
endpoint) is a genuine **per-oocyte sample sheet**. Its `Sample ID` joins onto the filenames
exactly — `GV.4386.1` ↔ `GV-4386-1.raw` — **36/36, zero orphans in either direction**, verified
row by row including the cross-group donors.

This is precisely what `PXD043473` and `PXD073250` lack (see `annotations/BLOCKED.md`). Candidate
datasets were pre-screened for this property before investing: the alternatives (`PXD045614`,
`PXD014256`, `PXD062702`) all mix pooled (`20cell`, `5cell`) or dilution (`1ngHeLa`, `10ng`) runs
into the same deposition.

## Key decisions and evidence

**Instrument — the artifact contradicts PRIDE deliberately.** PRIDE's `instruments` field says
`Q Exactive HF` (`MS:1002523`). The paper's LC-MS/MS section says "a **Q Exactive HF-X** Hybrid
Quadrupole-Orbitrap Mass Spectrometer". Range-reading the deposited raw file headers over HTTP
returns `Q Exactive HF-X` in every file sampled across all three groups. Annotated as
`MS:1002877`. The submitter's form field is simply wrong, and `MS:1002523` passes validation
cleanly — caught by review.

**`MII` = the paper's `IVO`.** Forced by elimination, not guessed: three groups of 12 in both the
paper (GV/IVM/IVO) and Table S1 (GV/IVM/MII); GV=GV, IVM=IVM, so MII=IVO. No IVO-named files
exist.

**Cell type is a developmental claim, not a lookup.** GV oocytes are arrested in prophase I →
`primary oocyte` (CL:0000654, "has not completed meiosis I"). IVM oocytes were scored mature "by
the presence of the first polar body" — meiosis I complete — so IVM *and* IVO are both
`secondary oocyte` (CL:0000655).

**The factor lives in free text, of necessity.** The study's variable is the three-way group.
`characteristics[developmental stage]` is defined in TERMS.tsv as the stage of *the organism or
donor* (adult), not the oocyte — wrong column despite appearances. It also cannot separate IVM
from IVO, both being MII. `characteristics[treatment]` accepts only NCIT/EFO, and **no ontology
term for "in vitro maturation" exists in either** (fuzzy fallbacks returned `Ganirelix Acetate`
and `oligodendrocyte precursor cell` — rejected). `characteristics[phenotype]` permits free text
per TERMS.tsv and is used instead.

**Search settings** (all from Methods): precursor **6 ppm** — the *main/recalibrated* search, not
the <20 ppm first search; fragment 20 ppm; `Carbamidomethyl` fixed on C (UNIMOD:4 — there *is* an
IAA alkylation step here, unlike PXD052416); `Oxidation` variable on M (UNIMOD:35); `Acetyl`
variable on `PP=Protein N-term` (UNIMOD:1, not the UNIMOD:21 swap); Trypsin.

## Values deliberately left `not available`

| column | why |
|---|---|
| `characteristics[age]` | Table S1 has 10 columns — Sample ID, Group, Individual ID, protein count, E2, progesterone, stimulation regimen, trigger, follicle count, E2/fol. **No age column**, and no ages anywhere in the text. `allow_not_available=true`. |
| `characteristics[disease]` | No source states the donors' diagnosis. These are ART patients, but the paper never says why. The tempting fabrication was `female infertility` — reviewer confirmed the abstention was correct. |

`characteristics[sex] = female` is not an inference — oocyte donors are female by definition.

`characteristics[single cell isolation protocol] = manual picking` **is** an inference: the paper
never states the transfer method. The column is `required` with `allow_not_available=false`, so an
enum member was mandatory, and Methods describe purely manual handling (per-oocyte zona removal
with Tyrode's solution, three PBS washes) with no FACS/microfluidics/nanoPOTS. Reviewer confirmed
it is the only enum member not contradicted by evidence.

## Reviewer findings (4 minor, none blocking)

1. `characteristics[ancestry category]` absent — recommended; correctly *not* inferred from the
   authors' Nanjing affiliation.
2. `characteristics[developmental stage]` absent — optional; not stated for the donors.
3. `characteristics[treatment]` absent — Table S1 holds real per-sample stimulation regimen
   (GnRH agonist/antagonist) and trigger (hCG / GnRH agonist) data that varies across donors and
   could populate this optional column.
4. `comment[reduction reagent]` / `[alkylation reagent]` absent — DTT and IAA are explicit in
   Methods; optional columns exist.

**Design note (recorded, not a defect):** donor is heavily confounded with group in the IVO arm —
2 donors × 6 oocytes, versus 6 and 8 donors in GV/IVM. That is a property of the study; the
artifact records it faithfully.
