## Study
"Phospho serine and threonine analysis of normal and mutated granulocyte colony stimulating factor receptors" — Dwivedi P, Muench DE, Wagner M, Azam M, Grimes HL, Greis KD. *Sci Data* 2019;6:21, doi:10.1038/s41597-019-0015-8, PMC6480977. Mouse Ba/F3 pro-B cells were retrovirally transduced to express wild-type G-CSFR, the CNL-associated proximal T618I mutant, or the SCN/AML-associated truncated Q741x mutant. Each line was SILAC-labelled. Heavy cells were stimulated with G-CSF (40 ng/mL) for 12.5 or 90 min, and light cells were left unstimulated. Each light/heavy mixture was digested with trypsin and the pY1000 immunoaffinity flow-through was used. That flow-through was fractionated by high-pH RP (60 fractions concatenated to 12), and each pool was TiO2-enriched. Runs were on a TripleTOF 5600+ and searched with ProteinPilot 5.0. Design: 3 receptors × 2 times × 2 biological replicates = 12 mixtures.

Not a duplicate: PXD009662 (linked) is the phospho-tyrosine part of the same mixtures, with different runs and enrichment.

## Key decisions and evidence
- **SILAC label masses — conflict.** The wet-lab text in PRIDE and the paper says heavy medium was "arginine-13C6 / lysine-13C6". The paper's own search settings, all 12 `.group` files and the companion PXD009662 protocol ("C-13, N-15 Lys and Arg") say Lys+8/Arg+10. The search identified heavy +8/+10 peptides at nearly the same rate as light ones. A +6 label could not give that. The SDRF therefore uses Label:13C(6)15N(2) K (UNIMOD:259) and Label:13C(6)15N(4) R (UNIMOD:267), `MT=Variable`. This is the judgement most worth a reviewer's attention.
- **Instrument.** `TripleTOF 5600+` (MS:1002584), from the paper and PRIDE protocol. The `.wiff` device string reads "TripleTOF 5600", but a reference 5600+ `.wiff` (PXD001997) carries the same string. The raw header therefore does not contradict the protocol. The PRIDE instruments field (MS:1000932) is the generic dropdown. `reconcile` flagged the 5600 value in round 1 and agrees after the fix.
- **Modifications.** ProteinPilot Paragon is probabilistic rather than fixed/variable. The SDRF lists:
  - Carbamidomethyl C as Fixed (Iodoacetamide set, probability 0.994);
  - Phospho S/T/Y ("Phosphorylation emphasis");
  - the two SILAC labels;
  - Oxidation M and Deamidated N/Q, the most frequent biological mods in the confident matches.
- **Tolerances.** 0.05 Da (MS) and 0.1 Da (MS/MS) are the ProteinPilot TripleTOF 5600 instrument preset selected by `UI_INSTRUMENT`, with auto-calibration on.
- **Cell line.** BA/F3 (EFO:0022749), CVCL_0161, pro-B cell, bone marrow, strain C3H. Cellosaurus notes the line was originally thought to be BALB/c and is in fact C3H.
- **Disease.** `normal`. Ba/F3 is non-leukaemic. PRIDE's "acute leukemia" describes the study context, not the samples.
- **Fraction identifier.** One deterministic post-build edit (`fix_fractions.py`) sets it from the `.wiff` sample-name fraction number. It changed 12 rows; the justification is in validation.txt.

## Values left `not available` / `not applicable`
- **`not available`**: `sex`, `developmental stage` and `passage number`. Cellosaurus has no sex or age, and no passage is reported.
- **`not applicable`**: `sampling time` on light rows, because those cells were never stimulated.
