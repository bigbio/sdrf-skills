## Design
There is one three-state SILAC mixture of HuCCT-1 cells (Cellosaurus CVCL_0324, HuCC-T1). It was measured twice: as a total proteome in 24 fractions and as a pTyr-IAP enrichment in a single run.

| label | condition (factor value[treatment]) | source names | rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| SILAC light | untreated (vehicle control) | HuCCT1_control_proteome / _pTyr | 1-24, 73 |
| SILAC medium (Lys4/Arg6) | NTRC 0652-0, 1 uM, 24 h | HuCCT1_NTRC_proteome / _pTyr | 25-48, 74 |
| SILAC heavy (Lys8/Arg10) | doxycycline-induced LCK knockout (HuCCT-1-sgLCK, 72 h) | HuCCT1_sgLCK_proteome / _pTyr | 49-72, 75 |

**Channel map source:** the paper's Methods states it explicitly: "light (control), medium (2H4-Lys and 13C6-Arg) (NTRC-treated) or heavy (13C615N2-Lys and 13C615N4-Arg) (sgLCK)". MaxQuant Labels1 Arg6;Lys4 and Labels2 Arg10;Lys8 agree.

**Data check:** LCK-target pY sites that the paper reports as decreased go down in both M/L and H/L, which confirms that light is the control:
- LCK Y192: -4.14 / -1.42
- LCK Y394: -3.06 / -1.37
- PRKCD Y313: -1.31 / -1.31
- EGFR Y1172: -1.42 / -1.88

The pan-SFK site Y394 drops more in medium than in heavy, which fits medium = drug. The M/L vs H/L correlation across pY sites is r = 0.74.

Biological replicate is 1 for every row: there is one culture per condition.

## Search parameters (from the deposited summary.txt and parameters.txt, MaxQuant 1.6.12.0)
- **Both searches:**
  - Fixed: Carbamidomethyl (C)
  - Variable: Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (Protein N-term)
  - Labels: Arg6/Lys4 and Arg10/Lys8, encoded as variable
  - Fragment tolerance: 20 ppm
- **Global search:** also used Deamidation (NQ) as variable.
- **pTyr search:** also used Phospho (STY), and did not use deamidation.
- The two searches were therefore built separately with identical headers, using `not applicable` slots, and concatenated.
- Cleavage agent is recorded as Trypsin (MS:1001251), the enzyme used for digestion. The search rule was Trypsin/P.
- **Instrument:** Orbitrap Fusion Lumos, taken from the raw headers. Fragmentation was HCD at 28 NCE with an Orbitrap MS2.
