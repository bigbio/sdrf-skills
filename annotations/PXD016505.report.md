## Study
Kennedy SA et al. "Extensive rewiring of the EGFR network in colorectal cancer cells expressing transforming levels of KRASG13D." *Nat Commun* 2020;11:499 (PMC6981206).

Design:
- 95 FLAG-tagged baits were transfected into HCT116 (KRAS G13D/wt) and HKE3 (HCT116 with the mutant allele knocked out).
- Cells were grown in triple-SILAC DMEM and lysates were IP'd with anti-FLAG M2. The medium channel is an empty-vector IP.
- The design is label-swapped (FOR/REV). Analysis was on an LTQ Orbitrap Velos (in-solution digests) or a Q Exactive (on-bead digests), and the search used MaxQuant 1.3.0.5.

The AP-MS data are split over seven PRIDE projects. This deposit holds 26 baits.

**SILAC multiplexing was verified (rule 14).** Every deposited proteinGroups file has Ratio H/M, M/L and H/L columns, so the multiplicity is 3.

**Sources.** Two explicit sources give the map, and they agree:

**Data check.** In every checked experiment, the bait is enriched in H and L over M (the empty vector). Checked baits: CASP9, HDAC1, BRAF, MAP2K1, PEBP1, RAC1 and RAF1, with median log2 H/M or L/M of 1.6–4.4.

**Orientation check.** Where a bait is expressed differently in the two lines, the difference flips with the swap:
- PEBP1: FOR H/M 1.57 < L/M 3.14; REV H/M 4.32 > L/M 2.79.
- RAC1: FOR 2.06 < 4.35; REV 4.27 > 2.41.

In both cases the HKE-3 bait channel is light in FOR and heavy in REV, as stated. No conflict between sources.

## Sample / replicate model

**Pooled samples.** Each MS run is one pooled SILAC triplet. It contains three cultures, and each culture is one source:
- the HCT116 bait IP;
- the HKE-3 bait IP;
- the empty-vector IP.

**Re-injections.** Runs from the same physical pool are recognised from the raw headers (first 16 KB of every raw). They share a source name and carry `comment[technical replicate]` 1..n. Two runs belong to one pool when:
- they come from the same autosampler vial on the same instrument PC in the same batch. For example, LDY1603 MEK F-1/F-2 are both from vial GB1, F-3/F-4 from GB2 and F-5/F-6 from GB3. ALI1408 GRB2-F-1-1/-1-2 are both from RA6, which confirms the explicit names; or
- the header's original acquisition name is another deposited run. LDY1275 BGRL-F-12 was acquired as F-1.

**Result.** The 350 runs form 207 pools:
- 71 pools injected once;
- 131 injected twice;
- 3 pools of 3 runs (LDY1275 SH3BGRL F and R; LDY1098 HDAC1 F, vial BB4);
- 2 pools of 4 runs (LDY1502 RAC1, one vial each).

No vial is shared by two bait/direction groups.

**Biological replicate.** It counts the distinct pools within each factor-value group (bait × cell line, and empty vector × cell line):
- bait groups have 1..6–12 replicates;
- the empty-vector groups are numbered 1..103 (HKE-3) and 1..104 (HCT116).

## Search coverage
No mqpar/summary is deposited. For every bait and direction, the number of raws equals the number of MaxQuant experiments in its proteinGroups file. There is one exception, **HDAC1 FOR: 7 raws against 6 experiments.** Summed experiment intensities place HDAC1FOR2 at the Q Exactive Plus scale (3.25e11, ALI1452) and the other five at Velos scale, so 5 Velos experiments cover 6 Velos raws. One Velos HDAC1 FOR run is unaccounted for (unsearched, or merged), and which one cannot be determined. All 7 runs are annotated with the search parameters. `LDY1098-S23-A-HDAC-F-2-No.raw` (round 1 excluded it) is the first acquisition of vial BB4 slot S23 and is now technical replicate 2 of `HDAC1_LDY1098_FOR_pool1`.

Some baits have more experiments than this deposit has raws: ELK1/ELK4/PXN FOR 8 vs 7, and RAF1 10 vs 7 per direction. The excess is **unexplained**. The round-1 claim that these raws are in PXD016461–5 was not verified; the reviewer found no ELK1/ELK4/PXN raws there and only 6+6 CRAF raws in PXD016463.

## Technical values
- **Instruments, from the raw headers.** LTQ Orbitrap Velos: 972 rows, 324 runs, CID 35 NCE, ion-trap MS2, 0.5 Da. Q Exactive Plus: 78 rows, 26 runs, HCD, Orbitrap MS2, 0.5 Da (Supp. Methods 12 gives one search-wide fragment tolerance; round 1 had `not available`). The paper and PRIDE say "Q-Exactive"; the raw header wins.
- **Per-instrument builds.** The two instruments were built separately with identical headers and concatenated by `scripts/concat.sh`. That is the only post-build step.
- **Modifications.**
  - Carbamidomethyl (C) is fixed.
  - Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (Protein N-term) are variable, per the Methods.
  - Deamidated (N,Q) is variable too. The Methods omit it, but all deposited proteinGroups files have `Deamidation (NQ) site IDs`.
  - SILAC labels are Lys4 = UNIMOD:481, Arg6 = 188, Lys8 = 259 and Arg10 = 267, all `MT=Variable`.
- **Search and digest settings.** Precursor tolerance 6 ppm (main search). Trypsin; DTT and iodoacetamide.

## Open items (`not available` and uncertainties)
- QE Plus rows have collision energy `not available` (HCD NCE not stated). Their fragment tolerance of 0.5 Da follows the single Methods value; MaxQuant's FTMS setting may have differed (no mqpar).
- One HDAC1 FOR Velos run is unmatched to a search experiment (see above).
- On the 17 RAC1 raws shared with PXD016512, the empty-vector sources have biological replicates 79–84 here and 41–46 in PXD016512. Empty-vector pools are numbered across all baits of each file, so the numbers are not comparable across files. All other columns are identical.
- `cell type`, `passage number` and `sample storage temperature` are `not available`.
- Pools from different batches are treated as independent cultures. The paper describes 3 biological × 2 technical per direction, but many baits have 5–10 runs per direction.
- Run-name suffixes `_U` (ALI0802 BRAF) and `-BIS` (LDY0905 RAC1 REV) are unexplained. Those runs are kept as separate pools.
- Each pool is given its own empty-vector culture. Whether one EV IP served several bait pools of a batch is not stated.
