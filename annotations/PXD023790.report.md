## SILAC part: design and channel map
**Channel map (rule 6)**
- **Stated in the sources.**
  - Light = unpulsed light-cultured reference cells.
  - Medium = proteins that existed before the pulse in the pulsed cells.
  - Heavy = proteins newly synthesised during the pulse.
  - Sources: the paper ("ratios of H/L and M/L reflect" synthesis and persistence) and the supplement ("Pulsed cells were mixed with the same number of 'light' labeled cells").
- **Checked against the data.** In every H run, HAX1 is 0 in the L, M and H channels. In every C run, CLPB is 0 in M and H. So the genotype letters are confirmed, and the light reference cells carry the same knockout as the pulsed cells. This fits the paper's WT-vs-HAX1-/- volcano plots based on light LFQ (n = 18 each).
- **Rows.** Each run has 3 rows. Medium and heavy come from one lysate but are separate sources (precedent PXD028934).

**Factors**
- `genotype`: wild type / HAX1 knockout / CLPB knockout.
- `cellular component`: cell / mitochondrion.
- `sampling time`: 0 / 6 / 24 hour, the pulse length of the mix.

**Biological replicate.** Calculated as (round − 1) × 3 + clone, so 1–6 per group. This follows the supplement: "three independent cell clones per genotype in two … biological replicates (two rounds of SILAC labeling)".

**Rows per group** (light rows = runs):

### Judgement calls (attack these)
1. **`2CELLS_t6_W1/W2/W3` are encoded as CLPB knockout.**
   - The submitter sheet says "CLPB ko, not wt".
   - CLPB is 0 in all channels of these runs.
   - The clone number is not documented. The source names say `clone-unconfirmed-fileWn`, and the biological replicates 4/5/6 follow file order. The clone signature supports W1 → C1, supports W3 → C3 only weakly, and does not resolve W2.
   - As a result, cells / WT / 6 h has round-1 runs only.
2. **Experiment `2CELLS_t0_H` (no clone number) is assigned to clone 3** (`clone3-inferred-fileH`, biological replicate 6).
   - HAX1 = 0, so the knockout is confirmed.
   - Its light-channel clone signature is the reciprocal best match of 2CELLS_t24_H3 and 2CELLS_t6_H3.
   - Round 2 cells t0 otherwise has no H3, and `2X_CELLS_t0_H1` already supplies H1.
3. **The `2X_` runs are assigned to round 2** even though their files are dated 20180312.
   - The experiment prefix says round 2.
   - In the M/L-ratio batch check, they correlate with round-2 runs at +0.43 to +0.60 and with round-1 runs at −0.17 to −0.56.
   - In the same check, 107/108 experiments match their prefix.
4. **Repeat runs are technical replicates of the same culture.**
   - Affected runs: `2X_` + `Repeat_` for WT clones 2 and 3 (cells 0 h), and `t24_H2` + `Repeat_t24_H2`.
   - Reason: only two SILAC rounds exist, and these runs are round 2. Repeat_t24_H2 and t24_H2 are the most correlated pair (M/L r 0.75, H/L r 0.80).
5. **Blocked run: 2MITO_t6_H1.**
   - HAX1 has 8 MS/MS peptides at WT-level intensity in all 3 channels.
   - Clone H1 is HAX1-null in its other 17 runs.
   - Low HAX1 traces in 1MITO_t6_H2 (2 peptides, ~5 % of WT) and 1MITO_t24_H2 (1 peptide) were treated as noise, and those runs were kept.
6. **Sampling time on the light rows.** The light rows carry the time point of the mix. They were mixed at that time point but never pulsed.
7. **Precursor tolerance is `not available`.**
   - `parameters.txt` does not record it.
   - The paper's sentence "7 ppm precursor / 10 ppm fragment" is contradicted by the deposited FTMS MS/MS tolerance of 20 ppm, so it was not used.
   - The fragment tolerance is 20 ppm, taken from `parameters.txt`.
