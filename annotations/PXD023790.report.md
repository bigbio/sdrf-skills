## Study

- **Dataset.** HAX1 deficiency causes Kostmann severe congenital neutropenia. The dataset has two MS experiments:
  1. **Pulse-SILAC (triple SILAC), Fig 3 / Supp Fig 3.**
     - Cells: PLB-985 WT, HAX1-/- and CLPB-/- CRISPR clones, 3 clones per genotype × 2 SILAC rounds.
     - Labelling: cells were labelled medium (Arg6/Lys4) and then pulsed with heavy (Arg10/Lys8) for 0, 6 or 24 h.

     - Acquisition: Q Exactive HF-X, DDA.

  2. **HSP27 FLAG-IP interactome, Fig 6F.**
     - Cells: HEK293T expressing HSP27-FLAG vs control.
     - Design: 2 experiments × 3 pulldowns.
     - Quantification: label-free (MaxQuant LFQ, Multiplicity 1).

## SILAC part: design and channel map
**Channel map (rule 6)**
- **Stated in the sources.**
  - Light = unpulsed light-cultured reference cells.
  - Medium = proteins that existed before the pulse in the pulsed cells.
  - Heavy = proteins newly synthesised during the pulse.
  - Sources: the paper ("ratios of H/L and M/L reflect" synthesis and persistence) and the supplement ("Pulsed cells were mixed with the same number of 'light' labeled cells").
- **Checked against the data.** In every H run, HAX1 is 0 in the L, M and H channels. In every C run, CLPB is 0 in M and H. So the genotype letters are confirmed, and the light reference cells carry the same knockout as the pulsed cells. This fits the paper's WT-vs-HAX1-/- volcano plots based on light LFQ (n = 18 each).
- **Rows.** Each run has 3 rows. Medium and heavy come from one lysate but are separate sources (precedent PXD028934).

**Factors**
- `genotype`: wild type / HAX1 knockout / CLPB knockout.
- `cellular component`: cell / mitochondrion.
- `sampling time`: 0 / 6 / 24 hour, the pulse length of the mix.

**Biological replicate.** Calculated as (round − 1) × 3 + clone, so 1–6 per group. This follows the supplement: "three independent cell clones per genotype in two … biological replicates (two rounds of SILAC labeling)".

**Rows per group** (light rows = runs):

### Judgement calls (attack these)
1. **`2CELLS_t6_W1/W2/W3` are encoded as CLPB knockout.**
   - The submitter sheet says "CLPB ko, not wt".
   - CLPB is 0 in all channels of these runs.
   - The clone number is not documented, so the source names say `clone-unconfirmed-fileWn`. The biological replicates 4/5/6 follow file order.
   - Two things support Wn → Cn: in acquisition order these files follow t6_H1-3 (the C slot), and their median log2 H/M (−2.16/−2.45/−2.24) matches the same-mix 2MITO_t6_C1/C2/C3 runs (−2.14/−2.43/−2.28).
   - As a result, cells / WT / 6 h has round-1 runs only.
2. **Experiment `2CELLS_t0_H` (no clone number) is assigned to clone 3** (`clone3-inferred-fileH`, biological replicate 6).
   - HAX1 = 0, so the knockout is confirmed.
   - Evidence is the acquisition order from the 7z member times (`acq_order_round2_cells.txt`): round-2 cells t0 was acquired W2, W3, H1 (the 2X_ files, 2018-06-13 10:02–14:04), then H2 (16:06), H (18:07), C1, C2, C3. That puts H in the H3 slot of the W1-3/H1-3/C1-3 order used in every block.
   - The round-1 "clone signature" argument is withdrawn: it groups runs by time point, and its anchor t24_H3 is now blocked.
3. **The `2X_` runs are assigned to round 2** even though their files are dated 20180312.
   - The experiment prefix says round 2.
   - Their 7z member times are 2018-06-13, inside the round-2 QX0 sequence. Round-1 QX8 runs date from 2018-03-13 to 03-18, so the 20180312 prefix is a reused name.
   - In the M/L-ratio batch check, they correlate with round-2 runs at +0.43 to +0.60 and with round-1 runs at −0.17 to −0.56.
   - In the same check, 107/108 experiments match their prefix.
4. **Two repeat runs are technical replicates of the same culture.**
   - `2X_t0_W3` + `Repeat_t0_W3`: median log2 H/M −2.88 / −2.77.
   - `t24_H2` + `Repeat_t24_H2`: H/M −0.34 / −0.20; M/L r 0.75, H/L r 0.80.
   - Reason: only two SILAC rounds exist, and these runs are round 2.
5. **Six whole-cell runs blocked: heavy incorporation contradicts the time point** (review round 1 blocker, verified independently in `hm_check.txt`).
   - Background: cells and mitochondria come from the same heavy+light mix at each time point, so each whole-cell run must match its same-mix mitochondria run.
   - Exactly 6 of the 54 whole-cell runs differ from their mitochondria run by more than 1 log2 unit:
     - 2CELLS_t24_C1/C2/C3/H3: −2.78/−2.88/−2.52/−2.69 (t0 level), against −0.29/−0.19/−0.74/−0.28 for their mitochondria runs.
     - 2CELLS_Repeat_t0_W1/W2: −0.58/−0.67 (t24 level), against −2.39/−2.45.
   - All 18 rows of these runs were removed via the BLOCKED list in `make_tables.py`, followed by a rebuild.
6. **Blocked run: 2MITO_t6_H1.**
   - HAX1 has 8 MS/MS peptides at WT-level intensity in all 3 channels.
   - Clone H1 is HAX1-null in its other 17 runs.
   - Low HAX1 traces in 1MITO_t6_H2 (2 peptides, ~5 % of WT) and 1MITO_t24_H2 (1 peptide) were treated as noise, and those runs were kept.
   - Caveat to "light = same clone": CLPB shows one light-only peptide (L 1.3–3.4e9, M = H = 0) in 1MITO_t6_C3, 1MITO_t24_C3 and 2MITO_t24_C3.
7. **Sampling time on the light rows.** The light rows carry the time point of the mix. They were mixed at that time point but never pulsed.
8. **Precursor tolerance is `not available`.**
   - `parameters.txt` does not record it.
   - The paper's sentence "7 ppm precursor / 10 ppm fragment" is contradicted by the deposited FTMS MS/MS tolerance of 20 ppm, so it was not used.
   - The fragment tolerance is 20 ppm, taken from `parameters.txt`.
