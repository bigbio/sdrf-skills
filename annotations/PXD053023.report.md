## Study

Method-development + proof-of-principle single-cell proteomics. The authors introduce
**electrophoresis-correlative ion mobility (Eco-IMS / "Eco-MS")**: a scheduled, narrowed ion-mobility
framing of **ddaPASEF** on a **timsTOF PRO**, hyphenated to a custom **capillary-electrophoresis (CE)-ESI**
platform. Two experimental systems:

1. **HeLa proteome digest standard** (Pierce, Thermo Fisher, part no. 88329) — the reagent used to
   *develop–test–optimize–validate* the method, measured at **500 pg (≈2 cells)** and **200 pg (≈1 cell)**
   loading, each in several technical replicates.
2. **Single *Xenopus laevis* blastomeres** — **N = 9** cells isolated from the animal cap of stage-8
   (blastula) embryos; the biological proof-of-principle.

Every sample was analyzed **twice**: once under **classical ddaPASEF** (control) and once under
**Eco-driven ddaPASEF**. Search: MSFragger/FragPipe 20.0, static Cys carbamidomethylation, variable Met
oxidation, 20 ppm precursor/fragment, trypsin, MBR off (HeLa) / on (Xenopus).

## Key decisions and evidence

**Instrument = timsTOF Pro (MS:1003005).** The Methods state "timsTOF PRO" four times; PRIDE's
`instruments` gives the generic family label "timsTOF" (less specific, not contradictory). I did **not**
range-read `analysis.tdf` for the model, because the paper is explicit and PRIDE agrees at the family
level; a reviewer wanting belt-and-braces can pull `GlobalMetadata.InstrumentName` from any `.d`.

**Modifications — the brief's carbamidomethyl warning applies and is flagged.** The search used
**static Cys carbamidomethylation + variable Met oxidation** — stated verbatim in Methods ("static
modification, cysteine carbamidomethylation; dynamic modifications, methionine oxidation") and echoed by
PRIDE `modifications` ("iodoacetamide derivatized residue", "monohydroxylated residue"). So
`comment[modification parameters]` carries `Carbamidomethyl` (UNIMOD:4, Fixed) + `Oxidation` (UNIMOD:35,
Variable) on **all 35 rows**, because that column documents the *search*. **However**, the single-cell
sample prep describes **no reduction and no alkylation step** (lysis in DDM/DMSO/AmBic → heat
denaturation → trypsin; no DTT/IAA). This is exactly the SCP pattern the brief warns about: a fixed
carbamidomethyl searched with no alkylation chemistry described. I did **not** invent a reagent —
`comment[reduction reagent]` and `comment[alkylation reagent]` are `not available` on every row (Thermo
reduced/alkylated the HeLa digest upstream but never names the reagents; the Xenopus arm names none).
**This is the #1 item for reviewer scrutiny:** keep `Carbamidomethyl Fixed` (faithful to the stated
search) vs. drop it (faithful to the absent wet-lab step). I chose to mirror the search and expose the
tension here rather than silently pick a side.

**HeLa runs are a dilution standard, not single cells** — the brief's "single-cell-equivalent is a mass,
not a count" rule, and the PXD022791 precedent. Although the abstract calls 200 pg a
"single-HeLa-cell-equivalent," it is a purchased, pre-digested peptide standard diluted by mass:
- `sample type` = `standard` (PRIDE:0000908)
- `cells per well` = `not applicable` (not `1`, not `2`/`3` — no cell was ever counted)
- `single cell isolation protocol` = `not applicable`, `cell identifier` = `not applicable`
- HeLa provenance retained: organism *Homo sapiens*, organism part `uterine cervix` (UBERON:0000002),
  cell type `HeLa cell` (CLO:0003684), disease `cervical adenocarcinoma` (MONDO:0005153),
  `material type` = `cell line`, `sex` = `female`.

**Xenopus blastomeres ARE whole single cells — `manual picking`, `cells per well` = 1.** This is the
key divergence from the sibling PXD031955, and it turns on what the capillary samples. In PXD031955 the
capillary aspirated intracellular content **in situ** from a blastomere still in the embryo (no cell
isolated → `not applicable`). **Here the whole cell is isolated first:** the animal-cap tissue is
dissociated to a cell suspension, cells are rinsed and **"individually pipetted into an array"** on a
microplate, then each is lysed **in its own droplet** and digested. The whole blastomere is captured.
The paper's "<4% of the total proteome analyzed" refers to loading only ~2 ng (15 nl) of the full
single-cell **digest** onto CE — a subsampling of the *digest*, not partial-cell aspiration. So
`single cell isolation protocol` = `manual picking` (whole-cell micromanipulation; nearest fixed-enum
member — the enum has no "capillary microsampling") and `cells per well` = `1` is correct, not an
over-claim. `sample type` = `single cell` (PRIDE:0000897), `cell identifier` = `cell_1…cell_9` (the
deposited filename token; the paper's hidden per-cell IDs were not disclosed).

**`BS##` is a per-day run counter, not a cell ID** — same trap as `SC##` in PXD031955. E.g. cell_1's two
runs are `BS02` and `BS03` on 2023-07-13; `BS02`/`BS03`/`BS04` recur across different days and cells.
Cell identity comes from the explicit `cell_N` token, never from `BS##`.

**Developmental stage.** *Xenopus* = `blastula stage` (UBERON:0000108) — Nieuwkoop-Faber stage 8 is the
blastula, stated. HeLa = `adult` (EFO:0001272), donor-derived provenance consistent with recording
`sex`/`disease`; a reviewer preferring `not applicable` for a reagent has a defensible alternative
(`allow_not_applicable` is true). (UBERON in an EFO-typed column passes clean, as in PXD031955.)

**Organism part for the blastomere = `animal hemisphere` (UBERON:0012284).** The animal cap is the roof
of the animal hemisphere of the blastula; this is the most specific correct UBERON region. No
Xenopus-specific "animal cap" term exists in UBERON.

**Dissociation = CID (MS:1000133).** Stated: "fragmentation via CID under control by ddaPASEF" (Fig. 1).
`ms2 mass analyzer` = `time-of-flight` (MS:1000084, the timsTOF detector). `ms min/max mz` = 300/1500
(global "MS1 scan range: m/z 300−1500", stated). Precursor/fragment tolerance = 20 ppm (stated).
`collision energy` omitted (not stated numerically).

## Factor value

`factor value[cell type]` — `blastoderm cell` (CL:0000353) for the 18 Xenopus rows vs `HeLa cell`
(CLO:0003684) for the 17 standard rows. Like PXD031955, this is a **method/proof-of-principle study with
no biological treatment factor**: the real manipulated axes are the acquisition scheme (classical vs
Eco) and the HeLa loading amount (500 vs 200 pg), and **neither has an SDRF column** (no acquisition-mode
characteristic; the `characteristics[mass]` licensing gap noted in the PXD031955 report applies again —
the amounts survive only in `source name` / `comment[data file]`). `cell type` is the closest honest
mirror of a real characteristics column and separates the two sub-studies.

- PRIDE `modifications` = "monohydroxylated residue" + "iodoacetamide derivatized residue" → these ARE

- **Stated:** 35-run structure (Suppl. S1–S6 + zip directories), instrument, tolerances, modifications

- **Inferred:** that classical+Eco runs of one `cell_N` share a biological source (same digest, two

  `animal hemisphere` as the animal-cap region; HeLa `adult` developmental stage.

## Judgment calls I want a reviewer to attack hardest

1. **`Carbamidomethyl;MT=Fixed` on rows whose prep describes no alkylation** (see modifications note).
2. **Whole-cell `manual picking` + `cells per well=1` for the blastomeres**, vs the PXD031955 sibling's
   `not applicable` — I argue the physical isolation step (dissociate → pipette individually → lyse in
   droplet) makes these genuine whole-cell isolations, unlike in-situ aspiration.
3. **HeLa as `standard` with `cells per well=not applicable`** despite "single-cell-equivalent" framing.
4. **One `vertebrates` template spanning human + frog**, and **not declaring `cell-lines`** for HeLa.
5. **`factor value[cell type]`** as a proxy in a study with no biological factor.
