## Encoding
- **One source per channel culture.** Source names follow `<SampleID>_<cell line>_<bait>_<treatment>_<L|M|H>`.
- **Factor values:** `bait` (a custom column), `cell line` and `treatment`.
- **Treatment.** DMSO vehicle channels are written as `untreated`, with `characteristics[compound]` = Dimethyl Sulfoxide (rule 4; precedent PXD004181). NGI-1 has no ontology term, so it is free text. Acalabrutinib uses NCIT:C113442.
- **EV / isotype channels:** `sample type` = negative control. `treatment` = `not available`, because File_assignment names no drug for them.
- **Biological replicate** is numbered within each (cell line, bait, treatment) group. The HBL-1 EV controls of 2310, 2311 and 539 are 1, 2 and 3. Every other group has a single culture, numbered 1.
- **pY R1/R2** are written as technical replicates 1 and 2 of one source per channel. File_assignment gives one Sample ID per experiment.
- **Fraction identifier** is the gel slice number parsed from the file name. This is a scripted post-build edit in `build_all.py`. It changes only experiment 505, where slice 6 is missing (45 cells: slices 7–21 × 3 channels).
- **Four builds are concatenated**, with headers asserted identical. They differ in instrument and in the search:
  - A: 505, 1580, 1637 (Q Exactive HF, Biotin (K))
  - B: 539 (Q Exactive HF)
  - C: 2310, 2311 (Q Exactive)
  - D: pY (Orbitrap Fusion, Phospho (STY))
- **Modifications**, from summary.txt and mqpar.xml:
  - fixed Carbamidomethyl (C)
  - variable Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (Protein N-term)
  - slot 4: Biotin (K) for group A, `not applicable` for groups B and C, Phospho (S,T,Y) for group D
  - SILAC labels Arg6 (UNIMOD:188), Lys4 (UNIMOD:481), Arg10 (UNIMOD:267) and Lys8 (UNIMOD:259), all `MT=Variable`
  - Multi modifications are empty in every search
- **Tolerances:** precursor 4.5 ppm (main search). Fragment 20 ppm on the Orbitrap MS2 of the Q Exactive runs, 0.5 Da on the ion-trap MS2 of the Fusion runs.
- **Cleavage agent:** trypsin (MS:1001251). The search rule was Trypsin/P.
- **Raw-header values:** HCD at NCE 28 (HF), 30 (QE) and 35 (Fusion), and DDA acquisition.
