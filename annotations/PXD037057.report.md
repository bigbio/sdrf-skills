## Study
Nandan D *et al.* "Comprehensive proteomic analysis of autophagosomes derived from Leishmania-infected macrophages." *PLoS One* 2023;18(4):e0284026, PMC10081754.

The experiment uses PMA-differentiated THP-1 cells (dTHP-1) in a 3-plex SILAC design:
- each SILAC state got one autophagy inducer;
- the cells were pooled;
- autophagosomes were purified on an iodixanol gradient, and fraction 4 (16 % iodixanol) was used;
- that fraction was run on SDS-PAGE and the lane cut into 5 slices for in-gel trypsin digestion;
- the peptides were measured on a Bruker impact II Q-TOF;
- there were 3 biological replicates;
- the search was MaxQuant 1.6.17.0 against human plus the *L. donovani* BPK282A1 proteome.

The SILAC check (rule 14) passes: `summary.txt` reports Multiplicity 3, Labels1 `Arg6;Lys4` and Labels2 `Arg10;Lys8`. Also checked:
- **Duplicates:** this is the only deposit for the paper.
- **Existing annotation:** none in sdrf-annotated-datasets.

  - I left out developmental stage (the donor is not an adult).

- **Culture medium:** RPMI-1640 (NCIT:C178973).
- **Instrument and acquisition:** instrument `impact II` (MS:1002666), DDA, CID, ms2 analyzer TOF, fractionation `SDS PAGE` (PRIDE:0000568).

  - Carbamidomethyl C, fixed;
