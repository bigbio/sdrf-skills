## Partial scope: stated and justified
- **Stated:** in three places.
  - The report status line: "PARTIAL … 96 of the 132".
  - The first manifest limitation.
  - The `BLOCKED.md` section "PXD003316 … MitoC arm only (partial block)".
- **Matches the deposit:** the 36 unannotated raws are exactly `20140715_…_MitoC_Fwd_C1–C18` and `_MitoC_Rev_D1–D18`, which are MaxQuant experiments C_MitoC_Fwd and D_MitoC_Rev in `txtstiffness.zip`.
- **Justified:** I confirmed independently that no source documents the MitoC arm.
  - The strings "mito"/"MitoC" do not occur in the paper (efetch PMC5556271), its Appendix or the PRIDE protocols.
  - The Europe PMC search for "PXD003316" returns only Reid et al. 2017. "Zanivan AND mitomycin AND stiffness" returns 0 hits.
  - The MitoC ratios are weak and have no stated label map: CYR61 H/L is 1.36 (Fwd) and 1.00 (Rev). Inferring a channel→condition map from them would be guessing. Excluding the arm is correct.

## Findings

1. **[important] Missing `characteristics[sample type]`. The 66 heavy SILAC-HUVEC standard rows are not marked as references.**
   - **Rows affected:** 74, 76, 78, 80, 82, 84, 91–96, 103–108, 115–120, 127–132, 139–144, 151–156, 163–168, 175–180 and 187–192.
   - **Evidence:**
     - The request says: "Spiked-in SILAC standards are a reference, not study samples."
     - In this SDRF, only the free-text source name and the `not applicable` factor values set the standards apart. convert-openms merges both experiments' standards into one condition (60 files).
     - The producer's reason for leaving the column out is wrong. Report decision 2 says "OLS lists no 'reference' child under PRIDE:0000895", but that check covered direct children only. In OLS4, **PRIDE:0000899 "reference"** (not obsolete) is a child of PRIDE:0000898 "multiplex sample", which sits under PRIDE:0000895. Its definition: "A reference sample used for normalization across multiplexed sets …".
     - TERMS.tsv and `sample-metadata.yaml` list `reference` as an allowed value; the validator's parent_term is PRIDE:0000895.
     - The passed campaign SDRFs PXD009868, PXD030422 and PXD012113 all use light = `study sample` and heavy = `reference`.
     - I tested a scratch copy with the column added: it validates with exit 0 and the check is CLEAN.
   - **Fix:**
     - Rebuild with `characteristics[sample type]`: `reference` on the 66 "SILAC-HUVEC heavy standard …" rows and `study sample` on the other 126 rows (both stiffness channels and every spike-in light channel).
     - Remove the incorrect OLS statement from the report and manifest, and add a manifest claim that cites PRIDE:0000899.
     - Re-run validation, then request a fresh review.
2. **[minor] `characteristics[biological replicate]` on the standards is `1`; the precedents use `pooled`.**
   - **Evidence:** the template says "Use pooled for pooled reference samples only". Here the standard is a single heavy HUVEC culture, so `1` can be defended.
   - **Fix:** optional. Either keep `1` and give the reason in the manifest, or switch to `pooled` to match the campaign.
3. **[minor] The siCCN1 standard (2014) and the Matrigel standard (2013) share one OpenMS condition.**
   - **Evidence:** they were probably separate preparations. Source names already tell them apart, and once sample type = reference is set the shared condition does no harm.
   - **Fix:** none required. Optionally note in the manifest that the condition is shared by design.
