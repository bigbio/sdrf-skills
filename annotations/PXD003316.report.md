## Key decisions with evidence
1. **Stiffness channel map.**
   - **What the paper states:** cells were cultured on 400 Pa or 22,000 Pa PAGs for 24 h, in "forward and reverse experiments, where labeling conditions were swapped".
   - **Ratio direction:** Dataset EV1 reports "22/0.4 KPa Fwd/Rev (log2)". The Appendix says the H/L ratio "was inverted in … reverse SILAC labeling experiments". So in Fwd the heavy channel is 22 kPa, and in Rev the light channel is 22 kPa.
   - **Which runs these are:** I joined Dataset EV1 to the stiffness proteinGroups.txt on MaxQuant id. EV1 "Intensity L/H … Fwd" equals `Intensity L/H A_Prolif_Fwd` exactly for 5,804/5,801 protein groups. "Rev" equals `B_Prolif_Rev` for 5,791/5,790. The MitoC experiments match 3 or fewer. So the paper's stiffness proteome is the Prolif runs.
   - **Data check:** CYR61/CCN1, which the paper reports as "highly induced by stiffness", has normalized H/L 2.33 in Fwd and 0.58 in Rev. CDH2 has 2.12 and 0.55.
2. **Spike-in arms.**
   - **What the paper states:** "a heavy SILAC-HUVEC standard was mixed at a 1:1 ratio with each of the lysates from the non-labeled samples". So light = sample and heavy = standard.
   - **Data check:** CYR61 H/L is 5.4/7.2/8.8 in siCCN1 1–3 and 1.1/1.1/1.8 in siCTL 1–3, which is what the knockdown predicts.
   - **Encoding:** each heavy aliquot is its own source named "SILAC-HUVEC heavy standard (<experiment>), aliquot spiked into <sample>", as in the campaign's super-SILAC precedents. Its biological replicate is 1 (one standard per experiment). Growth condition, genetic modification and sampling time are `not available`, because the standard's culture is not described. Its factor values are `not applicable`.
   - **Sample type:** `characteristics[sample type]` = `reference` (PRIDE:0000899) on the 60 heavy-standard rows and `study sample` (PRIDE:0001013) on the other 132 rows (both stiffness channels and every spike-in light channel). PRIDE:0000899 is a grandchild of PRIDE:0000895 via PRIDE:0000898 "multiplex sample" (verified live in OLS4, not obsolete). This follows the campaign precedents PXD009868, PXD030422 and PXD012113.
   - **Biological replicate on the standards is kept at `1`, not `pooled`.** The paper describes a single heavy-labelled HUVEC standard per experiment, aliquoted into every mixture. It is not a mix of distinct samples or cell lines, and the template reserves `pooled` for pooled reference samples. The donor pooling of all HUVECs is already recorded in `characteristics[pooled sample]` = `pooled`.
3. **The siCCN1 arm was on plastic, not Matrigel.** PRIDE's protocol reads "HUVECs silenced for CCN1 and cultured on Matrigel". The paper ("silenced for CCN1 and those cultured on Matrigel") and the Dataset EV3 legend ("HUVECs cultured on plastic dish and silenced or not for CCN1") show these are two separate experiments. I followed the paper. The siRNA pools are named in the Appendix: Qiagen CCN1 pool SI02626421/SI02626428/SI03028655/SI03053477, and the Dharmacon non-targeting pool D-001810-10-05.
4. **Technical values differ by arm, so I ran three builds and concatenated them (rules 5/17).**
   - **Raw headers:** I range-read headers for 9 files.
     - Stiffness: rCID, ion-trap MS2, Top15, NCE 35 (method `HeLa_150min_240_Top15_rCID_Elite.meth`).
     - siCCN1 and Matrigel: HCD, Orbitrap MS2, Top10, NCE 30.
     - Every sampled file reports "Orbitrap Elite MS".
   - **Tolerances:** both come from the Appendix and match parameters.txt. Precursor is 4.5 ppm. Fragment is 0.5 Da for the rCID runs and 20 ppm for the HCD runs.
   - **Fractionation:** stiffness is SDS PAGE (in-gel digestion, 18 slices); Matrigel is SAX (6 fractions, pH3→1 … pH11→6 as in the MaxQuant design); siCCN1 is `not applicable` (one run per sample).
5. **Modifications (rule 5/11).** The three searches use the same set: Carbamidomethyl (C) fixed (parameters.txt), and Acetyl (Protein N-term) and Oxidation (M) variable (summary.txt). The labels are Arg10 = UNIMOD:267 and Lys8 = UNIMOD:259, both MT=Variable.
6. **Cleavage (rule 2).** I wrote the digestion enzyme, `NT=Trypsin;AC=MS:1001251`. MaxQuant searched with Trypsin/P; that is in the manifest. There is no lysine derivatisation, so no CS= override.
7. **Factor values (rule 3b).** I used one factor per variable the study compares:
   - growth condition (stiffness 400 vs 22,000 Pa; Matrigel vs plastic in the cross-dataset comparison);
   - genetic modification (siCCN1 vs siCTL);
   - sampling time (Matrigel 4 h vs 22 h; 30 h is deposited too).
8. **Donor and pool columns.** `pooled sample` and `individual` = `pooled` (paper: "Cells from 3 to 5 cords were pooled"). Age, sex, ancestry and disease are `not available`. I did not add developmental stage, because no source states it.

What is and is not known:
- **Undocumented treatment:** "MitoC" presumably means mitomycin C. The word appears nowhere in the paper, the Appendix, Datasets EV1–3, the Review Process File or the PRIDE record.
- **Undocumented channel map:** the "Fwd/Rev" token suggests the Prolif layout, but nothing states it.
- **Not used by the paper:** the EV1 tables use the Prolif experiments only.
- **Data hint:** CYR61 H/L is 1.36 (C) and 1.00 (D), a much weaker response than in the Prolif runs.
- **No other paper:** Europe PMC lists only this paper as citing PXD003316.

Writing these rows would assert both a treatment and a stiffness per channel without evidence (rules 6/7/8).
