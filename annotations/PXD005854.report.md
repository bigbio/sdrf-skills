## Study
"eIF2B links translational control to mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation in Vanishing White Matter disease" (PRIDE title). The matching paper is Raini G … Geiger T, Elroy-Stein O, *J Neurochem* 2017, doi:10.1111/jnc.14024, PMID 28306143. PRIDE `references` is empty. The link rests on the same title and abstract wording and the same PI. Only the abstract could be read: the paper is not in PMC, and the Wiley PDF returned 403, which was not bypassed. The study compares mouse embryonic fibroblasts (MEFs) from Eif2b5^R132H/R132H^ VWM-model mice with wild-type MEFs. It is a spike-in SILAC design: each unlabelled MEF lysate was mixed 1:1 with a heavy (Lys8/Arg10) lysate of immortalized WT MEFs. Each mixture was split into 5 SCX fractions and run on a Q Exactive Plus, then searched with MaxQuant 1.5.2.10.

## Channel map (rule 6)
- **Explicit source.** The PRIDE sampleProcessingProtocol says: "Each unlabeled lysate was mixed at a 1:1 protein ratio with 25µg of total cell lysate of the SILAC labeled cells". It also says: "Spike-in SILAC standard was produced by culturing immortalized WT MEF in SILAC DMEM … lysine 8 … arginine 10". So light = sample and heavy = standard. MaxQuant Labels1 = Arg10;Lys8 agrees.
- **Data check** (`orientation.txt`, from proteinGroups.txt):
  - Median normalized H/L is 0.97–1.13 in every experiment, so both channels are present at about 1:1.
  - log2 Intensity H correlates across experiments with median Pearson 0.942 (min 0.900). Intensity L gives 0.933 (min 0.885). The heavy channel is slightly more uniform, consistent with one common standard.
  - The check is weak by design, because both channels are MEFs. There is no conflict between sources.
- **Genotype check attempted.** R132H removes the R132 tryptic site of Eif2b5 (SLGDVLR|DVDAK). SLGDVLR was identified only in the heavy state, by ISO-MSMS. Its light "partner" intensity is interference (H/L about 0.05) and is the same in WT and K samples, so the check is inconclusive. Genotype therefore rests on the submitter's names: WT, and K = "Mut" according to the PRIDE note.
