## Key decisions and evidence
1. **Raw files tied to their searches.**
   - For every annotated experiment, `summary.txt` lists the deposited raw names.
   - RunHeader last scans confirm 139 (control above), 983, 984 and the phospho runs (reviewer, round 2).
2. **Channel maps checked against the paper's Source Data (rule 6).**
   - For 139 and 984, the Source Data Fig. 3a columns "shCTRL" and "shKD" are *exactly* log2(Ratio M/L normalized) and log2(Ratio H/L normalized), for 2358/2362 and 1692/1692 genes. This confirms `File_assignment.txt`.
   - The light channel is the empty vector: the bait SLC3A2 is 2.3–3.7 log2 lower in light.
3. **Experiment 983 (XG-2): medium/heavy reversed against `File_assignment.txt`.**
   - Source Data Fig. 3a "XG2 shKRAS" ≡ log2 M/L and "XG2 shCTRL" ≡ log2 H/L for 2588/2589 genes. The deposit's orientation matches only 10 genes.
   - The decision rests on that exact identity. The RAS/MTOR ratios are not used as support, because they are within noise.
   - The override is recorded in the manifest and in `BLOCKED.md`.
4. **Phospho 96/96B.**
   - Paper Methods: L = shCtrl, M = shNRAS.1, H = shNRAS.2. `File_assignment.txt` gives the same order, writing ".56/.57" for the hairpins.
   - Fig. 4b correlates 0.87/0.82 with M/L and H/L, against ~0.48 when the channels are swapped.
   - 96 and 96B are encoded as technical replicates 1/2 of one SILAC triple (Source Data: "1 biological replicate").
5. **Hairpins `shKRAS.2` / `shNRAS.1` in the BioID2 arm.** These come from paper Methods: "infected with pLKO-shKRAS.2 or pLKO-shNRAS.1".
6. **Instrument from the raw headers.** 983 and 984 ran on a Q Exactive, not the HF the paper states. 139 and 96/96B ran on a Q Exactive HF.
7. **Search settings from the MaxQuant output.**
   - Modifications from `summary.txt`/`parameters.txt`:
     - Fixed: Carbamidomethyl (C).
     - Variable: Oxidation (M) and Acetyl (Protein N-term).
     - Biotin (K) in the BioID2 searches; Phospho (STY) in the 96 search. In each case the slot not searched is `not applicable`.
   - SILAC labels: UNIMOD 481, 188, 259 and 267, `MT=Variable`.
   - Cleavage agent: Trypsin (search rule Trypsin/P).
   - Precursor tolerance **4.5 ppm**. The `evidence.txt` |mass error| maxima are 983 4.4999, 984 4.4989, 139 4.4940, R1_96 4.4537 and R1_96B 4.4941, consistent with the PRIDE protocol's 4.5 ppm. The paper's 6 ppm is contradicted.
