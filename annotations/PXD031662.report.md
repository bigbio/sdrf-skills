## Study
Yang Y, Bolomsky A, Oellerich T *et al.* "Oncogenic RAS commandeers amino acid sensing machinery to aberrantly activate mTORC1 in multiple myeloma." *Nat Commun* 2022;13:5469. PMID 36115844, PMC9482638, doi:10.1038/s41467-022-33142-x. The paper is CC-BY. The full text came from NCBI efetch (numeric id, title checked). The supplementary PDF and the Source Data xlsx came from PMC OA S3. Analysis was done in MaxQuant 1.6.x.

The deposit has two kinds of experiment, both in multiple myeloma (MM) cell lines:

## Key decisions and evidence
1. **Channel maps were checked against the deposited ratios and the paper's Source Data (rule 6).**
   - For 136, 139 and 984, the Source Data Fig. 3a columns "<line> shCTRL" and "<line> shKD" are *exactly* log2(Ratio M/L normalized) and log2(Ratio H/L normalized) for 3007/3012, 2358/2362 and 1692/1692 genes. This confirms `File_assignment.txt`.
   - The light channel is the empty vector: the bait SLC3A2 is 2.3–3.7 log2 lower in light in all four experiments.
2. **Experiment 983 (XG-2): medium/heavy swapped relative to `File_assignment.txt`.**
   - `File_assignment.txt` says medium = SLC3A2 + shCtrl and heavy = SLC3A2 + shKRAS.
   - The Source Data Fig. 3a says the opposite:
     - "XG2 shKRAS" ≡ log2 M/L for 2588/2589 genes.
     - "XG2 shCTRL" ≡ log2 H/L. The reverse assignment matches only 10 genes.
   - The deposited biology agrees with the Source Data. RAS (log2 M/L 1.95 vs H/L 2.60) and MTOR (1.69 vs 2.20) are lower in **medium**. That is what shKRAS should do, and it is the paper's headline result (the SLC3A2–MTOR association falls on RAS knockdown).
   - In 984, 136 and 139, the heavy (knockdown) channel is the lower one, as their rows state.
   - Explicit label (Source Data) + data beat the single conflicting label. The conflict is recorded in the manifest.
3. **Phospho 96/96B.**
   - Paper Methods: Light = shCtrl, Medium = shNRAS.1, Heavy = shNRAS.2.
   - `File_assignment.txt` gives the same order but names the hairpins "shNRAS.56/.57". These are written as .1/.2, which is inferred.
   - Fig. 4b medians correlate 0.87–0.88 with deposited site log2 M/L and 0.81–0.83 with H/L, against ~0.48 for the swapped channels. RPS6 is reduced in M and H.
   - 96 and 96B are separate MaxQuant experiments (R1_96, R1_96B) under one `File_assignment.txt` row. The Source Data says "1 biological replicate". They are encoded as **technical replicates 1/2 of the same three sources** (inference).
4. **Instrument from the raw headers.**
   - 983 and 984 were acquired on a **Q Exactive**, not an HF. The paper Methods say HF.
   - 136, 139 and 96/96B were acquired on a Q Exactive HF.
5. **Modifications from MaxQuant `summary.txt`/`parameters.txt`.**

- `characteristics[culture medium]` = not available. The SILAC base medium is not named.
- `characteristics[age]` / `[developmental stage]` = not available for XG-2. Cellosaurus gives "Age unspecified".
- `characteristics[ancestry category]` = not available for SK-MM-1 and XG-2. Cellosaurus has no population for either.
- `comment[precursor mass tolerance]` = not available. The paper says 6 ppm and PRIDE says 4.5 ppm, and no `mqpar.xml`/`.apar` was deposited to settle it.
- `comment[fractionation method]` = not available for phospho. PRIDE says "pre-fractionation" without naming the method; the paper cites ref 67.
- `comment[modification parameters]` = not applicable in the Biotin slot (phospho rows) and the Phospho slot (BioID2 rows).
- The reduction and alkylation reagent columns are omitted. They are optional, and PRIDE (LDS/TCEP) and the paper (NuPAGE reducing agent) disagree; no alkylation reagent is stated.
