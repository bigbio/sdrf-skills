### Campaign rule 6 (explicit channel→sample source checked against deposited data)

**Met.**
- **Explicit source.** Dataset EV1 has the column headers "Upregulated/Downregulated by miR-31 in HEK 293T cell" with "Log fc (miR-31/Ctrl)". The deposit itself also ties b and d to miR-31: `ScatterPlot31.png` plots the axes "Ratio H/L normalized b" vs "d", and the deposit contains the `L31up/L31down` Perseus lists.
- **Deposited-data check (recomputed by me from the deposited proteinGroups.txt):**
  - EV1 HEK log fc = (log2 H/L d − log2 H/L b)/2, with r = 0.9956 (n = 1050).
  - b alone: r = −0.948, so in b the light channel is miR-31.
  - d alone: r = +0.959, so in d the heavy channel is miR-31.
  - The SDRF rows match this.
- **Cell line.**
  - The paper Methods and PRIDE protocol say HaCaT, and the PRIDE organismParts say Skin/Keratinocyte.
  - The data contradict both. b and d contain none of KRT5/KRT14/KRT6A/TP63/ITGB4/LAMB3/S100A2 among about 7300 protein groups. They do contain NEFM/NEFL/INA/LIN28B, which is a HEK293 signature.
  - So HEK293T (CVCL_0063, per the EV1 header) is supported by an explicit source plus two independent data checks. The conflict should be written out more fully in the manifest (minor finding 1).
