## Study
- **PRIDE:** "CHIP controls the expression of the neuropeptide precursor protein VGF" (Ball lab, Edinburgh, with Hernychova/Vojtesek, Brno). Submitted 2019-11, published 2021-07.
- **Samples:** SH-SY5Y neuroblastoma cells that are CHIP (STUB1) wild type or CRISPR/Cas9 STUB1 knockout.
- **Publications:**
  - No publication describes the SILAC arm.
  - Nita E *et al.*, *iScience* 2021;24:102878 (PMC8350547) cites PXD016299 for the SH-SY5Y **SWATH** data only. It describes the CRISPR KO (a gRNA in STUB1 exon 1).
  - It also cites "Nita E (2016) MSc thesis, Univ. Edinburgh", which could not be retrieved.
- **Where the SILAC design comes from:** the PRIDE protocol, the six deposited `.msf` searches and the raw headers.

### Channel meaning
- **Explicit statements in the PRIDE protocol:**
  - "biological triplicates of pulse SILAC samples";
  - the heavy label is 13C(6) Arg (+6.020) and 2H(4) Lys (+4.025);
  - the quantity is "the relative peak area of the peptides with the heavy isotope labels with CHIP WT/CHIP KO ratios".
- **Data check:** median H/(L+H) per run is 0.408 / 0.416 / 0.419 for WT and 0.368 / 0.367 / 0.361 for KO, so heavy is the minority, newly made pool.
- **Inferred, not stated:** the pulse direction, and that both channels come from the same culture.

### Genotype per file
- **Source.** The deposited file names: WT_1-3 and KO_1-3.
- **Conflict found.**
  - **What the files say.** The Xcalibur headers record the original names: `180327_SP007_CHIP_WT_A`, `_B`, `180328_SP008_CHIP_WT_C_4ul` (now WT_1-3) and `180404_SP007_CHIP_WT_D`, `_E`, `_F` (now KO_1-3). The msf FileInfos record the same names.
  - **Why it is not a genotype.** All six names say "WT", yet the protocol says 3 WT + 3 KO. So "WT" in the original names is a series name, not a genotype.
- **Data check, which resolves the conflict.**
  - VGF (O15240) is seen with 17 / 15 / 20 PSMs in KO_1 / 2 / 3 and with 0 PSMs in all three WT runs.
  - In the deposited SWATH t-tests, the group with low STUB1 has about 10× higher VGF: biorep1 VGF 6.30e6 vs 6.43e5, STUB1 4.28e4 vs 1.48e5; biorep2 7.12e6 vs 6.57e5. That group is the KO.
  - So the VGF-high SILAC runs are the KO, as their deposited names say.
  - STUB1 itself is not detected in any SILAC run.
- **Caveat.** The KO runs were acquired a week later (2018-04-04) than the WT runs (2018-03-27/28), so genotype and acquisition batch are confounded.
