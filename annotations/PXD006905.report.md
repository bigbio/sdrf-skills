## Study

First label-free single-cell MS proteomics performed *in situ* in live vertebrate embryos. A pulled
borosilicate microcapillary (~20 µm tip for frog, ~5 µm for fish) aspirates ~10 nL (frog) or ~5 nL
(fish) from an **optically identified cell inside a living embryo** — the cell is never dissected out.
Peptides are separated by capillary electrophoresis and sprayed via a custom electrokinetically
pumped nanoESI source.

## Why this dataset was annotatable — the key recovery

The obvious blocker: **no source states which raw file is which developmental stage.** 33 of 85 Xenopus
files are named only `Cell#N_<samplingdate>` with no stage token. The paper says cells were numbered
"#1 to #25 in the order of sampling" and *blinded during analysis*; Fig. 4B carries the identifiers
but they demonstrably do **not** join to the filenames (filename numbering restarts per sampling
batch — `Cel#5-10112017` and `Cell#5_10252017` are two different cells; Fig. 4B has one #5).

The map was recovered from the **column order of `Xenopus_1cell_temp/txt/proteinGroups_new.txt`**
inside the deposited 4.1 GB MaxQuant zip (read by HTTP range request over the zip central directory —
never downloaded whole). That file's 80 `LFQ intensity` columns are **not** alphabetical and **not**
acquisition order: the author re-exported them **grouped by experiment**. Collapsing the columns to
cells yields, in order:

| block | cells | paper states |
|---|---|---|
| zygote | 4 + 4 | "n = 4 different zygotes", 2 poles, technical duplicate |
| 16-cell D11 | **5** | "the left D11 cell in n = **5** different 16-cell embryos" |
| 32-cell D112 | **7** | "left D112 in n = **7** different 32-cell embryos" |
| 64-cell | **6** | "daughter cells of D112 in n = **6** different 64-cell embryos" |
| 128-cell | **7** | "grand-daughter cells of D112 in n = **7** different 128-cell embryos" |

**Four independent counts (5/7/6/7) match the paper exactly, plus the zygote 4+4.** This is not a
coincidence an arbitrary ordering could produce. The same constraint independently resolves three
filename anomalies without assumption:

- `Cell#1_10242017_1/_2` + `Cell#1_10252017_3` must be **one** cell (a typo in rep 3's date) — otherwise
  16-cell would have 6 cells, not 5.
- `64cells-#2_8-16_1` / `64-#2_8-16_2/_3` must be one cell — otherwise 64-cell would have 7, not 6.
- `32Cell#1_9-15_1` / `32cell#1_9-15_2/_3` (case variant) must be one cell — otherwise 32-cell would have 8.

Cross-check on the independent Fig. 4B evidence: the figure's colour bands measure (by pixel) to
6/7/7/5 columns for 64/128/32/16-cell = 25, and the text's named examples land in the right bands
("D11 cells #1, #11, #16 of the 16-cell embryo" ✓; "cells #4, #5, #20, #22, #24 … in the 128-cell
embryo" ✓). Fig. 4B therefore corroborates the *design* (25 cells, 5/7/6/7) even though its
identifiers cannot be joined to filenames — which is precisely why the column-order map was needed.

## Key decisions and evidence

**Full text was not open access** — Europe PMC `fullTextXML` 404s, Unpaywall reports `oa_status:
closed`, EPMC supplementary endpoint returns *"not open access"*, and the PMC `bin/` assets are
CAPTCHA-walled. The author manuscript XML was recovered via **NCBI eutils efetch** (`db=pmc`), and the
**Supporting Information** (which holds all the real acquisition/search parameters) from the Nemes lab's
own copy at `blog.umd.edu`. Per the brief, `is_open_access:false` was not treated as "no full text".

**No fixed modification — this is the highest-risk value in the file, and it is verified three ways.**
The SI states search parameters include "fixed modification, cysteine carbamidomethylation" but then:
*"Data that were generated by microprobe CE-ESI-MS were searched using the same parameters, **except
fixed modifications were excluded**."* All 94 deposited runs are microprobe. Confirmed independently by
(1) `txt/parameters.txt` in **both** zips: `Fixed modifications` is **empty**; (2) `summary.txt`
per-run `Fixed modifications` empty for all 89 searched runs; (3) PRIDE's sample protocol: *"Reduction
or alkylation steps were eliminated."* Annotating `Carbamidomethyl` here — the reflex for a bottom-up
search — would have been silently wrong on all 94 rows. Hence also
`comment[reduction reagent]` = `comment[alkylation reagent]` = `not applicable`.

**Modifications differ by organism, and the deposited data outranks the SI.** The SI says variable
mods are "methionine oxidation and asparagine and glutamine deamidation" for both organisms. The
actual searches disagree: Xenopus = `Oxidation (M);Deamidation (NQ)`; **zebrafish = `Oxidation (M)`
only**. The 9 zebrafish rows therefore carry `not applicable` in the second modification column.
PRIDE's `modifications` field lists the union ("monohydroxylated residue", "deamidated residue"),
which would have hidden this.

**Instrument agrees everywhere, and was still checked at the source.** Paper, SI, and PRIDE all say
Q Exactive Plus. Range-reading the first 256 KB of four deposited RAW files (both organisms, four
experiment groups), decoding utf-16-le, returns `Q Exactive Plus` in every one. `MS:1002634`.

**Search parameters** (all from the SI, which is the only source that has them — the main text does not):
precursor **5 ppm** (main search MS1 deviation), fragment **20 ppm** (MS2 match tolerance), HCD at
**28 % NCE** (`MS:1000422`), MS1 range **350–1600 m/z** for Xenopus but **300–1350 m/z** for zebrafish
(recorded per-organism).

**`single cell isolation protocol` = `not applicable`, and the reserved word is forced by the resolved
template.** The enum (FACS, cellenONE, microfluidics, LCM, manual picking, nanoPOTS, droplet
microfluidics, acoustic droplet ejection) has no member for in-situ capillary aspiration, and none is
defensible: the cell is **never isolated** — it stays alive in the embryo while a subcellular aliquot is
withdrawn. That is the study's entire point, so `manual picking` would be an outright misstatement.
Note the spec disagrees with itself here: `TERMS.tsv` says `allow_not_available=true`, but the
**resolved template says `allow_not_available: false`** (`allow_not_applicable: true`). The resolved
template is authoritative, so `not applicable` is both the semantically correct value and the only
permitted reserved word. (Worth reporting upstream as a TERMS.tsv/template drift.)

**`cells per well` = 1.** The aspirate is a *subcellular* fraction (0.006–0.25 % of cell protein), so
"1" needs justification: the template defines the column as "Number of cells per well/reaction. Use 1
for true single cells", and the material in each reaction derives from exactly one cell — not zero,
not pooled. `pooled sample` = `not pooled` records the absence of any carrier or pooling.

**Developmental stage — EFO is mandated but nearly empty, so precision won over compliance.**
`TERMS.tsv` and the `vertebrates` template specify EFO (validator `error_level: warning`). EFO has
**no** term for the 16/32/64/128-cell or 2-cell stage (searched directly; `cleavage stage`
UBERON:0000107 exists but would collapse the study's factor into one value and destroy it). Rule
applied: *use the mandated ontology where it has an exact term, otherwise the most precise verified
term.* Result — zygote → `fertilized egg stage` **EFO:0001322** (exact EFO term, matches the paper's
"the fertilized 1-cell embryo"); the four Xenopus cleavage stages → the Nieuwkoop–Faber series in
**XAO** (`XAO:1000015/16/17/18`, labels and 16-/64-/128-cell synonyms verified live against OLS);
zebrafish → `2 cell stage` **UBERON:0007232**.

**Cell type.** `CL:0000353` — preferred label `blastoderm cell`, synonym *blastomere*, definition
"An undifferentiated cell produced by early cleavages of the fertilized egg (zygote)" (fetched from
OLS, not assumed) — exactly these cells. Zygote aspirates → `animal zygote` `CL:0000365` (**not**
`CL:0010017`, the generic `zygote`, which smart-mode returned first). XAO also has `blastomere D1.1`
(XAO:0005088), but `cell type` licenses only CL/BTO/CLO and no XAO term exists for D1.1.2 or the
64-/128-cell descendants, so the D11/D112 identity lives in `source name` / `cell identifier` instead.

## The one thing that is genuinely lost: the zygote pole

The 16 zygote rows are the paper's animal-vs-vegetal validation experiment, and **animal/vegetal is
their factor**. It is not recoverable:

- Filenames carry no AN/VG token — only `1cell_#3 … #12`.
- The paper/SI never tabulate which sample is which pole.
- The `proteinGroups_new.txt` order *does* split them cleanly 4 + 4 (block A = #5,#6,#12,#11;
  block B = #3,#7,#8,#9), matching "n = 4 zygotes … each analysed in technical duplicate" — so the
  **grouping** is evidence, but which block is animal is a 50/50 coin flip on an ordering convention.
- The marker route was attempted and is **not executable**: the paper states vitellogenin/Plin2 are
  vegetal-enriched, which would settle it from the data — but the Xenopus quantification ran against
  the EST-derived PHROG database, and all 834 protein groups carry PHROG contig IDs with **empty**
  `Fasta headers` and no gene names. No UniProt accessions at all.

So the pole is **not annotated** rather than guessed. Consequence, recorded honestly: the 8 aspirates
come from 4 zygotes (2 poles each), but the aspirate→zygote pairing is equally unrecoverable, so each
aspirate is given its own `biological replicate` (1–8). **This over-counts biological replicates for
that group (8 recorded vs 4 real zygotes)** — a known, deliberate limitation, preferred over inventing
a pairing. Everything needed to fix it is one sentence from the authors.

**A clean pass here proves less than it looks, and I probed exactly how much.** Injecting deliberate
errors into a copy shows `parse_sdrf` 0.1.5 *does* enforce: organism (**error** level), cell type,
instrument, and the isolation-protocol enum (warning level) — my values pass those checks for real.
But a **bogus `NT=totally fake stage;AC=EFO:9999999` passes silently**: `characteristics[developmental
stage]` is **not ontology-validated at all**. The XAO terms therefore owe their clean pass to an absent
check, not to approval — they are justified only by the live OLS verification recorded above, and a
reviewer should re-verify them independently rather than trust the validator.

## Flagged for the reviewer

1. **Zygote biological replicate over-counts** (8 aspirates recorded as 8 replicates; really 4 zygotes
   × 2 poles). Deliberate — the pairing is unrecoverable.
2. **XAO/UBERON in an EFO-mandated column** (85 of 94 rows) — a defensible rule, but a judgement call,
   and unpoliced by the validator.
3. **`factor value[developmental stage]`** is a single factor across three sub-studies (clone
   development, zygote poles, buffer benchmark). It is the right factor for the 64 clone-cell files;
   it is *not* the factor for the 16 zygote files (whose real factor, pole, is unrecoverable) or the
   6 D11 AmBic benchmark files (whose factor, buffer, has no comparator deposited — only the AmBic arm
   was uploaded; the ACN/RapiGest/dissection arms are absent from the deposition entirely).
4. **The 6 `nESI_D11_*AmmBic` benchmark files double as 16-cell clone data points** (they are 2 of the
   5 D11 cells — forced by the 5-cell count). Annotated as 16-cell D11, which is what they are.
5. `characteristics[cells per well] = 1` for a *subcellular* aspirate — see reasoning above.
