## Design and the key finding: a second, undisclosed factor (genotype)
PRIDE and the abstract describe only L1–L4 stages in biological triplicate. The deposited MaxQuant
`summary.txt` (inside `txt.zip`, range-read) names every run's two SILAC channels explicitly:

| runs | stage | light (Lys0) | heavy (Lys8) | replicate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1–S4 | L1–L4 | NID1 | N2 | Rep1 |
| S5–S8 | L1–L4 | NID1 | N2 | Rep2 |
| S9–S12 | L1–L4 | N2 | NID1 | Rep3 (label swap) |

So each run is a 1:1 mix of a *nid-1* mutant and wild-type N2 lysate at one stage, with a label swap in
Rep3. **Cross-check against the data (rule 6):** in `proteinGroups.txt`, NID-1 (nidogen, Q93791)
intensity is non-zero only in the channel the map calls N2 and zero in the NID1 channel in all 9 runs
where it was detected (S1,S3–S7 heavy only; S9–S11 light only; undetected in S2,S8,S12). This confirms
both the map and the Rep3 swap, and that NID1 is a nid-1 loss-of-function strain. Global median
log2 H/L ≈ 0 in every run (1:1 mixing as stated).
