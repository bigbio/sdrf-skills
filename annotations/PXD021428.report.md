## Study
PRIDE title: "Schlafen family member 5 (SLFN5) regulates LAT1-mediated mTOR activation in castration-resistant prostate cancer (PART 1)". The lab is Zanivan/Leung at the CRUK Beatson Institute. PRIDE links the PXD to Salji MJ et al., *iScience* 2022;25(4):104056 (PMC8957019), whose Data Availability cites PXD021428. The same data underlie Martinez RS et al., *Cancer Res* 2021;81(13):3664 (doi:10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-20-3694). That article could not be fetched (publisher 403), so its Research Square preprint rs-67753/v1 was used instead.

The design is a super-SILAC comparison of 18 orthotopic prostate tumours grown in CD-1 nude mice: three matched pairs of hormone-naive (HN) and castration-resistant (CR) models, with 3 tumours each.

## Design / channel map
- **SILAC light = tumour lysate; SILAC heavy = super-SILAC standard.** The standard is an equal-protein mix of Arg10/Lys8-labelled CWR22Res, LNCaP clone FGC, LNCaP-AI and VCaP lysates, combined 1:1 with each tumour before FASP.
- **Explicit sources:** the iScience Methods (heavy Arg10/Lys8 media for the standard; "normalised Light / Heavy SILAC ratio ... increase in abundance of protein in the CRPC model") and the PRIDE/preprint protocol.
- **Check against the deposited ratios (proteinGroups.txt):**
  - Mouse-only protein groups come from the host stroma, which the cultured human standard cannot contain. They carry 87.5–95.0% of their intensity in L in every one of the 18 experiments.
  - SLFN5 log2 L/H, CR minus HN: +1.4 (22Rv1 vs CWR22Res), +1.6 (LNCaP-AI vs LNCaP; paper 1.58) and +1.15 (VCaP-CR vs VCaP; paper 1.05).
  - AR is strongly up in VCaP-CR (+3.2 vs −0.2), as the preprint states.
- **Conclusion:** the map is confirmed and the sources do not conflict.
- The heavy source names are 18 aliquots of one physical standard pool, one name per tumour mixture.

## Key values
- **Organism.** Homo sapiens, because the tumours are human cell-line orthografts. The mouse host is recorded in `characteristics[xenograft]`, and the search included the mouse proteome. Mouse-only protein groups carry about 31% of the light-channel intensity overall (18.6–68.2% per tumour).
- **Organism part.** `prostate gland` (UBERON:0002367), the orthotopic tumour site. It is `not applicable` for the standard.
- **Sample and material type.** Sample type is study sample / reference. Material type is tissue / cell line.
- **Cell lines.** The ATCC numbers in the preprint map to Cellosaurus as follows: CRL-1740 → LNCaP clone FGC CVCL_1379, CRL-2505 → 22Rv1 CVCL_1045, CRL-2876 → VCaP CVCL_2235. LNCaP-AI is CVCL_4791.
- **CWR22Res** ("hormone-responsive variant of CWR22", Case Western) has no Cellosaurus entry, so its accession and name are `not available`.
- **Donor metadata from Cellosaurus.** Sex male; ancestry Caucasian → European ancestry; developmental stage adult. Age is 50Y for the LNCaP lines, 59Y for VCaP, and `not available` for the CWR22 lines (Cellosaurus says only "Adult"). `characteristics[individual]` is the shared donor of each isogenic pair (CWR22_donor, LNCaP_donor, VCaP_donor). These are curator-made placeholder keys, not study IDs.
- **Storage, biorepository, authentication.** See *Repairs* above. Passage number and cell type are `not available`.
- **Search settings (mqpar.xml).**
  - Fixed: Carbamidomethyl (C).
  - Variable: Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term), Lys8 = UNIMOD:259 and Arg10 = UNIMOD:267 (`MT=Variable`).
  - Precursor tolerance 4.5 ppm (main search); fragment tolerance 0.5 Da (ITMS).
  - MaxQuant used the Trypsin/P rule.
- **Digestion.** Lys-C + trypsin (FASP), so there are two `comment[cleavage agent details]` columns. A deterministic post-build script inserts the second one.
- **Instrument and acquisition.**
  - Instrument: LTQ Orbitrap Velos, confirmed in two raw headers.
  - DDA top-10, CID, 36 NCE, linear-ion-trap MS2.
  - Fractionation: high-pH RP, PRIDE:0000564.

- Passage number and cell type are `not available`. Storage temperature is stated only for the standard (−80 °C), so it is `not available` on the tumour rows.
- The light channel is about 31% mouse host protein by intensity (18.6–68.2% per tumour), so it is not purely human.
