## Dataset
- Title: Calcium/calmodulin dependent protein kinase kinase 2 (CAMKK2), an alternative to curcumin in head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (IOB Bangalore, Chatterjee A).
- Design: CAL 27 cells, SILAC 2-plex (Arg6/Lys6), TiO2 pS/T phosphopeptide enrichment of 12 basic-pH RP fractions, LTQ Orbitrap Velos HCD, PD 1.4 (Mascot + Sequest HT).
- Publication: none found. PRIDE's reference (Solanki 2018, cigarette smoke) belongs to linked PXD007108. Europe PMC has no hit for the accession or the title. A same-lab pTyr arm with the identical design (Sathe 2016, PMC4908701, PXD002097) is used only to corroborate the channel map. That arm is a different dataset (pTyr, Orbitrap Fusion, different files), so this deposit is not a duplicate.
- Multiplexed SILAC confirmed: the MSF quantification method is "SILAC 2plex (Arg6, Lys6)". Each in-scope run has 427–717 confident 13C6-labelled PSMs.

## Channel map (source: PRIDE sampleProcessingProtocol + MSF quan method; checked against the data)
| label | source name | treatment | factor value[treatment] |
|---|---|---|---|
| SILAC light | CAL27_curcumin_11.5uM_4h | Curcumin 11.5 uM, 4 hour | Curcumin |
| SILAC heavy (13C6-Lys, 13C6-Arg) | CAL27_DMSO | untreated (vehicle; compound Dimethyl Sulfoxide) | untreated |

- PRIDE: "grown in regular media were treated with curcumin ... cells cultured in SILAC media were treated with DMSO."
- MSF: channel 1 Light = no label; channel 2 Heavy = Label:13C(6) on K and R; ratios are reported as Light/Heavy.
- Data check: collapsed to unique phosphopeptides and median-normalised, 220 have L/H ≥2 and 341 have L/H ≤0.5. The authors' abstract reports 275 hyper- and 335 hypo-phosphorylated phosphopeptides on curcumin, which fits light = curcumin. A swapped map would invert the asymmetry. This check supports the map but does not prove it.
