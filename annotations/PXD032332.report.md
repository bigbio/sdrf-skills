## Study
"TIAR and FMRP shape pro-survival nascent proteome of leukemia cells in the bone marrow microenvironment". Wolczyk M et al., *iScience* 2023;26(4):106543, doi:10.1016/j.isci.2023.106543, PMID 37123244, PMC10140000 (open access).

The method is QuaNCAT. K562 cells are pulsed for 4 h with L-HPG plus medium (Lys4/Arg6) or heavy (Lys8/Arg10) SILAC amino acids. One medium culture and one heavy culture are combined at equal cell counts. Nascent proteins are enriched by click-biotin/NeutrAvidin, digested on the beads with trypsin, and run on a Q Exactive HF-X (DDA, HCD). The data were searched with MaxQuant.

## Key encoding decisions
- **Light rows** are kept. They hold the pre-existing proteome, which the primary search quantified (precedent: QuaNCAT PXD005059).
  - Each run's light row has its own source name, with `characteristics[pooled sample]` = `SN=<medium source>;SN=<heavy source>`.
  - These are **not** aliquots of one shared pool: each run's light material comes only from that run's two cultures.
  - In a light row, a factor whose value differs between the two cultures is set to `not applicable`.
- **Factors:**
  - `factor value[genetic modification]`: shNEG / shTIAR / shFMRP / parental.
  - `factor value[growth condition]`: N / CO.
- **Custom characteristics:** `genetic modification`, `growth condition` and `protein population` (newly synthesized vs pre-existing).
- **Biological replicate** = independent experiment A/B/C (1c/1d/1e) → 1/2/3. Numbering restarts within each factor group.
  - shNEG appears in two mixes per experiment (PP_1x & PP_4x in N, PP_2x & PP_5x in CO). These rows share replicate numbers but have distinct source names.
  - The paper says "3 independent experiments" and does not say whether the shNEG cells in the two mixes were one culture or two (see limitations).
- **Cell line:** K562 / CVCL_0004 / K-562, from Cellosaurus.
  - pleural effusion; blast phase CML, BCR-ABL1 positive (MONDO:0006115); 53Y; female; European ancestry; adult.
  - Culture medium: RPMI-1640 (NCIT:C178973).
  - Storage: −80 °C.
- **Modifications** (from summary.txt, identical in all 3 searches; the Multi modifications column is empty):
  - fixed: Carbamidomethyl C;
  - variable: Oxidation M, Deamidated N/Q, Acetyl Protein N-term;
  - labels, all MT=Variable: Label:2H(4) K (481), Label:13C(6) R (188), Label:13C(6)15N(2) K (259), Label:13C(6)15N(4) R (267).
- **Search settings:**
  - Cleavage agent: Trypsin (MS:1001251). MaxQuant used the Trypsin/P rule.
  - Fragment tolerance: 20 ppm.
  - Precursor tolerance: `not available` (no mqpar.xml).
  - Fragmentation: HCD at 27 NCE, Orbitrap MS2.

## Limitations
1. The Arm 2 map is derived from deposited data matched to the authors' Table S2, not from an explicit statement. For the TIAR pairs it is backed by the knockdown target; the FMRP pairs have only Table S2.
2. It is unknown whether the shNEG cultures in the TIAR and FMRP mixes of one replicate were the same culture or two separate ones.
3. It is not stated whether K562 cells were separated from the HS-5 stroma before the pulse and lysis in CO.
4. The precursor tolerance is not available, so `convert-openms` exits 1.
5. The pp2 search used MaxQuant 1.6.17.0, although PRIDE and the paper say 2.0.3.0. The settings are otherwise identical.
6. parse_sdrf gives SN= warnings, which are refuted in validation.txt.
