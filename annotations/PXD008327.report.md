## Study
Touati SA, Kataria M, Jones AW, Snijders AP, Uhlmann F. "Phosphoproteome dynamics during mitotic exit in budding yeast." *EMBO J* 2018;37(10):e98745. PMID 29650682, PMC5978319, doi:10.15252/embj.201798745. The full text came from Europe PMC fullTextXML, and its Data availability section names PXD008327.

**Conflict:** PRIDE's `references` field cites a different paper (Ferreira *et al.*, Nat Commun 2018, a stem-cell study, PMID 30282987). The PRIDE title and protocol text match Touati *et al.*, and a Europe PMC search for PXD008327 returns Touati *et al.* The PRIDE reference was ignored.

Design: budding yeast (W303 background, GAL1-CDC20), SILAC duplex with a constant reference.
- **Heavy** ((13C6,15N4)-Arg + (13C6,15N2)-Lys, synthetic complete medium with raffinose + galactose): cells arrested in metaphase by Cdc20 depletion; 10 aliquots harvested on arrest.
- **Light** (YP medium): cells arrested the same way, then released by re-adding galactose; sampled at 0, 5, …, 45 min. Each sample was mixed with one heavy aliquot.
- After digestion, TiO2 enrichment. The phosphopeptides were injected three times (CID, HCD, MSA). The flow-through (non-phosphorylated peptides) was injected once (CID).
- Instrument: LTQ Orbitrap Velos. Search: MaxQuant 1.3.0.5, run as two separate searches (Phospho, Protein Level).

## Templates and why
- `ms-proteomics` — LC-MS/MS DDA. No other template applies:
  - Yeast is a fungus, and no organism template covers fungi (human/vertebrates/invertebrates/plants).
  - It is not a Cellosaurus cell line, so not `cell-lines`.
  - The data are not DIA and not single-cell.
- Consequence: `characteristics[genotype]`, `[strain or breed]` and `[culture medium]` are not licensed. Strain, genotype and media therefore appear only in source names and in this report.

## Key decisions and evidence
1. **SILAC channel map** (rule 6). Explicit source: the paper's SILAC section (heavy = metaphase reference, light = released time course) and the PRIDE protocol. I checked it against the deposited ratios:
   - In `Phospho (STY)Sites.txt`, 152 full Cdk-consensus sites (localisation probability > 0.85) have median log2[(H/L 45 min)/(H/L 0 min)] = **+1.12**, against 0.00 for all other sites. The light channel loses Cdk phosphorylation during mitotic exit, as the paper reports.
   - In `proteinGroups.txt`, H/L of the GAL-induced proteins falls over time (GAL7 1.13→0.69, GAL1 1.21→0.84, GAL10 1.33→0.89). Galactose was re-added only to the light culture.
   - Both checks agree with the stated map. A swapped map would give the opposite sign.
2. **Runs → activation method** come from the raw instrument-method text, read by HTTP range read of the first 2 MB of all 40 files:
   - RW7: CID, ITMS, NCE 30
   - RW8: HCD, FTMS, NCE 45
   - RW9: CID with "Multistage activation is enabled", ITMS, NCE 35
   - RW10: CID, ITMS, NCE 30
   - MS1 range 300–2000 m/z for all runs.
   - Fragment tolerance per run follows the MaxQuant analyzer-specific settings: ITMS 0.5 Da, FTMS 20 ppm. Precursor tolerance is `mainSearchTol` = 6 ppm.
3. **Modifications per search** (rule 5). The two parts were built separately with identical headers and then concatenated:
   - Both searches: Carbamidomethyl (C, Fixed), Oxidation (M), Acetyl (Protein N-term), Arg10 (UNIMOD:267), Lys8 (UNIMOD:259), all Variable (rule 11).
   - Phospho (STY) UNIMOD:21 only on the phospho rows 1–60. Rows 61–80 carry `not applicable` in that column.
4. **Factors.**
   - `sampling time` (0–45 minute). The heavy reference is `0 minute`: it was harvested on arrest, and the paper normalises to "time 0 … when cells … were arrested in metaphase".
   - `treatment`: light = galactose re-addition (release); heavy = `untreated` (rule 4).
   - The second factor keeps the never-released heavy reference out of the light 0-minute group, so the two media are not pooled as one condition.
5. **Biological replicate = 1 everywhere** (rule 3). There is one light culture, and each timepoint is its own group. There is one heavy culture whose 10 aliquots all fall in the single heavy group. Source names differ per heavy aliquot and per phospho/flow-through part. This keeps the (source, bio rep, tech rep, fraction) coordinate unique.
6. **Technical replicate** 1/2/3 = RW7 (CID) / RW8 (HCD) / RW9 (MSA) phospho injections of the same peptide sample. The flow-through run is 1 under its own source name.
7. **Cleavage agents** = Trypsin (rule 2: the digestion enzyme, not the search rule Trypsin/P) plus Lys-C in a second `comment[cleavage agent details]` column. Lys-C was the second digestion step.
8. **Sample type**: heavy metaphase channel = `reference` (paper: "This culture served as a reference"); light = `study sample`.
9. **Label isotopes**: the paper's Results say 13C6-Arg/13C6-Lys, the Methods say 13C6,15N4-Arg / 13C6,15N2-Lys, and the PRIDE protocol says "13C10/13C8". These were resolved to Arg10/Lys8 from the deposited mqpar. The Phospho search identified 294,278 MS/MS as SILAC pairs with those masses.

## Open items / limitations (also in the manifest)
- PRIDE cites the wrong publication (see Study).
- The paper's repeat SILAC time course (Fig EV3) is not deposited, although Data availability says the full data were. Only the core experiment is annotated (3,452 vs 3,456 sites).
- Phospho and flow-through source names of one timepoint are the same mixed digest: 10 light samples and 10 heavy aliquots, not 20 + 20.
- The label-isotope wording conflicts between the paper Results, Methods and PRIDE protocol (decision 9).
- MSA has no PSI-MS term. It is encoded as CID (MS:1000133) and told apart from plain CID only by NCE 35 vs 30 and by technical replicate.
- Strain genotype (Table EV3, not retrieved) and the culture media are not encodable under ms-proteomics.
- `characteristics[treatment]` is free text, which gives 2 parse_sdrf warnings.
- techsdrf is not installed. Raw-header range reads were used instead.
- The SDRF is two `build` outputs plus a post-build step (concatenation + Lys-C column). That step is outside `sdrf-tools build`. `convert-openms` passes only the first enzyme (Trypsin) to OpenMS.
