## Study
"CGG repeat RNA binding protein capture identifies SRSF protein kinase 1 as a modulator of RAN translation and suppressor of repeat toxicity" (PRIDE). Paper: Malik I *et al.* "SRSF protein kinase 1 modulates RAN translation and suppresses CGG repeat toxicity", *EMBO Mol Med* 2021;13(11):e14163, doi:10.15252/emmm.202114163, PMID 34542927, PMC8573603 (CC BY; full text and Dataset EV1 retrieved from PMC OA S3).

This is a triplex SILAC RNA-tagging pulldown in HEK293T cells. Each reporter RNA carries PP7 stem-loops and is co-expressed with PCP-NLS-3xFLAG, then captured by anti-FLAG IP. There are two independent experiments without stress and two with thapsigargin (ISR activation). Each IP mixture was run once, unfractionated, on an Orbitrap Fusion (180 min, DDA, HCD) and searched in Proteome Discoverer 2.1 (Sequest HT + Percolator).

## Key decisions and evidence
1. **Channel map (rule 6).** Two explicit sources agree:
   - The Methods say: "Light SILAC was used for AUG-nLuc-PP7, medium SILAC +1CGG100-nLuc-PP7, and heavy SILAC was used for 1(+) (GGGGCC)70-nLuc-PP7 reporter". The Results and the Fig 1D legend repeat the light/medium part.
   - The Proteome Discoverer exports in Dataset EV1 carry channel headers "F1: Light, Sample, AUG", "F1: Medium, Sample, CGG" and "F1: Heavy, Sample, GGGGCC" in all four Raw MS sheets.

   There is no independent data-internal check. The only custom FASTA entry is the PCP-NLS-3xFLAG bait, which is in every channel by design, and the PD study files were not deposited.
2. **Run → condition.** I matched the peptides of each `.msf` against the peptides of each Dataset EV1 sheet. Each run matches one sheet with Jaccard 0.979–0.985; every other pairing is ≤ 0.47. The raw-header sample names ("3xSILAC", "WCE IP#2", "Stress#1/#2") agree on which runs had stress.
3. **Replicate numbering.** Within each (bait, treatment) group, replicates are numbered 1–2. For the +TG runs the numbers follow the raw-header names, so 0508 "Stress#1" is 1 and 0465 "Stress#2" is 2. Dataset EV1 lists these two runs in the opposite order. This only affects identifiers, and it is recorded as a limitation.
4. **Treatment.** Thapsigargin is NCIT:C128634 (2 µM, 5 h before IP). The no-stress IPs are `untreated` (rule 4); no vehicle is described for them.
5. **Search parameters** come from the `.msf` Sequest HT nodes and are identical in all four runs:
   - Carbamidomethyl C is fixed.
   - Variable: Oxidation M, Deamidated N/Q, Acetyl (Protein N-term), and the four SILAC labels. Their UNIMOD accessions match the stated mass shifts: Arg6 188, Lys4 481, Arg10 267, Lys8 259.
   - Precursor tolerance 10 ppm, fragment tolerance 0.2 Da, trypsin with 2 missed cleavages.
   - PD's internal "MappingL" (X→Leu substitution) is not encoded.
6. **MS settings** come from the raw method summary, which is the same for all four runs: MS1 Orbitrap 120K at 400–1400 m/z; ddMS2 HCD at 32% NCE (stepping off) with Orbitrap detection at 15K; cycle time 3 s.
