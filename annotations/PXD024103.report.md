## Study
Lohraseb I *et al.* "Global ubiquitinome profiling identifies NEDD4 as a regulator of Profilin 1 and actin remodelling in neural crest cells." *Nat Commun* 2022;13:2018. PMID 35440627, PMC9018756, doi:10.1038/s41467-022-29660-3. The full text came from NCBI efetch (numeric PMC id, title checked). The Europe PMC XML returned HTTP 500.

- **Cells:** NCU10K, an immortalized mouse neural crest cell line, SILAC-labelled.
- **Treatment:** heavy (K8R10) cells got Nedd4 siRNA and light (K0R0) cells got low GC control siRNA, each for 48 h.
- **Sample prep:** the two lysates were mixed 5 mg + 5 mg, digested with trypsin and separated by basic RP into 80 fractions, pooled into 8. From each fraction, 50 µL was kept as "total" (pre-enrichment) and the rest went through PTMScan K-ε-GG enrichment.
- **MS:** Q Exactive, top-12 DDA, HCD.
- **Search:** MaxQuant 1.3.0.5. The Ub and Total runs were searched separately.

## Key decisions and evidence
1. **SILAC channel → condition.**
   - **Explicit statements:** the paper Methods and the PRIDE protocol say "5 mg K8R10 Nedd4 siRNA and 5 mg K0R0 control siRNA". The Fig. 1a legend says "low GC (control, light) or Nedd4 (heavy) siRNA".
   - **Labels in the search output:** summary.txt shows `Multiplicity 2`, with `Labels1 = Arg10;Lys8`.
   - **Data check (rule 6):** the deposited ratios agree with heavy = Nedd4 knockdown in every searched experiment, so there is **no label swap**:
     - NEDD4 protein normalized H/L is 0.28 in Total Rep1 and 0.18 in Rep2.
     - NEDD4 K-GG sites have H/L 0.04-0.30 in Ub Exp1, Exp2 and Exp3.
     - Ubiquitin K48 and K63 linkage peptides are down (H/L 0.32-0.44), as in paper Fig. 2e.
2. **Run series → replicate.**
   - The deposited experiment designs set OK20130809 = Rep1 and OK20130818 = Rep2. This matches the paper's "two biological replicates".
   - The Ub search contains a **third experiment**, PM20131210_Ub (raw folder `OK20131217_PeterUb`). Its K-GG site log2 H/L correlates with the other two at r = 0.79 and 0.81, the same as they correlate with each other (0.81). It is therefore not a re-injection, and I annotate it as **biological replicate 3**.
   - Replicate numbers restart within each treatment group (rule 3). Each (group, number) pair maps to one series.
3. **PM20131206_Total_1uL1-8.** These are in no deposited search and no design template. I infer that they are the pre-enrichment aliquot of the PM series (biological replicate 3) from three clues: the operator prefix, the raw folder `OK20131206_PMNedd4Total_1uL`, and the date four days before the PM Ub runs.
   - Per rule 5, their tolerances are `not applicable`.
   - Their modification columns keep only stated sample chemistry: Carbamidomethyl, plus the Arg10/Lys8 labels. Oxidation, Acetyl and GG are search choices, so they are `not applicable` here.

## Conflicts found and resolution
- **Paper vs deposit, replicate count:** the paper reports 2 biological replicates, but the deposit holds 3 series per arm. I followed the deposit (see judgement call 1).
- **Paper vs deposit, totals:** the paper's numbers (4988 protein groups observed, 4381 quantified, 4015 Ub sites) are larger than what the deposited searches yield (4147 / 3609 protein groups; 3294 quantified non-contaminant Ub sites). The published tables likely came from a search that was not deposited.
- **Total design vs search:** the Total design template and search include 8 undeposited runs (`OK20130809_PMSILACTotal_P1-8`) and leave out the deposited PM20131206_Total runs.
- **PRIDE experiment type:** PRIDE lists "Top-down proteomics", which is wrong; this is a bottom-up tryptic digest. The field is not used.

## Limitations / open items (also in the manifest)
1. The biological-replicate status of the PM series (rep 3) is inferred and conflicts with the paper's "two biological replicates".
2. For PM20131206_Total, the label map cannot be checked against data, and its series membership is inferred. `convert-openms` fails on the full file because of the `not applicable` tolerances on these rows.
3. Precursor tolerance comes from prose; no mqpar.xml was deposited.
4. NCU10K origin metadata is missing (stage, strain, sex, passage).
5. techsdrf is not installed. Raw verification was limited to the UTF-16 header strings of 4 files.
6. `sdrf-tools fetch` was not used; the record was gathered by hand.
7. Treatment values are free text (validator warning).

## Judgement calls a reviewer should attack hardest
1. **PM2013 series = biological replicate 3**, for both arms. The paper says two replicates. The evidence for an independent preparation is the deposited Ub search defining a third experiment, plus ratio correlations of r ≈ 0.8, the same as between the two named replicates. If it is actually a re-preparation from a rep-1 or rep-2 culture, the replicate numbering overstates independence.
2. **PM20131206_Total_1uL1-8 kept, as rep 3 total, with a partial modification set.** The runs were never searched. Their channel map rests only on the generic protocol statement, and their tie to the PM Ub series rests on file and folder names. The rows keep Carbamidomethyl and the SILAC labels but put `not applicable` for Oxidation, Acetyl and GG and for the tolerances, which breaks `convert-openms`. The alternatives were to drop the rows (rule 7 applies only when the map is undocumented, and here it is documented generically) or to copy the Total search's modifications onto them.
3. **Control treatment = `low GC control siRNA` rather than `untreated`.** Also: enrichment = generic `sample enrichment`, organism part = `neural crest` (an embryonic structure, taken from the PRIDE field) while developmental stage is `not available`, and disease = `normal` for an immortalized line.
