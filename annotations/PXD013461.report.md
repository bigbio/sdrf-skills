## Study
"Fusion partners influence cellular and molecular phenotypes of oncogenic BRAF fusions" (PRIDE title). Published as Stangl C, Post JB *et al.*, "Diverse BRAF Gene Fusions Confer Resistance to EGFR-Targeted Therapy via Differential Modulation of BRAF Activity", *Mol Cancer Res* 2020;18(4):537-548, doi:10.1158/1541-7786.MCR-19-0529, PMID 31911540. The paper is not open access. The methods come from the PRIDE protocols and the AACR figshare supplements (Supplementary Materials and Methods, Supplementary Figures and legends, and the construct/primer table).

The deposit holds only the HEK293 SILAC phosphoproteomics screen; the colorectal cancer organoids of the study are not in it. Design:
- HEK293 lines transduced with dox-inducible pInducer20 constructs: GFP, BRAF-WT, BRAF-kinase (aa 381–766), BRAF-V600E, AGAP3-BRAF, DLG1-BRAF and TRIM24-BRAF.
- Light (Arg0/Lys0) and heavy (Arg10/Lys8) cultures, one given doxycycline (1 µg/ml) and one ddH2O for 4 h, mixed 1:1.
- Lysis in TCEP/chloroacetamide, trypsin digestion, TiO2 phosphopeptide enrichment, then three high-pH stage-tip fractions per mix.
- Orbitrap Fusion DDA: MS1 in the Orbitrap at 240K (m/z 400–1500), MS2 by HCD 30% read out in the ion trap.
- One MaxQuant 1.6.1.0 search of all 42 raws: 14 experiments × 3 fractions, Multiplicity 2, Arg10;Lys8. SILAC multiplexing is verified (rule 14).

### How the map was established (rule 6)
- **Explicit in the sources:**
  - SILAC labels (PRIDE protocol; summary.txt).
  - The forward scheme light −dox / heavy +dox (Supp Fig S3A) and the label-swap design (Supp Methods: "forward and reverse (label-swap) experiments").
  - Every construct in both light and heavy lysates (Supp Fig S6D).
  - The 7 constructs, and each construct's start codon from its cloning primer (construct table). BRAF-WT and V600E start at MAALS (full length); BRAF-kinase starts at residue 381; the fusions start at their partner's N-terminus.
- **Not stated anywhere:** which experiment number holds which construct and orientation. This was derived from the deposited MaxQuant tables (`identity.tsv`, `sitecounts.txt`, `peppos.txt` in the scratch dir):
  - AGAP3 peptides change only in S1/S2 (median log2 H/L +3.13 / −3.63).
  - DLG1 N-terminal peptides change only in S3/S4 (+1.69 / −1.38).
  - TRIM24 N-terminal peptides change only in S5/S6 (+1.81 / −2.11).
  - BRAF N-terminal peptides (aa < 380, present only in the full-length constructs) change only in S7/S8 and S11/S12.
  - S9/S10 have BRAF aa 380–766 up, but neither N-terminal BRAF nor any partner peptides, so they are the kinase construct.
  - WT vs V600E: the Supp Fig S3B V600E-specific marker CDC27 S364 is induced only in S11/S12 (+2.45/+2.81 in dox orientation; one other value, S5). MARCKS S101 is highest in S11/S12 (+2.44/+2.63, vs +0.26/+0.32 in S7/S8). Supp Fig S3D gives V600E the most up-regulated phosphosites (202), and S11/S12 has the most in the deposit. Sites up >1.5 log2 in both experiments: S11/S12 149, S5/S6 99, S1/S2 84, S3/S4 51, S9/S10 14, S7/S8 7. S7/S8 show almost no ERK activation, as expected for BRAF-WT.

  - The resulting order (AGAP3, DLG1, TRIM24, WT, kinase, V600E, GFP) is also the row order of the construct table.

### Recorded source conflict: BRAF-WT vs BRAF-kinase (review F1)
- **Against the assignment.** The authors' labelled BRAF-Kinase result in Supp Fig S3B/D has four features, all of which match **S7/S8** (annotated BRAF-WT) rather than S9/S10:
  - 8 up-regulated sites, 37.5% shared. S7/S8 has 7 up-sites (4 shared with another pair here; 3 per the review). S9/S10 has 14 (11 shared).
  - No MAPK1/3 activation-loop rows. S7/S8 shows weak MAPK; S9/S10 shows MAPK1 Y175 +1.69/+3.12 and MAPK3 Y165 +1.74/+3.68.
  - One kinase-only row, RAF1 S465. It is induced +2.27/+2.62 in S7/S8 and not quantified in S9/S10.
- **For the assignment.**
  - The BRAF-kinase construct starts at BRAF aa 381 (construct-table primer ATG GAC TTG ATT AGA GAC C). It therefore cannot produce the N-terminal BRAF peptides (S151, S363/S365) that are dox-induced in S7/S8 (and in S11/S12) and flat in S9/S10.
  - Supp Fig S6C: BRAF-Kinase raises pERK by 4 h; BRAF-WT does so only weakly.
  - Supp Fig S3C KSEA: RAF1 and MAP2K1/2 are top hits for BRAF-Kinase; there are none for BRAF-WT.
- **Why the sequence evidence wins (rule 6).**
  - The construct sequence is explicit, and the transgene's own peptides are measured directly. Together they identify which pair carries the truncated construct, and two further author figures agree.
  - The S3B/D profile is a processed summary (protein-normalised, t-tested, thresholded).
  - Its kinase-only marker peptide IGSGSFGTVYK is also BRAF sequence (aa 463–473), present in both constructs.
- **Decision.** The coordinator kept S7–S10 as annotated, with the conflict documented. An author key is needed to settle it.
