## Conflicts found and how they were resolved

1. **Carbamidomethyl on the cellenONE 20-cell rows — the hardest call.** The deposited `..._20c_Noco.txt` (batch-1 20-cell) and `..._bulk_Noco.txt` (batch-1 bulk) Spectronaut setups **both** list `Fixed Modifications: Carbamidomethyl (C)`. I annotated **only the 6 bulk rows** with Carbamidomethyl and left the cellenONE 20-cell/1-cell rows without it, because:
   - the cellenONE proteoCHIP lysis/digestion buffer is `0.2% DDM, 100 mM TEAB, trypsin, Lys-C` — **no reductant (TCEP) and no alkylator (CAA/IAA)**, so cysteines cannot be carbamidomethylated;
   - the paper states this explicitly ("the protocol does not involve reduction and alkylation … database searches were performed with free cysteine sulfhydryls");
   - the other two deposited setups — `MTX_FLU_NoC_...DT.txt` (batch-2 single/20-cell) and `PROT_OT_...OT.txt` (batch-3 One-Tip) — show **empty** Fixed Modifications, matching the chemistry;
   - the repo brief's own landmine warns against asserting Carbamidomethyl without an alkylation step.
   The `20c_Noco` Carbamidomethyl entry is best explained as a leftover Biognosys factory-default that the submitter did not clear for that one early analysis. The **bulk** rows do carry Carbamidomethyl because the bulk PAC prep uses `5% SDS, 5 mM TCEP, 10 mM CAA` (reduction + chloroacetamide alkylation) — confirmed by both the chemistry and the `bulk_Noco` setup. **This is the decision I most want the reviewer to attack.**
2. **Paper says "carbamylation", chemistry/settings say Carbamidomethyl.** The Methods prose calls the bulk fixed mod "carbamylation"; the deposited setup and the 10 mM CAA alkylation both indicate **Carbamidomethyl (+57, UNIMOD:4)**. Resolved to Carbamidomethyl.
3. **PRIDE `modifications` field incomplete.** It lists only "monohydroxylated residue, acetylated residue" (= Oxidation, Acetyl) and omits the bulk Carbamidomethyl; superseded by the deposited Spectronaut settings for the bulk arm.
4. **Batch-1 cell line not in the filename.** The 20231028/AST1 files carry no `HeLa` token (unlike batch 2's `QC_HeLa`). Assigned **HeLa** by inference: batch 1 is the Figure 1 + Figure S3 nocodazole-synchronisation experiment, and the paper's Cell Culture / Synchronisation sections describe that work on HeLa (SCC-25 appears only in the One-Tip arm). Flagged as inferred.

## Modelling notes the reviewer should scrutinise

- **Two orthogonal factors, one single-valued `treatment` column.** Fixation (fixed vs non-fixed) is the study's headline comparison; the drug/synchroniser is a second dimension. `characteristics[treatment]` (single-valued, NCIT) holds the **chemical/drug** (nocodazole / MTX / 5-FU / DMSO). Fixation is carried by **`factor value[fixation]`** = `formaldehyde` | `none`. There is no licensed `characteristics[fixation]` term, so fixation lives only in the factor-value column (parse_sdrf requires only that factor-value columns be last, which they are); this is a deliberate, documented choice.
- **Replicates.** The paper calls the per-condition repeats "technical replicates" (n=12 single cells, n=4 20-cell, n=3 bulk, n=4 One-Tip). Each deposited run is nonetheless a **distinct** single cell / distinct pool / distinct bulk aliquot, so I modelled each as a separate **`biological replicate`** (1..N within a condition) with `technical replicate` = 1 (no re-injections). A reviewer could argue bulk/One-Tip repeats are technical replicates of one lysate instead.
- **One-Tip `cells per well`** left `not applicable` rather than guessing 1000 vs 3000.
- **`sample type` = not applicable** for non-single-cell rows (PRIDE vocabulary gap, above).
