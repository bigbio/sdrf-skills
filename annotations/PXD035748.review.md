- **m1.** Recommended `characteristics[sample type]` (base 1.1.0) is missing. Fix: add `study sample` on the medium and heavy rows, and a PRIDE:0000895 child such as `pooled` on the light rows.
- **m2.** The light SN= pool rows carry biological replicate 1–7. The spec TIP (README.adoc:333/365) suggests `pooled`. The current form matches precedent PXD016211, so this is optional.
- **m3.** A manifest limitation says the light channel "carries little signal". That is wrong: Intensity L per mix is 2.8–4.1e12, about the same as H. Fix: reword it. The SDRF does not change.
- **m4.** The Supp Data 5 correlation is partly circular, because logFC equals AveExpr, i.e. the authors' own H/M. The manifest should name the CT-antigen signature as the independent check.
- **m5.** The paper gives two DAC durations: 72 h (pulse section, used in the SDRF) and 78 h (general "Cell culture and treatment"). Record this conflict in the limitations.
