## Study

- **Title (PRIDE):** Mitochondrial retrograde signaling contributes to metabolic differentiation in
  yeast colonies — Proteomics of BY4742, BY-rtg1, BY-mks1, BY-rtg1mks1 and BY-rtg2mks1.
- **Publication:** Plocek V *et al.*, Int J Mol Sci 2021 22(11):5597, PMID:34070491, PMC8198273,
  doi:10.3390/ijms22115597 (CC BY; full text read via NCBI efetch `db=pmc&id=8198273`, title checked).
- **Organism:** *Saccharomyces cerevisiae* (BY4742 background; NCBITaxon:4932 verified by OLS).
- **Design (paper §4.3/4.4 + Supplementary Tables S1–S3 headers):** 5 strains (wt BY4742, rtg1Δ,
  mks1Δ, rtg1Δmks1Δ, rtg2Δmks1Δ) × 2 cell subpopulations (upper/U and lower/L cells, micromanipulated
  from giant colonies) × 2 developmental phases (acidic, alkali) × 3 biological replicates = **60**,
  which matches the 60 deposited RAW files. PRIDE protocol says colonies were "grown 5 and 15 days".

## Campaign mis-screen: this is NOT a SILAC dataset

PRIDE `quantificationMethods` lists `SILAC` (and `TIC`). The deposited searches say otherwise; deposited
search outputs outrank PRIDE fields (brief, precedence rule):

- both `mqpar.xml` files: `<multiplicity>1</multiplicity>`, `<labelMods><string></string></labelMods>`,
  `<maxLabeledAa>0</maxLabeledAa>`, `<lfqMode>1</lfqMode>`;
- both `summary.txt` (inside `txt.7z` / `2019_10_03_txt.7z`): `Multiplicity` = 1, `Labels0` empty for every run;
- `proteinGroups.txt` carries `LFQ intensity <n>` columns and no `Ratio H/L` columns;
- the paper describes label-free comparison ("Comparison of proteomes was performed by nano LC-MS/MS",
  t-tests on abundances, Perseus); SILAC is never mentioned.

So, if unblocked, the SDRF would be **label-free** (`comment[label]` = `label free sample`, 60 rows).

**Deliberately not used.** The `2019_10_03` LFQ intensities show a clear period-10 structure
(runs 31–40, 41–50, 51–60 repeat the same pattern: e.g. Rtg2 LFQ = 0 at runs 40, 50, 60 and roughly
halved at 39, 49, 59; Dld3/Cit2/Yat2/Crc1 10–100× higher at positions 6 and 10 of each block), consistent
with "10 samples × 3 replicate blocks". Turning that into strain + cell-type + phase labels would be
forensic inference: Mks1 is never quantified, Rtg1 only at trace levels in 6/30 runs, the deletion
cassettes (natR) are not in the search database, and U/L assignment would rest on marker biology the
study itself is testing. Campaign rules 6–8 forbid asserting a genotype/condition without an explicit
source, and rule 7 forbids keeping rows with `not available` conditions, so a partial "known-columns-only"
SDRF was not written either. Which MaxQuant batch (437 vs 452) is the acidic and which the alkali phase
is likewise unstated.
