## Study
Liu Y, …, Aebersold R. "Systematic proteome and proteostasis profiling in human Trisomy 21 fibroblast cells." *Nat Commun* 2017;8:1212. doi:10.1038/s41467-017-01422-6, PMID 29089484, PMC5663699 (CC BY). The study uses fetal skin fibroblasts from monozygotic twins discordant for trisomy 21 (T1DS = trisomy 21, T2N = normal), plus 11 unrelated DS and 11 control individuals.

## Is it SILAC with multiplicity > 1? (rule 14)

The deposited OpenSWATH output (`E1511041456_feature_alignment_ProteinTurnover.tsv`) has a light and a heavy (`K(UniMod:259)`, `R(UniMod:267)`) precursor for each peptide in every run. The library file is named `…R10K8…TraML`. There is no MaxQuant summary.txt: the Orbitrap Elite pSILAC raws that went through MaxQuant are not in the deposit.

## Design and channel map
The submitter run sheet `SampleInformation_201608upload.txt` maps every run to twin, replicate and time (e.g. `yanliu_Y151023_002_SW` = "T1DS_rep1_0hr"). Each run gets two rows from the same culture: **SILAC light = pre-existing proteins** and **SILAC heavy = newly synthesized proteins**. At 0 h the heavy row is a background channel, as in precedents PXD006015 and PXD028934. Factors are `disease` (Down syndrome vs normal) and `sampling time` (0/1/4/8/14/24 hour). The paper's three "experimental replicates" come from separate dishes (SI Fig. 1). They are numbered biological replicate 1–3 within each (disease, time) group (rule 3).
