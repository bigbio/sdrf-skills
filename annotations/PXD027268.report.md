## Study design and channel map
H1299 cells were SILAC-labelled and their cell surface was biotinylated. MaxQuant 1.5.5.1 ran a triple-SILAC search (light; Arg6/Lys4; Arg10/Lys8). There are two independent experiments, Expt1 (2017) and Expt2 (2018), each run as gel slices: 8 per lane in Expt1, 6 in Expt2.

| Lane | SILAC light | SILAC medium | SILAC heavy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Expt1_C, Expt2_C (nuclear capture) | internalised 30 min **+HGF** | **no internalisation** ("No int nuclear") | internalised 30 min, **no HGF** |
| Expt1_D, Expt2_D (surface) | **MesNa-reduced** | unused (no row) | **non-reduced** |

**Sources for the map:**
- the Supplementary Data 1 column headers;
- its legend in the Description of Supplementary Files (MOESM3);
- the deposited `proteinGroups.txt`. Supplementary Data 1 values are log2 of the deposited normalized ratios (the reviewer measured |r| ≥ 0.998).

**Expt2_D:** Supplementary Data 1 reports only Expt1_D. Expt2_D shows the same receptor pattern (H/L ≫ 1 for TFRC, EGFR, EPHA2, ITGB1, MET), so I gave it the same orientation. There is no label swap.

## Key decisions, with evidence
- **Instrument is Q Exactive HF (MS:1002523).** The raw-file headers (3 files, both batches) and the Methods agree. PRIDE's "Orbitrap Fusion Lumos" belongs to the companion TurboID dataset PXD027217.
- **Surface orientation is L = reduced, H = non-reduced.** The supplementary legend and the data agree on this. The main-text Methods (and the PRIDE protocol) say the opposite, so I used the supplementary legend and the data.
- **This is triple SILAC, not two-state.** The Methods describe heavy/light only. The Results text, Supplementary Data 1 and the search are three-state. The medium arm in lane C is documented only by the "No int nuclear" header.
- **Lane-D medium channel has no row.** It contains about 4% residual intensity, which fits `reQuantify=true`, and Supplementary Data 1 uses only L/H. The reviewer accepted this.
- **Modifications come from `mqpar.xml`.**
  - Fixed: Carbamidomethyl C.
  - Variable: Oxidation M; Acetyl Protein N-term.
  - Variable SILAC labels: UNIMOD:188 R (Arg6), 481 K (Lys4), 267 R (Arg10), 259 K (Lys8).
- **Search settings:**
  - Tolerances: 4.5 ppm precursor; 20 ppm fragment (FTMS).
  - Enzyme rule: Trypsin/P. The annotated enzyme is Trypsin (see Changes).
- **What `untreated` means in each lane:**

| Lane | `untreated` row | what it means | contrasted with | what both arms share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Expt1_C, Expt2_C (nuclear) | SILAC heavy (`*_nuclear_internalised_control`) | **no HGF** during the 30 min internalisation at 37 °C | SILAC light, `Hepatocyte Growth Factor` (10 ng/ml) | surface biotinylation, 37 °C chase, MesNa surface reduction, nuclei isolation |
| Expt1_D, Expt2_D (surface) | SILAC heavy (`*_surface_nonreduced`) | **not MesNa-reduced** (surface biotin left on) | SILAC light, `Mesna` | surface biotinylation at 4 °C, lysis, streptavidin pull-down |

  So `untreated` never means that the sample went through no treatment at all. It names the control arm of that lane's single contrast. The SILAC medium arm in lane C ("No int", no internalisation) has treatment `not available` because its HGF status is unstated.
- **`untreated` convention (detail).** `characteristics[treatment]` / `factor value[treatment]` encode the contrast **within each sub-experiment**:
  - In the nuclear lanes: `Hepatocyte Growth Factor` vs `untreated`, where `untreated` means no HGF. All nuclear arms were also MesNa surface-reduced and warmed to 37 °C. This follows the authors' wording ("HGF-treated (L) to untreated").
  - In the surface lanes: `Mesna` vs `untreated`, where `untreated` means not reduced. The legend says "heavy-labelled cells were left untreated (H)".
  - `untreated` is the control value the sample-metadata template's treatment validator prescribes. It produces the one validator warning (not an NCIT/EFO term).
- **Sampling time is the internalisation time:**
  - 30 minute for the two internalised nuclear arms;
  - 0 minute for the "No int" arm (inferred from that label);
  - `not applicable` for the surface lanes, which have no 37 °C step.
- **Cell line:** NCI-H1299 / CVCL_0060 (ATCC). Developmental stage: adult (donor 43Y).
  - Disease: lung large cell carcinoma (MONDO:0003050).
  - Organism part: lung. Sampling site: lymph node.
  - Donor: 43Y, male (Cellosaurus).
- **Biological replicate** is 1 for Expt1 and 2 for Expt2. Fig. 1b reports "two independent experiments".
