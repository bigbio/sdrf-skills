## Sample preparation and acquisition (from PRIDE protocols — authoritative for tech metadata)
- Papain dissociation of fresh prenatal brain tissue → FACS (Sony SH800, 100-µm nozzle) → live single cells into 384-well low-bind plates, 1 µL lysis buffer per well (100 mM TEAB pH 8.5, **10 mM TCEP**, **40 mM chloroacetamide/CAA**, 0.1% DDM).
- 95 °C 5 min lysis; overnight trypsin digestion (2.5 ng sequencing-grade trypsin, 37 °C); TFA quench; EvoTip Pure desalting; Evosep LC → **Orbitrap Eclipse**; label-free single-cell workflow.
- DIA-NN search: UniProt human reference, Trypsin/P, ≤2 missed cleavages, peptides 7–30 aa, charge 1–4. **Carbamidomethyl (C, fixed)**, **Met oxidation (variable)**, N-terminal Met excision. MS1 tol **5 ppm**, MS2 tol **10 ppm**; 1% FDR; MBR enabled.

### Carbamidomethyl IS justified here (not a reflex annotation)
The brief warns against asserting Carbamidomethyl without a documented alkylation step. Here alkylation **is** documented: the lysis buffer contains 40 mM chloroacetamide (CAA), and DIA-NN was run with Carbamidomethyl (C) as a **fixed** modification. CAA yields the same +57.02146 carbamidomethyl adduct as iodoacetamide. Reduction (TCEP) and alkylation (CAA) are additionally captured in dedicated `comment[reduction reagent]` / `comment[alkylation reagent]` columns.

## Fields deliberately left `not available` (and WHY)
- **`characteristics[cell type]` = not available.** This is the hardest call and the one I most want a reviewer to attack. The FACS sort was **agnostic** (the preprint: "we used FACS to agnostically isolate individual cells") — it did NOT select neurons. Cell-type identity (radial glia, oRG, IPC-EN, excitatory neurons, CGE-inhibitory neurons, OPC, vascular, microglia, and **erythroid/blood cells**) is a **downstream proteomic-clustering result**, not an experimental property of the sort. Critically, **805 of 2310 cells are erythroid (blood) cells**, not neural — so no single cell-type value is correct for all rows, and any neuron/neural-cell blanket value would be wrong for ~35% of rows. The deposited files carry **no** raw-file→cluster map, and none is exposed via Europe PMC (supplementaryFiles = HTTP 404). Assigning per-cell types would require re-deriving the authors' clustering — fabrication by the brief's standard. Hence `not available`. (The screen hint "neuron CL:0000540" was intentionally NOT applied: the sort is agnostic and the population is mixed incl. blood.)
- **`characteristics[organism part]` = `telencephalon`, not `prefrontal cortex`.** The three donors differ: GW15 & GW19 = germinal zone + cortical plate microdissected from **prefrontal cortex**; GW13 = whole nascent **frontal telencephalon including the ganglionic eminence** (subpallium, NOT cortex — cortex underdeveloped at GW13). Cells cannot be mapped to donor (see below), so a per-row region is unknowable. `telencephalon` is the tightest UBERON term that is correct for **every** donor (prefrontal cortex ⊂ frontal cortex ⊂ telencephalon; ganglionic eminence ⊂ telencephalon). Using `prefrontal cortex` for all rows would be wrong for the GW13 cells.
- **`characteristics[individual]`, `age`, `sex`, `ancestry category` = not available.** Three de-identified prenatal donors (GW13/15/19, ZSFGH/UCSF). The deposit provides no raw-file→donor map (the three MS batches WLBSC/WLBSC2/WLBSC3 are acquisition batches with counts 1050/597/663 that do **not** match any stated donor split, so batch≠donor is unproven and was not assumed). Donor sex/age/ancestry are not reported per cell. `developmental stage` is the only demographic field defensible at cohort level (all donors fetal).
- **`dissociation method`, `scan window lower/upper limit`, `isolation window width`, `dia method` = not available.** The DIA acquisition window scheme and HCD/dissociation settings are not stated in the PRIDE protocols or the preprint; not guessed. (These are recommended, not required, DIA columns.)

## Judgement calls to attack hardest
1. `characteristics[cell type] = not available` for all rows vs. attempting per-cell cluster labels — I chose honesty over fabrication (no deposited map; 35% are blood cells).
2. `organism part = telencephalon` (superset) vs. `prefrontal cortex` — correctness-for-all-donors over specificity.
3. `developmental stage = fetal stage` (single cohort value) vs. per-donor GW13/15/19 — unmappable per cell, so cohort-level only.
4. Including a constant `factor value[developmental stage]` — there is no per-run experimental factor recoverable from the deposit; this column reflects the study's biological axis (developmental profiling) and matches repo convention (constant factor values are used in sibling SDRFs).
5. All 2310 deposited runs annotated (no QC/blank exclusion) — the deposit already is post-QC and contains no control wells.
