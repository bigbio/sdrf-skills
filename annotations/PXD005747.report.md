## Study
Phosphoproteome arm of Fabrik I et al., "The Early Dendritic Cell Signaling Induced by Virulent *Francisella tularensis* Strain Occurs in Phases and Involves the Activation of ERKs and p38 In the Later Stage", *Mol Cell Proteomics* 2018;17(1):81-94 (doi:10.1074/mcp.RA117.000160, PMID 29046388, PMC5750852).
- Samples: SILAC-labelled (Arg6/Lys6, 13C6) GM-CSF bone-marrow-derived dendritic cells (BMDCs) from 6-8-week-old female C57BL/6 mice.
- Infection: *F. tularensis* subsp. *holarctica* FSC200 wild type or its attenuated ΔdsbA mutant, MOI 50, for 10, 30 or 60 min, against mock-infected controls.
- Processing: each infected/mock pair was mixed 1:1, digested with trypsin, split into 20 HILIC fractions and TiO2-enriched for phosphopeptides.
- Acquisition and search: Q Exactive (HCD, Top10), MaxQuant 1.5.2.8.

## Companion / duplicate check
PXD006759 ("– proteome dataset", PRIDE otherOmicsLinks) is a **companion deposit, not a duplicate**:
- **No shared runs.** Its 48 raw files (`*_ML_IF_*`, acquired 2016-09/10, Top14, hpHRP fractions) share no name with the 358 phospho raw files here (`*_IF_DC*`, HILIC + TiO2, Top10).
- **One MaxQuant search per deposit.** Each deposit holds its own MQ_output and experimentalDesignTemplate.txt. The only file in common is the search FASTA.
- **Same digests.** The paper says the proteome samples are "aliquots of 6 digests (60 min p.i./two bacterial strains/triplicate)". The PXD006759 SDRF therefore reuses the 60 min source names from this file.

Nothing was added to DUPLICATES.md. Each deposit has its own SDRF.

## Scope
All 358 deposited `.raw` files are annotated (2 rows each: light and heavy). FASTA, `experimentalDesignTemplate.txt` and `MQ_output.zip` are not data runs. Every raw file appears in the deposited MaxQuant design template and in `summary.txt` (0 missing, 0 extra), so no runs were excluded and nothing was added to BLOCKED.md.

SILAC was verified (rule 14): `summary.txt` has Multiplicity 2 and Arg6;Lys6 in every experiment. There are 18 MaxQuant experiments: 2 strains × 3 time points × 3 replicates. Two experiments have 19 fractions: DsbA_10m_Rep3_LS has no Fr18 file and DsbA_10m_Rep4 has no Fr20 file, both in the deposit and in MaxQuant.

**Sources.**
- PRIDE protocol: "'light' cells were infected ... 'Heavy' cells were left as control/untreated groups ... one label swap experiment (raw files containing 'LS' in name)".

- MaxQuant `summary.txt`: the LS experiments have Labels0 = Arg6;Lys6, so every MaxQuant H/L ratio is mock/infected.

Host-ratio correlations are negative between LS and non-LS experiments (-0.22 to -0.65) and positive between non-LS experiments (+0.32 to +0.52). The paper explains this as a label-specific bias from suboptimal BMDC labelling. Bacterial proteins are unlabelled in all experiments, so they cannot show the orientation.

## Key values
- **Samples**: Mus musculus; bone marrow; dendritic cell (CL); material type cell; C57BL/6; female; age 6W-8W; adult.
- **Disease**: `normal`. Healthy donor mice, infected in vitro for 60 min or less.
- **Sample type**: study sample (infected) or control sample (mock).
- **Infection columns**:
  - `characteristics[infection status]` (also the factor): FSC200 wild type infected, FSC200 dsbA deletion mutant infected, or mock-infected.
  - `characteristics[infectious agent]`: Francisella tularensis subsp. holarctica FSC200 (NCBITaxon:351581), `not applicable` for mock.
  - `characteristics[infectious agent strain]`.
  - `characteristics[multiplicity of infection]`: 50.
- **Time**: `characteristics[sampling time]` / factor = 10, 30 or 60 minute. Mock rows take the time of their paired infection.
- **Enrichment**: enrichment of phosphorylated protein.
- **Fractionation**: HILIC (PRIDE:0000554). `comment[fraction identifier]` is the deposited MaxQuant fraction number, set by a scripted post-build edit (see validation.txt).
- **Search** (MaxQuant `summary.txt` / `parameters.txt`):
  - fixed: Carbamidomethyl C;
  - variable: Oxidation M, Acetyl Protein N-term, Phospho STY, and Label:13C(6) on R and K (UNIMOD:188).
  - `summary.txt` has no "Multi modifications" column.
  - Search rule Trypsin/P; digestion Trypsin.
  - Tolerances: precursor 4.5 ppm (main search, from paper/PRIDE), fragment 20 ppm (FTMS).
  - Database: mouse UP000000589 plus the F. tularensis FSC200 proteome, as stated by the deposit.
- **Instrument**: Q Exactive, HCD, NCE 27, Orbitrap MS2, MS1 350-1750 m/z (raw header). Reagents DTT and IAA (paper).

## Judgement calls a reviewer should attack
1. **Mock replicate numbering (1-6 per time point).** It assumes each digest's mock lysate came from a separate culture. Supplemental Fig. S1 was not retrieved.
2. **Mock sampling time.** Inferred from "the respective mock-treated BMDCs".
3. **`disease = normal`.** PRIDE tags the project with tularemia (DOID:2123).
4. **Cell type.** `dendritic cell` (CL:0000451) was chosen over BTO "bone marrow-derived dendritic cell".
