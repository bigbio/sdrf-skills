## Label-direction question (campaign rule 6)

- **Explicit statement:** the PXD003940 protocol says control = heavy (Arg6/Lys8) and X2-shRNA1 = light. It also says SILAC labelling was confirmed "in control cells". The sister deposit PXD001618 says the same.
- **Label-specific data check (the producer missed this):** the knock-down target PPM1F/POPX2 is quantified at the evidence level.
  - Evidence 5417: AIGDVFQKPYVSGEADAASR, BR2_TR1, normalised H/L 3.48.
  - Evidence 121961: VNGTLAVSR, BR3_TR1, normalised H/L 10.1.
  - Heavy-only detections: 5418 (BR2_TR2), 113969 and 113970 (VAEELVAAAR, BR3 TR1 and TR2). Re-quantify was off, so these are real heavy-only detections.
  - One weak light-only detection: BR1_TR1, PEP 0.046.
  - At the protein level, Intensity H is 32.5e6 and Intensity L is 5.8e6. The global raw H/L median is 0.58.
  - So there is more POPX2 in the heavy channel, which means heavy is the non-knock-down control. The protein-level ratio is NaN only because Min. ratio count = 2 and this protein has 1 ratio.
- **Conflicting signal:** secreted cytokines, growth factors and exosome markers are higher in the heavy channel: IL6 1.73, CXCL8 1.72, CSF1 2.14, PDGFA 1.84, VEGFA 1.59, TIMP1 2.22, CD9 1.41, CD63 1.41, SDCBP 1.43. The abstract and the lab's 2020 review (PMC7547661) say the knock-down secretes more. This is an inference from the biological direction. It does not outweigh an explicit label statement that the target-protein data back up. The discrepancy remains unexplained and should stay recorded.
