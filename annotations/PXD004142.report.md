## Study

First proteome and secretome maps of human oocytes, and the first single-cell proteome of any human
cell type. Bulk pools of 100 oocytes establish the proteome; SP3 sample preparation then scales the
analysis down to 10-cell pools and to individual oocytes. The biological question is germinal
vesicle (GV, immature) versus metaphase II (MII, mature).

## Templates

`ms-proteomics v1.1.0`, `human v1.1.0`, `single-cell v1.0.0`.

`single-cell` is declared because 6 deposited runs are true single oocytes; its `cells per well`,
`sample type`, and `cell identifier` columns are precisely what separates those from the bulk runs.
No `cell-lines` (primary cells). No `dia-acquisition` (DDA).

**No `clinical-metadata`, unlike the sibling PXD023366.** That annotation needed it to license
`characteristics[phenotype]`, because its GV/IVM/IVO factor could not be expressed as a cell type
(IVM and IVO are both MII). Here the factor is exactly GV vs MII, which maps onto a real, verified
ontology distinction — `primary oocyte` CL:0000654 ("has not completed meiosis I") vs
`secondary oocyte` CL:0000655. So the factor rides on `characteristics[cell type]`, licensed by
`sample-metadata`, and `factor value[cell type]` mirrors it. Declaring `clinical-metadata` for a
free-text phenotype column would have been ceremony.

### The bulk-vs-single-cell reconciliation (the point of this dataset)

The screen's warning is correct and the deposition genuinely mixes four experiments. Every run was
assigned by joining the PRIDE file list against the `summary.txt` inside the four deposited MaxQuant
zips — each zip covers one experiment and the union is exactly the 18 raw files:

| runs | experiment | `sample type` | `cells per well` | `label` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 100-oocyte pools, conventional IVF culture | bulk control | 100 | label free |
| 4 | 100-oocyte pools, hanging drop (2 tech reps × 2 states) | bulk control | 100 | label free |
| 2 | secretome of 100 oocytes (conditioned medium) | *not applicable* | *not applicable* | label free |
| 1 | medium-only control (no oocytes) | negative control | *not applicable* | label free |
| **6** | **single oocytes — GV3/4/5, MII3/4/5** | **single cell** | **1** | **label free** |
| 3 (→6 rows) | 10-oocyte pools, dimethyl 2-plex | bulk control | 10 | DIMETHYL0 / DIMETHYL8 |

**Only the 6 `140818SL_*_singleoocyte.raw` runs are true single cells.** The paper corroborates:
"the six proteomes of single GV and MII oocytes … three cells in one maturation stage … three cells
of the other". The filenames number from 3, so GV1/2 and MII1/2 apparently exist but were never
deposited; 6 is the deposited count and the annotated count.

`bulk control` (PRIDE:0000907) is defined as "a bulk sample used as a control in single-cell
proteomics experiments to benchmark single-cell measurements against". That is a good fit for the
10-cell dimethyl pools, which the paper explicitly ran "to corroborate these label-free quantification
data". It is a **mild stretch** for the 100-cell pools, which were the discovery proteome in their own
right rather than a benchmark — but they are unambiguously bulk, they are the reference the
single-cell data is compared against, and no better child of PRIDE:0000895 exists (see below). The
unambiguous, purely factual discriminator is `cells per well` = 100 / 10 / 1, which needs no
interpretation. Flagged for the reviewer.

## Why this dataset was annotatable despite being multiplexed

The dimethyl arm is a 2-plex — per the brief, it needs a channel→sample map or it is not annotatable.
The map is **not** in the paper, which only ever says "pair-wise comparisons of GV versus MII".

It was recovered from the deposited `10oocytesdimethyl.zip` (3.07 GB) **without downloading it**, by
HTTP-range-reading the zip central directory and inflating three small members (entries are bzip2,
method 12, not deflate):

- `combined/txt/summary.txt` lists the MaxQuant experiment names: **`immature_light-mature_heavy1/2/3`**
- the same file and `mqpar.xml` give `Labels0 = DimethLys0;DimethNter0` and `Labels1 = DimethLys8;DimethNter8`

So **light = immature = GV**, **heavy = mature = MII**, stated by the submitter's own search
configuration. This is direct evidence, not inference. The three runs map 1:1 onto the three
biological replicates the paper describes ("three groups of 10 cells each of GV and MII").

Chemistry cross-check, all verified live and self-consistent:

| MaxQuant | SDRF label | PRIDE def | UNIMOD | Δmass |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DimethLys0/Nter0 | `DIMETHYL0` PRIDE:0000848 | "channel 0, base mass 28.0313 Da" | UNIMOD:36 Dimethyl | +28.0313 |
| DimethLys8/Nter8 | `DIMETHYL8` PRIDE:0000852 | "channel 8, delta +8.0444 Da" | UNIMOD:330 Dimethyl:2H(6)13C(2) | +36.0757 |

36.0757 − 28.0313 = 8.044 ✓. `sdrf-pipelines`' own DIA-NN converter uses the identical `DIMETHYL0`
/ `DIMETHYL8` strings with delta 8.0444, confirming these are the canonical SDRF label values.
**`Dimethyl:2H(6)13C(2)` is UNIMOD:330, not UNIMOD:510** — the plausible-looking guess is wrong, and
this was caught only because every accession was searched rather than recalled.

## Key decisions and evidence

**Instrument — PRIDE is wrong; the raw headers and the paper agree against it.** PRIDE's
`instruments` field says `LTQ Orbitrap Velos` (MS:1001742). The paper's LC-MSMS section says
"an **Orbitrap Velos Pro** mass spectrometer". Range-reading the first 256 KB of the deposited
`.raw` files (utf-16-le) returns the literal string `Orbitrap Velos Pro` in all six files sampled,
spanning all four experiments and all three acquisition dates. Annotated as
**`Orbitrap Velos Pro` MS:1003096** — an exact label match to the header string. These are distinct
instruments, not synonyms; PRIDE's submitter-entered value is simply wrong, exactly as the brief warns.

**Full text was reachable despite `is_open_access: false`.** PRIDE, the Europe PMC `isOpenAccess`
field, the NCBI OA service ("identifier PMC4974340 is not Open Access"), `fullTextXML` (404),
Unpaywall's PDF (403), and ScienceDirect (403) all failed or said no. The article is nonetheless
`cc by` and the PMC HTML rendered fine — full Methods and Results were read from it. Had I trusted
`is_open_access`, the entire channel map, the instrument correction, and the four-way experiment
split would have been lost.

**Acetyl (Protein N-term) is in the SDRF although the paper omits it.** The Methods list only
"cysteine carbamidomethylation and methionine oxidation … as fixed and variable modification". All
four deposited `mqpar.xml` / `parameters.txt` files agree that the actual search also carried
`Acetyl (Protein N-term)` as variable, and included it in protein quantification. The deposited
search configuration outranks the prose summary. Annotated `NT=Acetyl;AC=UNIMOD:1;PP=Protein N-term`
— **UNIMOD:1 = Acetyl** (not the UNIMOD:21/Phospho swap), and the positional value is in `PP=`, not `TA=`.

**Tolerances.** Precursor **6 ppm** = MaxQuant `mainSearchTol` (the recalibrated main search), not
the 20 ppm `firstSearchTol` — consistent across all four zips and with the PXD023366 precedent.
Fragment **0.5 Da**, not 20 ppm: fragmentation was CID "performed in the LTQ", so fragments are read
out in the ion trap, and the relevant parameter is `MS/MS tol. (ITMS) = 0.5 Da`. Taking the FTMS
value (20 ppm) here would have been a silent error inherited from the sibling annotation.

**Cell type is a developmental claim.** GV = prophase-I arrest, meiosis I incomplete → `primary
oocyte` CL:0000654. MII = meiosis I complete, arrested at metaphase II → `secondary oocyte`
CL:0000655. The Collection section notes immature oocytes were collected as "GV **or MI**", but
Results state the analyzed immature pools were "100 immature (GV stage)" and every filename says GV,
so GV is annotated.

**IVF vs hanging drop, assigned by elimination and confirmed twice.** `100oocytemature-immature.zip`
groups the 6 bulk runs into experiments `130403_oocytes_{im,}mature` (1 run each) and
`130724_oocytes_{im,}mature` (2 runs each). The paper says the IVF-cultured cells gave one run per
state (~1,100 proteins) while the hanging-drop cells were "analyzed in technical replicate LC-MSMS
runs" (>2,100 proteins). Only the 130724 batch has replicate pairs → 130724 = hanging drop. This is
independently confirmed because the secretome runs share the 130724 date prefix and secretome
analysis is only possible in the hanging drop system. Chronology agrees (IVF first, April 2013).

**The medium-only control is kept, not dropped.** It contains no human material, so
`characteristics[organism]` = `not applicable` — legal here (`allow_not_applicable: true`,
`allow_not_available: false`), so the brief's exclusion escape hatch does not apply and the row stays.
`sample type` = `negative control` (PRIDE:0000905) is exact: the paper uses it precisely to subtract
background ("338 … were not present in the medium prepared in the absence of oocytes").

**Secretome rows annotate the source cell type.** The analyte is cell-free conditioned medium, so
`cells per well` and `single cell isolation protocol` are `not applicable`, but `cell type` records
the oocytes that secreted it. This keeps the mature-vs-immature contrast — a real arm of the study —
visible in `factor value[cell type]`.

**Reduction/alkylation are split, and that split is a finding.** DTT (PRIDE:0000608) and IAA
(PRIDE:0000599) are explicit in the SP3 protocol, so the 6 single-cell and 6 dimethyl rows carry them.
The 100-oocyte prep is described as Rapigest lysis → TCA precipitation → digestion, with **no
reduction or alkylation step stated**, so those rows are `not available` — even though the search set
Carbamidomethyl (C) as a *fixed* modification for them too. Either the paper omits a step or that
fixed modification is questionable for the bulk runs. Recorded rather than smoothed over.

## Values deliberately left as reserved words

| column | value | why |
|---|---|---|
| `characteristics[age]` | `not available` (all rows) | No donor age appears anywhere in the paper. `allow_not_applicable: false` for `age`, so even the medium-only control cannot say `not applicable` — `not available` is spec-forced there. |
| `characteristics[disease]` | `not available` (oocyte rows) | Donors are IVF patients but no diagnosis is ever stated. The tempting fabrication is `female infertility`; the sibling's reviewer confirmed abstention is correct. `not applicable` for the medium. |
| `characteristics[ancestry category]` | `not available` | Not stated. Deliberately **not** inferred from the authors' Ljubljana affiliation. (Included as a column because it is `recommended`, and its absence was a reviewer finding on PXD023366.) |
| `characteristics[individual]` | `pooled` / `not available` | Pools draw on many IVF patients → `pooled` (`allow_pooled: true`). For the 6 single oocytes the donor is genuinely never stated → `not available`. |
| `characteristics[sample type]` | `not applicable` (secretome) | See below — the term the spec advertises does not exist. |

`characteristics[sex] = female` is definitional for oocyte donors, not an inference.
`characteristics[single cell isolation protocol] = manual picking` is **stated**, not inferred here:
oocytes were transferred "from one droplet of PBS to another by a denudation pipette … and at the end
transferred into a small microcentrifuge tube". Bulk rows use `not applicable` — no single-cell
isolation was performed on a pool.

## Spec defects found

**`study sample` does not exist.** `TERMS.tsv` advertises `values: PRIDE (children of PRIDE:0000895):
study sample, single cell, …` and `sample-metadata.yaml` repeats `study sample` as its first example.
Enumerating `hierarchicalDescendants` of PRIDE:0000895 in OLS returns 18 terms and **`study sample`
is not among them**. This is why the two secretome rows are `not applicable`: they are ordinary study
samples in a non-SCP arm, the ontology has no term for that, and forcing `bulk control` onto a
cell-free medium sample would be worse than abstaining. This matches CLAUDE.md's warning that the
`values` field is prose, not a closed grammar.

**Lys-C cannot be expressed.** The digest used "a mixture of trypsin and rLysC", but
`comment[cleavage agent details]` is `cardinality: SINGLE` in `ms-proteomics v1.1.0`. Annotated
`NT=Trypsin;AC=MS:1001251` — the primary protease, matching MaxQuant's `Trypsin/P` specificity and
the sibling. The rLysC co-digestion is unrepresentable and is recorded only here.

guarantee here is that every accession in the file was searched live in OLS and none was recalled
from memory.

## For the reviewer — where to push hardest

1. `sample type = bulk control` on the six 100-cell rows: defensible but interpretive (they are the
   discovery proteome, not a benchmark). `cells per well = 100` carries the fact unambiguously
   regardless of how this resolves.
2. `sample type = not applicable` on the two secretome rows — driven by the missing `study sample`
   term. `pooled` (PRIDE:0000902) was considered and rejected because its definition demands "used as
   a reference or normalization standard".
3. `cells per well = not applicable` rather than `0` on the medium control.
4. `characteristics[cell type]` on the secretome rows names the secreting cell, not the analyte.
5. `biological replicate` for the single oocytes numbers GV3/4/5 as 1/2/3 within each maturation
   group; donor identity is unknown, so independence is assumed, not established.

**Design note (recorded, not a defect):** the 100-cell arm has no biological replication at all —
the paper says outright that "the scarcity of samples did not permit us to run biological replicates
of 100 cells". Only the dimethyl arm (n=3) is biologically replicated. The artifact records this
faithfully.
