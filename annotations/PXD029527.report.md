### Channel map
**Owner decision of 2026-11-10.** No written source names the labels. The paper and PRIDE say only that there was a "label swap between replicate experiments".

The map rests on the deposited Ratio H/M normalized of FBL (P22087) itself:

| | rep1 | rep1_10p | rep2 | rep2_10p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FBL H/M | 9.25 | 7.95 | 0.151 | 0.243 |

So in mixture 1 heavy = shCtrl, and in mixture 2 heavy = shFBL. Two further checks agree:
- **Table S4 top hits** have the same orientation:

  | protein | log2FC shFBL vs shCtrl (Table S4) | H/M rep1 | H/M rep2 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | LGR4 | −3.98 | 23.9 | 0.080 |
  | MNDA | −3.83 | 35.7 | 0.173 |
  | CTSG | −3.55 | 18.0 | 0.136 |

- **Correlation:** log2 H/M of rep1 vs rep2 gives Pearson r = −0.72 (n = 5,819). This matches the paper's Fig. S6A, r = 0.73 after inversion.

### "10p" injections
`20181217_QE1_DF_Feng_int_rep{1,2}_10p.raw` are separate MaxQuant experiments: `shFBL_int_rep1_10p` and `_rep2_10p`. Each is a single unfractionated file, acquired the day before the fractions with the same method. Their label pattern places each in its parent mixture:
- On markers that are heavy-high in rep1, `rep1_10p` has 389 heavy vs 6 medium PSMs, and `rep2_10p` has 11 heavy vs 372 medium.
- FBL PSMs are heavy in `rep1_10p` and medium in `rep2_10p`.
- log2 H/M correlates with the parent mixture: r = 0.59 for rep1 and r = 0.82 for rep2.

They are therefore encoded as technical replicate 2 of the same sources, with fraction 1 and `comment[fractionation method]` = `not applicable`. The deposit does not define "10p". I read it as a ~10 % aliquot injection.

### Renamed raw files
The raw headers show that three files were acquired under other names:
- `shFBL_int_rep2_fr1.raw` and `shFBL_int_rep2_fr2.raw` were acquired as `20181218_QE1_DF_Feng_int_rep1_fr1/fr2.raw`.
- `shFBL_int_rep1_fr2.raw` was acquired as `..._rep1_fr2_20181220080509.raw`, which only adds a re-acquisition timestamp.

The label pattern decides the first two. On rep1-heavy-high markers, `shFBL_int_rep2_fr1` has 177 medium vs 7 heavy PSMs and `fr2` has 257 vs 9, the same pattern as every rep2 fraction. Every rep1 fraction is heavy-dominant. So the deposited names (rep2) are correct and the acquisition names were the error. I kept the deposited names, which are also the names MaxQuant searched.

**This departs from the coordinator's instruction** to map these files by their internal names: doing so would contradict the data.
