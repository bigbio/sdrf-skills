## Study
"A Dynamic rRNA Ribomethylome Drives Stemness in Acute Myeloid Leukemia" — Zhou F et al., *Cancer Discov* 2023;13(2):332-347, doi:10.1158/2159-8290.CD-22-0210, PMID 36259929, PMC9900322. The proteomics is nascent-chain proteomics: AHA click enrichment combined with pulsed SILAC (intermediate 2H4-Lys/13C6-Arg vs heavy 13C6 15N2-Lys/13C6 15N4-Arg; the light channel is unused). ## Why blocked (rules 6–7)
- **No source states which condition carried which label in any mixture.** The PRIDE protocol, the paper's Methods, the Supplementary Figure legends and the Fig. S6 image, and Tables S1–S4 were all checked. They state only that a label swap was done between replicates.

  - FBL H/M is 9.25 in rep1 and 0.15 in rep2.
  - The top hits in Table S4 agree.
  - The rep1 vs rep2 log2 H/M correlation is r = −0.72.
- **SNORD127 arm: two things are undocumented and the data does not settle either.**
  - The channel map is not stated.
  - It is not stated which raw files `snoRNA_1..4` belong to which MaxQuant experiment (C1_rep1, C1_rep2, C34_rep1, C34_rep2).
- Europe PMC was unavailable (HTTP 503) for the whole session. As a result, the search for a dataset-descriptor paper could not be run there.
