## Study and scope

This is histone-PTM super-SILAC MS from the TRADITIOM in-vitro dormancy model. MCF7 and T47D cells are grown for months under endocrine therapy without passaging. Each histone sample (light) is mixed 1:1 with a heavy-labelled super-SILAC histone internal standard. The data were quantified with EpiProfile 2.0 (the `pr_pic` variant), not with a database search.

- **Deposited:** 43 `.raw` files, `Sample_legend.txt` and `Magnani.zip` (6.7 GB of EpiProfile outputs).
- **Annotated:** all 43 raw files × 2 SILAC channels = 86 rows. Nothing was excluded.
- **Re-runs:** files 11, 13, 14, 16, 17, 18, 38 and 43 exist only as HF221020 re-runs. The HF220914 originals are not deposited as RAW; only their EpiProfile intermediates are in the zip. Table S8 marks these samples "bis", so they are the files used.

**Templates and why:**
- ms-proteomics: the data are MS.
- human: all samples are human.
- cell-lines: the samples are MCF-7 and T-47D.
- clinical-metadata was **not** declared. No sample comes from a patient (rule 15). The paper's patient cohorts are not in this deposit.

The heavy channel of every run is `super-SILAC heavy histone internal standard, aliquot spiked into sample n`, with these values:

- pooled sample = `pooled`
- biological replicate = `pooled`
- age and sex = `pooled`

On the heavy rows, `factor value[cell line]` mirrors `super-SILAC mix`, and treatment, adaptation phase and sampling time are `not applicable`. The rows are marked as the spiked-in reference, not study samples, by sample type `reference` and pooled sample `pooled`.

**Factor values: cell line, treatment, adaptation phase and sampling time.**
- Cell line is a compared variable: Table S8 has separate MCF7 and T47D sheets, and Fig. 6A has separate heat maps. Without the factor, MCF7 and T47D samples of the same treatment, phase and time would pool into one condition (review R2-F1).
- `adaptation phase` is a custom characteristics column (spec "Extending Templates"). Without it, awakening and TEP samples would collapse into one group.
- POT is the pretreatment baseline, headed "Time zero" in Table S8 (sampling time `0 day`). It is not the paper's separate untreated (UT) arm, which was serially passaged in parallel for 170–180 days and is not in this deposit.

## Key decisions and evidence

1. **File → sample map** (stated, then cross-validated).
   - The deposited `Sample_legend.txt` numbers samples 1–43, and that number is the raw file's suffix.
   - Supplementary Table S8 repeats the numbers under each sample name.
   - Check: for each S8 column, S8(mark)/S8(unmodified) must equal the (LightR/HeavyR) ratio of that mark over the unmodified form in EpiProfile `histone_ratios_SILAC.xls`, for the file with that suffix.
     - H3K9me2 and H3K4me1 match exactly for 33/33 MCF7 and 7/7 T47D samples.
     - A map shifted by one position gives 0–3 matches.
   - The T47D files (34–43) also cluster apart in the H3K4 profile.
2. **Multiplexing (rule 14).** EpiProfile ran with "histone with SILAC" and reports LightR/HeavyR for every file. The paper says: light = sample, heavy = internal standard.
3. **Heavy label = Arg10 only** (`Label:13C(6)15N(4)`, UNIMOD:267, TA=R, MT=Variable per rule 11).
   - The paper does not state the label amino acids.
   - Measured on co-eluting MS2 precursors in an `.ms2` file from the zip: the mass difference 10.01 Da occurs 267 times, while 18.03 Da (Lys8+Arg10) occurs 27 times, which is background level.
   - This matches the Bonaldi-lab histone super-SILAC precedent PXD013288.
4. **Modifications** come from the EpiProfile run and the paper's chemistry.
   - EpiProfile variant `EpiProfile2.0_pr_pic`, which quantifies K ac/me1/me2/me3.
   - Paper: propionic anhydride, then phenyl isocyanate N-terminal derivatisation.
   - Encoded as: Phenylisocyanate (UNIMOD:411, Any N-term, Fixed); Propionyl K, Acetyl K, Methyl K, Dimethyl K, Trimethyl K (Variable); plus the Arg10 label.
5. **Cleavage agent** is Trypsin (rule 2), the digestion enzyme. Because lysines are propionylated, cleavage is effectively Arg-C-like.
6. **Instrument and method** were read from range-read headers of 6 raw files covering both batches:
   - Q Exactive HF, DDA top-10, HCD at NCE 28, Orbitrap MS2, MS1 300–1350 m/z.
   - PRIDE and the paper agree.
7. **Cell-line facts** (Cellosaurus CVCL_0031 / CVCL_0553):
   - Disease: NCIt C4194, which MONDO:0004953 "invasive ductal breast carcinoma" declares as equivalentTo.
   - Organism part: breast. Sampling site: pleural effusion.
   - Age 69Y / 54Y, female, European ancestry (Cellosaurus "Caucasian"), adult.
   - The paper's "p53-mutant L194F T47D" matches Cellosaurus T-47D's TP53 p.Leu194Phe.
   - Culture medium: DMEM (NCIT:C185409).
8. **Treatments:**
   - TAM = 100 nM 4-hydroxytamoxifen, encoded as NCIT:C975 "Afimoxifene".
   - AI = estrogen deprivation (phenol red-free DMEM with charcoal-stripped FCS). No drug is added, and no ontology term exists, so the paper's term is used as free text.
   - POT = `untreated` (rule 4).
9. **Biological replicates** restart per factor group (cell line, treatment, phase, time) and are unique per flask within it (rule 3). Carbon copies are numbered in Greek or alphabetical order, e.g. MCF7 T TEP alpha–zeta → 1–6. For T-47D: POT, AI 30 day and AI awakening → 1–2 each; AI TEP A/B/D/F → 1–4.

## `not available` / `not applicable` values and why

- **Light rows:**
  - `cell type`, `passage number`, `sample storage temperature`: not stated.
  - `sampling time` for awakening and TEP: the harvest day differs per carbon copy and is only plotted (Fig. 2A).
- **Heavy rows:** the components of the super-SILAC mix are not named anywhere: not in the paper, not in ref. 60 (Cuomo 2011, a pairwise SILAC study), and not in the deposit. So disease, ancestry, organism part, developmental stage and Cellosaurus accession and name are `not available`. Age and sex are `pooled`. Treatment, phase and time are `not applicable`.
- **Precursor and fragment mass tolerance:** `not available`. EpiProfile is not a database search, and no tolerance is recorded. Because of this, `convert-openms` fails (kept per `references/validation.md`).

## Conflicts and inferred vs stated

- **POT vs T0 vs UT:** Table S8 and Fig. 6A label the POT columns "Time zero", so sampling time is `0 day`. The Methods also use T0 for samples collected 48 h after seeding, and they describe a separate untreated (UT) arm that is not deposited. I followed the deposited legend: POT, phase `pretreatment`, treatment `untreated`; the report and manifest state that this is not the UT arm.
- **Missing from Table S8:** samples 38, 39 and 43 are deposited and were quantified by EpiProfile, but are absent from Table S8. They are annotated from the legend.
- **PRIDE vs paper:** they agree on the instrument, organism and SILAC. PRIDE's PTM list (acetyl, monomethyl) is incomplete; the EpiProfile output also has me2 and me3.
- **Inferred, not stated:**
  - the heavy label is Arg10 only (measured from the data);
  - the heavy mix is human;
  - organism part / disease / ancestry mappings from Cellosaurus;
  - the replicate numbering order.

## Judgement calls a reviewer should attack

1. **Heavy-channel encoding.**
   - `characteristics[cell line]` = `super-SILAC mix` (free text; precedent PXD002098) was kept rather than `pooled` (review F3 found either acceptable).
     - Reason: TERMS.tsv sets `allow_pooled = false` for cell line. No validator enforces that, so my earlier wording that the column "forbids pooled" was wrong about the validators; the validation log is corrected.
     - The free text also names what the reference is.
   - Organism `Homo sapiens` is asserted although the mix components are unstated.
   - One source name per aliquot rather than one shared reference source.
   - `factor value[cell line]` = `super-SILAC mix` on the heavy rows (the mirror the review asked for); the reference status is carried by sample type and pooled sample.
2. **Modification parameters for an EpiProfile (non-search) analysis.**
   - Propionyl is Variable rather than Fixed; PIC is Fixed.
   - The Arg10-only label rests on a precursor mass-difference analysis of one `.ms2` file.
3. **Study design columns.**
   - The custom `characteristics[adaptation phase]` plus factor value.
   - Awakening and TEP `sampling time` = `not available`.
   - POT encoded as `untreated` / `pretreatment` / `0 day` (Table S8 "Time zero"); it is not the paper's UT arm.
   - "estrogen deprivation" as free-text treatment, and the disease mapped to "invasive ductal breast carcinoma" via the NCIt C4194 equivalence.
