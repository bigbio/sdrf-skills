## Open items / limitations
1. The TP msf factor table was read by the reviewer's range-read script. I re-tallied the extract but did not re-read the 49.7 GB file. The pY runs take their map from the TP study through the same-mix protocol statement, because the pY study has no factors.
2. TP and pY replicates are paired by the TP `Replicates` factor (Rep1–3 = RP01–03) and the pY sample names (Rep1–3). The pairing cannot change any label, because the orientation is the same in every replicate.
3. CVCL_LC92 and CVCL_LC96 assume the Horizon SW48 knock-ins. They are the only SW48 KRAS G12D/+ and G13D/+ entries in Cellosaurus, but the supplier could not be confirmed from the paper.
4. Reduction and alkylation reagents are not stated, so those columns are omitted.
5. The search database (RefSeq84 in the PD headers vs v73 in the PRIDE text) and the missed cleavages (2 vs 1) disagree with the PRIDE text. The SDRF follows the deposited search.
6. reconcile reports `organism_contradicted`. This is a false positive: the only species word is "rabbit monoclonal antibody", a reagent. It is refuted in validation.txt.
