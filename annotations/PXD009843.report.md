## Read first: how the channel map was found
No reachable source has a sentence saying which SILAC state holds which cell line. The paper is closed access: there is no PMCID, Unpaywall reports it closed, and pubs.acs.org returns 403, which was not bypassed. The PRIDE protocols only list the five label amino acids. The map below comes from the **deposited search results**, and three independent lines agree:

1. **Mutant KRAS peptides in the total-proteome search.** The TP search database was `human.RefSeq84_with KRAS G12D and G13D.fasta`.
   - G12D `LVVVGADGVGK` appears only with Lys4 (medium): 4 PSMs across rep1, rep2 and rep3.
   - G13D `LVVVGAGDVGK` appears with Lys8 (heavy) in rep1 and rep3. One rep3 PSM is light, with lower confidence, 54 scans after a heavy one; it is the single discordant hit.
   - No G13D peptide was seen with medium, and no G12D peptide with heavy.
2. **The pY Proteome Discoverer study.** In the msf AnalysisDefinition, the Light sample of Rep1, Rep2 and Rep3 is `SampleType="Control"`. The PRIDE data protocol computes "G12D/Parental and G13D/Parental ratios", so Light is the parental line.
3. **A marker check in the pY data.** MPZL1 pY263 (`SESVVpYADIR`) gives log2 M/L of +1.41, +1.30 and +1.29, and log2 H/L of −0.52, −0.68 and −0.61. File medians are about −0.53 for both ratios. The abstract says MPZL1 is "hyperphosphorylated in G12D cells", which fits Medium = G12D.

This is genotype-identity evidence: it shows which allele each channel carries. It is not a ratio-direction inference. Even so, if batch rule 6 is read strictly (an explicit label statement is required), the reviewer may decide to block the runs. That possibility is recorded in the manifest `limitations`.

## Study
Tahir R, Renuse S, Udainiya S, et al., Pandey A. *Mutation-Specific and Common Phosphotyrosine Signatures of KRAS G12D and G13D Alleles.* J Proteome Res 2021;20(1):670-683, doi:10.1021/acs.jproteome.0c00587, PMID 32986951.

- **Design:** triple-SILAC of isogenic SW48 colorectal cancer cells: parental, KRAS G12D/+ knock-in and KRAS G13D/+ knock-in. There are three replicate mixtures.
- **Phosphotyrosine runs:** an anti-pY peptide IP (P-Tyr-1000) gives one 150-min run per replicate.
- **Total proteome:** a 600 µg aliquot of the same mix, bRPLC-fractionated into 24 fractions per replicate.
- **Acquisition and search:** Orbitrap Fusion Lumos, HCD; Proteome Discoverer 2.2 with Sequest HT and Percolator. pY quantification used PyQuant.

## Open items / limitations
1. The channel map comes from deposited search data, not from a textual statement (see above). For the pY runs, medium vs heavy rests on the PD Control designation plus the MPZL1 check only; the pY runs contain no KRAS peptide.
2. The TP RP0N ↔ pY RepN replicate pairing is assumed from the run names.
3. CVCL_LC92 and CVCL_LC96 assume the Horizon SW48 knock-ins. They are the only SW48 KRAS G12D/+ and G13D/+ entries in Cellosaurus, but the supplier could not be confirmed from the paper.
4. Reduction and alkylation reagents are not stated, so those columns are omitted.
5. The search database (RefSeq84 in the PD headers vs v73 in the PRIDE text) and the missed cleavages (2 vs 1) disagree with the PRIDE text. The SDRF follows the deposited search.
6. reconcile reports `organism_contradicted`. This is a false positive: the only species word is "rabbit monoclonal antibody", a reagent. It is refuted in validation.txt.
