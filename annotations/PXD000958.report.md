# PXD000958: SDRF annotation report

**Status: BLOCKED. No SDRF was built.** The dataset is genuine triplex SILAC: 30 Orbitrap Elite runs, from 3 biological replicates (Set1, Set2, Set3) × 10 gel-band fractions. It was searched with MaxQuant 1.4.1.2 at Multiplicity 3 (light / Arg6;Lys4 / Arg10;Lys8). No accessible source names the three conditions or says which SILAC channel carries each one, and the cell line is not stated either. Campaign rules 6–7 therefore block all 30 runs. See `annotations/BLOCKED.md`, section "PXD000958".

## Study
PRIDE: "Silencing RNA-binding protein CELF1 inhibits tumor growth and alters gene expression in oral squamous cell carcinoma". It was submitted on 2014-05-08 by V. Palanisamy (MUSC) and made public on 2022-02-28. The PRIDE record has no publication linked. The closest paper is House RP, Talwar S, Hazard ES, Hill EG, Palanisamy V, *Oncotarget* 2015;6(41):43620-43634, doi:10.18632/oncotarget.6204, PMC4791255. It comes from the same lab and has nearly the same title, but it reports no proteomics.

## Evidence examined
- **PRIDE v3 project record and file list** (31 files: 30 RAW and `MaxQuantOutput.zip`). All of them carry `projectAccessions` = PXD000958.
  - Sample protocol: "Cells labeld with Light, medium, and Heavy Lys/ARg isotopes were lysed and combined 1:1:1. Three biological replicates were processed. Samples were then separated on a 1D gel."
  - The data protocol gives MaxQuant 1.4.1.2, the Human IPI v3.72 database, Carbamidomethyl (C) as a fixed modification and Oxidation (M) as a variable one.
  - The record does not name any condition or any cell line.
- **MaxQuant summary.txt**, downloaded and then deleted; sha256 values are in `scratchpad/b11-PXD000958/downloads.sha256`.
  - All 30 raws: Multiplicity 3, Labels1 `Arg6;Lys4`, Labels2 `Arg10;Lys8`, Trypsin/P, Oxidation (M), no multi modifications.
  - Experiments are named only `SILAC1`, `SILAC2` and `SILAC3`. This confirms rule 14.
- **Oncotarget 2015 full text and all 8 supplements** (PMC OA S3). They contain no SILAC, MS or proteomics. Only RNA-seq (GSE57254), qRT-PCR, splicing, RNP-IP and xenograft work are reported.
- **RNA Biol 2013 paper** (PMC3594286, same lab). It has no proteomics.
- **Europe PMC searches** for a proteomics paper or a dataset descriptor found none: `PXD000958`, `CELF1/CUGBP1 AND SILAC`, `"1322 proteins"`, and author searches for Palanisamy, Talwar and House.
- **Raw headers** (byte-range reads of 2 raws). They contain only the file path, the method `120mingrad_FT_onlyCID_TOP20.meth` and the instrument `Orbitrap Elite ETD`. There is no sample name or comment.
- **Data cross-check** (proteinGroups table, deleted after use).
  - CELF1 (Q92879) was not identified, so I could not check which channel is the knockdown.
  - ELAVL1 is strongly lower in the heavy channel (H/L normalized 0.06–0.14).
  - Heavy sits about 3–6-fold below light in every experiment, even after normalization (`scratchpad/b11-PXD000958/ratio_medians.txt`). This does not fit a 1:1:1 mix and is a data-quality flag.
  - Rule 6 forbids building a channel map from the data alone.
- **Duplicate / already annotated check:** PXD000958 is in neither `annotations/` nor `sdrf-annotated-datasets/datasets/`.

## What would unblock it
An author statement naming the three conditions, their SILAC labels and the cell line. With that, the SDRF would have 90 rows (30 runs × 3 channels) and use the human + cell-lines templates. Fractions would be numbered 1–10 within each Set, and biological replicates 1–3 (Set1–3) within each condition.

## Validation
I ran no validation, because no SDRF exists. No `.sdrf.tsv`, `.evidence.json` or `.validation.txt` was written, following the precedent of the fully blocked PXD002454 and PXD035841.
