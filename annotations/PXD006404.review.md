## Important

1. **Deamidation (NQ) is missing from `comment[modification parameters]`, and the manifest wrongly says it was not searched.**
   - In summary.txt, the column `Multi modifications` reads `Deamidation (NQ)` for all 36 SILAC raws (18 ALK-IP, 18 ALK-Phospho).
   - parameters.txt lists `Deamidation (NQ)Sites.txt` under Site tables.
   - modificationSpecificPeptides.txt has quantified deamidated peptides in ALK-IP-E1/E2 and ALK-Phospho-E1/E3: 472–566 singly deamidated peptides per experiment, plus many combined with Phospho or Oxidation.
   - Batch rule 5 says the modification columns must describe the search each file actually went through.
   - **Fix:** add a 10th `comment[modification parameters]` column with `NT=Deamidated;AC=UNIMOD:7;TA=N,Q;MT=Variable` on all 108 rows. UNIMOD:7 is labelled "Deamidated" in OLS. Rebuild from the build tables and correct the manifest claim.
   - Methyl (KR) is not affected. Methyl (KR)Sites.txt exists, but no Methyl-modified peptide appears for the SILAC experiments in modificationSpecificPeptides.txt, so leaving Methyl out is correct.
   - Tool gap: `sdrf-tools search-params` does not read the `Multi modifications` column.

## Minor

- The DMSO vehicle is written as `Dimethyl Sulfoxide`, not `untreated` as rule 4 asks. The cells did get 0.1 % DMSO, so `untreated` would be false. Keep the value and note the deviation in the manifest.
- Doses (crizotinib 500 nM, TAE684 100 nM, LDK378 250 nM, 0.1 % DMSO) and the 30 min treatment time are recorded only in the manifest. A custom characteristics column for them is optional.

## What I checked and confirmed

- **Channel map:** the PRIDE projectDescription gives the map for Experiments 1–3 word for word as annotated.
- **IP-arm data check (new; the producer checked only the phospho arm):** I read the log2 ratios in proteinGroups.txt.
  - GRB2, PTPN11, IRS2 and SHC1 are enriched in heavy over both light and medium (H/L and H/M about 2.2–3.9) in E1–E3, while the ALK bait stays flat. So heavy = DMSO in the IP arm too.
  - The IRS2 medium/light ratios give one consistent ordering of the three inhibitors (TAE684 < crizotinib < LDK378), which is what the stated map predicts.
- **Files:** of the 176 deposited raws, the 36 SILAC raws match the SDRF exactly. The 140 excluded raws (88 + 24 PeptidePulldown, 28 NB1proteome) all have Multiplicity 1 and are listed in BLOCKED.md.
- **Instruments:** the raw headers I re-read match the instrument column. Q Exactive for the Jan 2014 QE5 and Mar 2014 QE3 runs; Q Exactive Plus for the Aug 2014 QE5 runs.
- **Fractions and technical replicates:** summary.txt fraction numbers 201–206 are the six IP gel slices, with IgG as slice 206. Numbers 301–303 are the three phospho enrichments (pTyr IP, then two TiO2 rounds). Technical replicates 1 and 2 are the two injection series, and each file carries a single technical replicate.
- **Biological replicates:** unique per experiment within each treatment group. Using the same numbers in both arms fits the paper's "three triple-SILAC experiments" and "pooled lysates from each triple-SILAC experiment".
- **Cell line and sample metadata:** Cellosaurus CVCL_1440 confirms sex, age, population, site and disease. The medium, reducing/alkylating reagents and enzymes match the paper's Methods per arm.
- **Ontology terms:** every term resolves in OLS4 and none is obsolete. That covers NCIT C74061, C171615, C115112, C437 and C178973, plus the MS, PRIDE, UNIMOD, MONDO, UBERON, HANCESTRO, HsapDv and EFO terms.
