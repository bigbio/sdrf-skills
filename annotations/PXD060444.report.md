### MS1 SILAC-ratio cross-check
- **Method:** converted the MS1 of four runs with ThermoRawFileParser (bioconda) and measured isotope-confirmed, co-eluting L/M/H triplets for proteinGroups peptides. Compared the results with each experiment's normalized ratios.
  - Scripts: `scripts/silac_ratio2.py` and `scripts/assign.py`.
  - Outputs: `ratio/*.tsv` and `assign*.txt` in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user/a98b4c07-45f2-5d08-aa74-0c95b1e5e538/scratchpad/b12-PXD060444/`.
- **With 388–593 proteins per run:** measured ratios fit both candidate experiments equally well. Median absolute deviation was 0.10–0.14 log2 either way, because most protein ratios hardly differ between the two mixtures.
- **Restricted to proteins whose ratios differ by > 0.5 log2 between the mixtures (n = 6–17 per run):**
  - D06 tracks `0-3-6 nM` (MAD 0.10–0.17 vs 0.68–0.77).
  - T06 tracks `0-4-8 h` on M/L (0.11 vs 0.53).
  - D18 and T18 are internally contradictory: D18 M/L favours `0-18-54 nM` but its H/L favours `0-3-6 nM`, and the difference-test correlations are negative for both D runs.
- **Conclusion:** this is weak support for the obvious reading (first numbered block = first-listed mixture), but it is not a documented map. Per rules 6–8 the channel→condition map was not asserted.
