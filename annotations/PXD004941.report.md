## Design

- **It is SILAC, multiplicity 2.** `mqpar.xml` gives `labelMods ['', 'Lys6']` and `multiplicity 2`. Both
  searches have Ratio H/L columns.
- **Channel map:**
  - **SILAC light:** PC9/GR (gefitinib-resistant), cultured with 3 µM gefitinib.
  - **SILAC heavy:** PC9 parental (gefitinib-sensitive), no drug (`untreated`).
  - **Sources:** the PRIDE sample protocol, paper §2.1, the Fig. 1 legend and the supplementary
    legend of Table S1.
  - **Data check against the deposited ratios:**
    - Proteome normalized H/L: ALDH1A1 0.11 (paper: up in PC9/GR); HMGA2 7.6 and CHEK2 7.3 (down);
      ALOX5 and NUCB2 up; EPHA2 and CNNM3 down.
    - 401 proteins have H/L ≤ 0.5 and 148 have H/L ≥ 2, against the paper's 404 up / 148 down.
    - Ubiquitylome sites agree in direction for ALOX5, NUCB2, EPHA2, CNNM3 and ABCA12.
- **Replicates:** one culture per condition and one SILAC mixture, so biological replicate = 1 in each
  factor group. There is no label swap.
- **Proteome:** 18 fractions (high-pH RP).
- **Ubiquitylome:** 12 fractions (`mqpar.xml` fractions 1–12), anti-diGly enrichment.
- **Factor values:**
  - `cell line` = PC9/GR vs PC-9.
  - `treatment` = Gefitinib vs untreated.
  - The two are confounded by design, and the paper says so.

## Templates

ms-proteomics v1.2.0, human v1.1.0, cell-lines v1.1.0. sdrf version v1.1.0.

## Key values and their sources

- **Instrument:** Q Exactive Plus, from the RAW headers. PRIDE's "LTQ Orbitrap" is wrong.
- **Acquisition:** HCD, Orbitrap MS2, MS1 350–1800 m/z.
- **NCE:** 28 for the proteome and 30 for the Kub runs (RAW headers).
- **Search modifications:**
  - **Ubiquitylome:** from `mqpar.xml`: Carbamidomethyl C (fixed); Acetyl protein N-term, GlyGly K,
    Oxidation M and Lys6 (variable). Tolerances 5 ppm / 0.02 Da.
  - **Proteome:** from `parameters.txt`: Carbamidomethyl C (fixed); Acetyl N-term and Oxidation M
    (variable). Lys6 comes from the paper. GlyGly was not searched (there is no site table).
    Fragment tolerance 0.02 Da; precursor tolerance `not available`.
- **Cell line:** PC-9 = CVCL_B260. Donor values come from Cellosaurus: 45Y, male, East Asian ancestry,
  adult, lung adenocarcinoma, organism part lung, sampling site lymph node.
  - PC9/GR has no Cellosaurus entry. CVCL_E7RA "PC-9/GR" is a different group's line, so the accession
    and name are `not available`.

## Open items (unattended)

1. **Proteome precursor tolerance is `not available`.**
   - No `mqpar.xml` or `summary.txt` was deposited for the proteome search.
   - The paper's 10 ppm conflicts with the 5 ppm in the deposited ubiquitylome `mqpar.xml`.
   - As a result, `convert-openms` exits 1 for the proteome file.
2. **Fraction numbers differ from the paper.**
   - The paper says the peptides were combined into 8 fractions, but 18 and 12 RAW files were deposited.
   - The proteome fraction numbers follow the file numbering, because no design file was deposited.
3. **The proteome SILAC label comes from the paper,** not from a deposited `labelMods`.
4. **`not available`:** cell type, passage number, and the Cellosaurus accession and name for PC9/GR.
5. **Validator warnings (justified):**
   - `untreated` is not in NCIT/EFO (template-prescribed value).
   - `enrichment of ubiquitinated protein` has no OLS term (follows precedent).
   - `PC9/GR` is not in CLO/BTO/EFO.
6. **`sdrf-tools check` exits 1.**
   - `PC9/GR` is expected: it has no ontology term.
   - `GG` is a false positive: OLS4 shows that UNIMOD:121 has the label "GG" and is not obsolete.
   - The refutation is recorded in `PXD004941.validation.txt`.
