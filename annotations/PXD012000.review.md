## Scope (partial annotation)
- The deposit holds 689 RAW files plus `combined_NAT.zip`. The SDRF annotates 680 RAW files: 113 samples × (SILAC light + heavy) = 1360 rows.
- The 9 unannotated RAW files are `20160706_ASK_CS_32D1–7` (experiment 32D, searched) and `27C6/27C7` (experiment 27C, not searched). `annotations/BLOCKED.md` and the manifest limitations state this and justify it. EV1 and EV2 define only sample types A, B and C, and EV1 lists patient 32 as 32A, 32B and 32C. No source assigns 27C to 27aC or to 27bC.

## What was verified independently
- **Clinical values:** every one of the 113 light samples was compared by script against Dataset EV1, which is a per-patient table, so the values are individual-level. Fields compared: disease, age, ST-code individual, treatment status, pre- and post-treatment tumour grade, Miller & Payne score, relapse (7 patients), and subtype/RFS/OS in clinical data. **0 mismatches.** Sex is `female` from the Methods statement that covers every individual. Stage II/III is a cohort range, so it was correctly not broadcast to every patient.
- **Super-SILAC heavy channels:** annotated as `reference`, `pooled`, `cell line`, with every factor value `not applicable`. They are not study samples. This matches the precedent PXD002882. Label direction was checked on EV2: median log2 H/L for COL1A1, HBB, DCN and LUM is between −6.1 and −6.5, so light is the tissue.
- **File mapping:** the PRIDE v3 file list was compared with the MaxQuant `experimentalDesignTemplate.txt`, read from the zip by HTTP range. Experiment and fraction match for all 680 files. The 2015 `*_CS_21*` raws are experiments 1A/1B/1C, consistent with the EV2 columns.
- **Instruments:** raw-header spot checks give `Animal_`/`Kermit_` 2015 = Q Exactive Plus and `20160706_` = Q Exactive HF.
- **Ontology terms:** every controlled term resolves in OLS4 with an exact label, and none is obsolete.
- **Deterministic tools:**
  - `parse_sdrf validate-sdrf` with ms-proteomics, human, clinical-metadata and oncology-metadata: exit 0.
  - `sdrf-tools check`: CLEAN.
  - `sdrf-tools score`: 98/100.
  - `sdrf-tools structure`: OK.

## Findings (all minor)
1. **Age on surgical rows:** EV1 `Age` (listed under Diagnosis) is written on the B and C rows. It matches neither age at diagnosis nor age at surgery consistently: it is off by 1 year from one of them for 21 patients. This is disclosed in the manifest. Optional note.
2. **Columns:** RFS and OS are in free-text `clinical data`, although the template has `characteristics[survival time]`. Miller & Payne and relapse are custom columns, although `treatment response` exists. The manifest's "no template column fits" should be reworded.
3. **Fractions 6 and 7** on the 14 early-batch samples are re-injections of pooled SCX fractions 1–3 and 4–5. This is as the deposited search defines them, and it is disclosed.
4. **Manifest counts:** the instrument claims say 330 and 359 raws. The actual counts are 328 Animal/Kermit and 361 20160706 raws.
5. **Reference disease:** the reference uses `not applicable` for disease, following the precedent. `not available` would be more conservative.
